Experimental Section
Materials
K2CO3, NaI, TiCl4, Bis(triphenylphosphine)palladium(II) dichloride, tetrakis(triphenylphosphine)palladium(0) , malononitrile,
ammonium acetate, pyridine, trimethylamine methanol solution (30%), bis(pinacolato)diboron, tetrahydrofuran, dioxane,
dichloromethane, toluene, 3-(4,5-Dimethylthiazol-2-yl)-2,5-diphenyl tetrazolium bromide (MTT), Dimethylsulfoxide (DMSO), 9,10-
anthracenediyl-bis(methylene) dimalonic acid (ABDA) and all other materials for organic synthesis were purchased from Sigma-
Aldrich and used without further purification. Hoechst 33342, CellMask™ Green Plasma Membrane Stain, fetal bovine serum (FBS),
and phosphate-buffered saline (PBS) buffer were purchased from Invitrogen. Compound 4-(4-(2,2-bis(4-methoxyphenyl)-1-
phenylvinyl)phenyl)-7 bromobenzo[c][1,2,5]thiadi-azole was synthesized according the reference.[S1] (4-bromophenyl)(3',5'-dihydroxy-
[1,1'-biphenyl]-4-yl)methanone and (4-bromophenyl)(4'-hydroxy-[1, 1'-biphenyl]-4-yl)methanone was synthesized according the
reference.[S2] All synthetic reactions of TBD-3C were carried out according to previous literature. [S3]
Characterization Techniques
1H NMR spectra of the intermediates and final products were measured on a Bruker NMR spectrometer. The ultraviolet-visible (UV-
vis) and fluorescence spectra were recorded using an Shimadzu UV-1700 and Edinburgh FS5 spectrometers, respectively. Leica
SP8 confocal laser scanning microscope (CLSM) was used for confocal imaging. Flow Cytometry analysis was carried on a CytoFlex
LX (Beckman Coulter). Observation of cell morphology was carried on a Field emission scanning electron microscope (FESEM,
JEOL JSM-7610F Plus).
Cell culture
Three cancer cell lines, i.e. Human uterine carcinoma cell (HeLa), mouse breast tumor cells (4T1), and rat glioma cells (C6) were
obtained commercially (American Type Culture Collection (ATCC), USA) and were cultured in Dulbecco’s Modified Eagle Medium
(DMEM) (Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc, USA) supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS) (Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., USA)
and 10 μg/mL gentamicin (Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc, USA). All cell cultures were maintained in T25 flasks at 37°C in a humidified
incubator containing 5% CO2.

Flow cytometry study
All the cells were incubated in the 6-well plate (Costar, IL, USA) at a density of 2×105 cells/well for 20 h and then cultured with
different AIE PSs for selected time. After incubation, the cells were washed twice with 1× PBS (pH = 7.4) buffer and then treated with
trypsin. After washed with 1× PBS (pH = 7.4) twice, the cells were subjected to flow cytometry analysis on a CytoFlex LX (Beckman
Coulter). The mean fluorescence intensity and cell labeling efficiency were determined by counting 10,000 events of each sample.
Cell ablation studies
A cell counting Kit-8 (CCK-8) assay was used to evaluate the cell viabilities before and after photodynamic therapy. All cells were
planted in the 96-well plates at a density of 4×104 cells/mL and cultured for 20 h. After 2 h incubation with different AIE PSs in DMEM
medium with various concentrations (from 0 μM to 25μM), the white light at a power of 40 mW/cm2 was then applied to designated
wells for 10 min. All cells with or without light irradiation were cultivated for another 24 h to support the cell proliferation. After 24 h
culturing, 10 μL of CCK-8 solution was added to each well, and the 96-well plate was subsequently incubated at 37°C for 2 h. The
optical density (OD) value was determined at a wavelength of 450 nm to detect the cell viability using Genios Tecan microplate
reader. The cell viability was determined by the ratio of absorbance at 450 nm for treated cells to the cells incubated with culture
medium only.
Lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) and cytokines release assays
Pyroptosis was analyzed by detecting the activity of lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) released into cell culture supernatants with the
LDH kit (CytoTox 96 Non-Radioactive Cytotoxicity Assay Kit) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Meanwhile, the IL-18 and
IL-1β in culture supernatants were quantified by ELISA method.
SEM imaging
The cells were seeded in a 24-well plate containing silicon slice at a density of 6×104 cell/mL and further incubated with complete
medium at 37 °C in 5% of CO2 for 24 hours for sufficiently adherence. The cells were cultured with the AIE PSs for 1h and then upon
light irradiation at a power of 40 mW/cm2 for 2 min, 5 min and 10 min. After treatment, the cells were washed with PBS and fixed in
2.5% glutaraldehyde at 37 °C. Then, sequential dehydration was applied for 15 min each with 25%, 40%, 60%, 80%, 90% and 100%
ethanol, followed by drying in a desiccator. The cells were viewed under a field emission scanning electron microscope (FESEM).
Western blotting analysis

polyvinylidene difluoride (PVDF) membranes (Bio-Rad). Following the blockage of nonspecific sites with 0.2% I-Block reagent
solution containing bovine saline albumin (5%) and incubation with indicated primary antibodies, the PVDF membranes were
subjected to horse radish peroxidase (HRP)-conjugated secondary antibodies. A chemiluminescence (ECL) system (Thermo Fisher)
was used to detect the immunoreactive bands. ImageJ software was employed to quantify the densitometric values of the resulting
bands. The primary antibodies of anti-mGSDMD, anti-cleaved mGSDMD, anti-cleaved caspase-1, anti-caspase-1, anti-caspase-3,
anti-cleaved caspase 3, anti-Bax, anti-Bcl-2 and anti-β-actin were purchased from Abcam (Cambridge, UK).
Flow cytometry
For flow cytometry analyses, 4T1 cells with various treatments (control, TBD-1C, TBD-2C and TBD-3C) upon light irradiation or under
dark were collected, washed with PBS twice and stained using the annexin V-FITC/propidium iodide (PI) Apoptosis Assay Kit
(ThermoFisher) according to the manufacturer’s protocols. The stained cells were detected on a Beckman Coulter's CytoFLEX flow
cytometer and data were processed with FlowJo software. Annexin-V can recognize phosphatidylserine exposed on the external
leaflet of the plasma membrane in apoptotic cells but can also label pyroptotic cells owing to the membrane rupture, which allows
annexin-V to diffuse into cells and recognize phosphatidylserine on the inner leaflet. PI, a non-cell-permeable DNA dye can only
stained the cells with membrane rupture. The cells only stained with annexin V-FITC (singlepositive cells) indicate apoptotic cells and
the cells stained with both PI and annexin V-FITC (double-positive cells) demonstrate pyroptotic cells. [S4]
