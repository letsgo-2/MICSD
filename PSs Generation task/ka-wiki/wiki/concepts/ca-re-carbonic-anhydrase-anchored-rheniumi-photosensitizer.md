# CA-Re carbonic anhydrase-anchored rhenium(I) photosensitizer

**CA-Re** is a rhenium(I) photosensitizer described as a [[carbonic anhydrase IX]]-anchored complex developed for photodynamic anti-tumor studies. In the source material, it is identified as `CA-Re [Re(CO)3(IP-TT)L]PF6` and presented in the context of a study titled *A Carbonic Anhydrase IX (CAIX)-Anchored Rhenium(I) Photosensitizer Evokes Pyroptosis for Enhanced Anti-Tumor Immunity*. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:8-13, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:156-173]

## Composition and synthesis

CA-Re is formulated as `[Re(CO)3(IP-TT)L]PF6`, where the ligand `L` is `N1-(pyridin-4-ylmethyl)-N4-(4-sulfamoylphenyl)succinamide`. The supporting information states that the complex was synthesized from `[Re(CO)5Cl]` and `IP-TT` through a DMF intermediate, followed by reaction with ligand `L`, flash chromatography purification, and precipitation with `NH4PF6` to give orange solids in 39.6% yield. Mass spectrometry and NMR data are reported for characterization. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:142-173]

## Photophysical properties

The supporting information reports solvent-dependent absorption and emission behavior for CA-Re. In deaerated solution at 20 μM, the absorption maxima were 422 nm in PBS, 410 nm in CH2Cl2, and 408 nm in CH3CN; emission maxima were 685 nm in PBS, 691 nm in CH2Cl2, and 493 nm in CH3CN, with low emission quantum yields in all three solvents. The authors further discuss the 685 nm emission as being attributable to `3MLCT`, while the 450-550 nm band is associated with the organic chromophore `IP-TT`, with the dominant emission depending on solvent effects. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:407-416, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:491-507]

The source also includes transient absorption measurements in deaerated CH2Cl2, a computed [[Jablonski diagram]], and excited-state calculations. Reported assignments include several singlet `ILCT` and `MLCT` transitions, along with triplet-state transitions and a listed `3O2 -> 1O2` energy relation relevant to [[singlet oxygen]] generation. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:184-195, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:422-424, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:498-507]

## Reactive oxygen species generation

The supporting methods describe assays for [[singlet oxygen]], hydroxyl radicals, and ESR-detectable radical species under light irradiation. Singlet oxygen production was measured with ABDA under 425 nm irradiation using `[Ru(bpy)3]Cl2` as a standard, while hydroxyl radical generation was monitored with HPF fluorescence. ESR experiments used DMPO as a spin trap in irradiated methanolic solution containing CA-Re. Corresponding figures report ABDA decay, HPF fluorescence spectra, and ESR signals after 425 nm irradiation. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:196-218, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:430-434]

In cellular experiments, intracellular ROS in normoxic MDA-MB-231 cells was measured with DCFH-DA after 24 h incubation with CA-Re followed by 425 nm irradiation. Additional experiments assessed specific ROS involvement by applying scavengers for hydrogen peroxide, hydroxyl radicals, superoxide anions, singlet oxygen, peroxynitrite, and lipid peroxides before irradiation and flow-cytometric analysis. Separate assays were also described for intracellular superoxide anion detection with DHE and lipid peroxidation measurement with an MDA assay. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:259-293]

## Carbonic anhydrase interaction and anchoring rationale

CA-Re was evaluated for inhibition of several [[carbonic anhydrase]] isoforms using a 4-nitrophenylacetate hydrolysis assay. The discussion accompanying Figure S12 states that CA-Re inhibited CAIX, CAII, CAVB, and CAXII with similar in vitro IC50 values of about 3.7 μM, indicating little isoform selectivity in the enzyme assay. The same discussion attributes inhibition to the sulfonamide moiety coordinating the zinc ion in the catalytic cavity. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:225-228, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:439-445]

The source argues that selectivity in living cells is expected to arise from localization rather than intrinsic enzyme selectivity. It states that CA-Re would encounter transmembrane CAIX first and that, as shown in the associated figure discussion, the complex remained anchored on the cell membrane rather than entering the cytoplasm even after 24 h incubation, thereby favoring interaction with transmembrane CAIX/CAXII in cells. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:246-251, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:445-449]

## Biological evaluation

The supporting information describes in vitro cytotoxicity testing of CA-Re and cisplatin by MTT assay, using both dark conditions and light activation. Cells were irradiated after incubation with either a 425 nm laser for 10 min or a 525 nm laser for 60 min. In 4T1 cells, Table S3 reports dark IC50 values greater than 100 μM for CA-Re under both normoxia and hypoxia, while light-activated IC50 values were 0.036 μM at 425 nm and 0.210 μM at 525 nm under normoxia, and 0.042 μM at 425 nm and 0.220 μM at 525 nm under hypoxia. Reported phototoxicity indices were 2780 and 475 under normoxia, and 2380 and 455 under hypoxia, respectively. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:236-245, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:508-520]

The methods also describe experiments used to examine cellular uptake, localization, membrane morphology, extracellular ATP release, HMGB1 and calreticulin staining, and western blot analysis of CAIX, HIF-1α, GPX4, GSDMD-N, and related proteins. In vivo, CA-Re was tested in a bilateral 4T1 tumor model in immunocompetent BALB/c mice, with intratumoral administration to the primary tumor and 525 nm irradiation, while distant tumors were used to assess abscopal and immune-related effects. Flow cytometry and ELISA-based assays were then used to analyze dendritic cells, T cells, and cytokines including IL-12p70, IL-6, TNF-α, IL-10, IL-18, and IL-1β. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:246-257, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:294-364, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:376-381]

## Experimental context

The supporting information includes methods for molecular docking using the cocrystallized CAIX-AAZ complex (`PDB: 3IAI`) as the starting structure, as well as HPLC-based photostability and reduction studies. Reported HPLC retention times were 3.508 min for ligand `L` and 15.867 min for CA-Re. The document notes that solubility limitations affected intravenous use in the in vivo study design, contributing to the choice of intratumoral administration for evaluation of local and distant tumor effects. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:174-183, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:219-224, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:376-381]
