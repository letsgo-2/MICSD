# NaHCO3 nanoparticles induce caspase-1/GSDMD-dependent pyroptosis

`NaHCO3` nanoparticles (`NaHCO3 NPs`) are described in the source as a drug-free inorganic nanomaterial that can trigger [[pyroptosis]] in tumor cells. In the study, the particles were designed not only to regulate [[lactic acid metabolism]] through acid-base neutralization, but also to release large amounts of `Na+` inside tumor cells, increasing intracellular osmolarity and activating inflammatory cell death pathways linked to antitumor immunity. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:55-72, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:123-147]

## Mechanistic basis

The source reports that `NaHCO3 NPs` act as an intracellular `Na+` reservoir. After uptake by 4T1 tumor cells, they increased intracellular `Na+` levels, raised intracellular osmolarity, and enhanced reactive oxygen species (`ROS`), which the authors present as upstream events leading to pyroptotic cell death. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:181-187, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:240-253]

Morphological and ultrastructural observations were consistent with [[pyroptosis]]. Compared with control and free sodium bicarbonate groups, `NaHCO3 NP`-treated 4T1 cells showed obvious cellular swelling with large bubbles, as well as cytomembrane rupture and cellular cavitation on bio-TEM. The study also notes mitochondrial swelling and cavitation under treatment, which it attributes to osmotic-pressure imbalance and ROS generation. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:253-278]

## Caspase-1/GSDMD dependence

The paper directly links the pyroptotic effect of `NaHCO3 NPs` to the [[caspase-1]]/[[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] axis. Immunofluorescence showed increased caspase-1 in the nanoparticle-treated group, and western blot analysis confirmed increased cleaved caspase-1. The authors also tested gasdermin-family proteins and found upregulation of the `GSDMD-N` fragment, identifying this as evidence that `NaHCO3 NPs` activate a caspase-1/GSDMD-dependent pyroptosis pathway. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:279-292]

The source explains this interpretation by noting that cleavage of gasdermin proteins by inflammatory caspases is a key step in pyroptosis because the resulting fragments bind membrane phospholipids and form pores in the cell membrane. In this study, the observed increase in `GSDMD-N` was therefore used as molecular evidence that pore-forming gasdermin activity accompanied caspase-1 activation in treated tumor cells. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:284-292]

## Functional readouts of pyroptosis

Consistent with membrane pore formation and loss of membrane integrity, `NaHCO3 NP` treatment caused strong release of intracellular and inflammatory contents. The level of [[lactate dehydrogenase|LDH]] in culture medium increased to 221-fold and 52-fold relative to the control and free sodium bicarbonate groups, respectively, and `IL-1beta` release was also higher than in the comparison groups. The authors interpret the marked LDH and `IL-1beta` release as evidence of strong pyroptosis. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:292-302]

The study further states that `NaHCO3 NPs` induced features of [[immunogenic cell death]] (`ICD`) alongside pyroptosis. In the nanoparticle group, extracellular `ATP` was about 5.9-fold and 5.5-fold higher than in the control and free sodium bicarbonate groups, and calreticulin (`CRT`) exposure was also increased. These DAMP-related changes are presented as mechanisms that can strengthen antigen presentation and downstream antitumor immune responses. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:319-332]

## Biological context in the study

The in vitro pyroptosis findings were generated in 4T1 mouse breast cancer cells, which the authors selected because of their high glycolytic metabolism. In the same study, `NaHCO3 NPs` showed concentration-dependent cytotoxicity in 4T1 cells, with viability dropping below 10% at `400 ug/mL` and an `IC50` of `168 ug/mL`, while toxicity in L929 fibroblasts was lower. The authors suggest this difference may reflect the higher intracellular ion concentrations of cancer cells and their greater sensitivity to osmolarity imbalance. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:205-239]

In vivo, the study places this pyroptosis mechanism within a broader immunotherapy strategy. The authors report that `NaHCO3 NPs` combined pyroptosis/ICD induction with neutralization of lactic-acid-driven tumor acidity, leading to greater dendritic-cell maturation, increased tumor-infiltrating `CD4+` and `CD8+` T cells, polarization from `M2` to `M1` tumor-associated macrophages, and reduced `MDSC` and `Treg` frequencies. They further report inhibition of primary and distal tumor growth and reduced pulmonary metastasis. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:382-433, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:447-506]
