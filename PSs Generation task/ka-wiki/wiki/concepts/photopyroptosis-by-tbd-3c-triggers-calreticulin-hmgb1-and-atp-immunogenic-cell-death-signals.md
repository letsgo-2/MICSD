# Photopyroptosis by TBD-3C triggers calreticulin, HMGB1, and ATP immunogenic cell-death signals

`TBD-3C` is a membrane-targeted, aggregation-induced emission ([[AIE]]) [[photosensitizer]] used in [[photodynamic therapy]] to induce [[pyroptosis]] in pancreatic cancer models. In this study, the authors investigated whether photodynamic pyroptosis driven by `TBD-3C` also produced hallmarks of [[immunogenic cell death]] (ICD). ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:153-169, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:469-477]

The reported ICD-associated signals were [[calreticulin|calreticulin (CRT)]], [[HMGB1]], and [[ATP]]. In the study’s framework, these molecules were treated as danger-associated or immunogenic signals linked to pyroptosis-mediated ICD, and were measured after `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment in pancreatic tumor models. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:134-143, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:267-279, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:468-477]

## Evidence for ICD signals

In vitro, the authors describe `CRT` as a typical feature of ICD and state that `CRT` shifts to the cell membrane to facilitate antigen-presenting-cell recruitment, recognition, and antigen presentation. Using an anti-`CRT` antibody, they found that KPC and Panc02 cells treated with `TBD-3C` under irradiation showed significantly enhanced `CRT` expression on the cell surface. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:267-279]

In vivo, the study further examined `CRT` and `HMGB1` in tumor tissue as markers related to pyroptosis-mediated ICD. By western blot and immunofluorescence, the `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment group showed higher expression of both `HMGB1` and `CRT` in KPC and Panc02 tumor models than the control group. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:463-475, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:557-559]

The study also monitored `ATP` release as an important ICD marker. Tumors treated with `TBD-3C` photodynamic therapy showed increased `ATP` secretion relative to controls. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:475-477, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:557-559]

## Relationship to pyroptosis

The ICD-signal measurements were presented in the context of photodynamically induced pyroptosis. The same in vivo treatment condition that increased `CRT`, `HMGB1`, and `ATP` also showed higher LDH release and more cleavage of full-length [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], which the authors used as evidence that `TBD-3C` induced pyroptosis in tumors. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:463-477]

Earlier cell-based experiments in KPC and Panc02 cells likewise supported a pyroptotic mechanism under light exposure. `TBD-3C` treatment increased reactive oxygen species, caused cell swelling and membrane bubbling, elevated LDH release, increased `N-GSDMD`, and raised [[caspase-1]] activity, while obvious apoptotic features were not detected under irradiation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:195-216, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:226-264]

## Immunologic significance in the study

The paper links these ICD-associated signals to downstream immune activation. It states that pyroptotic cells can emit danger signals needed for recruitment and maturation of antigen-presenting cells, and that `CRT` exposure helps initiate immune responses by facilitating APC recruitment, recognition, and antigen presentation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:134-143, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:271-275]

Consistent with that interpretation, the study reports that `TBD-3C`-induced pyroptotic cells promoted M1 polarization of macrophages, maturation of dendritic cells, and activation of `CD8+` cytotoxic T lymphocytes in vitro and in vivo. The authors therefore position `CRT`, `HMGB1`, and `ATP` release as part of a broader pyroptosis-driven immunogenic program that helps convert pancreatic tumors from an immunosuppressive "cold" state to a more immunogenic "hot" microenvironment. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:24-31, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:326-353, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:400-436, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:592-595]
