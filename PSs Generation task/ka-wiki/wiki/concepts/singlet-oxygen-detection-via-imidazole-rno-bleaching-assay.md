# Singlet oxygen detection via imidazole-RNO bleaching assay

The imidazole-RNO bleaching assay is a method for detecting [[singlet oxygen]] by monitoring a change in absorbance after a sequence of reactions involving imidazole and N,N-dimethyl-4-nitrosoaniline (RNO). In the provided source, it is described as a well-established protocol used to evaluate whether an illuminated material can generate singlet oxygen. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:73-76]

## Principle

In this assay, singlet oxygen reacts first with imidazole, denoted as A in the source. The source describes this as capture of ^1O2 by the imidazole ring, producing a trans-annular peroxide intermediate, AO2. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:77-85]

The intermediate AO2 then reacts with RNO and induces bleaching of RNO to `-RNO` and other products. This bleaching causes a change in absorbance at 440 nm, which can be recorded using a UV-VIS spectrometer. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:82-86]

## Reaction scheme

The source gives the detection mechanism as the following sequence: singlet oxygen plus imidazole forms an intermediate `[AO2]`, which proceeds to `AO2`; `AO2` then reacts with RNO to yield bleached `-RNO` and products. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:77-85]

## Readout

The assay readout is the absorbance change at 440 nm. According to the source, this spectral change is the measurable consequence of RNO bleaching and is recorded with a UV-VIS spectrometer. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:84-86]

## Use in the source study

In the cited study, the assay was used to evaluate the capability of Gd-PNPs to generate singlet oxygen under illumination. The source presents the imidazole-RNO mechanism specifically in that context. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:73-76]
