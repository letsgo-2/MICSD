# Mitochondrial localization of the photodrugs

Mitochondrial localization of the photodrugs refers to the subcellular distribution analysis performed for the study’s photodrugs in U251-MG cells using confocal laser scanning microscopy after co-staining with [[MitoTracker]] or [[Lysotracker]]. In the supporting information, this localization was evaluated after incubating cells with the photodrugs at `0.5 μM` for `30 min`, followed by organelle staining and colocalization analysis, including Pearson’s correlation coefficients. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:217-232, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:443-445]

## Experimental assessment

The source describes intracellular distribution measurements by confocal laser scanning microscopy in U251-MG cells cultured in confocal dishes. Cells were incubated with photodrugs at a fixed concentration of `0.5 μM` for `30 min`, then washed and stained either with [[Lysotracker]] Green DND 26 to assess endo-/lysosomal colocalization or with [[MitoTracker]] Green FM to confirm mitochondrial distribution. Imaging used separate blue, green, and far-red channels for Hoechst, organelle dyes, and photodrugs, respectively. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:217-232]

Figure S18 is identified as the key source for this experiment. It contains colocalization images of photodrugs shown in red with mitochondria or lysosomes shown in green, together with the corresponding Pearson’s correlation coefficients after `30 min` of incubation with cells. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:443-445]

## Study context

The supporting information frames this mitochondrial localization as part of the characterization of a [[mitochondria-targeting type-I photodrug]]. The document title names the work as “Mitochondria-Targeting Type-I Photodrug: Harnessing Caspase-3 Activity for Pyroptotic Oncotherapy,” and Table S2 classifies the approach developed in this study as “mitochondria targeting” in contrast to other reported photodrug strategies that are described as non-specific, plasma-membrane-targeting, or endosome-targeting. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:9-13, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:523-542]

## What the supporting information specifically establishes

From the provided supporting information, the directly stated evidence for mitochondrial localization is that the authors performed mitochondria-versus-lysosome colocalization imaging and quantified the result with Pearson’s correlation coefficients in Figure S18. The excerpted source material does not include the numerical Pearson values or a prose interpretation of which individual photodrugs showed stronger mitochondrial localization than others. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:443-445]
