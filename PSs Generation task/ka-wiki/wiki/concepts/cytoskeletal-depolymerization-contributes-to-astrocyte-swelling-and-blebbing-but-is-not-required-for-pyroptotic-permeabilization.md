# Cytoskeletal depolymerization contributes to astrocyte swelling and blebbing but is not required for pyroptotic permeabilization

In pyroptotic astrocytes, cell swelling and membrane blebbing are characteristic morphological changes, and this study examined how intracellular mechanical and osmotic factors contribute to those changes. The authors report that protein nanoparticle-induced osmotic pressure (PN-OP) is closely associated with inflammasome production and cytoskeletal depolymerization, and that these processes shape the deformation of pyroptotic astrocytes. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:18-30]

## Overview

The study used pyroptotic astrocyte models to test whether cytoskeletal depolymerization helps drive the visible morphology of pyroptosis. Under `4-OHT` stimulation, astrocytes showed increased cytoplasmic osmotic pressure, increased intermediate filament tension, increased intracellular protein nanoparticle counts, and elevated intracellular `Ca2+` and `Cl-`, together with swelling, blebbing, and membrane permeabilization. Similar overall trends were also observed in an `LPS-NIG` pyroptosis model. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:281-309]

## Evidence from cytoskeletal stabilization

To test the role of cytoskeletal depolymerization, the authors stabilized [[microfilaments]] with jasplakinolide and [[microtubules]] with taxol. These treatments partly attenuated `4-OHT`-induced increases in cytoplasmic osmotic pressure, intermediate filament tension, intracellular protein nanoparticle number, and intracellular `Ca2+` and `Cl-` levels. They also reduced cell swelling and membrane blebbing, with combined treatment showing a stronger effect on blebbing than either agent alone. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:310-351]

Despite these effects on morphology and osmotic readouts, cytoskeletal stabilization did not reduce pyroptotic permeabilization as measured by propidium iodide positivity, and it did not suppress caspase-1 activity. The study therefore concludes that decreasing protein nanoparticles through cytoskeletal stabilization attenuated swelling and blebbing but failed to suppress pyroptotic permeabilization. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:352-377]

The same interpretation was reinforced when nonselective membrane pores were blocked with `PEG8000`. Adding cytoskeletal stabilizers on top of `PEG8000` partly decreased blebbing, cytoplasmic osmotic pressure, protein nanoparticle number, intermediate filament tension, and intracellular ion levels, but still did not change membrane permeabilization or caspase-1 activity relative to the `4-OHT-PEG8000` condition. The authors state that this confirms cytoskeleton stabilization suppresses osmolarity-dependent astrocyte swelling rather than membrane permeabilization. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:510-534]

## Mechanistic interpretation

The paper proposes that cytoskeletal depolymerization contributes to pyroptotic deformation by generating additional intracellular protein nanoparticles, including actin and tubulin species derived from depolymerized [[microfilaments]] and [[microtubules]]. These particles can contribute to PN-OP, which in turn promotes astrocyte swelling and blebbing. However, the authors argue that this cytoskeletal contribution is an accompanying event and not the dominant determinant of pyroptotic membrane permeabilization. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:705-720]

In the study’s broader model, [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3 inflammasomes]] are the main source of the protein nanoparticles that elevate osmotic pressure during astrocytic [[pyroptosis]]. Inflammasome production and cytoskeletal depolymerization both increase intracellular nanoparticles and osmotic pressure, promoting swelling and blebbing, but permeabilization is more directly tied to downstream pore-forming events such as [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] activation. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:691-704, inflamasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:736-768]

## Relationship to pyroptotic permeabilization

The authors explicitly state that inflammasome-induced osmotic pressure increase is necessary for astrocyte swelling and blebbing but is not absolutely required for membrane permeabilization. In this framework, swelling and blebbing reflect a mechanically regulated deformation stage of astrocytic pyroptosis, whereas high permeabilization and cell disintegration occur later with massive nonselective ion and water influx through [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] pores. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:736-760]

This distinction explains the page’s central claim: cytoskeletal depolymerization contributes to astrocyte swelling and blebbing because it adds to PN-OP, but it is not required for pyroptotic permeabilization. Permeabilization can persist even when cytoskeletal stabilization reduces osmotic and morphological changes, indicating that the mechanisms governing astrocyte deformation and membrane permeabilization are related but separable within pyroptosis. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:370-377, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:736-768]
