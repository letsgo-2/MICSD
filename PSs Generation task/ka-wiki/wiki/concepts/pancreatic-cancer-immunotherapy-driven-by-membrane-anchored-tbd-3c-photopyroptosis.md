# Pancreatic-cancer immunotherapy driven by membrane-anchored TBD-3C photopyroptosis

`Pancreatic-cancer immunotherapy driven by membrane-anchored TBD-3C photopyroptosis` refers to a strategy in which the membrane-targeted, aggregation-induced emission ([[aggregation-induced emission|AIE]]) photosensitizer `TBD-3C` is used in [[photodynamic therapy]] to trigger [[pyroptosis]] in pancreatic cancer cells and thereby reshape the tumor immune microenvironment toward antitumor immunity. The study presents this approach as a response to the limited efficacy of existing pancreatic-cancer immunotherapies in an immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:15-31, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:120-176]

## Rationale

The source describes pancreatic cancer as highly lethal, poorly responsive to conventional local and systemic therapies, and difficult to treat with immunotherapy because of its complex, immunosuppressive [[tumor microenvironment]]. It frames [[pyroptosis]] as a highly inflammatory form of programmed cell death that can relieve immunosuppression, promote systemic immune responses, release tumor-associated antigens and danger-associated molecular patterns, and help convert an immune “cold” tumor into a “hot” one. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:56-76, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:120-152]

The study positions `TBD-3C` as a noninvasive, light-controlled way to induce pyroptosis through [[photodynamic therapy]], in contrast to previously reported pyroptosis-inducing chemotherapeutic approaches that are limited by side effects and drug resistance. The authors specifically evaluate whether `TBD-3C`-induced pyroptosis can reverse suppressive tumor microenvironment features and produce immunotherapeutic effects against pancreatic cancer in vitro and in vivo. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:144-176]

## TBD-3C and photopyroptosis

In murine pancreatic cancer cell lines `KPC` and `Panc02`, `TBD-3C` localized mainly to the cell membrane, with successful membrane anchoring achieved after `30 min` incubation at `10 uM`. Under white-light irradiation, treated cells showed increased reactive oxygen species generation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:179-203]

The reported cell-death phenotype was consistent with pyroptosis rather than apoptosis. Irradiated `TBD-3C`-labeled cells developed swelling and large membrane bubbles, showed increased lactate dehydrogenase release, increased `GSDMD` cleavage to `N-GSDMD`, and elevated caspase-1 activity. The proportion of annexin V/propidium iodide double-positive cells increased with longer irradiation, while obvious apoptotic features were not detected in the `TBD-3C`-treated irradiated cells. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:203-264]

The extent of pyroptosis increased with longer light exposure. In viability assays under illumination, the reported `IC50` values for `TBD-3C` were `4 uM` in `KPC` cells and `0.5 uM` in `Panc02` cells, while dark cytotoxicity was negligible in both lines. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:211-216, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:255-264]

## In vitro immune activation

The study tested whether photodynamic pyroptosis induced by `TBD-3C` could trigger [[immunogenic cell death]]. As one marker, treated `KPC` and `Panc02` cells showed significantly increased surface exposure of [[calreticulin|CRT]] under irradiation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:267-280]

Using a co-culture system with pyroptotic tumor cells, bone-marrow-derived macrophages (`BMDMs`), bone-marrow-derived dendritic cells (`BMDCs`), and spleen-derived T lymphocytes, the authors found that pyroptotic tumor cells promoted macrophage polarization toward an `M1`-like phenotype and induced dendritic-cell maturation. `CD86` and `MHC II` expression increased on macrophages, `CD206` decreased, and `CD86` increased on dendritic cells, with patterns comparable to the `LPS` positive-control condition. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:316-353]

Macrophages exposed to pyroptotic tumor cells also showed enhanced phagocytic clearance of fresh `KPC` and `Panc02` cells. The study further reports that pre-existing `M2`-like macrophages could be re-educated toward an `M1`-like state after co-culture with pyroptotic tumor cells, based on increased `CD86` and `MHC II` expression. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:354-399]

When T lymphocytes were co-incubated with pyroptotic tumor cells together with macrophages and dendritic cells, `CD8+` T cells showed increased proliferation and maturation. In the same setting, `TBD-3C`-aroused pyroptotic cells markedly increased perforin-positive and granzyme-B-positive `CD8+` T cells, supporting activation of [[cytotoxic T lymphocyte|cytotoxic T-lymphocyte]] responses. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:400-436]

## Local antitumor effects in vivo

In immunocompetent `C57` mice bearing subcutaneous `KPC` or `Panc02` tumors, intratumoral `TBD-3C` followed by white-light irradiation significantly inhibited tumor growth relative to `PBS` control, as shown by tumor weights and growth curves. The study reports no body-weight loss, longer survival in treated mice, no inhibitory effect without irradiation, no hemocytolysis, no organ toxicity, and no abnormal blood biochemistry, supporting good biocompatibility under the tested conditions. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:439-493, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:840-850]

Tumors from the treated mice showed evidence of pyroptosis and immunogenic cell death in vivo, including increased lactate dehydrogenase release, greater `GSDMD` cleavage, higher `HMGB1` and `CRT` expression, and increased `ATP` release. Immunohistochemistry further showed lower `Ki-67`, reduced `alpha-SMA`, and increased `CD8+` T-cell infiltration after `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:463-486]

## Tumor-microenvironment remodeling

Flow-cytometric analysis of local tumors showed that `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment increased the proportion of tumor-infiltrating `CD8+` cytotoxic T lymphocytes and increased activation-associated markers `CD69` and `CD103` on those cells. `PD-1` expression on intratumoral cytotoxic T lymphocytes was reduced, and serum `TNF-alpha` and `IFN-gamma` concentrations were elevated relative to controls. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:494-524]

High-dimensional [[mass cytometry|CyTOF]] profiling of `KPC` tumors showed broad remodeling of the immune landscape after treatment. The authors report downregulation of immunosuppressive markers including `PD-L1`, `Lag3`, and `Tim3`, increased `IFN-gamma` expression on `CD8+` T cells, increased numbers of immune-active cell populations such as `gamma delta T` cells, `NK` cells, and `CD4+ TEMRA` cells, and reduced [[myeloid-derived suppressor cell|MDSCs]]. They interpret these changes as conversion of the pancreatic tumor microenvironment from immunosuppressive “cold” to immunogenic “hot.” ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:525-595]

## Effects on distant orthotopic tumors

The study also tested whether local treatment could drive systemic antitumor immunity. In a combined model with a subcutaneous `KPC-Luc` local tumor and an orthotopic pancreatic `KPC-Luc` distant tumor in the same `C57BL/6` mouse, only the local subcutaneous tumor received intratumoral `TBD-3C` followed by irradiation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:596-612, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:857-874]

Under this design, `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment inhibited growth of both the treated subcutaneous tumor and the untreated orthotopic tumor, as measured by in vivo bioluminescence imaging and final tumor weights. The spleen analysis showed a higher proportion of effector-memory T cells, which the authors interpret as an immune-memory effect associated with distant-tumor inhibition. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:612-637, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:932-939]

Immune profiling of the distant orthotopic tumors showed increased infiltration of `CD8+` T cells, `IFN-gamma+ CD8+` T cells, and perforin-positive `CD8+` T cells, together with reduced `Tregs`, reduced monocytic and granulocytic `MDSCs`, and increased `M1` macrophages without a significant change in `M2` macrophages. The authors present these findings as evidence that local `TBD-3C` photopyroptosis can induce systemic immunological responses capable of attacking distant pancreatic tumors. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:637-661]

## Interpretation

Within this study, membrane-anchored `TBD-3C` photopyroptosis is presented as a light-controlled immunotherapy platform for pancreatic cancer that links local [[photodynamic therapy]]-induced [[pyroptosis]] to broader immune activation. The reported mechanism connects tumor-cell pyroptosis with [[immunogenic cell death]], [[antigen-presenting cell]] activation, [[macrophage polarization]] toward `M1`, enhanced `CD8+` T-cell responses, and reduction of immunosuppressive features in both local and distant tumor sites. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:24-31, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:662-674]
