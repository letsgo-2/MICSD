# Caspase-4/11-caspase-3-GSDME signaling pathway downstream of MPT

The caspase-4/11-caspase-3-GSDME signaling pathway downstream of [[mitochondrial permeability transition|MPT]] is a sterile pyroptotic pathway in which MPT activates caspase-4/11 through an [[Apaf-1 pyroptosome]], leading to caspase-3 cleavage, GSDME activation, and pyroptotic cell death. The source presents this pathway as distinct from the canonical LPS-triggered caspase-4/11 route that directly cleaves [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]]. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:58-66, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:133-140]

## Overview

In this study, persistent MPT induced by bile acids, calcium overload, or ANT1 activation was identified as the upstream trigger for caspase-4/11-dependent pyroptosis under sterile conditions. Rather than binding the stimulus directly, caspase-4/11 is activated after MPT promotes assembly of a protein complex containing Apaf-1 and caspase-4/11. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:58-66, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:133-140, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:223-235]

Downstream of this complex, caspase-4/11 acts upstream of caspase-3, and caspase-3 then cleaves [[GSDME]] to execute pyroptosis. In this MPT-linked setting, GSDMD is not the main executor: bile acid treatment did not generate the pore-forming GSDMD p30 fragment, and GSDME deficiency, rather than GSDMD deficiency, protected cells from pyroptosis. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:191-223, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:303-346]

## Upstream activation by MPT

The source states that bile acids do not directly bind or directly activate recombinant caspase-4/11, indicating that cell-intrinsic factors are required for caspase-4/11 activation in this context. Bile acid treatment instead induced persistent MPT, marked by loss of mitochondrial cristae, inner membrane integrity, and mitochondrial transmembrane potential. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:223-240, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:268-275]

Pharmacologic evidence linked this mitochondrial event to the downstream pyroptotic cascade. The ANT inhibitor bongkrekic acid blocked bile acid-induced MPT, reduced cell death and LDH release, and abolished activation of caspase-11/4, caspase-3, and GSDME. Similar results were observed when MPT was triggered by thapsigargin-induced calcium overload or by the ANT1-targeting agent lonidamine, supporting caspase-4/11 as a general sensor of MPT. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:276-302, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:303-346]

## [[apaf-1-pyroptosome|Apaf-1 pyroptosome]] assembly

To explain how MPT activates caspase-4/11, the authors identified an Apaf-1-caspase-4/11 complex. Co-immunoprecipitation, pull-down, and binding assays supported a specific interaction between Apaf-1 and caspase-4 at the CARD domain, while caspase-9 was not required for formation of this complex. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:347-367, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:392-410]

Functional experiments showed that Apaf-1 was required for this signaling route. In APAF-1 knockout HepG2 cells, bile acids and lonidamine failed to activate caspase-4, caspase-3, and GSDME and failed to induce pyroptotic cell death and LDH release. In a reconstituted cell-free system, Apaf-1 activated caspase-4 only in the presence of ATP and cytochrome c, and the authors therefore termed this MPT-triggered complex the [[Apaf-1 pyroptosome]]. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:411-467]

## Downstream caspase-3 and GSDME execution

The study places caspase-3 immediately downstream of caspase-4/11 in this pathway. In the reconstituted Apaf-1-caspase-4 system, pro-caspase-3, but not caspase-8, was specifically activated, and active recombinant caspase-4 directly cleaved caspase-3 in a cell-free assay. Consistently, caspase-3 was not activated in bile acid-treated Caspase-11-deficient macrophages. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:446-464]

GSDME functions as the terminal gasdermin effector in this cascade. Bile acids triggered caspase-11-dependent cleavage of GSDME to a p30 fragment, and both GSDME-knockout macrophages and CASPASE-3- or GSDME-silenced HepG2 cells were resistant to bile acid-induced pyroptosis. The source further notes that in this context GSDMD was cleaved by caspase-3 to an inactive p43 fragment rather than to the pore-forming p30 fragment. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:197-223]

## Relationship to apoptosis and other pyroptotic routes

The paper distinguishes this pathway from both [[apoptosome]]-mediated apoptosis and LPS-triggered noncanonical pyroptosis. Under MPT conditions induced by high-dose bile acids, caspase-11 and caspase-3 were rapidly activated, whereas caspase-9 and caspase-1 were not. By contrast, low-dose bile acids and doxorubicin promoted MOMP-associated Apaf-1/caspase-9 apoptosome assembly and apoptosis. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:522-573]

The source also reports that MPT caused earlier cytoplasmic cytochrome c release and an immediate increase in cytoplasmic ATP, and that caspase-4 recruitment to Apaf-1 required a higher ATP level than caspase-9 recruitment. The assembled pyroptosome showed an Apaf-1:caspase-4 stoichiometry of about 7:2, distinct from the 7:4 Apaf-1:caspase-9 stoichiometry described for the apoptosome. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:574-610]

In contrast to LPS-triggered caspase-4 activation, which directly cleaves GSDMD, caspase-4 activated in the [[apaf-1-pyroptosome|Apaf-1 pyroptosome]] preferentially activates caspase-3 and thereby GSDME. The authors propose that GSDMD cannot be correctly cleaved in this setting because it is not recruited to the caspase-4 pyroptosome as it is in the LPS-bound complex. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:734-758]

## Pathophysiologic relevance

The study connects this pathway to [[cholestatic liver injury]]. In bile duct-ligated mice, the authors observed persistent MPT, Apaf-1/caspase-11 pyroptosome formation, caspase-11 activation, and cleavage of caspase-3 and GSDME in liver tissue. In primary mouse hepatocytes, bile acid-induced MPT, caspase-11 activation, and GSDME cleavage were blocked by bongkrekic acid pretreatment. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:611-646]

Genetic evidence further supported a dominant contribution of this cascade in that model. Caspase-11 deficiency reduced caspase-3 activation, GSDME cleavage, and release of IL-1alpha, IL-1beta, and HMGB1 after bile duct ligation, while GSDME deficiency completely rescued bile duct ligation-induced mortality. Although the paper notes concurrent caspase-1/GSDMD activity, it concludes that caspase-11-caspase-3-GSDME-mediated pyroptosis is dominant in cholestatic liver failure. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:647-660]
