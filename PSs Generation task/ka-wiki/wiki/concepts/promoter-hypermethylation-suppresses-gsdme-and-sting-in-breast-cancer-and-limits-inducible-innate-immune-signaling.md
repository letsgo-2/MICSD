# Promoter hypermethylation suppresses GSDME and STING in breast cancer and limits inducible innate immune signaling

In the source study, reduced inducibility of both [[pyroptosis]] and the [[cGAS-STING pathway]] in breast cancer is linked to promoter hypermethylation of `DFNA5` (`GSDME`) and `TMEM173` (`STING`). The authors frame insufficient intracellular expression of GSDME and STING as a practical barrier to therapies that aim to trigger innate antitumor immunity through caspase-driven pyroptosis or cytosolic DNA sensing. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:49-66, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:88-109]

## Overview

The paper describes [[pyroptosis]] as an immunogenic cell death program in which caspases cleave gasdermin proteins to generate pore-forming N-terminal fragments, while the [[cGAS-STING pathway]] is presented as a cytosolic double-stranded DNA sensing pathway that activates `TBK1` and `IRF3` and promotes type I interferon and other immune-stimulated genes. In this framework, GSDME expression is required for caspase-3-driven pyroptosis, and STING expression is required for downstream signaling after cGAS detects cytosolic DNA. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:88-109]

In breast cancer, the authors specifically propose that promoter hypermethylation suppresses both `DFNA5` and `TMEM173`, thereby limiting the cell’s ability to convert upstream stress signals into these two innate immune outputs. They position pharmacologic demethylation as a way to restore this missing signaling capacity. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:120-132, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:284-316]

## Evidence in breast cancer

Analysis of public datasets in the study showed that both `DFNA5` and `TMEM173` were significantly downregulated in breast cancer tissues relative to normal tissues, and immunohistochemical images were presented as further validation of lower `GSDME` and `STING` expression in breast cancer samples. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:287-303, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:323-343]

The same analysis found that downregulation of `DFNA5` and `TMEM173` in breast tumors was strongly correlated with their promoter methylation status. Based on these results, the authors argue that hypermethylation is a plausible explanation for reduced expression of both genes in breast cancer and a key reason these tumors may respond weakly to efforts to induce GSDME-mediated pyroptosis or STING-dependent innate immune signaling. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:293-303]

## Why this limits inducible innate immune signaling

In the study’s photodynamic model, treatment with the photosensitizer nanoparticle `NP1` plus light generated intracellular ROS, caused mitochondrial dysfunction, increased cleaved caspase-3, and promoted cytosolic escape of mitochondrial DNA in `MCF-7` cells. These are the kinds of upstream events expected to support GSDME-mediated pyroptosis and activation of the [[cGAS-STING pathway]]. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:224-257]

However, despite cleaved caspase-3 and released cytosolic dsDNA, `NP1` plus light did not significantly upregulate key proteins involved in either pyroptosis or the cGAS-STING pathway. The paper directly connects this failure to the low-expression background of `GSDME` and `STING`, which it later attributes to promoter methylation of `DFNA5` and `TMEM173`. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:247-257, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:287-303]

This establishes the paper’s central point: upstream danger signals alone were not sufficient in these breast cancer cells, because the downstream executors needed to translate those signals into innate immune activation had been suppressed. In the authors’ model, promoter hypermethylation therefore limits inducible innate immune signaling by reducing the availability of both the pyroptotic effector `GSDME` and the adaptor `STING`. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:255-257, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:284-316]

## Decitabine-mediated restoration

The paper explains that [[DNA methyltransferase inhibitor|DNA methyltransferase inhibitors]] can block addition of new methylation marks and permit passive loss of existing marks during cell division, allowing previously silenced genes to be re-expressed. Among the candidates tested in `MCF-7` cells, `decitabine` was the only agent reported to restore both `GSDME` and `STING` expression effectively. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:304-316]

At `20 uM`, decitabine increased `GSDME` expression by `70.3%` and `STING` expression by `40.8%` relative to PBS-treated cells, whereas `azacytidine` and `gamma-oryzanol` showed no evident effect on expression of either protein in this assay. The authors therefore selected decitabine for subsequent combination experiments. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:309-316]

## Functional consequence of restoring GSDME and STING

When decitabine-loaded `NP2` was combined with `NP1` plus light, the death phenotype shifted away from predominantly apoptotic morphology toward pyroptotic morphology, including cell swelling and large plasma-membrane bubbles. This combination also increased `GSDME-N` and raised extracellular `ATP` and `LDH`, which the authors interpret as evidence that restored GSDME expression allowed caspase-3 activation to drive pyroptosis more effectively. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:364-385]

The same combined treatment also increased phosphorylation of `STING`, `TBK1`, and `IRF3`, indicating stronger activation of the [[cGAS-STING pathway]]. In the authors’ interpretation, restoring STING expression with decitabine enabled the mitochondrial-DNA signal generated by photodynamic injury to propagate into downstream innate immune signaling. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:386-406]

Conditioned by these combined effects, the treatment increased the proportion of mature `CD80+ CD86+` dendritic cells in vitro, and in vivo it was associated with higher tumor and lymph-node mature dendritic-cell frequencies, increased NK-cell and T-cell activation, elevated serum `TNF-alpha`, `IL-6`, and `IFN-beta`, and reduced immunosuppressive `MDSC` and `Treg` infiltration. The study presents these findings as the downstream immune consequence of jointly relieving methylation-linked repression of `GSDME` and `STING` and then activating both pathways with photodynamic stress. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:402-406, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:514-571]

## Significance

The study’s conclusion is that promoter methylation of `DFNA5` and `TMEM173` is not just a passive expression change in breast cancer, but a constraint on the tumor’s ability to mount therapy-triggered innate immune responses. By reversing that methylation with decitabine, the investigators report restoration of `GSDME`-dependent pyroptosis and `STING`-dependent DNA sensing, which together enhanced antitumor immune activation in their model. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:595-621]
