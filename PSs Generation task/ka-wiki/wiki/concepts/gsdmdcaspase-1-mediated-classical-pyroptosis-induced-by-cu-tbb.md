# GSDMD/caspase-1-mediated classical pyroptosis induced by Cu-TBB

GSDMD/caspase-1-mediated classical pyroptosis induced by Cu-TBB is the pyroptotic cell-death program reported for the copper-bacteriochlorin nanosheet Cu-TBB after activation in a glutathione-rich tumor microenvironment. In the study, Cu-TBB was shown to switch from an “off” state to an “on” state in tumors, produce reactive oxygen species through Cu-driven cascade chemistry and photodynamic therapy, and trigger GSDMD-dependent pyroptosis with caspase-1 activation and release of IL-1beta and IL-18. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:140-155, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:228-282, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:439-455]

## Mechanism

Cu-TBB is a bacteriochlorin-based nanosheet composed of Cu2+ and TBB. In the presence of elevated glutathione, the nanosheet disassembles and releases Cu+ and TBB. Cu+ then catalyzes oxygen conversion to superoxide, and the released TBB can generate additional reactive oxygen species under 750 nm irradiation. This ROS production is the upstream trigger for classical pyroptosis in the study. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:140-155, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:239-282, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:360-387]

The authors report that Cu-TBB-induced ROS activated caspase-1, which cleaved GSDMD into N-GSDMD. They describe this as the classical pyroptosis pathway, distinct from the GSDME pathway, and note that caspase-4 was not detected in any treatment group. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:439-455]

## Experimental evidence

In cultured HeLa, HepG2, and 4T1 cells, Cu-TBB alone showed little toxicity, while Cu-TBB plus glutathione reduced viability, and the addition of 750 nm laser irradiation produced near-complete cell death. Pyroptosis-like membrane bubbling was observed in treated cells, supporting the morphological phenotype of pyroptosis. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:388-424]

Western blot analysis showed full-length GSDMD and GSDME in all groups, but N-GSDMD only in Cu-TBB + GSH and Cu-TBB + GSH + Laser groups. Cleaved caspase-1 was also detected in those groups, and IL-1beta and IL-18 increased accordingly. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:439-455]

Blocking caspase-1 with VX-765 reduced cleaved caspase-1 and N-GSDMD and lowered IL-1beta and IL-18 release, which supports a caspase-1-dependent classical pyroptosis mechanism. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:455-462]

## Immune consequence

Pyroptosis induced by Cu-TBB increased release of DAMPs including HMGB1, LDH, and CRT exposure. The study also reports increased dendritic cell maturation in co-culture and stronger DC activation when laser was added. These findings were used to support pyroptosis-driven enhancement of tumor immunogenicity. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:463-488]

In vivo, Cu-TBB increased N-GSDMD and cleaved caspase-1 in tumors, elevated IL-1beta and IL-18, and was associated with stronger CRT expression and higher serum HMGB1. The authors conclude that Cu-TBB-induced GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis contributed to antitumor immune activation, tumor regression, and reduced metastasis. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:605-665, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:591-603, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:673-707]
