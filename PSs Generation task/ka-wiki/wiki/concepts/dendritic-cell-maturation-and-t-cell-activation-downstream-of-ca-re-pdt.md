I’m pulling the immune-response lines from the CA-Re paper and will keep the page tightly scoped to what the source actually says. Then I’ll format it as a wiki entry with related wikilinks and a source list.# Dendritic-cell maturation and T-cell activation downstream of CA-Re PDT

Dendritic-cell maturation and T-cell activation are described in the CA-Re study as downstream immune effects of photodynamic treatment in an immunocompetent bilateral 4T1 mouse model. After CA-Re plus light treatment of the primary tumor, the authors reported increased CD80+CD86+ dendritic cells in tumor-draining lymph nodes, higher TNF-alpha, IL-6, and IL-12p70 with reduced IL-10, and increased CD3+CD8+ and CD3+CD4+ T-cell populations in distant tumors, together indicating activation of adaptive anti-tumor immunity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:576-615]

## Role in the study

The paper positions these immune changes as part of the anti-tumor effect of CA-Re photodynamic therapy. CA-Re was administered intratumorally to the primary tumor, while the untreated contralateral tumor served as the distant lesion for assessing systemic immune effects; the reported abscopal inhibition of the distant tumor was interpreted alongside dendritic-cell and T-cell readouts. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:536-575]

## Dendritic-cell maturation

The authors first assessed dendritic-cell maturation in tumor-draining lymph nodes, describing it as essential for initiating adaptive anti-tumor immunity. By flow cytometry, the proportion of CD80+CD86+ dendritic cells increased from 16.0% to 28.4% in the CA-Re plus light group compared with the light-only and CA-Re-only groups. The study also reports increased TNF-alpha, IL-6, and IL-12p70 and decreased IL-10 in serum, which the authors interpret as evidence of enhanced antigen-presenting capacity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:576-592]

## T-cell activation

The study then measured adaptive immune response by quantifying CD8+ cytotoxic T cells and CD4+ helper T cells in distant tumors. In the CA-Re plus light group, the proportions of CD3+CD8+ and CD3+CD4+ T lymphocytes were reported as 28.3% and 32.3%, respectively, both higher than in the control groups. The authors also note enhanced CD8 infiltration in primary and distant tumor tissues by immunohistochemistry, supporting T cell-dependent anti-tumor immunity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:593-615]

## Mechanistic context

Within the paper, these immune effects follow CA-Re-induced pyroptotic cell death and release of immunogenic signals such as HMGB1, ecto-CRT, and ATP. The authors connect this sequence to enhanced tumor immunogenicity, dendritic-cell activation, and downstream T-cell responses rather than to direct treatment of the distant tumor. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:451-514, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:607-615]
