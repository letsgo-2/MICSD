# MK-2206 reverses aesculin-mediated cardiomyocyte protection

**MK-2206 reverses aesculin-mediated cardiomyocyte protection** refers to the finding that the allosteric [[Akt]] inhibitor MK-2206 abolished the protective effects of [[Aesculin (AES)|aesculin]] in neonatal rat cardiomyocytes exposed to [[oxygen-glucose deprivation/restoration|OGD/R]]. In the study, aesculin improved cell viability, reduced cell death, lowered the proportion of TUNEL-positive cardiomyocytes, and mitigated mitochondrial membrane potential loss, but these benefits were lost when MK-2206 was added. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:264-283, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:454-470]

The source presents this reversal as mechanistic evidence that aesculin's cardioprotective effect depends on [[Akt]] signaling. Aesculin increased phosphorylation of Akt at Ser473 and GSK3β at Ser9 while decreasing phosphorylation of NF-κB p65 at Ser536 in OGD/R-injured cardiomyocytes, and these signaling effects were abolished in the presence of MK-2206. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:437-453, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:458-475]

## Experimental context

In the in vitro model, neonatal rat cardiomyocytes were subjected to 120 minutes of oxygen-glucose deprivation followed by 4 hours of restoration. Aesculin was administered at final concentrations of 1, 3, and 10 μM when restoration began, and MK-2206 was added at 3 μM. Cell viability, LDH release-based cell death, TUNEL staining, mitochondrial membrane potential, and signaling or inflammasome-related proteins were then assessed. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:264-307]

The paper identifies MK-2206 specifically as an allosteric Akt inhibitor and lists it among the materials used in the study. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:140-146]

## Effect on aesculin's protective phenotype

Under OGD/R conditions, cardiomyocytes showed reduced viability, increased cell death, increased TUNEL positivity, and mitochondrial membrane potential loss. Aesculin at 3 and 10 μM improved these injury-related readouts. However, addition of MK-2206 caused a notable decline in viability and a marked rise in cell death relative to the aesculin-treated OGD/R group, and it also abolished aesculin's beneficial effects on TUNEL-positive cells and mitochondrial membrane potential. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:417-427, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:454-470]

These results are summarized in the abstract as showing that MK-2206 abolished aesculin-mediated cardioprotection. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:45-50]

## Link to NLRP3 inflammasome suppression

In OGD/R-injured cardiomyocytes, protein levels of NLRP3 and the active fragments of caspase-1, IL-1β, and GSDMD were elevated, indicating activation of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] and [[pyroptosis]]-related machinery. Aesculin at 3 and 10 μM reduced these levels, but in the presence of MK-2206, the aesculin-mediated suppression of NLRP3 inflammasome activation markedly subsided. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:430-436, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:470-475]

The study therefore uses MK-2206 as a pharmacologic probe linking Akt activity to aesculin-driven suppression of inflammasome-mediated cardiomyocyte injury. The authors state that aesculin could suppress NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis through the Akt/GSK3β/NF-κB pathway and that MK-2206 eliminated both the protective phenotype and the associated inflammasome suppression. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:471-475, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:560-569]

## Signaling interpretation in the study

The study describes a signaling sequence in which Akt phosphorylates GSK3β at Ser9, producing an inactive form of GSK3β that prevents NF-κB p65 Ser536 phosphorylation and activation. Active NF-κB p65 is described as promoting transcription of NLRP3 inflammasome-associated modules such as NLRP3, pro-IL-1β, and pro-IL-18. Within this framework, reversal by MK-2206 supports the interpretation that aesculin requires Akt activation to disrupt the GSK3β/NF-κB axis and limit inflammasome activation. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:548-569]
