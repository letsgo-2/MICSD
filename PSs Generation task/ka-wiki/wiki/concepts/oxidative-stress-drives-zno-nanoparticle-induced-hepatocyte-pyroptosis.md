# Oxidative stress drives ZnO nanoparticle-induced hepatocyte pyroptosis

ZnO nanoparticles (ZnO NPs) were reported to cause liver toxicity by disrupting zinc homeostasis, inducing oxidative stress in rat liver and HepG2 cells, and triggering canonical caspase-1-dependent pyroptosis in hepatocytes. The study specifically found that oxidative stress inhibition with N-acetyl-L-cysteine (NAC) protected against ZnO NP-induced pyroptosis, supporting oxidative stress as a driver of this death pathway. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:65-72, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:188-208, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:1169-1180]

## Overview

The paper describes ZnO NPs as a liver toxicant that accumulates in rat liver more than in other examined organs and injures hepatocytes through oxidative stress, inflammasome assembly, caspase-1 activation, GSDMD cleavage, and release of IL-1beta and IL-18. The authors frame this as a newly identified form of hepatocyte death induced by ZnO NPs in vivo and in vitro. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:499-509, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:539-557, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:1169-1180]

## Key findings

In rats treated intraperitoneally with ZnO NPs, the liver showed inflammatory injury, abnormal histology, elevated serum ALT, AST, and gamma-GT, and oxidative stress marked by increased MDA and decreased SOD, GSH, and CAT. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:510-538]

In HepG2 cells, ZnO NPs increased intracellular zinc, reduced viability, and induced oxidative stress, with higher ROS and lower antioxidant indices after exposure. The study also reports increased Nrf2 and HO-1 protein expression after ZnO NP treatment, alongside evidence of oxidative damage. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:558-579, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:594-604]

## Pyroptosis evidence

The authors found upregulation of NLRP3, ASC, cleaved caspase-1, GSDMD-NT, IL-1beta, and IL-18 in rat liver after ZnO NP exposure. They also observed stronger IL-1beta and GSDMD immunofluorescence signals and increased TUNEL positivity, which they interpreted as pyroptotic liver damage. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:539-557]

In HepG2 cells, ZnO NPs similarly increased NLRP3 inflammasome assembly, cleaved caspase-1, GSDMD-NT, IL-1beta, IL-18, LDH release, Annexin V/PI double positivity, membrane pore formation, and TUNEL signal, supporting canonical pyroptosis. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:605-644]

## Role of oxidative stress

NAC reduced ROS, restored CAT and SOD, lowered MDA, improved cell viability, reduced LDH release, and suppressed NLRP3, ASC, caspase-1, and GSDMD expression in ZnO NP-treated HepG2 cells. The authors conclude that antioxidant intervention alleviated ZnO NP-induced pyroptosis by inhibiting oxidative stress. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:668-841]

## Mechanistic interpretation

The study presents a model in which ZnO NP exposure causes oxidative stress, which in turn promotes inflammasome assembly, caspase-1 activation, GSDMD pore formation, and release of IL-1beta and IL-18, culminating in hepatocyte pyroptosis. VX-765, a caspase-1 inhibitor, reduced these pyroptotic markers, supporting a caspase-1-dependent canonical pathway. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:645-667, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:1169-1180]
