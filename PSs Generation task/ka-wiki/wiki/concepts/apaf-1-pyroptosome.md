# Apaf-1 pyroptosome

The **Apaf-1 pyroptosome** is a protein complex described as a cellular machinery for sensing [[mitochondrial permeability transition|mitochondrial permeability transition (MPT)]] and triggering pyroptosis under sterile conditions. In the source, MPT induced by bile acids, calcium overload, or an [[ANT1]] activator promotes assembly of a complex containing Apaf-1 and caspase-4/11, which activates a downstream pyroptotic program. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:58-66, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:133-143]

The study defines this complex as distinct from the better-known pathways in which caspase-4/11 directly senses cytosolic LPS or in which Apaf-1 forms an apoptosome with caspase-9. In this MPT-linked setting, Apaf-1 recruits and activates caspase-4/11 rather than caspase-9, providing a proposed mechanism for intrinsic pyroptosis in response to host-derived danger signals. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:56-66, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:136-143, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:711-718]

## Composition and assembly

According to the source, the pyroptosome is composed of Apaf-1 and caspase-4/11. Immunoprecipitation identified Apaf-1 as a caspase-11-interacting protein in macrophages, and follow-up pull-down and microscale thermophoresis assays supported a specific interaction between Apaf-1 and caspase-4, including interaction through the caspase-4 CARD domain. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:355-367, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:392-410]

The source further reports that MPT-triggering conditions increased colocalization of Apaf-1 with caspase-4 and that APAF-1 knockout prevented activation of caspase-4, caspase-3, and GSDME, as well as pyroptotic cell death and LDH release in HepG2 cells exposed to bile acids or lonidamine. In a reconstituted cell-free system, Apaf-1 activated caspase-4 only when cytochrome c and ATP were present as cofactors. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:400-423, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:478-489]

## Upstream trigger: mitochondrial permeability transition

The paper presents MPT as the upstream signal that drives pyroptosome assembly. Bile acids induced persistent MPT, shown by mitochondrial cristae loss, dissipation of mitochondrial membrane potential, and loss of inner mitochondrial membrane integrity, and these changes were linked to caspase-4/11-dependent pyroptosis rather than direct bile acid binding to caspase-4/11. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:223-235, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:236-302]

Pharmacologic results in the study point to [[ANT1]]-facilitated MPT as an important upstream step. The ANT inhibitor bongkrekic acid protected against bile acid-induced MPT, cell death, LDH release, and activation of caspase-11/4, caspase-3, and GSDME, while calcium overload induced by thapsigargin and ANT1 activation by lonidamine also triggered MPT-associated pyroptotic death in a caspase-11-dependent manner. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:283-302, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:303-346]

## Downstream signaling and execution

In this pathway, caspase-4/11 activated within the Apaf-1 pyroptosome acts upstream of [[caspase-3]], which then cleaves [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]] to execute pyroptosis. The source contrasts this with LPS-triggered caspase-4/11 activation, where caspase-4/11 directly cleaves [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]]. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:61-64, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:191-222]

Experimental evidence in the paper supports this signaling order. Bile acid-induced pyroptosis depended on GSDME rather than GSDMD, and in cell-free assays the Apaf-1/caspase-4 system activated caspase-3, while GSDMD added to the Apaf-1/caspase-4/caspase-3 system was cleaved by caspase-3 to an inactive fragment. The authors therefore describe the pyroptosome as an MPT-triggered scaffold that facilitates caspase-4/11 activation and channels signaling toward caspase-3 and GSDME. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:197-222, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:446-467]

## Relationship to the apoptosome

The source positions the Apaf-1 pyroptosome alongside, but distinct from, the [[Apaf-1 apoptosome]]. Under conditions associated with mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization (MOMP), such as low-dose bile acids or doxorubicin, Apaf-1 assembled with caspase-9 and activated apoptotic signaling. Under high-dose bile acid conditions associated with MPT, Apaf-1 instead assembled with caspase-11/4 and activated pyroptotic signaling. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:522-543, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:570-573]

The study also reports mechanistic differences between these two Apaf-1-based complexes. MPT caused earlier release of cytochrome c and immediate ATP release into the cytoplasm, and caspase-4 recruitment to Apaf-1 required a higher ATP level than caspase-9. At high ATP levels, caspase-4 competitively inhibited caspase-9 activation. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:574-586]

## Stoichiometry

The paper reports that the Apaf-1 pyroptosome has a stoichiometric ratio of 7:2 between Apaf-1 and caspase-4. Caspase-4 activity in reconstructed systems peaked at an approximate ratio corresponding to two caspase-4 molecules per Apaf-1 heptamer, and size-exclusion chromatography of the purified complex gave calculated ratios consistent with about 1.8 to 2.1 caspase-4 molecules per Apaf-1 heptamer. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:61-62, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:587-610]

For comparison, the source states that the Apaf-1 apoptosome has a 7:4 stoichiometry with caspase-9. The authors propose that these different stoichiometries may help determine whether mitochondrial stress leads to apoptosis or pyroptosis. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:603-610]

## Pathophysiologic relevance

The study links Apaf-1/caspase-11 pyroptosome formation to [[cholestatic liver injury]]. In a bile duct ligation mouse model, the authors observed persistent MPT, formation of the Apaf-1/caspase-11 complex in liver tissue, caspase-11 activation, and cleavage of caspase-3 and GSDME. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:611-646, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:639-645]

Genetic evidence in the same model supported functional importance of this pathway. Caspase-11-deficient mice were resistant to bile duct ligation-induced activation of caspase-3, GSDME cleavage, and release of IL-1alpha, IL-1beta, and HMGB1, while GSDME deficiency completely rescued bile duct ligation-induced mortality. The paper concludes that caspase-11-caspase-3-GSDME pyroptotic signaling is dominant in this cholestatic liver failure model, although it notes that contribution from caspase-1/GSDMD cannot be excluded. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:647-660]

## Significance

In the authors' model, the Apaf-1 pyroptosome provides a mechanism by which cells sense MPT and convert severe mitochondrial stress into rapid pyroptotic death. The paper presents this as a form of sterile, intrinsic pyroptosis that broadens the role of mitochondria beyond apoptosis, necroptosis, and ferroptosis. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:664-680]

The source also suggests that defining MPT-driven cell death as caspase-4-sensed pyroptosis may be relevant to diseases associated with calcium overload, oxidative stress, bile acid accumulation, ischemia-reperfusion injury, and neurologic disease, while noting that the exact molecular basis for how Apaf-1 differentially recruits caspase-4 versus caspase-9 remains unresolved. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:670-688, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:722-760, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:761-770]
