# Inflammasome protein nanoparticles drive osmotic pressure increases in pyroptotic astrocytes

In this study, pyroptotic astrocytes were described as undergoing cell swelling, membrane blebbing, and membrane permeabilization, and the authors investigated whether these morphological changes are linked to increases in intracellular osmotic pressure driven by protein nanoparticles. They report that [[inflammasome]] production, especially [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] assembly, acts as a major source of intracellular protein nanoparticles that raise protein nanoparticle-induced osmotic pressure (PN-OP) during astrocytic [[pyroptosis]]. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:18-30, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:123-130]

## Overview

The paper frames astrocytic pyroptosis as a mechanically coupled process in which outward osmotic pressure and inward cytoskeletal tension together shape cell deformation. Building on prior work that protein nanoparticles can adsorb cations and drive ion and water influx, the authors tested whether pyroptosis-associated nanoparticle accumulation increases cytoplasmic osmotic pressure and thereby promotes swelling and blebbing. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:40-43, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:84-87, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:123-130]

Using 4-hydroxytamoxifen-treated U87 astrocyte-like cells and an LPS-nigericin pyroptosis model, the study found that pyroptotic stimulation increased cytoplasmic osmotic pressure, intermediate filament tension, protein nanoparticle counts, and intracellular Ca2+ and Cl- levels. The authors interpret these coordinated changes as evidence that abundant intracellular protein nanoparticles arise during pyroptosis and are closely associated with osmotic pressure elevation across the cell membrane. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:281-309]

## Evidence that inflammasomes act as the key protein nanoparticles

To test whether inflammasome assembly contributes directly to PN-OP, the authors knocked down ASC, a core component of the NLRP3 inflammasome. Blocking ASC reduced the 4-OHT-induced increases in intracellular osmotic pressure, protein nanoparticle abundance, caspase-1 activity, intermediate filament tension, membrane permeabilization, and intracellular Ca2+ and Cl- levels. It also rescued microfilament and microtubule cleavage, which the authors interpret as evidence that inflammasomes themselves serve as intracellular protein nanoparticles that increase osmotic pressure and facilitate astrocyte pyroptosis. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:378-395]

The study also used pharmacologic inhibitors of NLRP3 assembly and activation, including cantharidin, SP600125, PF431396, and [[MCC950]]. These interventions reduced cytoplasmic osmotic pressure, protein nanoparticle number, intermediate filament tension, permeabilization, blebbing, and caspase-1 activity, with MCC950 showing the strongest effect and reducing osmotic pressure and nanoparticle abundance nearly to control levels. The authors therefore propose that [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] complexes are a predominant source of the protein nanoparticles that drive PN-OP during pyroptosis. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:396-427]

## Relationship to cytoskeletal remodeling

Cytoskeletal depolymerization was presented as a secondary contributor to osmotic pressure elevation. Stabilizing microfilaments and microtubules with jasplakinolide and taxol partly reduced the pyroptosis-associated increases in osmotic pressure, intermediate filament tension, nanoparticle count, and intracellular ion levels, and it reduced swelling and blebbing. However, these treatments did not significantly alter propidium iodide positivity or caspase-1 activity, indicating that cytoskeleton-derived nanoparticles contribute to astrocyte deformation but are not absolutely required for pyroptotic permeabilization. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:337-377]

In the discussion, the authors distinguish this mechanism from swelling driven mainly by cytoskeletal breakdown in ischemic edema. In astrocytic pyroptosis, they argue that PN-OP regulation depends mostly on inflammasome nanoparticles, with cytoskeleton-depolymerized actin and tubulin acting as accompanying contributors rather than the dominant cause. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:695-720]

## Links to caspase-1, calcium, and GSDMD

[[Caspase-1]] inhibition by Z-VAD-FMK or CASPASE-1 siRNA reduced osmotic pressure, protein nanoparticle abundance, intermediate filament tension, intracellular Ca2+ and Cl- levels, and permeabilization, while also reducing NLRP3 inflammasome levels. The authors interpret this as feedback regulation in which caspase-1 promotes further inflammasome generation and thereby amplifies PN-OP. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:432-466]

The paper further reports that Ca2+ released from the endoplasmic reticulum regulates this process. Caffeine increased osmotic pressure and nanoparticle counts, whereas 2-APB or dantrolene reduced osmotic pressure, intermediate filament tension, intracellular ion levels, swelling, permeabilization, LDH release, and caspase-1 activity. The authors conclude that ER-derived calcium is a crucial regulator of inflammasome production, PN-OP, and astrocyte blebbing and permeabilization. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:535-632]

Overexpression experiments with [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD-NT]] showed that the N-terminal fragment, but not GSDMD-CT, increased osmotic pressure and protein nanoparticle number and induced swelling, blebbing, permeabilization, and caspase-1 activity. These effects were abolished by calcium-lowering treatments and reduced by caspase-1 inhibition, supporting the study’s model that GSDMD activity modifies typical pyroptotic features through inflammasome-induced osmotic pressure upregulation and calcium signaling. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:633-683]

## Role of membrane pores versus osmotic pressure

Blocking non-selective membrane pores with PEG8000 inhibited permeabilization, lowered the proportion of blebbing cells, and partly reduced osmotic pressure, intermediate filament tension, and intracellular ion levels. However, it did not alter caspase-1 activity or intracellular protein nanoparticle production. Based on these results, the authors argue that non-selective ion influx is not the primary cause of PN-OP upregulation, and that inflammasome nanoparticle accumulation can be uncoupled from pore blockade. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:467-534]

The study therefore proposes a distinction between astrocyte swelling and membrane permeabilization in pyroptosis. Inflammasome-induced osmotic pressure increase appears necessary for swelling and blebbing, but not absolutely required for membrane permeabilization, which is more directly tied to GSDMD pore formation and later-stage cell disintegration. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:736-769]

## Proposed mechanism

In the authors’ model, neuroinflammatory signaling promotes [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] assembly and caspase-1 activation, generating large numbers of intracellular protein nanoparticles. These negatively charged nanoparticles raise PN-OP, likely in part through Donnan-like ion adsorption effects, which in turn increase intracellular ion and water influx, elevate intermediate filament tension, and promote cell swelling and membrane blebbing. Calcium signaling from the endoplasmic reticulum and feedback involving caspase-1 and GSDMD-NT further reinforce inflammasome production and osmotic dysregulation. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:721-735, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:770-810]

## Significance

The central conclusion of the study is that inflammasomes function not only as signaling complexes but also as protein nanoparticles that mechanically shape pyroptotic astrocyte morphology by increasing intracellular osmotic pressure. This framework clarifies how swelling, blebbing, and permeabilization are related but separable features of astrocyte pyroptosis. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:28-30, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:801-810]
