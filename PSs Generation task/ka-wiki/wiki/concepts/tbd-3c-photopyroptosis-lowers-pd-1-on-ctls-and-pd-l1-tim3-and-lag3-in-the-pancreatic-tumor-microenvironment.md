# TBD-3C photopyroptosis lowers PD-1 on CTLs and PD-L1, Tim3, and Lag3 in the pancreatic tumor microenvironment

`TBD-3C` is a membrane-targeted, aggregation-induced emission ([[aggregation-induced emission|AIE]]) photosensitizer used to trigger [[pyroptosis]] through [[photodynamic therapy|photodynamic therapy (PDT)]] in pancreatic cancer models. In this study, the authors examined whether PDT-aroused pyroptosis could reverse the immunosuppressive [[tumor microenvironment|tumor microenvironment (TME)]] and strengthen antitumor immunity in pancreatic tumors. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:21-32, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:162-176]

## Key finding

In KPC-bearing mice, `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment reduced `PD-1` expression on intratumoral [[cytotoxic T lymphocyte|cytotoxic T lymphocytes (CTLs)]]. The reported `PD-1`-positive fraction on CTLs was `18.53 +/- 4.9%` after treatment versus `27.64 +/- 5.8%` in controls. The paper states that `PD-1` is an immune inhibitory receptor whose overexpression on CTLs is associated with tumor immune evasion, and it interprets the decrease in `PD-1` as a change that could help limit tumor immune escape. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:494-516, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:554-562]

High-dimensional [[mass cytometry|CyTOF]] analysis further showed that the overall expression of `PD-L1` in the TME, together with `Tim3` and `Lag3` on `CD8+` T cells (cluster 35), was significantly downregulated in the `TBD-3C` PDT group. The authors describe these molecules as immune-suppressive markers. In the same analysis, `IFN-gamma` expression on `CD8+` T cells increased observably. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:525-548, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:569-595]

## Experimental context

The in vivo TME findings came from subcutaneous pancreatic cancer models in immunocompetent `C57` mice using KPC and Panc02 tumors. `TBD-3C` or PBS was delivered by intratumoral injection, followed by white-light irradiation at `40 mW cm^-2` for `10 min` once tumors reached an average volume of `50 mm^3`. The study then assessed tumor growth, immune infiltration, and immune-marker expression within tumors. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:439-448, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:840-850]

For broader immune-landscape profiling, the authors performed CyTOF on tumors from KPC-bearing mice. Immune cells were grouped into 40 clusters using 42 markers, and six major immune-cell categories were identified: `CD4+` T cells, `CD8+` T cells, macrophages, natural killer cells, B cells, and dendritic cells. This analysis was used to compare the immune composition and suppressive-marker expression between control tumors and tumors treated with `TBD-3C` PDT. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:525-545, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:919-931]

## Relationship to CTL activation

The decrease in `PD-1` on CTLs occurred alongside other signs of stronger `CD8+` T-cell activity in tumors. After `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment, the proportion of tumor-infiltrating CTLs increased, `CD69+` cells within the `CD8+` T-cell population rose markedly, and `CD103` expression on `CD8+` T cells was also upregulated. Serum `TNF-alpha` and `IFN-gamma` were elevated as well, consistent with a stronger antitumor immune response in treated mice. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:481-485, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:494-524]

The study also links these T-cell changes to upstream innate immune activation caused by pyroptotic tumor cells. In vitro, `TBD-3C`-induced pyroptotic cells promoted [[M1 macrophage|M1-like macrophage]] polarization, re-educated existing `M2`-like macrophages toward `M1`, drove [[dendritic cell|dendritic-cell]] maturation, and enhanced `CD8+` T-cell proliferation, maturation, perforin secretion, and granzyme B secretion in co-culture systems. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:326-353, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:387-436]

## Tumor-microenvironment remodeling

The authors interpret the combined reduction in `PD-1`, `PD-L1`, `Tim3`, and `Lag3`, together with increased `IFN-gamma`, expanded immune-active populations, and reduced suppressive myeloid cells, as evidence that `TBD-3C` PDT-aroused pyroptosis remodeled the pancreatic TME from an immunosuppressive "cold" state toward an immunogenic "hot" state. Specifically, the treatment group showed increased `gamma delta` T cells, natural killer cells, and `CD4+ TEMRA` cells, while [[myeloid-derived suppressor cell|myeloid-derived suppressor cells (MDSCs)]] were significantly reduced. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:581-595]
