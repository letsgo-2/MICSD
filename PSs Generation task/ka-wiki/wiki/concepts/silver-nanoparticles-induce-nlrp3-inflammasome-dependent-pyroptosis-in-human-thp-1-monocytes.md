# Silver nanoparticles induce NLRP3 inflammasome-dependent pyroptosis in human THP-1 monocytes

Silver nanoparticles of approximately 15 nm (`AgNP15`) were reported to trigger a rapid, non-apoptotic inflammatory cell-death program in human [[THP-1]] monocytes. In this study, higher AgNP15 concentrations caused atypical [[endoplasmic reticulum stress]], degradation of the ER-stress sensor [[ATF-6]], activation of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]], and [[pyroptosis]]. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:26-31, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:43-50]

## Study context

The source frames this finding within broader concern about the cellular toxicity of [[silver nanoparticles]] and other nanoparticles used across industrial and medical applications. It notes that nanoparticle exposure has been linked to ER-stress-associated cytotoxicity and that understanding the molecular effects of AgNP in human cells is important because human exposure is expected to increase. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:19-25, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:51-63]

The study specifically tested AgNP15 in human THP-1 monocytes and compared responses across concentrations. Low concentrations induced ER-stress-related responses without cell death, whereas `25 ug/ml` caused a distinct response associated with ATF-6 degradation and pyroptotic death through inflammasome activation. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:132-137, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:358-385]

## Evidence for pyroptosis

At `25 ug/ml`, AgNP15 rapidly altered THP-1-cell morphology and induced annexin-V and propidium iodide positivity within 1 hour, a pattern the authors distinguished from [[apoptosis]] induced by staurosporine. Biochemically, AgNP15 at this concentration processed caspase-4 and caspase-7 but not caspase-3, and caspase-3 activity was not induced, supporting the conclusion that the death program differed from classical apoptosis. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:333-344, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:386-456]

The source identifies several canonical features of pyroptosis after AgNP15 exposure. Treatment with `25 ug/ml` AgNP15 caused caspase-1 processing to the p20 fragment, increased caspase-1 activity, induced secretion of `IL-1beta`, and promoted formation of ASC-containing [[pyroptosome]] structures in about 20% of cells under the tested conditions. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:485-540]

## Dependence on the NLRP3 inflammasome

The study tied the response specifically to the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]]. In THP-1 cells transfected with `NLRP3`-targeting siRNA, AgNP15-induced `IL-1beta` secretion was significantly reduced after efficient knockdown, indicating that NLRP3 was required for the cytokine response. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:541-579]

The response was also caspase-1-dependent. Pharmacologic inhibition of caspase-1 with `YVAD-CHO` completely abolished AgNP15-induced `IL-1beta` secretion, which the authors used to confirm that caspase-1 was crucial for AgNP15-mediated NLRP3 inflammasome activation. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:571-579]

## Link to ATF-6 and ER stress

AgNP15 produced concentration-dependent ER-stress-related changes in THP-1 cells. Lower concentrations activated parts of the PERK pathway and induced HSP-70, while `25 ug/ml` caused rapid degradation of ATF-6 within 1 hour together with other atypical ER-stress features. The authors interpret this as an unusual ER-stress response associated with the pyroptotic phenotype. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:358-385, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:693-705]

To test whether ATF-6 processing was functionally involved, the authors used inhibitors of Site-1 and Site-2 proteases. Blocking ATF-6 processing, especially with the Site-2 protease inhibitor `1,10-phenanthroline`, reduced caspase-1 activity, `IL-1beta` secretion, and the proportion of PI-positive cells. On that basis, the paper concludes that ATF-6 degradation is closely linked to AgNP15-induced NLRP3 inflammasome activation and pyroptotic cell death. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:613-642, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:823-835]

## Role of caspase-4

The study describes [[caspase-4]] as a regulator of this pathway. In caspase-4-deficient THP-1 cells, pro-`IL-1beta` expression was severely impaired even after LPS priming, indicating that caspase-4 was important for inflammasome priming. At the same time, these deficient cells showed higher caspase-1 activity, more PI-positive cells, and more pyroptosome formation after AgNP15 exposure than control cells. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:580-612, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:858-886]

The authors therefore interpret caspase-4 as having a dual role: it supports the priming step needed for pro-`IL-1beta` synthesis, but it may negatively regulate downstream NLRP3 inflammasome activation under these conditions. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:609-612, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:860-869]

## Comparison with primary human monocytes and macrophages

The source also compared THP-1 cells with primary human monocytes and monocyte-derived macrophages. Basal expression of `NLRP3` and pro-`IL-1beta` differed among these cell types, and this influenced cytokine output. Notably, primary monocytes secreted significant `IL-1beta` in response to AgNP15 even without LPS priming, whereas THP-1 cells and macrophages required priming for strong mature `IL-1beta` release. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:643-679, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:761-776]

## Interpretation

According to the paper, AgNP15 act as both cytotoxic and proinflammatory stimuli in human monocytes. The central finding is that, at sufficiently high concentration, AgNP15 induce a rapid ER-stress-associated pathway involving ATF-6 degradation that leads to caspase-1 activation, `IL-1beta` release, and NLRP3-inflammasome-dependent [[pyroptosis]] rather than classical apoptosis. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:685-706, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:751-789]
