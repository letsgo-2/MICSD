I’ll keep the related links limited to concepts supported by the source.# BTP-induced cell death is caspase-dependent pyroptosis rather than lipid-peroxidation-driven ferroptosis

BTP photocatalysis triggered a lytic form of cell death that the authors identify as pyroptosis. In their comparison with ferroptosis, they report that the lipid peroxidation inhibitor Liproxstatin-1 was less effective than the pan-caspase inhibitor z-VAD-fmk, and conclude that BTP-triggered cell death depends on caspase activation rather than lipid peroxidation. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:639-652]

The study links this death pathway to GSDMD cleavage and non-canonical inflammasome caspases. BTP photocatalysis increased detection of GSDMD-NT, pyroptotic morphology and LDH release were abolished in GSDMD-deficient macrophages, and western blotting showed cleavage of caspase-4/5 rather than caspase-1. The authors also note ATP secretion and the absence of efficient cleaved IL-18 and IL-1β secretion, consistent with lack of active caspase-1. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:653-676]

The paper distinguishes this from ferroptosis because its oxidizing chemistry did not produce the lipid oxidation pattern they expected. Although BTP generated H2O2, hydroxyl radicals, and oxidative membrane stress, UPLC-MS analysis did not detect newly generated lipid oxidation peaks, and the oxidation ratio of 15:0–18:1 phosphatidylcholine did not change after photocatalysis. The authors therefore state that lipid oxidation caused by BTP photocatalysis might be inefficient in cells. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:292-313]

The proposed upstream trigger is membrane-protein oxidation rather than lipid peroxidation. BTP localized to intracellular membranes, oxidized membrane proteins, destabilized GlpG folding, and enriched oxidation in protein quality control-related membrane proteins of the ER, Golgi apparatus, and mitochondria. The authors connect these changes to misfolded-protein accumulation, ER stress, maladaptive UPR, cation mobilization, and subsequent caspase-4/5 activation. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:247-286, oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:314-410, oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:459-527, oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:667-676]
