# BTP as an intracellular membrane-localized amphiphilic organic photocatalyst

`BTP` is described as an intracellular membrane-localized organic photocatalyst with an amphiphilic, fatty acid-like structure composed of a hydrophobic linear `pi`-conjugated core and a hydrophilic head. The authors report that this structure enables BTP to localize in intracellular membranes and to drive photocatalysis that damages membrane proteins oxidatively. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:116-120, oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:149-160]

## Structure and localization

The paper states that BTP was synthesized by pairing benzothiadiazole and triphenylamine as electron donor and acceptor units. Its amphiphilic character is presented as the basis for membrane localization, and cell experiments showed that BTP was found in the Golgi apparatus and endoplasmic reticulum, but not in mitochondria before photocatalysis. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:172-182, oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:257-260]

Emission measurements in cells and artificial membranes supported proximity to intracellular membranes: BTP’s emission peak shifted from 630 nm in water-like environments to 580 nm in nonpolar membrane-like environments, including bicelles and cellular environments. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:269-286]

## Photocatalytic behavior

Under light irradiation, BTP undergoes photocatalytic cycles that generate hydrogen peroxide (`H2O2`) and hydroxyl radicals (`•OH`) through water oxidation. The authors also report that hypoxia accelerates `H2O2` production, and that BTP photocatalysis can generate `•OH` in the presence of water and H2O2, while not producing singlet oxygen. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:183-245]

The paper further describes the excited-state redox properties of BTP as supporting water oxidation and oxygen reduction. Measured redox potentials were used to argue that BTP can generate strongly oxidizing radicals under light, including in hypoxic conditions. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:180-219, oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:779-786]

## Membrane oxidation and protein damage

BTP photocatalysis produced oxidative stress focused on intracellular membranes. Confocal and fluorescence assays showed increased ROS signals and `O2•−` generation in HeLa cells, and proteomic analysis found that oxidized proteins were enriched in membrane-specific proteins rather than cytosolic proteins. The most affected membrane proteins were associated with the ER, mitochondria, nucleus, and Golgi apparatus. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:292-313, oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:412-494]

A membrane protein stability assay using GlpG showed that BTP photocatalysis destabilized membrane protein folding and promoted aggregation. Single-molecule magnetic tweezer experiments then confirmed irreversible oxidative damage to GlpG folding stability after BTP photocatalysis. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:314-411]

## Cellular consequences

The study links membrane oxidation by BTP to misfolded protein accumulation, endoplasmic reticulum stress, maladaptive UPR, calcium and potassium mobilization, and loss of mitochondrial membrane potential. These changes were interpreted as downstream consequences of oxidative damage to intracellular membranes and membrane protein quality control systems. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:443-527]

BTP photocatalysis induced highly lytic cell death in HeLa cells and hypoxic pancreatic cancer cells, with pyroptotic morphology, LDH release, GSDMD cleavage, and caspase-4/5 activation reported. The authors conclude that intracellular membrane-focused oxidation by BTP triggers non-canonical pyroptosis. ^[oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:566-676, oxidative-photocatalysis-on-membranes-triggers-non-canonical-pyroptosis.md:678-699]
