# Abscopal anti-tumor immunity in a bilateral 4T1 mouse model

Abscopal anti-tumor immunity in this study refers to inhibition of a non-irradiated distant tumor after local treatment of a primary tumor. To examine this effect, the authors used a bilateral [[4T1]] breast tumor model in immunocompetent [[BALB/c]] mice and treated only the primary tumor, so that any inhibition observed in the distant tumor could be attributed to an abscopal effect and associated anti-tumor immune responses rather than direct drug accumulation. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:376-381]

## Model design

Female SPF BALB/c mice, 4-5 weeks old, were implanted subcutaneously with 4T1 tumor cells on the right flank and then, 7 days later, on the left flank using `2 x 10^6` cells in `100 uL` PBS. The right-flank tumor served as the primary tumor for local photodynamic treatment, while the left-flank tumor served as the distant tumor for assessing systemic anti-tumor immunity. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:339-348]

The study notes that although in vivo anti-tumor efficacy is often evaluated in BALB/c nude mice bearing MDA-MB-231 tumors, nude mice are immunodeficient and therefore unsuitable for anti-tumor immune studies. For that reason, the authors used immunocompetent BALB/c mice with a syngeneic 4T1 model to stimulate systemic immune responses. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:351-354]

## Treatment protocol

Tumor-bearing mice were randomly assigned to three groups: control, `Re`, and `Re+light`. CA-Re was formulated in `2% DMSO`, `4% PEG 400`, `4% Tween 80`, and `92% PBS`, then administered by intratumoral injection at `5 mg/kg` (`50 uL`) on days 0, 4, and 8. One hour after each injection, the primary tumor was irradiated with a `525 nm` laser at `15 mW/cm^2` for `60 min`. Body weight and tumor volume were recorded every two days, and mice were sacrificed on day 16 for further analysis. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:344-350]

Intratumoral injection into only the primary tumor was chosen deliberately. The source explains that CA-Re had limited solubility for intravenous administration, and more importantly, local injection helped avoid accumulation at the distant tumor so that inhibition of the distant lesion could be interpreted as evidence of an [[abscopal effect]] mediated by anti-tumor immunity. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:376-381]

## Immune readouts

To investigate in vivo anti-tumor immunity, the authors analyzed immune cells from distant tumors and tumor-draining lymph nodes by [[flow cytometry]]. Tissues from `n = 3` mice per group were dissociated into single-cell suspensions using collagenase IV and DNase I, filtered, and processed after red blood cell lysis. The resulting cells were stained with antibodies for dendritic-cell markers (`CD11c`, `CD80`, `CD86`) and T-lymphocyte markers (`CD3`, `CD4`, `CD8a`). ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:355-361]

Serum samples collected after treatment were used for cytokine analysis by [[ELISA]]. The measured factors included proinflammatory cytokines `IL-12p70`, `IL-6`, and `TNF-alpha`, the anti-inflammatory cytokine `IL-10`, and the interleukins `IL-18` and `IL-1beta`. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:361-364]

## Related tumor-cell immunogenicity measurements

The supporting information also reports immunogenic cell death-related signals in treated 4T1 cells. Figure S18 includes confocal imaging of surface `HMGB1` and `CRT`, analysis of extracellular `ATP` after photodynamic treatment, and western blot analysis of `CAIX`, `HIF-1alpha`, `HSP70`, `GPX4`, and cleaved `Caspase-1` expression in 4T1 cells treated with CA-Re followed by light irradiation. These measurements provide related cellular context for the in vivo anti-tumor immunity experiments. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:465-470]

Additional in vivo follow-up measurements listed in the supporting information include serum cytokine data, mouse body weight over the study period, and histological examination of major organs by H&E staining on day 16 after treatment. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:477-485]

## Interpretation within the study

Within this experimental setup, abscopal anti-tumor immunity was operationally defined by the response of the untreated contralateral 4T1 tumor after localized treatment of the primary tumor. The bilateral design, use of immunocompetent mice, and restriction of CA-Re administration to the primary tumor were all intended to distinguish systemic immune-mediated anti-tumor effects from direct local photodynamic action. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:342-344, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:351-354, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:376-381]
