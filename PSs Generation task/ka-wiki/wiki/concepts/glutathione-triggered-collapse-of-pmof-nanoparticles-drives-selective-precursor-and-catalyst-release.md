# Glutathione-triggered collapse of PMOF nanoparticles drives selective precursor and catalyst release

Glutathione-triggered collapse of PMOF nanoparticles refers to the response of precursor-loaded, F-127-coated [[MOF-199]] nanoparticles in which intracellular [[glutathione|GSH]] reduces Cu(II) in the framework to Cu(I) and concurrently disrupts the particle structure, causing release of both photosensitizer precursors and copper species. In the study, this process is the central activation step that links cancer-cell-associated GSH to selective intracellular synthesis of the mitochondria-targeting AIE photosensitizer `TPATrzPy-3+`. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:145-150, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:164-175]

## PMOF nanoparticle design

The authors loaded the two photochemically inert precursors `TPA-alkyne-2+` and `MePy-N3` into nanoscale [[MOF-199]] to form `PMOF`, then coated the material with `F-127` to obtain `PMOF NPs`. MOF-199 served both as a carrier for the precursors and as a source of copper that could become a Cu(I) click catalyst after reduction in cancer cells. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:45-48, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:156-172]

Precursor loading into MOF-199 was supported by multiple characterization results. The source reports a total loading of `51.3 wt%`, with `TPA-alkyne-2+` at `42.8 wt%` and `MePy-N3` at `8.5 wt%`. FT-IR showed azide and alkyne groups in PMOF, nitrogen mapping by EDS supported uniform distribution of the loaded molecules, and reduced `N2` adsorption relative to pristine MOF-199 indicated that the precursors were trapped in pores and channels rather than adsorbed only on the surface. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:278-306]

## GSH-triggered collapse mechanism

The paper proposes that elevated intracellular [[glutathione|GSH]] in cancer cells acts as a selective trigger for PMOF activation. ESR measurements showed that before GSH exposure, copper in MOF-199 was present as paramagnetic Cu(II), whereas after GSH treatment it became diamagnetic Cu(I), confirming reduction of the framework copper to the catalytically active monovalent state. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:268-277]

PXRD analysis showed that PMOF retained the crystallinity of MOF-199 before treatment, but after GSH exposure it lost crystallinity and became almost amorphous, consistent with framework decomposition. TEM, DLS, and zeta-potential measurements on `PMOF NPs` further showed that GSH treatment caused loss of the core-shell structure, fragmentation of the particles, reduced hydrodynamic diameter, and increased zeta-potential. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:306-319, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:342-349]

After particle collapse, all loaded precursors and copper ions were released from MOF-199 upon GSH exposure for `20 min` in similar profiles, indicating near-simultaneous release of both the reaction substrates and the catalyst source. By contrast, less than `15%` of the two precursors and copper ions were released after `24 h` in `PBS 7.4` or `PBS 5.5`, showing that release was strongly favored under GSH conditions rather than simple neutral or acidic buffer exposure. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:350-360]

## Selectivity basis

The selectivity of this release mechanism is tied to the difference in intracellular GSH levels between the cell models used in the study. Measured GSH concentrations were `68.5 +/- 11.4 ug` per million [[HeLa]] cells and `6.3 +/- 2.2 ug` per million [[3T3]] cells, a more than tenfold difference that the authors describe as supporting GSH as a cancer-cell-specific trigger for GSH-responsive PMOF nanoparticles. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:390-397]

Consistent with that trigger model, confocal imaging showed strong `TPATrzPy-3+` signal in PMOF-treated HeLa cells, while signal was almost indiscernible in precursor-treated HeLa cells, in BSO-pretreated HeLa cells, and in PMOF-treated or precursor-treated 3T3 cells. The paper interprets these differences as evidence that upregulated GSH in cancer cells reacts with PMOF nanoparticles to generate the catalyst and enable selective photosensitizer synthesis. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:398-417]

Additional cellular imaging indicated that PMOF nanoparticles remained intact during uptake and then degraded after internalization. In HeLa cells, the signal associated with loaded `TPA-alkyne-2+` was mainly lysosomal after `4` and `8 h`, but by `16 h` it was largely associated with mitochondria, and the authors state that PMOF NPs are stable during uptake and degrade to release precursors after internalization by cancer cells. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:418-430]

## Functional consequence of collapse and release

The simultaneous release of precursors and copper species enables intracellular [[click reaction]] chemistry. In GSH-treated PMOF NPs, an emission peak near `595 nm` gradually emerged and matched that of `TPATrzPy-3+`, whereas free precursors treated with GSH did not show this behavior. This was taken as evidence of successful in situ photosensitizer formation driven specifically by the MOF-mediated GSH response. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:357-364]

HPLC analysis of HeLa cell lysates showed that intracellular `TPATrzPy-3+` production increased over time after PMOF NP treatment, with calculated synthesis yields of `21.4%`, `61.2%`, `70.4%`, and `70.5%` at `8`, `12`, `16`, and `24 h`, respectively. The paper states that this indicates in vitro production was essentially completed by `16 h` with high yield. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:431-460]

This release-and-synthesis sequence also switched on photosensitizing activity. PMOF NPs alone showed only modest ABDA degradation under light, similar to the free precursors, but after GSH treatment they degraded ABDA efficiently at `25 uM mL^-1`, matching `TPATrzPy-3+`. The source therefore links GSH-triggered collapse and release to activation of singlet-oxygen-generating photodynamic function. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:335-345]

## Role in selective photodynamic therapy

In cell experiments under light irradiation, PMOF NPs showed potent toxicity toward HeLa cells but inhibited light toxicity toward 3T3 cells, while dark toxicity remained negligible across tested formulations. HeLa cells pretreated with the GSH scavenger `BSO` became markedly less sensitive to PMOF NPs, further tying therapeutic activation to intracellular GSH. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:465-498]

In the zebrafish liver tumor model, red fluorescence from `TPATrzPy-3+` appeared in PMOF-NP-injected embryos only in the tumor region from `1 dpi`, whereas directly injected `TPATrzPy-3+` or `TPATrzPy-3+@MOF NPs` produced fluorescence throughout the body from `0 dpi`. The study presents this as evidence that GSH-triggered PMOF collapse supports cancer-cell-specific photosensitizer generation and more selective image-guided PDT than systemic delivery of the active photosensitizer itself. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:549-567]
