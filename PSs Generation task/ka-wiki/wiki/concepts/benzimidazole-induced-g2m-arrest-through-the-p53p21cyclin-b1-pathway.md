# Benzimidazole-induced G2/M arrest through the P53/P21/cyclin B1 pathway

Benzimidazole compounds were reported to arrest human glioblastoma (GBM) cells at the G2/M phase through the P53/P21/cyclin B1 pathway. In the study, flubendazole, mebendazole, and fenbendazole all inhibited proliferation of U87 and U251 cells, and RNA-sequencing plus follow-up experiments supported cell-cycle regulation as one of the main mechanisms involved. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:20-25, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:99-106, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:455-471]

## Mechanism

Flow-cytometry analysis showed that benzimidazoles arrested the GBM cell cycle at G2/M in both U87 and U251 cells in a dose-dependent manner. In mechanistic assays, flubendazole increased P53 and P21 expression while reducing cyclin B1 expression, and also downregulated cdc2 expression and phosphorylation in U251 cells; the authors concluded that G2/M arrest occurred through the P53/P21/cyclin B1 pathway. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:455-471]

## Experimental context

The study tested three benzimidazoles: flubendazole, mebendazole, and fenbendazole. U87-MG and U251-MG glioblastoma cells were treated with 0.125, 0.25, and 0.5 μmol/L compounds for 24 hours in cell-cycle assays, with flow cytometry used to determine phase distribution. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:21-25, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:258-266, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:457-466]

RNA-sequencing of U87 and U251 cells treated with 0.25 μmol/L flubendazole for 24 hours found that the common differentially expressed genes were enriched in cell proliferation, cell cycle, cell migration, NF-κB, and P53 signaling pathways, providing additional support for the cell-cycle findings. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:411-441]
