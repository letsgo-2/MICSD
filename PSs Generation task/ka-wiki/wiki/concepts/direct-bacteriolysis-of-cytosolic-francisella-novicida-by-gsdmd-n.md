# Direct bacteriolysis of cytosolic Francisella novicida by GSDMD-N

Direct bacteriolysis of cytosolic [[Francisella novicida]] by [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD-N]] refers to the reported host-defense mechanism in which caspase-1-cleaved GSDMD releases its N-terminal pore-forming fragment, and that active fragment attacks intracellular *F. novicida* after the bacterium escapes into the cytosol. The source describes this as a bactericidal activity of GSDMD that contributes to clearance of *F. novicida* infection in cells. ^[gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:684-702]

## Context

*F. novicida* is described as a gram-negative, facultative intracellular member of the Francisellaceae family that is closely related to the highly virulent [[Francisella tularensis]]. The source states that it is highly infectious, can enter through inhalation, ingestion, or transmission from infected animals, and proliferates strongly after escaping disrupted phagosomes into the host-cell cytosol. ^[gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:684-699]

Within this setting, GSDMD is presented as a host factor that promotes death of *F. novicida* in infected cells. The review states that, after caspase-1 cleavage, liberated GSDMD-N attacks and kills *F. novicida*, thereby helping clear infection. ^[gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:700-703]

## Upstream activation

The source identifies the [[AIM2 inflammasome]] as central in *F. novicida* infection. AIM2 detects double-stranded DNA from *Francisella* and, together with ASC and caspase-1, forms an inflammasome complex that supports IL-1beta and IL-18 maturation as well as [[pyroptosis]]. It also states that this response depends on AIM2, since loss of AIM2 prevents the process. ^[gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:704-717]

GSDMD must be activated before it can exert this antibacterial effect. According to the source, activated caspase-1 cleaves GSDMD to separate the inhibitory C-terminal region from the active N-terminal fragment, releasing GSDMD-N. The review further states that GSDMD is further cleaved in association with activated AIM2 inflammasome signaling. ^[gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:715-724]

## Mechanism of direct bacteriolysis

The source attributes *F. novicida* susceptibility to the presence of phospholipids and cardiolipins in bacterial membranes. After activation, GSDMD-N is described as attacking *F. novicida* in the cytosol, causing bacteriolysis and release of bacterial DNA. ^[gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:718-724]

A subsequent summary in the review states that active GSDMD-N permeates the cell membrane and mitochondria of *F. novicida* because of cardiolipins, forms pores in the bacterial membrane, and thereby drives bacterial killing and clearance. ^[gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:725-733]

## Relationship to inflammasome signaling

In this account, direct bacteriolysis is linked to a feed-forward innate immune response. AIM2, ASC, and caspase-1 generate the conditions for GSDMD activation, while GSDMD-mediated attack on cytosolic *F. novicida* causes release of bacterial DNA, which the source places within the same AIM2-centered response framework during infection. ^[gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:704-724]

The review also includes *F. novicida* among examples where GSDMD has a protective role against intracellular pathogens, contrasting with other infections in which GSDMD-driven pyroptosis can worsen disease. In the *F. novicida* section specifically, the reported effect is bacterial clearance rather than pathogen persistence. ^[gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:700-703, gasdermin-d-kills-bacteria.md:792-803]
