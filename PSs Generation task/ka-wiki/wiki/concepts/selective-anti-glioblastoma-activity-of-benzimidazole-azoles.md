# Selective anti-glioblastoma activity of benzimidazole azoles

[[Benzimidazoles]] showed selective anti-[[glioblastoma multiforme|glioblastoma]] activity in the study, while other tested azole classes did not. After WGCNA/CMAP drug repurposing suggested multiple azoles as candidates, the authors experimentally found that only the benzimidazole compounds [[flubendazole]], [[mebendazole]], and [[fenbendazole]] potently inhibited proliferation of U87 and U251 glioblastoma cells, with reported IC50 values below 0.26 μM. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:18-25, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:91-97, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:387-405]

## Study basis

The authors used TCGA GBM transcriptome data, WGCNA hub-gene analysis, and the CMAP platform to identify azole compounds with potential anti-GBM activity. They then tested representative azoles from the imidazole, triazole, and benzimidazole groups and reported that only benzimidazoles were active against GBM cells. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:64-67, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:73-76, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:388-397]

## Cellular effects

In U87 and U251 cells, benzimidazoles dose- and time-dependently reduced proliferation and DNA synthesis, inhibited migration and invasion, and altered EMT marker expression. The study also reports G2/M cell-cycle arrest through the [[P53/P21/cyclin B1 pathway]]. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:22-25, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:402-410, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:442-471]

The same compounds also triggered concurrent cell-death programs. The paper describes balloon-like membrane protrusions, LDH release, increased NLRP3 and caspase-1, nuclear translocation of NF-κB, and GSDMD cleavage as evidence for [[pyroptosis]] via the [[NF-κB/NLRP3/GSDMD pathway]], while JC-1 and western blot data supported mitochondria-dependent apoptosis. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:25-26, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:512-615]

## In vivo activity

In a nude mouse U87 xenograft model, flubendazole given intraperitoneally at 12.5, 25, or 50 mg/kg/day for 3 weeks suppressed tumor growth in a dose-dependent manner. The source reports no obvious adverse effects, no significant body-weight change, and no clear organ toxicity on gross or histologic assessment. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:26-30, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:645-665]

## Interpretation

Within the study, the selectivity claim is that benzimidazole azoles had anti-GBM activity whereas tested imidazoles and triazoles did not. The authors conclude that benzimidazoles, especially flubendazole, are promising candidates for GBM treatment because they combine cell-cycle arrest with concurrent apoptotic and pyroptotic cell death. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:95-97, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:679-680, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:750-755]
