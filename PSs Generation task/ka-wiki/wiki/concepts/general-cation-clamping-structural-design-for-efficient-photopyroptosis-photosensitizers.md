# General cation-clamping structural design for efficient photopyroptosis photosensitizers

The general cation-clamping structural design for efficient photopyroptosis photosensitizers is a molecular design principle proposed to build photosensitizers that trigger highly efficient [[photopyroptosis]]. In the source, the core idea is to clamp cationic moieties such as pyridinium or imidazolium onto a single photosensitive core so the resulting molecules strongly target mitochondria, maximize site-specific photogenerated reactive oxygen species, and activate [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]]-mediated pyroptosis. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:15-31]

## Overview

The study frames photopyroptosis as an emerging branch of [[photodynamic therapy]] for which clear molecular structural rules had been lacking. It proposes a general and rational design principle in which cationic groups are integrated with a photosensitive core to promote strong electrostatic interactions with mitochondria, with the goal of concentrating photogenerated ROS at the mitochondrial site and thereby facilitating pyroptotic signaling. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:76-82, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:107-127]

According to the source, this design also aims to improve water solubility, making the compounds easier to disperse in cells and in vivo, while remaining compatible with photosensitizers whose absorption maxima span from the UV to the red spectral region. The representative compound `1d` was selected for mechanistic and in vivo studies because it could be excited by red light, which the authors describe as having deeper tissue penetration and lower energy. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:127-145]

## Design principle

The proposed structural paradigm combines three linked factors: a photosensitive core, cationic moieties such as pyridinium or imidazolium for mitochondrial targeting, and strong electrostatic interactions between the synthetic molecule and mitochondria. The source states that this combination enables recognition of both the inner and outer mitochondrial membranes and supports centralized ROS photoproduction from mitochondria under irradiation. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:115-123, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:537-547]

The rationale depends on electrostatic attraction. The authors argue that sufficient mitochondrial targeting via strong electrostatic interactions is crucial for maximizing ROS production at the specific subcellular site needed to facilitate the pyroptotic pathway. In their control comparisons, compounds conforming to the cation-clamping design showed possible pyroptotic morphology and LDH release after irradiation, whereas reference compounds lacking either adequate cationic periphery or a photosensitive core did not show comparable pyroptotic phenomena. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:102-111, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:190-214]

## Experimental basis for the design

To test universality, the study synthesized a series of compounds: `1a-1d` following the design and `2a-2b`, `3`, and `4` as references with functional-group changes. The source reports that all compounds with photosensitive cores except `3` generated singlet oxygen under continuous irradiation, but efficient photopyroptotic behavior depended on more than ROS generation alone. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:122-126, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:148-189]

Electrostatic interaction strength was examined using ATP as an anionic model. The reported binding constants were `K1a = 2.6 x 10^5 M^-1`, `K4 = 3.3 x 10^4 M^-1`, `K1b = 3.7 x 10^5 M^-1`, `K1c = 2.4 x 10^6 M^-1`, and `K1d = 7.4 x 10^5 M^-1`. The authors interpret higher binding constants as evidence of stronger electrostatic interactions, which in turn are linked to stronger mitochondrial targeting and greater ability to facilitate photopyroptosis; weaker interaction in compound `4` was associated with less pyroptosis. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:215-242]

## Mitochondrial targeting and ROS localization

Using `1d` as the representative molecule, colocalization studies in EMT-6 cells showed a Pearson correlation coefficient of `0.91` for mitochondria relative to other organelles, which the source presents as strong evidence of mitochondrial targeting. High-resolution HIS-SIM imaging further indicated that `1d` localized simultaneously to both the inner and outer mitochondrial membranes, a distribution the authors describe as superior to commercial mitochondrial probes that mostly target cristae. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:243-259]

The source reports that intracellular ROS in the `1d + light` group exceeded that of the nonirradiated `1d` group by more than 16-fold, based on DCFH-DA fluorescence imaging. Because the molecular structure of `1d` was unchanged before and after irradiation, the study attributes the observed effect to ROS photoproduction. It further argues that broad targeting across mitochondrial membranes concentrates ROS production at mitochondria and supports subsequent pyroptosis. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:260-279]

The cellular ROS analysis indicated that singlet oxygen and superoxide anion accounted for the main part of total ROS induced by `1d`, based on the effects of `NaN3` and `Tiron` as specific scavengers. The study also reports increased LDH release, reduced intracellular ATP activity, and increased extracellular ATP content in the irradiated `1d` group, consistent with cell death during the photopyroptotic process. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:279-293]

## Pyroptosis mechanism in the study

The study presents the photopyroptotic route as a ROS-caspase-3-GSDME pathway. After irradiation of cells incubated with `1d`, the authors observed cell membrane fragmentation, loss of mitochondrial membrane potential, mitochondrial structural damage, nuclear shrinkage, and cellular contents outflow. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:98-101, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:318-339]

Western blot data showed that HMGB-1 was upregulated in the `1d + light` group, while caspase-3 activation and obvious `GSDME-N` expression were detected only after irradiation rather than in untreated or dark `1d` controls. The source interprets these findings as evidence that light-triggered ROS activates caspase-3, which cleaves GSDME and drives pyroptosis. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:335-345]

The article also notes that caspase-3 is commonly associated with apoptosis, but argues that when ROS from the photosensitizer causes caspase-3 to cleave GSDME, the signaling balance shifts toward pyroptosis. This is used to explain how competing cell-death programs may coexist while the designed system still yields a predominantly pyroptotic outcome. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:381-388]

For background on pyroptotic execution and inflammasome-linked pyroptosis pathways, see [[Canonical and noncanonical inflammasome pathways in pyroptosis]]. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:88-104, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:674-695]

## Efficiency characteristics

A central claim of the study is that this structural design enables very rapid photopyroptosis. In live-cell imaging, EMT-6, Hepa1-6, and MCF-7 cells incubated with `1d` showed bubbling and rupture under `640 nm` irradiation, and the source states that cells could begin swelling with membrane bubbling after only `10 s` of CLSM laser exposure. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:356-363]

Flow-cytometry analysis with annexin V and propidium iodide showed that the pyroptotic proportion of EMT-6 cells increased continuously after irradiation and that most cells became annexin V+/PI+ within `5 h`. The study further states that nearly all observed cells exhibited bubbling under CLSM laser irradiation at the indicated time point, supporting the claim of high pyroptosis efficiency across the sampled cell population. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:363-380]

The authors emphasize that pyroptosis could be triggered within `10 s` at a light dosage of `0.4 J cm^-2`, which they describe as substantially lower than doses reported in related work, cited as up to `60 J cm^-2`. They also report that reducing laser power density from `40 mW cm^-2` to `2 mW cm^-2` still allowed pyroptosis, although with longer irradiation time. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:24-27, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:376-380, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:548-550]

## In vivo immune and therapeutic effects

The source links photopyroptosis to anti-tumor immune activation. Transcriptome analysis comparing control, `1d`, and `1d + light` cell samples found upregulation of immune-related genes in the irradiated group, and KEGG analysis enriched these differences in pathways including `IL-17`, `TNF`, and `NOD-like receptor` signaling. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:402-429]

In EMT-6 tumor-bearing mice, `1d` showed persistence in tumor tissue with higher fluorescence than in other organs at `24 h` after injection, while the source reports little change in body weight and no significant histopathological abnormalities in major organs. Under treatment, the `1d + light` group showed extensive tumor restraint, with an EMT-6 tumor inhibition rate of `79.5%` on day 16. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:430-456, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:457-499]

The in vivo immune readouts included increased numbers of white blood cells, granulocytes, lymphocytes, and monocytes after `1d + light` treatment, as well as significantly higher proportions of intratumoral `CD4+` and `CD8+` T cells compared with control groups. Tumor sections also showed cleaved caspase-3, `GSDME-N`, and increased `CD8` and granzyme B staining, which the authors interpret as evidence that photopyroptosis awakened anti-tumor immunity and enabled effective tumor immunotherapy using the synthetic molecule alone, without added immunoadjuvants. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:499-523]

## Significance

The study presents this cation-clamping approach as a general structural paradigm for mitochondrial-targeted [[photosensitizers]] used in photopyroptosis-oriented [[photodynamic therapy]]. Its claimed value is that it ties molecular structure to subcellular localization, ROS concentration, rapid GSDME-mediated pyroptotic triggering, and downstream anti-tumor immune effects. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:537-554]
