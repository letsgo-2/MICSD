# WNK4 is dispensable for WNK1-mediated control of NLRP3

[[WNK1]] and [[WNK4]] are both described in the source as chloride-sensitive kinases, prompting the authors to test whether WNK4 also regulates [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3]] activation or compensates for loss of WNK1 in macrophages. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:596-600]

The study found that [[WNK4]] knockout bone marrow-derived macrophages did not show increased NLRP3 activation relative to wild type cells. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:600-602]

To test whether WNK4 might substitute for WNK1, the authors generated myeloid [[WNK1]] conditional knockout / [[WNK4]] null mice (`Wnk1flox/flox LysMCre+ Wnk4−/−`), referred to as WNK1/4 double-knockout mice. In vitro, bone marrow-derived macrophages from these mice did not show an additive increase in inflammasome activation compared with WNK1 conditional knockout macrophages, as measured by `IL-1β` release, `LDH` release, or caspase-1 activation across multiple inflammasome stimuli. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:603-609, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:723-731]

The same pattern was observed in vivo in the [[MSU-induced peritonitis]] model. Compared with WNK1 conditional knockout mice, WNK1/4 double-knockout mice showed no observable difference in percent neutrophils, number of infiltrating neutrophils, or `IL-1β` release after `MSU` injection. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:609-618, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:728-731]

Based on these results, the authors conclude that [[WNK1]], but not [[WNK4]], is critical for regulating NLRP3 activation and the associated immune response, and that WNK4 does not compensate for WNK1 loss in this system. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:614-618]

In the discussion, the authors note that this lack of effect is notable because WNK4 is reported to be more chloride-sensitive than WNK1. They suggest possible explanations, including that WNK4 may not be expressed at functional levels in macrophages, or that WNK1 may be uniquely relevant because its activity is also regulated by cell volume, a process linked to NLRP3 regulation, whereas WNK4 is not sensitive to volume changes. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:750-761]
