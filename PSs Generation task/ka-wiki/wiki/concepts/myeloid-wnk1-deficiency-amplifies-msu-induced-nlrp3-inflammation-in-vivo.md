# Myeloid WNK1 deficiency amplifies MSU-induced NLRP3 inflammation in vivo

Myeloid WNK1 deficiency increases NLRP3 inflammasome activation and pyroptosis in macrophages and in vivo. In the study, mice with conditional Wnk1 deletion in myeloid cells showed higher IL-1β release and greater neutrophil recruitment after intraperitoneal monosodium urate (MSU) challenge, indicating that WNK1 normally suppresses MSU-induced inflammatory responses. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:18-24, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:575-595, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:699-705]

## Overview

The paper identifies WNK1 as a negative regulator of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] in macrophages. It reports that loss or inhibition of WNK1 increases caspase-1 activation, IL-1β production, ASC oligomerization, and pyroptosis in response to NLRP3 stimuli, including MSU crystals. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:17-24, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:146-160, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:274-283]

## In vivo MSU response

To test physiological relevance, the authors injected 8- to 12-week-old `Wnk1flox/flox LysMCre+` mice and `Wnk1+/+ LysMCre+` littermates with 1 mg MSU for either 6 hours or 16 hours and analyzed peritoneal exudates. WNK1-deficient mice had significantly higher IL-1β levels and increased neutrophil infiltration at both time points, supporting an amplified NLRP3-dependent innate immune response in vivo. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:575-595, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:699-705]

## Mechanism

The study links WNK1’s inhibitory role to chloride sensing and ion homeostasis. WNK1 is described as a chloride-sensitive kinase that activates the OXSR1/STK39 pathway, which in turn stimulates cation-chloride cotransporters to help restore intracellular chloride and potassium and limit further NLRP3 activation. When WNK1 is absent, macrophages lose more intracellular Cl- and K+, which the authors associate with stronger inflammasome activation and pyroptosis. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:392-451, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:482-493, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:762-770]
