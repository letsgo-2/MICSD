# TBE-loaded ROS-responsive NP1 drives mitochondrial photodamage, mtDNA release, and caspase-3 activation under 808 nm irradiation

`NP1` is an oxidation-sensitive nanoparticle assembled from the photosensitizer `TBE` and a ROS-sensitive polymer to protect `TBE` during circulation while enabling tumor-microenvironment-responsive release. In the study, `TBE` showed broad NIR-region absorption and efficient singlet-oxygen generation under `808 nm` irradiation, and these properties were used to produce photodynamic effects after `TBE` was formulated into `NP1`. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:149-189, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:202-220]

`NP1` was reported to form uniform spherical particles with an average hydrodynamic diameter of `93.3 +/- 7.7 nm`. It remained stable for 7 days under physiological conditions but underwent marked morphological change after `12 h` in `10 mM H2O2`, supporting its ROS-responsive degradability. In `MCF-7` cells, `NP1` was efficiently internalized, with intracellular fluorescence increasing over time and reaching a plateau after `7 h` incubation. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:202-223]

Under in vitro photodynamic conditions, intracellular ROS was measured with `DCFH-DA` after `1 min` of `808 nm` irradiation at `1 W/cm^2`. Relative to the non-irradiated group, `NP1` plus light (`NP1+L`) increased intracellular ROS by `19.0%`, indicating that the nanoparticle retained photodynamic activity after cellular uptake. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:224-228]

The study linked this ROS increase to mitochondrial injury. Using `TMRE`, the authors found that `NP1+L` caused loss of mitochondrial membrane potential by `35.6%` and `28.1%` relative to `PBS` and `NP1`, respectively. `NP1+L` also increased cytosolic cytochrome c release by `42.1%` versus `PBS`, consistent with mitochondrial photodamage and membrane permeabilization. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:228-233]

Direct imaging further supported mitochondrial disruption and `[[mtDNA]]` escape. In `PBS`-treated `MCF-7` cells, mtDNA nucleoids stained with `PicoGreen` remained enclosed within `TOM20`-positive mitochondria with continuous outer membranes. After `NP1+L`, the mitochondria showed discontinuous crescent-shaped `TOM20` staining together with reduced mtDNA-mitochondria colocalization, which the authors interpreted as mitochondrial membrane permeabilization and cytosolic release of mtDNA. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:233-246]

`NP1+L` also activated apoptotic signaling associated with downstream pyroptosis competence in this system. The level of cleaved `[[caspase-3]]` increased by `56.8%` compared with `PBS`, and apoptosis analysis showed a significantly higher apoptotic fraction for `NP1+L` (`11.5%`) than for `NP1` alone (`6.8%`). Meanwhile, `NP1` without irradiation showed negligible cytotoxicity up to `20 ug/mL`, whereas `NP1+L` produced marked tumor-cell growth inhibition with an `IC50` of `7.5 ug/mL`, indicating strong light-dependent activity with minimal dark toxicity. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:232-257]

Within the broader mechanism proposed by the paper, these `NP1`-driven events provide the photodynamic inputs for two immune-related pathways: mitochondrial dysfunction releases cytosolic mtDNA that can engage the `[[cGAS-STING pathway]]`, and caspase-3 activation can cleave `[[GSDME]]` when its expression has been restored by decitabine-loaded `NP2`. The authors therefore position `NP1` as the light-triggered mitochondrial-damage module of a combined epigenetic/photodynamic nanotheranostic system. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:59-72, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:120-132, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:595-620]
