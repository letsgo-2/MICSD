I’m extracting only the source-backed claims about the gene signature, then I’ll format them as a concise wiki page with a source section and line-accurate citations.# Pyroptosis-associated gene signature induced by {BiW8}

{BiW8} was reported to induce a pyroptosis-associated gene signature in MG63 osteosarcoma cells. In the RNA-sequencing analysis, 10 pyroptosis-related genes were upregulated, including `CASP3`, `CASP4`, `CASP5`, `GSDMB`, `GSDME`, `IL18`, `NLRP3`, `NLRC4`, `NLRP10`, and `TXNIP`. The authors verified these findings with qRT-PCR for selected genes, including `GSDME`, `IL-18`, `NLRP3`, and `TXNIP`. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:458-487, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:496-501]

The study places this gene signature in the context of a broader antitumor response. {BiW8} induced pyroptotic morphology, increased LDH release, reduced ATP levels, and raised ROS levels in MG63 cells, while transcriptome analysis also showed downregulation of glutathione metabolism genes and DNA repair/replication genes. The authors conclude that {BiW8} acts through multiple antitumor mechanisms, including activation of pyroptosis. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:294-343, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:439-452, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:502-567]
