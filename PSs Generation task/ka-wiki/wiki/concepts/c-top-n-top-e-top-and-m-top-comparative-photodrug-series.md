# C-TOP, N-TOP, E-TOP, and M-TOP comparative photodrug series

The C-TOP, N-TOP, E-TOP, and M-TOP comparative photodrug series is the set of four photodrugs analyzed together in the study’s supplementary methods and calculations. The source identifies all four compounds by name, reports their syntheses or referenced syntheses, and states that all optimized ground-state geometries and excited-state calculations were performed comparatively for C-TOP, N-TOP, E-TOP, and M-TOP. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:69-85, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:105-125, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:161-187]

## Composition of the series

C-TOP and N-TOP are presented as precursor compounds whose syntheses followed prior literature references, while E-TOP and M-TOP were synthesized from N-TOP by coupling reactions shown in Figure S1. In the reported procedures, E-TOP was prepared by reacting N-TOP with biotin, whereas M-TOP was prepared by reacting N-TOP with `TPP-COOH`. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:69-85, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:105-125, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:139-178]

Figure S1 is explicitly described as the synthetic route of M-TOP and E-TOP, and the accompanying structures label N-TOP as the shared intermediate used to access both products. This makes the four-compound set a comparative series centered on N-TOP and its derivatives E-TOP and M-TOP, alongside C-TOP as an additional comparator. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:105-125, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:139-178]

## Comparative experimental use

The supplementary methods describe side-by-side biological comparison of the four photodrugs in [[U251-MG]] cells. For western blot and inflammatory-factor analysis associated with Figure 1i, cells received `1 uM` C-TOP, N-TOP, E-TOP, or M-TOP, were washed, irradiated at `15 mW/cm^2` for `5 min`, and then analyzed after an additional `3 h` culture period. Supernatants were collected for `IL-1beta` and `LDH` measurements, and cell lysates were analyzed for `caspase-3`, cleaved `PARP`, and `GSDME` by western blotting. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:263-289]

The series was also compared for cellular uptake and intracellular localization behavior. U251-MG cells were incubated with photodrugs at a fixed concentration of `0.5 uM`, uptake was measured by [[flow cytometry]], and intracellular distribution was examined by [[confocal laser scanning microscopy]] using [[Lysotracker]] and [[Mitotracker]] colocalization workflows. Figure S18 is identified as showing colocalization of photodrugs with mitochondria or lysosomes after `30 min` incubation, and Figure S21 is identified as flow-cytometry analysis of different photodrugs showing their cellular uptake features. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:210-247, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:443-466]

The four compounds were further compared in reactive oxygen species readouts and cytotoxicity-related assays. The methods specify use of `DCFH-DA`, `SOSG`, and `DHE` to evaluate general ROS, singlet oxygen, and superoxide anion generation after photodrug treatment and `640 nm` irradiation, while MTT assays were used to measure cell cytotoxicity after dark or light treatment. Figure S19 is described as emission-based comparison of PBS solutions containing `1 uM` photodrug after irradiation. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:233-247, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:296-305, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:450-453]

## Theoretical comparison

The source states that theoretical calculations were carried out for all four members of the series using a common computational framework. Ground-state geometries were optimized at the `PBE0/6-31G(d,p)` level with a water continuum model, excited states were calculated by time-dependent DFT with the same functional and basis set, and spin-orbit coupling matrix elements were calculated with ORCA 5.0 at the `PBE0/def2-TZVP` level. Counterions were not included because the frontier molecular distribution was described as almost independent of the anions. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:179-187]

Figure S16 is explicitly a comparative presentation of frontier molecular orbital distribution, energies, and spin-orbit coupling matrix elements for `(a) M-TOP`, `(b) E-TOP`, `(c) N-TOP`, and `(d) C-TOP`. The figure note further states that excluding counterions should improve spin-orbit coupling and enhance triplet exciton population, while leaving frontier molecular orbital distribution nearly unchanged with respect to anion identity. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:428-434]

## Role within the study

Within the supplementary information, this four-compound series functions as the comparative chemical and photobiological framework used to benchmark the study’s photodrug design. The source later contrasts a “mitochondrial-targeting Type-I photodrug (this work)” with other photodynamic strategies and attributes to this work features including mitochondria targeting, low oxygen dependency, caspase-3-mediated signaling-pathway switching, controllable inflammatory response, and controllable immunotherapy against primary, distant, and recurrent tumors. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:522-599]

Because the cited comparative cellular methods explicitly include all four photodrugs, the series provides the basis for identifying which compound best matched the study’s intended design features before downstream immune and in vivo assays focused on M-TOP. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:263-289, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:306-311, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:317-366]
