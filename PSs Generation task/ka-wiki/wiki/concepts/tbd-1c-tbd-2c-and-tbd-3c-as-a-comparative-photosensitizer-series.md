# TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C as a comparative photosensitizer series

**TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C** are presented in the supporting information as a three-member comparative series of [[AIE photosensitizer]]s used in parallel across synthesis, photophysical characterization, cellular imaging, and photodynamic treatment experiments. The document consistently groups them together in ROS assays, confocal imaging, flow cytometry, cell viability studies, SEM imaging, western blotting, and apoptosis/pyroptosis-related analyses. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:182-240]

## Overview

The series was prepared as closely related red solids and treated as a matched set for comparison. TBD-1C was synthesized from compound 5 and isolated in 66% yield, TBD-2C from compound 10 in 53% yield, and TBD-3C from compound 11 in 22% yield; each final step used trimethylamine solution in methanol in anhydrous THF at -78 °C followed by stirring at room temperature and purification by preparative TLC. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:97-106, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:163-181]

Scheme captions identify the comparative design explicitly: Scheme S1 gives the synthetic route to TBD-1C, while Scheme S2 gives the synthetic routes to TBD-2C and TBD-3C. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:246-248]

## Comparative role in the study

The three compounds were evaluated side by side in a solution-phase [[reactive oxygen species]] assay using ABDA as the indicator. In that assay, ABDA at 50 uM was mixed with TBD-1C, TBD-2C, or TBD-3C at 5 uM in DMSO/PBS (1/99, v/v), irradiated with white light (400-700 nm, 30 mW/cm2), and monitored by the decrease in ABDA absorbance at 378 nm; Rose Bengal was used as a benchmark. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:182-185, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:340-343]

They were also compared in photoluminescence measurements in toluene/MeOH mixtures with varying toluene fractions. Figure S17 tracks PL spectra for TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C and includes a plot of relative emission intensity at 650 nm versus solvent composition. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:339-343]

In cell-based imaging workflows, the three photosensitizers were applied to 4T1, HeLa, and C6 cells at 10 uM for 1 hour before confocal imaging. The red AIE photosensitizer channel used excitation at 405 nm and emission collection at 600-680 nm; for membrane co-staining in HeLa cells, CellMask Green and Hoechst 33342 were also used. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:186-198]

The comparative series was carried through quantitative and functional cellular assays as well. Flow cytometry protocols measured mean fluorescence intensity and labeling efficiency after incubation with different AIE photosensitizers, and CCK-8 cell ablation studies tested concentrations from 0 to 25 uM with white-light irradiation at 40 mW/cm2 for 10 minutes, followed by 24 hours of further culture before viability readout. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:199-212]

The same three-compound comparison was used in assays related to [[pyroptosis]] and [[photodynamic therapy]]. LDH release and cytokine release assays quantified LDH, IL-18, and IL-1beta in culture supernatants, while western blotting compared control, TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C under light or dark conditions for proteins including mGSDMD, cleaved mGSDMD, caspase-1, caspase-3, Bax, and Bcl-2. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:213-231]

Flow cytometry analysis of annexin V-FITC and propidium iodide was also structured around control, TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C under dark or light-treated conditions in 4T1 cells. The supporting text states that annexin V single-positive cells indicate apoptotic cells, whereas double-positive annexin V/PI cells indicate pyroptotic cells in this assay design. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:232-240, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:380-381]

## Supporting figures for the series

The supporting figures document the series across characterization and biological comparison. These include NMR and mass spectra for each final compound, PL spectra for all three compounds, ABDA-based ROS measurements, confocal images of treated C6 and HeLa cells, time-dependent flow cytometry for TBD-3C labeling, dark-toxicity cell viability data, real-time observations of membrane expansion and pyroptosis release, SEM images of pyroptotic bodies, membrane-anchoring comparisons in HeLa cells, and annexin V/PI flow cytometry after dark or light treatment. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:284-290, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:326-343, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:349-381]

## Distinguishing synthetic endpoints

The final characterization entries distinguish the three members by their reported mass-spectrometric endpoints: TBD-1C is reported with HR ESI-MS as `[M-I]+` calculated 988.4255 and found 988.4830; TBD-2C is reported as `[M-2I]2+` calculated 573.2897 and found 573.3240; TBD-3C is reported as `[M+Na]+` calculated 1708.4382 and found 1708.4358. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:101-106, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:167-181]

TBD-3C also differs from TBD-1C and TBD-2C in that its precursor, compound 11, was synthesized according to previous literature rather than being fully detailed in this supporting information. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:30, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:170-175]
