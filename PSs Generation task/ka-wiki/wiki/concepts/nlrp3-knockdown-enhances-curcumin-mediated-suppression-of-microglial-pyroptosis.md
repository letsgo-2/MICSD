# NLRP3 knockdown enhances curcumin-mediated suppression of microglial pyroptosis

This study reports that curcumin suppresses [[microglial pyroptosis]] after ischemic stroke and that direct reduction of [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3]] further strengthens that effect. In both the mouse MCAO model and LPS+ATP-stimulated primary microglia, NLRP3 knockdown combined with curcumin produced greater decreases in pyroptosis-related and proinflammatory markers than curcumin alone. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:38-43, omcl1552127-125.md:957-978, omcl1552127-125.md:1627-1634]

## Experimental context

In vivo, mice underwent transient middle cerebral artery occlusion and received curcumin at `150 mg/kg` intraperitoneally immediately after reperfusion and daily for 7 days. To test the role of NLRP3, the authors silenced it in the ischemic cortex using AAV-based shRNA 2 weeks before MCAO, then compared `MCAO+shRNA-Con+vehicle`, `MCAO+shRNA-Con+curcumin`, `MCAO+shRNA-NLRP3+vehicle`, and `MCAO+shRNA-NLRP3+curcumin` groups. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:30-32, omcl1552127-125.md:194-214, omcl1552127-125.md:219-221]

In vitro, primary microglia from neonatal mice were stimulated with LPS (`100 ng/mL`) for 24 hours and ATP (`1 mM`) for 3 hours to induce pyroptosis. NLRP3 was silenced with siRNA, and cells were treated with curcumin (`12.5 μM`) to determine whether NLRP3 loss altered curcumin’s anti-pyroptotic effect. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:224-228, omcl1552127-125.md:587-602, omcl1552127-125.md:1345-1353]

## Evidence that NLRP3 knockdown enhances curcumin's anti-pyroptotic effect

The paper states that either curcumin treatment or NLRP3 knockdown alone reduced pyroptosis-associated proteins in peri-infarct tissue after MCAO, including cleaved caspase-1, GSDMD-N, pro-IL-1β, and IL-1β. When NLRP3-shRNA was combined with curcumin, these reductions were stronger, which the authors describe as a marked enhancement of curcumin’s effects. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:963-978]

The same pattern was observed in primary microglia in vitro. siRNA-mediated NLRP3 reduction lowered GSDMD-N, cleaved caspase-1, pro-IL-1β, IL-1β, pro-IL-18, and IL-18 in LPS+ATP-treated microglia, while curcumin also attenuated these pyroptosis-related changes in the siRNA control group. The authors specifically note that NLRP3 knockdown enhanced the inhibitory effects of curcumin on microglial pyroptosis. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:1197-1203, omcl1552127-125.md:1627-1634]

The study summarizes these combined findings as synergistic inhibition of stroke-induced microglial pyroptosis by curcumin and NLRP3 suppression in vivo and in vitro. In the discussion, the authors reiterate that NLRP3 silencing synergistically enhanced curcumin’s anti-pyroptotic and neuroprotective effects and confirmed in vitro that NLRP3 knockdown significantly enhanced curcumin-mediated pyroptosis inhibition. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:1721-1728, omcl1552127-125.md:2295-2308]

## Relationship to inflammatory signaling

The source frames this effect within the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] pathway that drives cleavage of [[caspase-1]], [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], and maturation of [[IL-1beta]] and [[IL-18]]. Curcumin reduced NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1, GSDMD-N, IL-1β, and IL-18 in vivo and in vitro, and the authors interpret the stronger response after NLRP3 knockdown as evidence that curcumin’s anti-pyroptotic action is closely tied to NLRP3 inflammasome inhibition. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:115-124, omcl1552127-125.md:935-956, omcl1552127-125.md:2282-2300]

The paper also links curcumin’s action upstream to [[NF-kappaB|NF-κB]] suppression. In both ischemic brain tissue and LPS+ATP-treated microglia, curcumin reduced phosphorylation of IKKα/β, IκBα, and p65, and the authors conclude that curcumin inhibits NLRP3 inflammasome activation through suppression of NF-κB signaling in microglia. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:1655-1664, omcl1552127-125.md:1666-1680, omcl1552127-125.md:1812-1824, omcl1552127-125.md:2393-2402]

## Functional significance in the stroke model

Beyond molecular markers, NLRP3 knockdown improved behavioral and white matter outcomes after stroke and strengthened the benefits seen with curcumin. The authors report that NLRP3-shRNA reduced time to contact and remove adhesive tape, enhanced curcumin’s effects on sensorimotor recovery, and markedly enhanced curcumin-induced protection against white matter damage measured by Luxol fast blue staining. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:1635-1654, omcl1552127-125.md:1883-1893]

These results are presented as support for a model in which stroke-induced [[NF-kappaB|NF-κB]] activation promotes NLRP3 inflammasome signaling and microglial pyroptosis, while curcumin interrupts that pathway. Under this model, direct NLRP3 knockdown removes more of the pyroptotic signaling burden and therefore amplifies curcumin-mediated suppression of microglial pyroptosis and its associated white matter protection. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:2364-2372, omcl1552127-125.md:2416-2419, omcl1552127-125.md:2475-2481]
