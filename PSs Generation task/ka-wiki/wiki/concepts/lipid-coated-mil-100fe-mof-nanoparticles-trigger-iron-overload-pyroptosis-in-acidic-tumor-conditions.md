# Lipid-coated MIL-100(Fe) MOF nanoparticles trigger iron-overload pyroptosis in acidic tumor conditions

Lipid-coated MIL-100(Fe) metal-organic framework nanoparticles ([[MOF nanoparticles|MOF nanoparticles]]) were described as a hybrid nanomaterial designed to deliver large amounts of `Fe3+` directly into cells. The particles were built from `Fe3+` and trimesic acid, then coated with `1,2-dioleoyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine` ([[DOPC]]) to reduce recognition of surface ions and facilitate cellular endocytosis. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:604-613]

The source presents this system as a way to disrupt cellular ion homeostasis in tumors. Liposome-like coating gave the Lip-MOFs good biocompatibility and a significant [[enhanced permeability and retention effect|EPR effect]], while also supporting uptake into cells by clathrin-mediated endocytosis. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:604-616]

## Mechanism

After cellular uptake, Lip-MOFs were transported into lysosomes, where they were degraded into `Fe3+` ions and trimesic acid through a cysteine-involving reduction process. The review states that intracellular degradation and iron release could be controlled by the acidic extracellular environment, linking the system to acidic [[tumor microenvironment|tumor conditions]]. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:616-623]

A large amount of released `Fe3+` was reported to trigger lysosomal rupture and then induce [[pyroptosis]]. This pyroptotic death was described as being mediated by caspase activation, [[gasdermin]] cleavage, and `IL-1beta` release. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:622-624]

## Experimental observations

For visualization of intracellular breakdown, calcein was loaded into the MOFs before DOPC coating. In lysosomes, the dye was initially quenched by the MOFs and then became fluorescent after MOF disintegration. After about 40 hours of incubation, fluorescence turned on in acidic lysosomes, indicating that the Lip-MOFs had been phagocytosed and had reached the lysosomal compartment; within seconds, the fluorescence spread throughout cells, consistent with lysosomal disintegration. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:625-644]

The source further states that lysosomal Lip-MOF collapse released iron through cysteine-based reduction, and that this process was reinforced in a slightly acidic extracellular environment. Pretreatment with `z-YVAD-FMK`, described here as an inhibitor for pyroptosis-inducing caspases, enhanced cell viability, reduced full-length `GSDMD`, and altered `IL-1beta` release, which the review interprets collectively as evidence that pyroptosis dominated the cell death pathway. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:644-652]

## Cell-type context

Clear pyroptotic morphology after Lip-MOF treatment was reported in `A431`, `MCF7`, and macrophage cells, but not in `A549` and `MCF-10A` cells. The review highlights macrophages because of their role in the tumor immune system and suggests that Lip-MOF uptake by macrophages could help elicit an immune response. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:652-658]

## Significance

In the review's interpretation, Lip-MOFs and related nanocomposites may be useful for attacking cancer cells in acidic tumor microenvironments through a pyroptosis-based strategy, with possible relevance to [[cancer immunotherapy]]. The proposed advantage is selective activation tied to tumor acidity and iron release after lysosomal processing. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:659-661, cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:1053-1062]
