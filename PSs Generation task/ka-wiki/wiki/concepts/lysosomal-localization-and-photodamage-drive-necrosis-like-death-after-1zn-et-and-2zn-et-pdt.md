I’m extracting only the relevant claims from the source and will keep the page scoped to localization, photodamage, and the death-mode evidence.# Lysosomal localization and photodamage drive necrosis-like death after 1Zn-Et and 2Zn-Et PDT

In HeLa cells, both `1Zn-Et` and `2Zn-Et` localized primarily to lysosomes before irradiation. After photodynamic treatment, the photosensitizers were redistributed from lysosomes to the cytoplasm, followed by secondary damage to mitochondria, plasma membrane, and eventually the nucleus. The study concludes that this sequence reflects primary lysosomal damage with necrosis-like cell death predominating over classical apoptosis. ^[jm5014852-114.md:401-418, jm5014852-114.md:462-535, jm5014852-114.md:667-678]

The authors observed that irradiation caused rapid loss of punctate lysosomal staining, leakage of `1Zn-Et` from lysosomes into the cytoplasm, rounding and loss of mitochondrial structure, and eventual cellular debris formation. These morphological changes were described as typical of necrotic cell death. ^[jm5014852-114.md:444-491]

Flow cytometry supported this interpretation. After photodynamic treatment, most cells were initially annexin V-FITC negative and PI negative, but later populations of annexin V-FITC positive / PI positive cells and increased debris appeared, especially at higher photosensitizer doses. The paper also reports that caspase 8, 9, and 3/7 activities did not show a pattern consistent with canonical apoptosis as the main pathway, and instead suggests lysosomal enzyme release as a plausible trigger for downstream damage. ^[jm5014852-114.md:536-573, jm5014852-114.md:574-609]

The study further notes that no significant autophagosome signal was detected after treatment, making autophagy unlikely to be a major contributor under the tested conditions. ^[jm5014852-114.md:610-626]
