# Caspase-8 Acts Upstream of Caspase-3 in PDT-Induced Pyroptosis

In photodynamic therapy (PDT)-treated esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC), the reported signaling sequence places caspase-8 upstream of caspase-3 in a pyroptotic pathway that ends in GSDME cleavage and membrane rupture. The study describes PDT as targeting the PKM2/caspase-8/caspase-3/GSDME axis and notes that inhibition of caspase-8 suppresses caspase-3 cleavage and pyroptosis, supporting an upstream role for caspase-8 in this context. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:23-31, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:356-405, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:519-532]

PDT-induced pyroptosis in ESCC was characterized by swollen cells, membrane bubbles, plasma membrane rupture, LDH release, and increased inflammatory mediators. The authors report increased cleaved caspase-3 and N-GSDME after PDT, while GSDMD and caspase-1 were not implicated, indicating a GSDME-centered pyroptotic program rather than a GSDMD/caspase-1 pathway. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:282-309, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:318-355, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:509-518]

Evidence for caspase-8 being upstream comes from inhibitor experiments and expression correlations. In GEPIA analysis, GSDME expression correlated with caspase-3 and caspase-8, and caspase-3 correlated with caspase-8. In cells, the caspase-8 inhibitor Z-IETD-FMK reduced pyroptosis, lowered IL-1β release, and inhibited caspase-3 cleavage; the caspase-3 inhibitor did not block caspase-8 activation, which the authors interpret as caspase-8 contributing to caspase-3 activation rather than the reverse. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:380-405, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:527-532]

The study places this cascade within a broader metabolic model in which PDT downregulates PKM2, leading to caspase-8 and caspase-3 activation and then N-GSDME release. PKM2 overexpression reduced pyroptotic morphology and lowered caspase-3, caspase-8, and N-GSDME signals, reinforcing the proposed order of events in the PDT response. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:442-453, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:487-498, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:547-563, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-595]
