# MOF-199 as a dual carrier and intracellular Cu(I) click-catalyst source

MOF-199 was used as both an inert carrier for photosensitizer precursors and a source of intracellular Cu(I) for click chemistry. In the study, TPA-alkyne-2+ and MePy-N3 were loaded into MOF-199 to form PMOF, and the framework was then shown to release the precursors while GSH reduced Cu(II) nodes to Cu(I), enabling in situ synthesis of the photosensitizer TPATrzPy-3+ inside cancer cells. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:45-48, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:156-175, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:265-277]

## Dual function

As a carrier, MOF-199 loaded both photochemically inert precursors at a total weight percentage of 51.3%, with the materials retained inside the pores and channels rather than merely adsorbed on the surface. As a catalyst source, its Cu(II) nodes were reduced by GSH to monovalent Cu ions, which the authors state could catalyze the azide-alkyne click reaction. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:278-305, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:341-364]

## Intracellular activation

After cellular uptake, PMOF NPs remained intact during transport and then collapsed in the GSH-rich cancer-cell environment, releasing both precursors and copper ions. The study reports that this setup produced cancer-cell-specific TPATrzPy-3+ synthesis, with intracellular yield reaching about 70.5% by 24 h in HeLa cells. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:417-460, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:447-460]

## Functional outcome

The in situ formed TPATrzPy-3+ showed mitochondria-targeting behavior and efficient ROS generation under light, supporting image-guided cancer-cell ablation. In HeLa cells, PMOF NPs were selectively phototoxic compared with 3T3 cells, while light toxicity was reduced when GSH was depleted with BSO, consistent with a GSH-triggered intracellular activation mechanism. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:405-416, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:465-498]
