# Primary human monocytes show stronger baseline readiness for AgNP15-induced IL-1beta release than THP-1 cells or macrophages

In this study of `15 nm` silver nanoparticles (`AgNP15`), primary human monocytes showed a distinct baseline state that made them more immediately permissive for [[IL-1beta]] release than either [[THP-1]] cells or monocyte-derived macrophages. The key difference was that resting primary monocytes already displayed detectable basal `pro-IL-1beta`, whereas `pro-IL-1beta` was undetectable in THP-1 cells and macrophages under the compared conditions. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:643-652]

The authors also found cell-type differences in [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP-3]] expression dynamics. In THP-1 cells, `NLRP-3` expression remained similar even after `6 h` of [[LPS]] priming, whereas in primary monocytes and macrophages it increased over time after `3-6 h` of LPS stimulation. This indicates that the compared monocyte-lineage cell types did not start from the same inflammasome-related baseline before `AgNP15` exposure. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:653-656]

Functionally, this difference appeared in the `IL-1beta` secretion assay. LPS priming alone was sufficient to induce `IL-1beta` secretion in THP-1 cells and macrophages. By contrast, primary monocytes produced a significant amount of `IL-1beta` in response to `AgNP15` alone, without LPS priming. In all three cell types, the strongest `IL-1beta` response occurred when LPS priming was followed by `AgNP15` stimulation. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:656-679]

In the discussion, the authors interpret these results as evidence that basal expression differences in inflammasome components help explain the stronger unprimed response of primary monocytes. They state that the slightly different baseline expression profiles across THP-1 cells, primary monocytes, and macrophages led to marked variation in extracellular mature `IL-1beta`, and specifically note that resting monocytes had detectable `NLRP-3` and `pro-IL-1beta`, possibly explaining why `AgNP15` alone triggered `IL-1beta` secretion in that cell type. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:761-776]

## Context

Across the paper, `AgNP15` is presented as an inducer of [[pyroptosis]] and [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation in human monocytes, with evidence including caspase-1 processing and activity, [[ASC]] pyroptosome formation, and `IL-1beta` secretion. The comparison among THP-1 cells, primary monocytes, and macrophages adds a cell-state dimension to that mechanism by showing that primary monocytes require less preparatory signaling to release `IL-1beta` after `AgNP15` exposure. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:26-50, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:485-579, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:643-679]
