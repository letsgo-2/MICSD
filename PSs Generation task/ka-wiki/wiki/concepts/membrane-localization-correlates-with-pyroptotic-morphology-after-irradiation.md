# Membrane localization correlates with pyroptotic morphology after irradiation

This study’s supporting information describes a set of membrane-anchoring AIE photosensitizers, `TBD-1C`, `TBD-2C`, and `TBD-3C`, and evaluates their localization and cell-death phenotypes after light exposure. In HeLa cells, membrane localization was assessed by confocal imaging with membrane co-staining, while pyroptotic morphology after irradiation was assessed by real-time imaging and scanning electron microscopy. These experiments together support the relationship between stronger membrane anchoring and pyroptosis-associated morphological change after irradiation. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:186-198, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:217-223, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:358-370]

## Experimental basis

For localization, cells were incubated with different AIE photosensitizers and imaged by confocal microscopy. In the membrane co-staining experiment, HeLa cells were first treated with AIE photosensitizers, then stained with `CellMask Green` as a plasma membrane tracker and `Hoechst 33342` for nuclei before imaging. This setup was used to visualize how strongly each photosensitizer associated with the cell membrane. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:186-198]

For morphology after irradiation, the supporting information reports both real-time confocal observation and scanning electron microscopy. In SEM experiments, cells were incubated with the AIE photosensitizers and then irradiated at `40 mW/cm2` for `2`, `5`, or `10` minutes before fixation and imaging. Supplementary figure captions state that real-time observation showed membrane expansion and pyroptosis release, and SEM identified pyroptotic bodies in irradiated cells. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:217-223, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:358-361]

## Correlation between membrane localization and pyroptotic morphology

Figure S24 explicitly links membrane anchoring and pyroptotic outcome in HeLa cells. Its panels include: a larger-view SEM comparison of cells with and without pyroptosis after irradiation, confocal images of HeLa cells with membrane anchored by `TBD-1C`, `TBD-2C`, and `TBD-3C`, and a quantitative analysis of average fluorescence densities at the cell membrane together with pyroptosis cell ratios after PDT treatment. This figure therefore presents membrane fluorescence density and pyroptosis frequency as paired measurements across the three photosensitizers. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:367-370]

The source material does not provide the numerical values in the extracted text, but it does state that membrane fluorescence density and pyroptosis cell ratios were quantified together for the `TBD-R` series. Based on that figure description, the reported relationship is that membrane localization correlates with the proportion of cells showing pyroptotic morphology after irradiation. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:367-370]

## Pyroptotic morphology described in the source

The morphological hallmarks used here are consistent with [[pyroptosis]]-like membrane damage. Real-time confocal observation is described as showing cell membrane expansion and pyroptosis release, while SEM images highlight pyroptotic bodies with yellow arrows. Another SEM figure distinguishes cells with pyroptosis from cells without pyroptosis after treatment with `TBD-1C`, `TBD-2C`, and `TBD-3C` under irradiation. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:358-361, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:367-370]

The supporting methods also define how pyroptotic cells were identified in flow cytometry: annexin V and propidium iodide double-positive cells were interpreted as pyroptotic, because membrane rupture permits annexin V entry and PI staining. This complements the imaging-based morphology measurements by connecting visible membrane disruption with an assay framework for [[cell death]] classification. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:232-240]

## Interpretation

Within the scope of the supporting information, the central point is not just that irradiation causes cell damage, but that the extent of [[plasma membrane]] localization by these photosensitizers tracks with how often irradiated cells display pyroptosis-associated morphology. The study’s imaging design, figure organization, and quantitative analysis were all set up to compare membrane anchoring and pyroptotic cell ratios side by side for the same compounds. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:186-198, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:217-223, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:367-370]
