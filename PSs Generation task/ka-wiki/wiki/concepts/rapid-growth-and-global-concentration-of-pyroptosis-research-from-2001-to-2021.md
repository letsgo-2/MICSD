# Rapid growth and global concentration of pyroptosis research from 2001 to 2021

Pyroptosis research expanded rapidly between 2001 and 2021. In the bibliometric study’s dataset, 2,845 articles and reviews on pyroptosis were retrieved from the Web of Science Core Collection, and the annual publication count increased year by year across the study period. The authors characterize 2001 to 2012 as a low-output, early-stage period, 2012 to 2017 as a phase of steady growth, and 2017 to 2020 as a period of explosive expansion, with publication output reaching 822 papers in 2020. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:24-29, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:153-168, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:205-215]

This growth was interpreted as evidence that pyroptosis had become an active and increasingly important research area by the late 2010s. The study also notes that many co-cited references were cited heavily in the most recent decade and suggests that pyroptosis-related research was likely to continue expanding. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:338-342, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:439-481]

## Global concentration

Although publications came from 70 countries and 418 institutions, output was highly concentrated in two countries: China produced 1,302 papers, or 45.76% of the total, and the United States produced 905 papers, or 31.81%. Together, they accounted for 77.57% of all publications in the dataset, making them the dominant national centers of [[pyroptosis]] research during this period. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:216-222, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:218-240, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:482-487]

At the institutional level, the field was also concentrated. The Chinese Academy of Sciences had the largest number of publications among institutions with 67 papers, followed closely by St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital with 65 and Shanghai Jiao Tong University with 64. Among the top 10 institutions, seven were based in China and two were based in the United States, reinforcing the strong concentration of research activity in those two countries. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:223-240, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:494-497]

## Collaboration patterns

The study found that publication leadership did not translate into dense global collaboration. While some countries and institutions showed active cooperation, most were described as scattered and lacking stable, intensive cooperation and communication. Examples of more connected countries included England, Sweden, Israel, Denmark, and Slovenia by centrality, while cooperation links were observed among a smaller set of countries and institutions such as England, Italy, Australia, France, Belgium, Switzerland, Israel, Portugal, Scotland, the Chinese Academy of Sciences, the University of Massachusetts, and Yale University. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:225-256]

England had the highest country centrality score among the top 10 countries at 0.29, indicating an important bridge role in the international collaboration network despite having far fewer papers than China or the United States. The Chinese Academy of Sciences had the highest institutional centrality among the top institutions at 0.20. The authors emphasize, however, that major producers such as China, the United States, the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital did not form a strong collaborative network, indicating limited academic exchange across leading nodes. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:226-227, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:230-240, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:487-507]

## Related signs of field consolidation

The broader structure of the literature also reflects a maturing and concentrated field. The 2,845 pyroptosis papers were published across 829 journals, but a small number of journals accounted for a notable share of output. *Frontiers in Immunology* published the most papers on the topic with 90 articles, followed by *Cell Death & Disease* with 73 and *Journal of Immunology* with 59. On the citation side, *Nature* was the most frequently co-cited journal with 11,443 citations, followed by *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America* with 6,283 and *Journal of Immunology* with 6,274. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:279-296, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:349-370, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:543-560]

The author landscape showed a similar pattern of concentration around a relatively small set of visible contributors. The study identified 605 authors, with Thirumaladevi Kanneganti having the largest number of publications at 50. Feng Shao and Petr Broz had comparatively high centrality among leading authors, and Shi J.J. was the most frequently co-cited author with 1,127 citations. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:257-278, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:321-332, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:508-542]

## Research context

The bibliometric analysis links the field’s growth to expanding interest in the mechanisms and disease relevance of pyroptosis. The most frequent keywords after “pyroptosis” included [[NLRP3 inflammasome]], [[apoptosis]], activation, cell death, inflammasome, [[GSDMD]], mechanism, and [[caspase-1]]. The study concludes that pathway mechanisms, the relationship between pyroptosis and other forms of [[programmed cell death]], and the role of pyroptosis in disease were the main research hotspots and likely future directions. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:32-40, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:377-397, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:405-436, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:571-583, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:905-922]

## Takeaway

From 2001 to 2021, pyroptosis research moved from a small, early-stage topic to a fast-growing international field. That expansion was not evenly distributed: research output was strongly concentrated in China and the United States, and especially in a relatively small group of institutions and authors. At the same time, the study argues that collaboration across countries and institutions remained comparatively weak relative to the field’s growth and would need strengthening in future work. ^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:27-38, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:205-256, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:439-507, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:905-922]
