# Endoplasmic-reticulum-targeted PDT promotes ER stress and immunogenic cell death

[[Photodynamic therapy|Photodynamic therapy]] (PDT) kills cancer cells by generating reactive oxygen species (ROS) after light activation of a photosensitizer. Because ROS have a very short lifetime and limited diffusion range, their effects are strongest near the site where they are produced. In this context, directing photosensitizers to the [[endoplasmic reticulum]] is presented as a way to localize oxidative damage to an organelle that is central to protein synthesis, folding, secretion, and cellular homeostasis. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:16-24, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:123-142, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1459-1464]

The review describes ER-targeted PDT as a strategy in which ROS generated within the ER disrupt that organelle’s protein-folding capacity and trigger [[ER stress]]. This localized stress response is highlighted as a key mechanism by which ER-directed photodamage leads to tumor-cell death and strengthens PDT efficacy relative to non-targeted approaches. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:285-287, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:395-400, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1461-1464]

A central consequence of ER-targeted PDT in the review is induction of [[immunogenic cell death|immunogenic cell death (ICD)]]. The article states that ER stress caused by ER-localized ROS promotes surface exposure of calreticulin (`CRT`), described as an "eat me" signal and a biomarker of ICD. This change enhances dendritic-cell antigen-presenting activity and is linked to stronger antitumor immune responses. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:285-288, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:343-355, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1472-1479]

The review’s summary table characterizes the biological effects of ER-targeted PDT as ROS generation and ER-stress induction, followed by CRT upregulation, enhanced dendritic-cell activity, and ICD. It identifies the relevant signaling framework as an ER-stress-mediated ICD pathway and presents the main advantage of this approach as boosting antitumor efficacy through immune activation in addition to direct tumor-cell killing. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:343-355]

## Mechanistic basis

The ER is described as a multifunctional organelle that maintains homeostasis and supports protein synthesis, folding, and secretion. Because ROS produced by PDT can impair these ER functions, the review argues that the ER is a critical subcellular target when the therapeutic goal is to provoke stress signaling that extends beyond immediate oxidative injury. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:176-183, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1459-1464]

More broadly, the review states that both ER stress and ROS production are important for activating intracellular signaling pathways that regulate ICD. On that basis, ER-targeting specificity is presented as a means to intensify the immune-relevant consequences of PDT, not only its direct cytotoxic effects. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:395-400]

## Representative ER-targeting strategies

One example uses [[pardaxin]] as an ER-targeting peptide. In the reviewed study, pardaxin and indocyanine green (`ICG`) were incorporated into multifunctional nanoparticles together with oxygen-carrying hemoglobin liposomes to address hypoxia. In hypoxic tumors, this system showed ER affinity, generated singlet oxygen under irradiation, triggered strong ER stress, and induced CRT exposure, which then promoted dendritic-cell maturation, CD8-positive T-cell proliferation, and cytotoxic cytokine secretion, yielding enhanced antitumor effects. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1466-1480]

The same research group later reported a `Pardaxin-ICG-Liposome` made by microfluidic methods. Under near-infrared irradiation, this ER-targeted platform was likewise reported to induce ICD and activate the immune system through ER-targeted PDT. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1481-1485]

A second strategy uses [[p-toluenesulfonamide]], which the review says targets the ER by binding the sulfonamide receptor. In one example, reduction-responsive `Ds-sP` nanoparticles carrying the ER-targeting photosensitizer `TCPP-TER` accumulated in the ER and generated ROS under 670 nm irradiation, triggering ER stress and amplifying ICD. The resulting immune effects included dendritic-cell activation, increased CD8-positive T-cell infiltration, and enhanced cytokine secretion, with better antitumor activity than a non-ER-targeted comparator. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1486-1497]

The review also notes a p-toluenesulfonamide-modified hemicyanine system, `ER-ZS`, described as a type I photosensitizer that generates superoxide and hydroxyl radicals in the ER. In that example, the ROS produced in the ER damaged the organelle and activated a [[pyroptosis]] cascade, which was then associated with DNA breakage and tumor-growth inhibition. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1498-1504]

Additional examples extend the same principle. A rhodamine-decorated iridium(III) complex, `Ir-Rho-G2`, showed predominant ER localization, improved singlet-oxygen generation, and greater photocytotoxicity than a related control, which the review attributes to disruption of protein folding in the ER and activation of ER-stress-induced apoptosis. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1505-1526]

Two phosphindole oxide-derived type I photosensitizers, `alpha-TPA-PIO` and `beta-TPA-PIO`, also selectively accumulated in the ER. The review reports that `beta-TPA-PIO` had higher tumor-inhibition efficiency because of stronger ROS generation, and that ER stress from local ROS activated apoptosis and autophagy pathways that contributed to PDT efficacy. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1535-1545]

Another ER-localizing example is `TCy5-Ph-3F`, a fluorinated thiopentamethine dye. The review states that it showed the highest singlet-oxygen generation and cell-killing activity among related compounds, localized spontaneously to the ER, induced enhanced ER stress, and triggered the ICD cascade in cancer cells. In vivo, its inhibition of primary and distant tumors exceeded that of a comparator group, which the review attributes to an ER-targeting-activated immune response. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1546-1560]

## Therapeutic significance

Within the review’s broader framework of organelle-specific PDT, ER-targeted PDT is presented as valuable because it couples localized oxidative injury to immune activation. Rather than relying only on direct tumor-cell destruction, it can convert ER stress into ICD-associated signals such as CRT exposure, dendritic-cell maturation, T-cell activation, and cytokine production, thereby improving overall antitumor efficacy. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:21-33, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:285-288, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:395-400, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:1475-1480]

The review therefore treats ER-targeting as one of several organelle-specific design strategies for overcoming major PDT limitations and for engaging tumor immunity more effectively. In its summary table, the distinctive advantage assigned to ER-targeted systems is that they boost antitumor efficacy through activation of ICD. ^[targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:131-142, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:343-355, targeted-photodynamic-therapy-enhancing-efficacy-through-specific-organelle-engagement.md:418-425]
