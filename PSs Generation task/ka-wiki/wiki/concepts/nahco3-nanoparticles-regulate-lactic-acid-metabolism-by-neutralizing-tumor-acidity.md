# NaHCO3 nanoparticles regulate lactic acid metabolism by neutralizing tumor acidity

`NaHCO3` nanoparticles (`NaHCO3 NPs`) are described as a drug-free inorganic nanomaterial that regulates [[lactic acid metabolism]] in tumors through acid-base neutralization. In the source, this function is presented as a way to reverse the mildly acidic, immunosuppressive [[tumor microenvironment|tumor microenvironment (TME)]], while also supporting broader antitumor immunity when combined with nanoparticle-induced [[pyroptosis]] and [[immunogenic cell death]]. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:53-72, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:119-147]

## Why tumor acidity matters

The source frames tumor acidity as a consequence of the [[Warburg effect]], in which tumor cells convert large amounts of glucose to lactic acid through glycolysis even when oxygen is sufficient. It states that lactic acid accumulation in the TME promotes tumor progression, angiogenesis, invasion, and metastasis, and also suppresses antitumor immunity by promoting M2-type macrophage polarization, inhibiting dendritic-cell maturation and antigen presentation, restraining cytotoxic T-cell lactic acid export and cytotoxicity, and supplying regulatory T cells with lactic acid. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:80-103]

Because lactic acid helps maintain an immunosuppressive TME, the authors propose that targeting lactic acid metabolism is a potentially effective cancer-therapy strategy. They position `NaHCO3 NPs` specifically as an alkalizing material that can neutralize tumor-associated acidity rather than as a conventional drug payload. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:100-103, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:119-127]

## Nanoparticle properties relevant to neutralization

After `DSPE-PEG` modification, the aqueous `NaHCO3 NPs` suspension was weakly alkaline, with pH increasing from `7.83` to `8.76` across `12.5-400 ug/mL`, similar to free sodium bicarbonate. The authors state that this weak alkalinity is conducive to acid-base neutralization with lactic acid accumulated in the TME. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:172-181]

The study therefore treats the nanoparticles as local neutralizing agents within the tumor milieu. In the paper’s mechanistic summary and schematic description, `NaHCO3 NPs` regulate lactic acid metabolism through acid-base neutralization and thereby reverse the mildly acidic tumor environment associated with immunosuppression. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:148-151, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:621-625]

## In vitro evidence for pH normalization and reduced lactate burden

In 4T1 breast-cancer cells, intracellular pH measurements with `pHrodo Green AM` showed attenuated fluorescence after `NaHCO3 NP` treatment relative to control and free sodium bicarbonate, which the authors interpret as an increase in intracellular pH. They note that free sodium bicarbonate did not produce an obvious intracellular pH change, which they attribute to low endocytic efficiency. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:333-343]

Extracellular measurements also supported neutralization of tumor-associated acidification. After `4 h`, the control 4T1 culture medium reached `pH 7.29`, whereas free sodium bicarbonate and `NaHCO3 NPs` maintained higher values of `pH 7.43` and `pH 7.69`, respectively. After `24 h`, free sodium bicarbonate fell to `pH 6.77`, close to the control (`pH 6.74`), while the `NaHCO3 NP` group remained at `pH 7.00`, near the blank-medium value (`pH 7.02`). The authors attribute this sustained effect to the nanoparticles’ combined tumor-growth inhibition and acid-base neutralization capacity. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:343-364]

The paper further reports that lactic acid in the culture medium was dramatically reduced in the `NaHCO3 NP` group, which the authors cite as evidence of strong potential to regulate lactic acid metabolism. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:364-368]

## Immunologic consequences in vivo

The authors link lactic acid consumption and pyroptosis/[[immunogenic cell death|ICD]] induction to improved immune performance in 4T1 tumor-bearing BALB/c mice. In tumor-draining lymph nodes, `NaHCO3 NPs` produced the highest dendritic-cell maturation, while in tumors they increased infiltrating `CD4+` and `CD8+` T cells relative to controls. The source attributes this to the combined effects of pyroptosis/ICD and lactic acid metabolism regulation. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:382-415]

The treatment also shifted the immunosuppressive cell landscape in tumors. The study reports polarization from M2 to M1 tumor-associated macrophages together with reduced frequencies of [[myeloid-derived suppressor cell|MDSCs]] and [[regulatory T cell|Treg]] cells after `NaHCO3 NP` administration, which the authors interpret as remission of the immunosuppressive TME. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:416-433]

## Therapeutic significance in the study

In vivo, `NaHCO3 NPs` significantly inhibited primary 4T1 tumor growth without obvious body-weight loss, major-organ damage, or hemolytic reaction. Tumor sections also showed the highest `CD8+` T-cell infiltration in the nanoparticle group. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:447-464]

The source further reports inhibition of untreated distal tumors and reduced pulmonary metastasis in mouse models, and concludes that `NaHCO3 NPs` suppress primary and distal tumor growth as well as metastasis through the combined actions of pyroptosis induction and lactic acid consumption. In the conclusion, the authors describe the nanoparticles as pyroptosis and metabolism immunoadjuvants that amplify cancer immunotherapy through acid-neutralization-mediated relief of immunosuppression and pyroptosis/ICD-driven immune activation. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:465-506]
