# ROS generation measured by ABDA photobleaching assay

The ABDA photobleaching assay was used here as a solution-phase method to detect reactive oxygen species (ROS) generation by the photosensitizers TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C. In this assay, ABDA served as the ROS indicator, and its loss under irradiation was tracked spectroscopically. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:182-185]

## Principle

ABDA (9,10-anthracenediyl-bis(methylene) dimalonic acid) was used as the indicator molecule for ROS detection. ROS generation was inferred from the decrease in ABDA absorbance at 378 nm during light exposure. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:24-26, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:182-185]

## Assay conditions

The assay was performed in DMSO/PBS mixtures with a volume ratio of 1:99. ABDA was used at 50 μM, while TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C were each tested at 5 μM. Samples were irradiated with white light over 400-700 nm at a power density of 30 mW/cm2. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:182-185]

## Readout

The experimental readout was the decrease in ABDA absorbance at 378 nm upon irradiation. The supporting information also includes absorption-spectrum changes for ABDA mixed with each of the three photosensitizers under white-light irradiation at 30 mW cm-2, corresponding to this photobleaching measurement. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:184-185, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:340-343]

## Comparator

[[Rose Bengal]] was used as the benchmark in the ROS assay, at the same concentration of 5 μM. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:185-185]
