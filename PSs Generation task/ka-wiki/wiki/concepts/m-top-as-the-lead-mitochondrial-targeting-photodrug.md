# M-TOP as the lead mitochondrial-targeting photodrug

M-TOP is the lead compound in a series of mitochondria-targeting type-I photodrugs described in the supporting information for *Mitochondria-Targeting Type-I Photodrug: Harnessing Caspase-3 Activity for Pyroptotic Oncotherapy*. The source identifies M-TOP as one of the synthesized photodrugs in the series and reports that it was used as the representative lead for cellular uptake, intracellular localization, photodynamic activity, and in vivo antitumor studies. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:105-105, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:161-168, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:250-257, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:317-330]

## Compound identity

M-TOP was synthesized by coupling N-TOP with TPP-COOH using EDCI, HOBt, and DMAP in dry DMF, followed by silica gel purification. The supporting information reports a dark-blue solid obtained in 35.7% yield and provides NMR and mass spectrometry characterization data for the product. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:161-178]

## Role in the study

The paper positions M-TOP within a broader comparison of photodrug designs and lists the mitochondrial-targeting type-I photodrug as the study’s controllable immunotherapy platform. In the supporting information table, this strategy is contrasted with conventional type-I, type-II, and nanoparticle-based photodrugs, with the mitochondrial-targeting type-I design described as non-specific in nature but enabled by mitochondria targeting, low oxygen dependency, and signaling-pathway switching via caspase-3. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:523-595]

## Cellular behavior

For uptake studies, U251-MG cells were incubated with photodrugs at 0.5 μM and analyzed by flow cytometry over 0 to 60 minutes. Intracellular distribution was assessed by confocal microscopy after 30 minutes of incubation, using LysoTracker or MitoTracker staining to evaluate endo/lysosomal and mitochondrial colocalization. These methods were used to compare M-TOP with the other photodrugs in the series. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:210-232]

## Photodynamic and immune effects

The supporting information reports that M-TOP was used in flow-cytometry assays for apoptosis and pyroptosis, with cells treated at 0 to 1 μM under 640 nm light, and that its effects were also evaluated in MTT viability assays. Supernatants from photodrug-treated tumor cells were co-cultured with DC2.4 dendritic cells to assess maturation markers CD40 and CD86, as well as TNF-α and IL-1β release. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:248-311]

## In vivo use

M-TOP was tested in female C57BL/6 mice bearing subcutaneous Hepa1-6 tumors. In the primary tumor model, M-TOP was injected intratumorally at 1 μM and irradiated with a 640 nm laser at 150 mW/cm2 for 10 minutes, repeated three times every two days. The study also used a distal tumor model in which only one flank tumor received M-TOP and light, allowing the untreated contralateral tumor to be evaluated for systemic antitumor effects. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:317-358]
