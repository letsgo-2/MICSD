# GSDMD-N-terminal fragment promotes calcium- and caspase-1-dependent osmotic swelling and blebbing

[[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] N-terminal fragment (GSDMD-NT) is described in this study as a downstream effector of [[caspase-1]] cleavage that not only contributes to membrane pore formation and permeabilization, but also promotes osmotic changes associated with pyroptotic cell deformation. In the authors' astrocyte pyroptosis model, GSDMD-NT was linked to increased cytoplasmic osmotic pressure (OP), greater numbers of intracellular protein nanoparticles (PNs), and the characteristic morphological changes of cell swelling and membrane blebbing. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:100-106, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:633-667]

## Overview

The source states that active caspase-1 cleaves GSDMD into C-terminal and N-terminal fragments, and that GSDMD-NT localizes to the cell membrane to facilitate pore formation. The study extends this by examining whether GSDMD-NT also regulates [[protein nanoparticle-induced osmotic pressure]] during pyroptosis. Overexpression experiments showed that GSDMD-NT, but not GSDMD-CT or vector control, increased cytoplasmic OP and PN abundance, indicating that the N-terminal fragment specifically promotes osmotic remodeling linked to pyroptotic morphology. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:100-106, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:636-647]

## Experimental evidence

To test fragment-specific effects, the authors individually overexpressed GSDMD-NT and GSDMD-CT in 293T cells. Neither vector control nor GSDMD-CT changed cytoplasmic OP or PN number, whereas GSDMD-NT caused a significant increase in both measures. The GSDMD-NT condition was also associated with PNs distributed mainly in the `50-100 nm` and `100-1000 nm` ranges, which the authors suggest may reflect [[NLRP3 inflammasome]]-related particles. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:636-647]

Consistent with those osmotic changes, GSDMD-NT rather than GSDMD-CT induced cell swelling, membrane blebbing, membrane permeabilization, and increased caspase-1 activity. Based on these results, the authors suggest that GSDMD-NT can facilitate further NLRP3 production, linking pore-forming activity to amplification of inflammasome-associated osmotic stress and pyroptotic deformation. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:663-667]

## Calcium dependence

The study found that reducing intracellular calcium with `2-APB` or `dantrolene` completely abolished the GSDMD-NT-induced increases in cytoplasmic OP and PN number, with a particularly strong effect on the smaller `50-100 nm` protein granules. These calcium-lowering treatments also significantly suppressed GSDMD-NT-induced blebbing and permeabilization. The authors therefore conclude that free intracellular Ca2+ is a key regulator of PN-OP during pyroptosis. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:668-676]

More broadly, the paper reports that calcium released from the endoplasmic reticulum regulates inflammasome production, PN-OP, astrocyte blebbing, and permeabilization during pyroptosis. Increasing Ca2+ with caffeine raised OP and PN counts, while lowering Ca2+ with `2-APB` or `dantrolene` reduced osmolarity, intermediate filament tension, swelling, and caspase-1 activity. This broader context supports the conclusion that GSDMD-NT-driven swelling and blebbing depend on calcium signaling. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:560-632]

## Caspase-1 dependence

The source also identifies [[caspase-1]] as necessary for the full GSDMD-NT phenotype. Inhibition of caspase-1 with `Z-VAD-FMK` reduced the GSDMD-NT-induced increases in cytoplasmic OP, PN abundance, and pyroptosis severity, including swelling and blebbing. The authors interpret this to mean that caspase-1, together with calcium signaling, is a pivotal regulator of pyroptotic cell swelling. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:677-683]

Outside the GSDMD-NT overexpression experiments, caspase-1 inhibition in pyroptotic astrocytes reduced OP, PN number, intracellular Ca2+ and Cl-, intermediate filament tension, and permeabilization to near-control levels, while also reducing NLRP3 inflammasome signals. The paper describes this as feedback regulation in which caspase-1 promotes NLRP3 inflammasome generation and helps control intracellular calcium levels. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:432-466, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:777-787]

## Mechanistic interpretation

In the study's model, GSDMD-NT does more than open nonselective membrane pores. The authors propose that GSDMD-NT promotes pyroptosis together with further generation of NLRP3 inflammasomes and the resulting increase in PN-OP, so that intracellular protein nanoparticles, rather than ion influx alone, are major contributors to osmotic pressure elevation. They further suggest that when calcium or caspase-1 is inhibited, GSDMD-NT-dependent pyroptosis is blocked, implying that GSDMD-NT membrane localization or function depends on intracellular calcium signaling and caspase-1 activation. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:761-769, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:792-800]

## Relationship to pyroptotic morphology

Within this framework, osmotic pressure elevation is presented as necessary for astrocyte swelling and blebbing but not absolutely required for membrane permeabilization. The study argues that inflammasome-generated PNs and associated osmotic forces drive early cell deformation, while GSDMD pores ultimately permit massive ion and water influx leading to high permeabilization and cell disintegration. GSDMD-NT therefore sits at an important junction between pore formation, osmotic remodeling, and the visible morphology of [[pyroptosis]]. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:736-760, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:801-811]
