# Cu-TBB copper-bacteriochlorin nanosheet

[[Cu-TBB copper-bacteriochlorin nanosheet|Cu-TBB]] is a [[glutathione]]-activatable nanosheet developed as a tumor-specific [[pyroptosis]] inducer for cancer immunotherapy. It is composed of `Cu2+` and 5,10,15,20-tetra(p-benzoato) bacteriochlorin ([[TBB]]), a bacteriochlorin photosensitizer derivative. The study describes Cu-TBB as being in an inactive "off" state under normal conditions and switching to an active "on" state in the [[tumor microenvironment]] when exposed to elevated GSH, releasing `Cu+` and TBB. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:17-29, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:140-147]

## Design and composition

Cu-TBB was synthesized from TBB by solvothermal reaction with copper under nitrogen. The resulting material showed a 2D nanosheet morphology with an approximate thickness of `1.0 nm`, homogeneous distribution of Cu, N, and C, sharp XRD peaks consistent with high crystallinity, and spectroscopic evidence that `Cu2+` coordinates both the carboxyl groups and the center of TBB. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:186-216]

Compared with free TBB, Cu-TBB showed slightly red-shifted UV-vis absorption and fully quenched fluorescence, which the authors attribute to Forster resonance energy transfer between `Cu2+` and TBB. Dynamic light scattering indicated an average size of `193 nm` and a zeta potential of `-24.7 mV`, and the reported size remained stable for one month. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:216-227]

## Activation mechanism

The source proposes that Cu-TBB is selectively activated in tumors because high GSH levels reduce `Cu2+` to `Cu+` and disassemble the nanosheet, producing TBB, `Cu+`, and oxidized glutathione. In solution, GSH treatment caused a red shift in absorption, produced a glutathione disulfide signal, restored fluorescence, and progressively disassembled the nanosheet. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:228-245, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:252-282]

After activation, released `Cu+` drove redox cascade reactions that generated `O2−•`, and the study further links these reactions to formation of `·OH` and recycled `O2` through endogenous [[superoxide dismutase|SOD]] disproportionation, Haber-Weiss chemistry, and a Fenton-like reaction. Upon `750 nm` laser irradiation, released TBB additionally generated `O2−•` and `1O2`, so Cu-TBB combined copper-driven chemistry with [[photodynamic therapy]]. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:147-155, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:320-387]

## In vitro effects

In HeLa, HepG2, and 4T1 cells, Cu-TBB alone showed little apparent cytotoxicity, while Cu-TBB plus GSH reduced viability, and the combination with `750 nm` laser further increased killing to about `90%` mortality in the in vitro assays. Cell imaging also showed membrane bubbling in treated groups, which the authors interpret as a typical morphological feature of pyroptosis. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:388-425]

Mechanistically, the reported pyroptotic pathway was [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]]-mediated rather than [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]]-mediated. Western blotting detected `N-GSDMD` and cleaved [[caspase-1]] in the `Cu-TBB + GSH` and `Cu-TBB + GSH + Laser` groups, while caspase-4 was not detected; these groups also showed increased `IL-1beta` and `IL-18`. Inhibition with the caspase-1 inhibitor `VX-765` reduced pyroptosis-related morphology, cleaved caspase-1, `N-GSDMD`, and cytokine release. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:439-462]

The study also reports features associated with [[immunogenic cell death]]. Relative to controls, treated cells released more [[HMGB1]] and [[lactate dehydrogenase|LDH]] and showed greater surface exposure of [[calreticulin|CRT]]. In a transwell coculture system, 4T1 cells undergoing Cu-TBB-induced pyroptosis promoted [[dendritic cell]] maturation, and this effect was reduced when pyroptosis was blocked with `VX-765`. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:463-488]

## In vivo tumor targeting and antitumor activity

In 4T1 tumor-bearing BALB/c nude mice, intravenously injected Cu-TBB produced detectable tumor fluorescence by `3 h` and reached peak intensity at `48 h`. The source attributes low fluorescence in blood and several normal organs to low blood GSH and restricted leakage into normal tissues, while tumor fluorescence increased because of elevated GSH and the enhanced permeability and retention effect. The authors report minimal observed hepatotoxicity and low kidney toxicity in these measurements. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:489-525]

In bilateral [[4T1]] tumor-bearing BALB/c mice, mice received intravenous Cu-TBB at `20 mg kg−1`, followed by `750 nm` laser irradiation for `10 min` one day later. Cu-TBB significantly inhibited primary tumor growth, and the `Cu-TBB + Laser` group nearly eradicated primary tumors. The treated groups also had lower tumor GSH levels, no significant worsening of tumor hypoxia, reduced distant tumor growth, and fewer metastatic lung nodules, with the strongest effects in the laser-treated group. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:526-603]

## Immune effects in vivo

In tumors, Cu-TBB treatment increased `N-GSDMD`, cleaved caspase-1, `IL-1beta`, and `IL-18`, supporting in vivo induction of GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis. It also increased tumor [[calreticulin|CRT]] expression and serum [[HMGB1]], which the authors use as indicators of immunogenic cell death linked to pyroptosis. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:604-627]

The immune analyses further showed enhanced maturation of splenic dendritic cells and higher proportions of splenic `CD8+` and `CD4+` T cells after Cu-TBB treatment, with larger effects in the `Cu-TBB + Laser` group. Immunohistochemistry also showed greater infiltration of `CD8+` and `CD4+` T cells in tumors. The authors conclude that Cu-TBB-induced pyroptosis promoted proinflammatory signaling, dendritic-cell maturation, T-cell priming, and adaptive antitumor immunity. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:628-665]

## Safety and significance

The paper reports no significant body-weight changes and no evident histologic damage in major organs, supporting good in vivo biocompatibility in the reported experiments. Overall, the study presents Cu-TBB as a tumor-microenvironment-activated nanosheet that combines copper-driven ROS chemistry and light-triggered photodynamic activity to induce tumor-selective pyroptosis and strengthen antitumor immune responses while minimizing systemic side effects. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:666-706]
