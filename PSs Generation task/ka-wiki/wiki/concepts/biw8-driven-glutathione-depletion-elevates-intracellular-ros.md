I’m extracting only the BiW8-specific claims from the source and will keep the page centered on the glutathione/ROS mechanism, with related wikilinks and line-level citations.# BiW8-driven glutathione depletion elevates intracellular ROS

{BiW8} was reported to increase intracellular reactive oxygen species (ROS) in MG63 cells while reducing glutathione (GSH) content over time. The source states that ROS levels in the treated cells were twice those of control, and that GSH decreased by 59%, 65%, 75%, 94%, and 97% at 6, 12, 24, 36, and 48 hours, respectively. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:347-354, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:383-390, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:423-429]

## Mechanism

The study links the ROS increase to GSH depletion. It reports that {BiW8} reacted with GSH, inhibited the OPA-based fluorescence signal, did not react with GSSG under the same assay conditions, and lowered intracellular GSH, T-GSH, and GSSG levels in MG63 cells. The authors describe this as a combined effect of transcriptional repression and chemical reaction that decreased GSH and consequently increased ROS. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:410-429, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:517-527]

## Supporting evidence

Transcriptome analysis found that {BiW8} downregulated genes involved in glutathione metabolism, including `GPX1`, `GPX8`, `GSTM1`, and `GSTT2B`. The paper also notes that KEGG pathway analysis implicated glutathione metabolism among the affected pathways, supporting suppression of the cell’s antioxidant machinery. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:439-452, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:458-464, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:476-482]

The source further states that {BiW8} did not show GPX or GR enzyme activity in the absence of system components, but did reduce NADPH absorbance when the full GSH/GR/GPX system was present. This was interpreted as evidence that {BiW8} can alter the GSH/GSSG redox system and impair glutathione-dependent ROS clearance. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:355-374]

## Biological context

The paper frames this ROS elevation as part of a broader antitumor effect in MG63 osteosarcoma cells. Alongside reduced GSH and increased ROS, {BiW8} also inhibited proliferation, migration, and invasion, and the authors propose that excessive ROS may contribute to cell death and DNA damage after GSH depletion. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:260-288, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:528-540, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:554-564]
