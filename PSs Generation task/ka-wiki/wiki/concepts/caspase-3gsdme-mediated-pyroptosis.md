# Caspase-3/GSDME-mediated pyroptosis

**Caspase-3/GSDME-mediated pyroptosis** is a pyroptotic cell-death route highlighted in this study’s mitochondria-targeting type-I photodrug strategy. The supporting information describes the work as “harnessing Caspase-3 activity for pyroptotic oncotherapy,” and a comparison table states that the signaling pathway switch in this approach occurs “via caspase-3.” ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:9-13, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:523-551]

In the cellular experiments, the authors evaluated expression changes in [[caspase-3]], cleaved PARP, and [[GSDME]] in U251-MG cells after photodrug treatment, alongside release of [[IL-1beta]] and LDH into culture supernatants. These measurements were used in the context of apoptosis/pyroptosis analysis after irradiation, indicating that caspase-3 and GSDME were central molecular readouts for the pyroptotic response studied here. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:248-285]

The study’s apoptosis-and-pyroptosis workflow combined Annexin V-FITC/PI staining, microscopy of morphological changes, and flow cytometry to determine apoptotic and pyroptotic indices after M-TOP phototreatment. Cells were exposed either to varying M-TOP doses at fixed irradiation or to varying irradiation times at fixed M-TOP dose, followed by post-treatment incubation before flow-cytometric analysis. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:248-262]

The source further links this caspase-3-based pathway to a broader therapeutic rationale. In Table S2, the mitochondrial-targeting type-I photodrug used in this work is contrasted with other photodynamic strategies as having a signaling-pathway switch “via caspase-3” and a controllable inflammatory response, while also being described as enabling controllable immunotherapy against primary, distant, and recurrent tumors in vivo. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:523-599]

Within this source set, [[GSDME]] is identified as a measured protein in the pathway, but the supporting information does not spell out a narrative mechanism for how caspase-3 acts on GSDME step by step. What it does establish is that caspase-3 and GSDME were explicit mechanistic markers chosen for western blot analysis in the photodrug-induced pyroptosis model. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:263-285]

## Experimental context

The relevant cell-based assays were performed primarily in U251-MG human glioblastoma cells. For mechanistic analysis, cells treated with different photodrugs, including M-TOP, were irradiated at `15 mW/cm^2` for `5 min`, then cultured for another `3 h` before supernatants were collected for inflammatory-factor assays and cell lysates were prepared for western blotting. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:198-209, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:263-285]

The same supporting information also documents that M-TOP showed mitochondrial colocalization in cells and frames the overall platform as a “mitochondrial-targeting type-I photodrug.” This places the caspase-3/GSDME-associated pyroptotic response within a mitochondria-targeted photodynamic therapy design rather than a generic pyroptosis model. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:444-445, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:523-551]
