# NLRP3 inflammasome as a central research hotspot in pyroptosis

The [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] is identified as a central research hotspot in the pyroptosis field. In a bibliometric analysis covering 2,845 pyroptosis-related articles and reviews from 2001 to 2021, “NLRP3 inflammasome” was the most frequent keyword after “pyroptosis,” appearing 764 times, alongside other high-frequency terms such as [[apoptosis]], cell death, inflammasome, [[GSDMD]], and [[caspase-1]]. The study concluded that pathway mechanisms, relationships with other forms of programmed cell death, and disease roles are major and continuing focal points in pyroptosis research.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:27-40, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:33-35, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:377-383, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:456-467, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:905-922]

## Why NLRP3 stands out

Keyword and clustering analyses place NLRP3 at the center of the field’s main themes. High-frequency keyword analysis showed NLRP3 inflammasome as one of the most prominent terms in the literature, and timeline analysis found that from 2005 to 2011 pyroptosis research focused mainly on inflammasome function and related mechanisms, with NLRP3 inflammasome among the main keywords. From 2011 to 2021, attention shifted more strongly toward downstream pyroptosis pathways, but NLRP3 remained embedded in the core mechanistic landscape.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:377-383, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:398-412, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:431-436, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:571-583]

The article further states that among pyroptosis-associated inflammasomes studied extensively, [[NLRP1 inflammasome|NLRP1]], NLRP3, [[NLRC4 inflammasome|NLRC4]], and [[AIM2 inflammasome|AIM2]] are the main subtypes, and that NLRP3 is the inflammasome studied most concretely, with a relatively well-understood mechanism. This combination of high bibliometric prominence and comparatively mature mechanistic understanding helps explain why NLRP3 functions as a central anchor for pyroptosis research.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:592-608]

## Role of NLRP3 in pyroptosis mechanisms

Pyroptosis is described as a pro-inflammatory programmed cell death process in which inflammasome assembly and activation play a critical role. Canonical inflammasome activation through [[caspase-1]] and noncanonical activation through caspase-4, caspase-5, and caspase-11 can both lead to pyroptosis, ultimately linking inflammasome signaling to membrane pore formation, inflammatory mediator release, and cell lysis.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:88-104, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:585-591, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:646-672]

Within this framework, NLRP3 is presented as a core inflammasome platform. Inflammasomes are polyprotein complexes composed of pattern recognition receptors, the adaptor [[ASC]], and pro-caspase effectors. The NLRP3 protein contains a leucine-rich repeat domain involved in ligand recognition, a central NACHT domain important for oligomerization and activation, and an N-terminal pyrin domain that binds ASC. After stimuli are recognized, NLRP3 undergoes structural change, oligomerizes, recruits ASC through PYD-PYD interaction, and then enables ASC to recruit pro-caspase-1 through CARD-CARD interaction, completing inflammasome assembly.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:592-628]

The assembled NLRP3 inflammasome can self-activate and promote hydrolysis of pro-caspase-1 into active caspase-1, which in turn promotes maturation of [[IL-1β]] and [[IL-18]]. Caspase-1 also cleaves GSDMD, whose N-terminal fragment forms pores in the plasma membrane, leading to secretion of inflammatory mediators, cell swelling, membrane rupture, and pyroptotic cell death.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:626-628, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:646-659, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:674-692]

## Relationship to broader pyroptosis trends

The bibliometric study frames pyroptosis research as rapidly expanding, especially after 2017, and identifies mechanistic molecules as one of the principal concentrations of the field. NLRP3 sits within that concentration because it connects upstream danger sensing to downstream caspase activation, cytokine maturation, and gasdermin-mediated membrane pore formation. The prominence of NLRP3 in the keyword data reflects this central mechanistic position in the literature.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:208-215, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:377-397, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:477-481, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:919-922]

The study also highlights that pyroptosis research increasingly examines crosstalk with other forms of programmed cell death, especially apoptosis, autophagy, necroptosis, and ferroptosis. Because NLRP3 is one of the most visible keywords in the field’s hotspot analysis, it is part of the broader effort to map how inflammasome-mediated cell death interfaces with other regulated cell death pathways.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:377-397, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:571-583, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:760-829]

## Disease relevance

Pyroptosis is reported to be involved in infectious diseases, nervous system diseases, cardiovascular diseases, metabolic diseases, and tumors, and excessive or inappropriate activation can amplify local and systemic inflammation. Within this disease-oriented research, NLRP3 appears repeatedly as a mechanistic target or mediator, including examples in epilepsy, atherosclerosis, alcoholic liver disease, ischemic heart disease, food-borne gastroenteritis, colon cancer, and diabetic nephropathy listed in the study’s disease table.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:105-133, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:431-436, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:830-846, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:927-993]

The paper specifically notes that targeted therapy strategies have begun to focus on molecules across the pyroptosis pathway, including NLRP3 inflammasome, caspase-1, and GSDMD. It names several NLRP3 inflammasome inhibitors, including colchicine, OLT1177, and MCC950, which are described as inhibiting ATPase activity and preventing NLRP3 inflammasome assembly and activation. This therapeutic attention reinforces NLRP3’s position as a major research hotspot rather than only a descriptive keyword.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:847-888]

## Research significance

In this source, NLRP3 emerges as a central pyroptosis hotspot for two linked reasons: it is one of the most dominant themes in the field’s publication landscape, and it occupies a well-developed mechanistic position within pyroptotic signaling. The combination of bibliometric prominence, pathway relevance, disease association, and therapeutic interest makes NLRP3 a recurring focal point in both basic and translational pyroptosis research.^[a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:33-40, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:377-383, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:592-608, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:847-888, a-bibliometric-analysis-of-pyroptosis-from-2001-to-2021.md:905-922]
