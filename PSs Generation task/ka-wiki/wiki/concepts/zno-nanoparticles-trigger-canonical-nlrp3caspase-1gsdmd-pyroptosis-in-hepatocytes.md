# ZnO nanoparticles trigger canonical NLRP3/caspase-1/GSDMD pyroptosis in hepatocytes

[[Zinc oxide nanoparticles|ZnO nanoparticles]] were reported to induce hepatocyte pyroptosis in both rat liver and HepG2 cells, linking [[liver toxicity]] from nanoparticle exposure to activation of the canonical [[NLRP3 inflammasome]]/[[caspase-1]]/[[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] pathway. The study frames this as a previously unresolved question in ZnO nanoparticle hepatotoxicity and presents it as the first verification of hepatocyte pyroptosis caused by ZnO nanoparticles in vivo and in vitro. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:23-32, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:39-43, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:65-72]

## Experimental context

The study used Sprague-Dawley rats given intraperitoneal ZnO nanoparticle injections at 12.5, 25, or 50 mg/kg body weight for 7 consecutive days, and used HepG2 cells exposed to 0-25 ug/mL ZnO nanoparticles in culture. The authors assessed liver injury, oxidative stress, inflammasome activation, pyroptosis-related proteins, membrane damage, and DNA fragmentation, and also used the caspase-1 inhibitor VX-765 and the antioxidant N-acetyl-L-cysteine (NAC) to test pathway dependence and oxidative-stress involvement. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:189-208, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:251-274, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:339-367]

## Liver targeting and injury

ZnO nanoparticles disrupted zinc homeostasis in rats and accumulated most strongly in the liver compared with heart, spleen, kidney, lung, and brain, identifying the liver as the primary target organ in this model. In exposed rats, this accumulation was accompanied by inflammatory and structural liver injury, including disordered hepatocyte arrangement, vacuolar degeneration, portal-vein congestion, inflammatory-cell infiltration, nuclear pyknosis, mitochondrial swelling, and elevated serum ALT, AST, and gamma-GT, together with increased MDA and reduced SOD, GSH, and CAT. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:495-509, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:510-538]

## Evidence for canonical pyroptosis in rat liver

In rat liver, western blotting showed increased NLRP3, ASC, cleaved caspase-1, GSDMD-NT, IL-1beta, and IL-18 after ZnO nanoparticle exposure. Immunohistochemistry also showed stronger staining for NLRP3, ASC, IL-1beta, and IL-18, while immunofluorescence showed increased IL-1beta-positive cells and increased GSDMD-NT; TUNEL staining was also enhanced. The authors interpret these findings as evidence that a representative pyroptosis pathway contributes to ZnO nanoparticle-induced liver damage. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:539-557]

## Evidence for canonical pyroptosis in HepG2 cells

In HepG2 cells, ZnO nanoparticles caused dose-dependent intracellular zinc accumulation and reduced viability, with a working concentration of 20 ug/mL chosen because it was close to the 50% inhibitory concentration. Exposed cells showed swelling, detachment, reduced density, increased ROS, reduced antioxidant markers SOD and CAT, increased MDA, and increased Nrf2 and HO-1, indicating oxidative-stress injury during cytotoxicity. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:558-580, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:594-604]

The same cell model showed activation of inflammatory signaling and inflammasome assembly. HMGB1 increased at 15 ug/mL, NF-kappaB increased at 20 ug/mL, and western blotting showed dose- and time-dependent upregulation of NLRP3, ASC, and cleaved caspase-1. Immunofluorescence further showed increased colocalization signals for NLRP3/ASC and ASC/caspase-1, supporting assembly of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] in hepatocytes exposed to ZnO nanoparticles. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:605-620]

Downstream pyroptosis markers were also increased in HepG2 cells. GSDMD-NT, IL-1beta, and IL-18 rose in dose- and time-dependent fashion, LDH release increased, and Annexin V/PI double-positive cells increased. Scanning electron microscopy showed swollen cells with punched plasma membranes and homogeneous small holes replacing normal protrusions and external vesicles, while TUNEL staining showed increased DNA-strand breakage. The authors conclude that hepatocyte damage induced by ZnO nanoparticles is associated with initiation of pyroptosis. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:621-644]

## Caspase-1 dependence

Pharmacologic inhibition with VX-765 supported a canonical caspase-1-dependent mechanism. After VX-765 treatment, cleaved caspase-1 and GSDMD-NT were reduced, mature IL-1beta and IL-18 secretion decreased, membrane integrity improved, LDH leakage and Annexin V/PI double positivity fell, TUNEL positivity decreased, cell viability improved, and ASC/caspase-1 colocalization was slightly reduced. Based on these results, the study concludes that ZnO nanoparticle-induced hepatocyte pyroptosis proceeds through caspase-1-dependent canonical pyroptosis. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:645-667]

## Relationship to oxidative stress

The paper links this pyroptotic response to oxidative stress. In HepG2 cells, NAC reduced ROS fluorescence, restored CAT and SOD, normalized MDA, enhanced the Nrf2/HO-1 antioxidant pathway, improved viability, reduced LDH release, downregulated NLRP3, ASC, caspase-1, and GSDMD, and reduced cytoplasmic GSDMD-NT accumulation. The authors interpret these findings as evidence that antioxidation can alleviate ZnO nanoparticle-induced pyroptosis in hepatocytes. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:668-841]

## Interpretation

The study concludes that ZnO nanoparticles induce inflammatory liver injury together with assembly of the inflammasome and activation of the typical caspase-1-dependent pyroptosis pathway in vivo and in vitro. It further states that ZnO nanoparticles generate ROS and weaken antioxidant capacity, and that antioxidant intervention can protect against pyroptosis-related damage, making oxidative stress a proposed upstream driver of the canonical pyroptotic response in hepatocytes. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:1169-1180]
