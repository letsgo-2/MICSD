# [[caspase-3gsdme-mediated-pyroptosis|Caspase-3/GSDME-mediated pyroptosis]] induced by NI-TA

`NI-TA` is described as a photocatalytic [[superoxide radical]] (`O2−•`) generator that induces [[pyroptosis]] in cancer cells under photoexcitation. In this study, the authors identify the resulting cell-death pathway as caspase-3/GSDME-mediated rather than the canonical caspase-1/GSDMD route. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:17-27, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:151-159]

## Overview

The paper presents NI-TA as a light-activated agent designed to generate `O2−•` through a Type-I photochemical process. A central conclusion of the study is that, after photoirradiation, NI-TA triggers pyroptosis through cleavage of [[caspase-3]] and formation of the N-terminal fragment of [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]], distinguishing its mechanism from canonical inflammasome-linked pyroptosis. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:20-25, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:507-516]

## Experimental evidence in T47D cells

Confocal imaging of T47D cells incubated with NI-TA and then photoexcited showed morphological changes consistent with pyroptosis, including bubble-like protrusions described as pyroptotic bodies. At the same time, Hoechst staining indicated that the nucleus remained intact rather than fragmenting, and cleaved PARP expression was negligible, which the authors interpret as evidence that apoptosis was minimally involved. Little morphological change was seen with NI-TA in the dark or with light alone. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:482-506]

The study further reports that pretreatment with vitamin C, used here as a radical scavenger and `O2−•` trap, reduced these pyroptosis-like morphological changes. The authors take this as evidence that pyroptosis induction was triggered by superoxide generated upon photoexcitation of NI-TA. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:500-506]

## Molecular pathway

Western blot analysis in T47D cells showed that photoirradiated NI-TA increased cleaved caspase-3 and the N-terminal fragment of GSDME (`GSDME-N`) in NI-TA-treated groups, whereas the NI-DF-treated and control groups did not show this response under the same conditions. In the absence of photoirradiation, none of the groups showed obvious variation in cleaved caspase-3 or GSDME-N levels. These findings support the conclusion that photogenerated `O2−•` activates pyroptosis through the [[caspase-3]]/[[Gasdermin E|GSDME]] pathway. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:507-516, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:534-535]

The authors also tested whether the canonical caspase-1/[[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] pathway contributed to this response. They report negligible changes in cleaved caspase-1 and the N-terminal fragment of GSDMD across treatment groups with or without light, and therefore conclude that NI-TA plus light does not activate pyroptosis through the caspase-1/GSDMD pathway. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:553-567]

## Downstream pyroptotic effects

Because GSDME-N is expected to form membrane pores, the study measured downstream readouts associated with membrane disruption and inflammatory leakage. `IL-1β` in the supernatant was elevated in NI-TA-treated groups after photoirradiation, and intracellular `LDH` progressively leaked into the extracellular compartment after treatment with `5 μM` NI-TA plus light. The reported LDH release reached about `23%`, `55%`, and `61%` of total cellular LDH at `2`, `4`, and `6 h`, respectively. The authors interpret these findings as support for membrane lysis and caspase-3-dependent pyroptosis. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:536-550]

## Relationship to NI-TA photochemistry

The proposed upstream trigger for this pyroptotic pathway is NI-TA-mediated superoxide generation under light. In solution and in cells, NI-TA produced strong `O2−•` signals after photoirradiation, while experiments using probes for `^1O2` and `OH•` showed little or no evidence for substantial generation of those reactive oxygen species. The paper therefore frames the caspase-3/GSDME pyroptotic response as a consequence of NI-TA acting as a selective Type-I `O2−•` photogenerator. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:269-305, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:334-377]

## Broader study context

The same study states that NI-TA could induce pyroptosis under hypoxic conditions (`2% O2`) and observed similar `O2−•`-associated pyroptosis activation in other breast cancer cell lines, including MDA-MB-231 and BT474. In the paper’s conclusion, the authors describe this work as the first example of light-controlled pyroptosis proceeding through the caspase-3/GSDME pathway and position it as a basis for further development of photoinduced pyroptosis strategies for solid tumors. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:151-159, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:550-552, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:639-656, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:708-750]
