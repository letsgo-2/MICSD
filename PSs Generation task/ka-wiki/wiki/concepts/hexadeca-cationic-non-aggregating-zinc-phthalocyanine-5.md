# Hexadeca-cationic non-aggregating zinc phthalocyanine 5

`Hexadeca-cationic non-aggregating zinc phthalocyanine 5` is a newly synthesized [[zinc phthalocyanine]] photosensitizer developed for [[photodynamic therapy]]. The source describes it as a zinc phthalocyanine bearing sixteen quaternized imidazolyl moieties on the periphery, with high water solubility, lack of aggregation in water, and high singlet-oxygen quantum yield in aqueous medium. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:27-32]

## Design and synthesis

The compound was designed to address a common limitation of zinc phthalocyanines in water: although these molecules can have favorable photophysical properties, they often show poor water solubility and strong aggregation in polar media, which reduces photodynamic activity. The reported design combined two anti-aggregation strategies, using both rigid bulky substituents and charged peripheral groups, to produce a water-soluble, highly efficient, non-aggregating zinc phthalocyanine. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:33-51, rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:57-66]

As presented in the source, complex `5` is a hexadeca-cationic zinc complex peripherally substituted with imidazole-functionalized phenoxy moieties. It was prepared by metal-ion-mediated cyclotetramerization of the corresponding phthalonitrile precursor `2`, followed by quaternization with `CH3I`. The compound was fully characterized by elemental analysis, IR, `1H NMR`, and UV/vis spectroscopy. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:67-78]

The source further states that the precursor crystal structures supported the expected spatial arrangement of the substituents. Although crystals of complexes `4` and `5` were not obtained, the authors presume that `5` adopts a similar orthogonal orientation, in which quaternized imidazolyl groups are held in rigid positions above and below the macrocycle plane, creating aggregation-inhibiting charged substituents. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:79-84, rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:117-123]

## Solubility and aggregation behavior

Compound `5` was reported to be well soluble in water and in polar organic solvents including `DMF`, ethanol, and methanol, with a partition coefficient of approximately zero. Its absorption spectra in water and organic solvents showed the typical shape of monomeric phthalocyanines, including a B-band at `362 nm` and a Q-band above `680 nm`, with extinction coefficients above `200,000 M^-1 cm^-1`. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:123-129]

The study assessed aggregation in water over a broad concentration range. The absorption data followed the Beer-Lambert law from `10` to `500 uM`, the extinction coefficient remained constant, and normalized spectra at different concentrations overlaid without Q-band shape changes. The authors interpret these findings to mean that no aggregates were present in water at any tested concentration. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:129-139]

Additional tests also supported the non-aggregating behavior of `5`. Changes in pH, ions in buffers, and culture media containing serum did not produce spectral changes, and `1H NMR` spectra in `D2O` at `3-20 mM` remained sharp and well resolved without peak broadening. The paper states that this implies aggregation was totally absent in water and describes such a low tendency of zinc phthalocyanines to aggregate in water as unprecedented in the literature. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:136-152]

## Photophysical properties

Fluorescence emission spectra of compound `5` in water and `DMF` mirrored the absorption spectra, with maxima only slightly red-shifted and a Stokes shift below `10 nm`. The fluorescence excitation and absorption spectra matched closely, further supporting the absence of aggregates. The reported fluorescence quantum yield in water was `0.16`, and the fluorescence lifetime was `2.03 ns`, values described as comparable to those in `DMF`. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:153-160, rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:186-188]

The source reports singlet-oxygen quantum-yield values for `5` of `0.61` in `DMF` and `0.37` or `0.33` in `D2O`, depending on the reference used. Although the aqueous value was lower than in `DMF`, the authors state that the compound still showed high singlet-oxygen production in water and that this could not be explained by aggregation, which had been ruled out experimentally. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:181-193, rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:203-216]

## In vitro photodynamic activity

The favorable aggregation and photophysical results led to in vitro testing of dark toxicity (`TC50`) and photodynamic activity (`EC50`). In non-malignant `3T3` mouse fibroblasts, compound `5` had a reported `TC50` of `395 uM`, which the authors interpret as very low toxicity to normal cells. In `HeLa` human cervix carcinoma cells without irradiation, the `TC50` was `628 uM`. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:217-229]

Under red-light irradiation (`lambda > 570 nm`, `12.4 mW cm^-2`, `11.2 J cm^-2`), `HeLa` cells showed a strong photodynamic response with an `EC50` of `36.7 nM`. The source notes that the resulting `TC50/EC50` therapeutic ratios exceeded `10,000`, which the authors describe as an excellent prerequisite for further drug development. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:230-238]

## Cellular localization and observed cell effects

Fluorescence microscopy in `HeLa` cells showed a punctate cytoplasmic distribution for compound `5`, with predominant colocalization in [[lysosome|lysosomes]]. The source states that there was no significant colocalization with [[mitochondria]] or other cellular compartments. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:239-242]

Time-lapse microscopy showed that visible morphological changes began by the end of light exposure and continued afterward. The reported sequence included retraction of filopodia, disruption of the cellular monolayer, cellular shrinkage, and eventual formation of cellular debris. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:242-248]

## Conclusion

The paper presents compound `5` as a promising [[photosensitizer]] for [[photodynamic therapy]]. In the authors' summary, its two rings of positive charges positioned above and below the macrocycle core fully cover the hydrophobic phthalocyanine macrocycle, preventing aggregation and enabling high singlet-oxygen production in water together with high photodynamic activity in `HeLa` cells and low inherent dark toxicity. ^[rscccc3cc44609c-35.md:249-260]
