# Apaf-1/caspase-4 pyroptosome stoichiometry of 7:2

The [[Apaf-1 pyroptosome]] described in this study is an [[Apaf-1]]- and [[caspase-4]]-containing complex assembled under [[mitochondrial permeability transition|MPT]] conditions. The authors report that when MPT is induced by bile acids, calcium overload, or an [[ANT1]] activator, Apaf-1 recruits caspase-4/11 to form a pyroptosome, and the summary explicitly states that this complex has an Apaf-1:caspase-4 stoichiometric ratio of `7:2`. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:58-66]

## Definition and context

In this paper, the Apaf-1/caspase-4 pyroptosome is presented as a distinct signaling platform for sterile pyroptosis rather than infection-driven [[LPS]] sensing. Unlike LPS-bound caspase-4, which directly cleaves [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], caspase-4 activated within the [[apaf-1-pyroptosome|Apaf-1 pyroptosome]] proceeds through [[caspase-3]] and [[GSDME]], linking the complex to MPT-triggered pyroptotic death. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:61-66, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:734-742]

The study further distinguishes this pyroptosome from the [[apoptosome]]. Under MOMP-like conditions, Apaf-1 preferentially assembles with caspase-9, whereas under MPT conditions it mainly facilitates assembly with caspase-4/11, supporting the idea that mitochondrial stress state influences which Apaf-1 complex forms. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:540-542, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:571-573]

## Evidence for the 7:2 ratio

To define how Apaf-1 recruits caspase-4, the authors incubated pre-assembled Apaf-1 with varying concentrations of caspase-4 or caspase-9 and plotted protease activity against molar ratio. In that assay, caspase-4 activity reached its maximum at an approximate caspase-4:Apaf-1 ratio of `2:7`, whereas caspase-9 peaked at `4:7`. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:587-595]

The paper then reports a more direct stoichiometric estimate using [[size-exclusion chromatography|SEC]]. A stable complex of `Apaf-1(1-591 aa)-His` and `caspase-4(CARD)-Fc-HA` was formed and quantified, yielding an Apaf-1:caspase-4 molar ratio of `3.4-3.9` across three experiments. The authors interpret this as corresponding to about `1.8-2.1` caspase-4 molecules per Apaf-1 heptamer, and therefore conclude that the pyroptosome platform has a `7:2` Apaf-1:caspase-4 stoichiometry. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:597-606]

The methods section supports how this estimate was obtained. For SEC analysis, the study pre-incubated `4 uM` `Apaf-1(1-591)-His` with `5 mM` ATP overnight, added `2 uM` `caspase-4-CARD-Fc-HA`, incubated the mixture for 2 hours at 37 C, and then analyzed the purified complex by SEC to determine the composition ratio. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:1445-1447, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:1549-1558]

## Relationship to apoptosome stoichiometry

The source explicitly contrasts the pyroptosome with the canonical Apaf-1/caspase-9 apoptosome. It states that the `7:2` Apaf-1:caspase-4 stoichiometry differs from the reported apoptosome stoichiometry of `7:4` between Apaf-1 and caspase-9. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:603-606]

The authors further suggest that this differential stoichiometry may help determine whether cells undergo [[apoptosis]] or [[pyroptosis]] in response to different mitochondrial stress conditions. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:607-610]

## Functional significance in the study

Within the study’s model, the 7:2 pyroptosome stoichiometry is part of a broader mechanism in which MPT releases cytochrome c and ATP, activates Apaf-1, recruits caspase-4/11, and leads to downstream activation of caspase-3 and cleavage of GSDME. This mechanism is proposed to underlie sterile, intrinsic pyroptosis and to differ from direct LPS-driven caspase-4 activation. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:133-140, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:665-669, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:749-752]

The paper also notes that caspase-4 recruitment to Apaf-1 requires a higher ATP level than caspase-9 recruitment, and that high ATP conditions can competitively inhibit caspase-9 activation. These observations are presented alongside the stoichiometry data as part of the explanation for why MPT favors pyroptosome assembly over apoptosome assembly. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:580-586]
