# Photodynamic therapy induces GSDME-mediated pyroptosis in esophageal squamous cell carcinoma

## Overview

This study examined how [[photodynamic therapy]] (PDT) kills [[esophageal squamous cell carcinoma]] (ESCC) cells and identified [[GSDME]]-mediated [[pyroptosis]] as a key mechanism. The authors report that PDT induces pyroptotic morphology and inflammatory release in ESCC cells and propose that this response is driven through a [[PKM2]]/[[caspase-8]]/[[caspase-3]]/GSDME signaling axis. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:23-33, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:491-499]

PDT is described as a treatment that uses a photosensitizer and visible light to generate reactive oxygen species that induce cancer cell death. In ESCC, it is already used clinically and is noted for low systemic toxicity and limited organ damage, but the mechanism of PDT-mediated cell death had remained unclear prior to this work. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:45-56]

## Background

The paper frames ESCC as an aggressive cancer with poor survival despite existing treatments such as endoscopic therapy, surgery, and chemoradiotherapy, supporting the need for new therapeutic targets and mechanistic insight. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:35-44]

Pyroptosis is presented as a gasdermin-mediated form of programmed necrotic cell death. In the case of GSDME, caspase-3 cleavage releases an N-terminal domain that forms membrane pores, causing plasma membrane rupture and release of intracellular contents and pro-inflammatory mediators. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:79-90]

The study also focuses on PKM2, a glycolysis-associated enzyme that is overexpressed in many tumors and supports the [[Warburg effect]]. The authors connect this metabolic context to PDT because prior work suggested that PDT can inhibit PKM2 and alter glycolytic behavior in esophageal cancer cells. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:91-100]

## GSDME expression in ESCC

The authors first assessed GSDME expression in ESCC and found that it was higher in tumor tissue than in normal esophageal tissue across public datasets and their own patient samples. They report higher GSDME mRNA in tumor tissue in GEPIA and Oncomine, higher copy number in TCGA, and higher expression by PCR and western blot in ESCC specimens. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:267-281]

## Evidence that PDT induces pyroptosis in ESCC

After PDT treatment, Eca109 and Ec9706 ESCC cells developed morphology consistent with pyroptosis, including peripheral swelling and large membrane bubbles observed at 4, 12, and 24 hours. These changes were not seen in negative control, photosensitizer-only, or light-only groups. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:282-288]

Additional assays supported pyroptotic cell death. Hoechst 33342/PI staining showed an increased proportion of PI-positive cells with round morphology and large red-stained bubbles, transmission electron microscopy showed nuclear pyknosis, cytoplasmic swelling, and multiple membrane pores, and LDH release was significantly increased after PDT. The authors also measured increased TNF-alpha, IL-1alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6, and IFN-gamma, consistent with membrane rupture and inflammatory mediator release. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:289-306]

The same general pattern was also observed with other photosensitizers, including protoporphyrin IX dimethyl ester and chlorin e6, which increased LDH and N-GSDME and produced pyroptotic morphology. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:306-317]

## GSDME, not GSDMD, as the effector

The authors distinguish the observed response from canonical [[GSDMD]]/[[caspase-1]] pyroptosis. In western blots, PDT-treated ESCC cells did not show detectable N-GSDMD or cleaved caspase-1, but did show increased N-terminal GSDME and cleaved caspase-3. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:318-324]

GSDME knockdown further supported this mechanism. In Si-GSDME cells, pyroptotic morphology was abrogated and swelling was reduced after PDT, while caspase-3 activation remained unaffected relative to controls. Cell survival in CCK-8 assays was similar between PDT-treated Si-GSDME and vector cells, though LDH and IL-1beta release were still slightly elevated. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:324-355]

Together, these findings place GSDME downstream of caspase-3 in PDT-induced pyroptosis in ESCC. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:509-518]

## Role of caspase-8 and caspase-3

The study reports that expression levels of GSDME, caspase-3, and caspase-8 were correlated in esophageal tissue datasets. Experimentally, pretreatment with the caspase-3 inhibitor Ac-DEVD-CHO or the caspase-8 inhibitor Z-IETD-FMK reduced the pyroptosis ratio and decreased formation of pyroptotic-like cells after PDT. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:358-389]

These inhibitors also improved CCK-8 readouts, reduced LDH release, and inhibited IL-1beta release and N-GSDME generation. The caspase-3 inhibitor did not block caspase-8 activation, whereas the caspase-8 inhibitor inhibited caspase-3 cleavage, supporting a model in which caspase-8 contributes upstream of caspase-3. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:389-405, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:519-538]

## Role of PKM2

PKM2 was reported to be elevated in ESCC across multiple public datasets and cell-line data, and its mRNA levels were correlated with caspase-3, caspase-8, and GSDME in ESCC. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:406-441]

When PKM2 was overexpressed, PDT-induced pyroptotic morphology was reduced, swelling decreased, and overall cell death measured by CCK-8 was lessened relative to controls. PKM2 overexpression also inhibited LDH release and IL-1beta release, and reduced evidence of N-GSDME production and caspase-3/caspase-8 cleavage compared with the PDT-only condition. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:442-453]

Based on these results, the authors conclude that PDT induces pyroptosis at least in part by downregulating PKM2, thereby permitting activation of caspase-8, caspase-3, and GSDME cleavage. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:496-499, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:546-549]

## Effect of glycolysis inhibition with 2-DG

Gene set enrichment analysis indicated a relationship between GSDME expression and the glycolysis pathway in ESCC. The authors then tested the glycolytic inhibitor [[2-DG]] with PDT. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:454-458]

In the 2-DG/PDT combination group, pyroptotic morphology was abrogated, and LDH release, IL-1beta release, and N-GSDME were inhibited relative to PDT alone. CCK-8 values were slightly decreased, and PKM2 expression was not obviously changed compared with PDT alone. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:458-467]

The authors interpret these results as showing that 2-DG enhanced PDT cytotoxicity while inhibiting PDT-mediated pyroptosis. They propose that pyroptosis may be energy-intensive and that glycolysis inhibition may disrupt the energy supply needed for this form of cell death without substantially altering PKM2 expression in this setting. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:550-565]

## In vivo findings

In mouse xenograft models using control cells, GSDME-silenced cells, and PKM2-overexpressing cells, PDT increased serum LDH and IL-1beta relative to controls. However, these increases were smaller in the Si-GSDME, 2-DG, and OE-PKM2 groups treated with PDT than in the PDT-only group. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:468-489]

Immunohistochemistry showed increased cleaved caspase-3 and decreased PKM2 in PDT-treated groups, while activated caspase-3 was lower in the 2-DG, Si-GSDME, and OE-PKM2 groups than in the PDT-only group. Western blotting likewise showed lower N-GSDME in these modified-treatment groups than in the PDT-only group. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:480-489]

## Proposed mechanism

The paper proposes that PDT suppresses PKM2, which then enables caspase-8 activation, followed by caspase-3 activation, GSDME cleavage, and pyroptotic cell death in ESCC. This pathway is presented as the central mechanistic model emerging from the study. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:28-31, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-595]

## Significance

The authors describe this work as the first in-depth demonstration of a PKM2-mediated pyroptosis mechanism under PDT in ESCC. They argue that the findings provide mechanistic support for targeting the PKM2/caspase-8/caspase-3/GSDME axis and identify PKM2 as a potentially relevant target for improving PDT efficacy in ESCC. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:31-33, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:628-632]
