# CA-Re-driven immunogenic cell death hallmarks

CA-Re-driven immunogenic cell death hallmarks are the measured danger-associated signals and related membrane-disruptive features observed after photodynamic treatment with [[CA-Re carbonic anhydrase-anchored rhenium(I) photosensitizer]]. In the source, CA-Re plus light is described as inducing [[pyroptosis]], promoting rapid release of inflammatory cytokines and DAMPs, and enhancing tumor immunogenicity rather than producing a poorly immunogenic apoptotic response. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:56-63, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:64-85, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:117-132]

## Overview

The study characterized immunogenicity enhancement by measuring three biochemical hallmarks: release or translocation of [[HMGB1]], cell-surface exposure of [[calreticulin|CRT]], and extracellular secretion of [[ATP]]. These measurements were presented after CA-Re photodynamic treatment as evidence that the resulting pyroptotic cells had high immunogenicity and therefore strong potential to activate anti-tumor immunity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:451-514]

In the same system, these hallmarks were linked to a membrane-anchored photosensitizer design. CA-Re remained localized on the cell membrane, generated in situ ROS and lipid peroxidation, induced membrane rupture, and simultaneously allowed visual tracking of membrane integrity loss. The source frames these membrane events as part of the process that promotes DAMP release and increased tumor immunogenicity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:117-132, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:373-377, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:436-450]

## Core hallmarks

### HMGB1 redistribution

[[HMGB1]] was assessed as one of the three explicit immunogenic cell death hallmarks in the study. After CA-Re plus 425 nm irradiation, HMGB1 fluorescence in treated MDA-MB-231 cells shifted from the nucleus toward the cytoplasm, and flow-cytometric analysis showed a concentration-dependent decrease in intracellular HMGB1 signal. The magnitude of this change was reported as comparable to that produced by hypericin, which served as a positive control for immunogenic cell death induction. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:491-511]

Before the in vivo experiments, the same HMGB1 nucleus-to-cytoplasm translocation was also demonstrated in treated [[4T1]] cells, where the supplementary data were cited as showing enhanced immunogenicity after CA-Re plus light. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:521-535]

### Calreticulin surface exposure

Cell-surface exposure of [[calreticulin|CRT]] was another named hallmark used to assess immunogenicity. In MDA-MB-231 cells, ecto-CRT staining was negligible in the dark and became strongly enhanced after CA-Re plus light treatment, while flow cytometry showed a concentration-dependent increase in intracellular fluorescence associated with ecto-CRT labeling. The reported extent was again comparable with hypericin. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:491-511]

The source also reports activation of ecto-CRT in 4T1 cells after CA-Re plus light before animal studies, supporting the same immunogenicity pattern in the murine tumor-cell model used for in vivo work. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:521-535]

### Extracellular ATP secretion

Extracellular [[ATP]] secretion was the third hallmark measured directly. CA-Re plus light caused a concentration-dependent increase in ATP in culture supernatants, with reported ATP concentrations rising from 10 to 200 pmol L^-1 upon irradiation. The source further states that hypericin, even at a 100-fold higher concentration, caused less ATP secretion in this comparison. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:506-514]

ATP secretion after CA-Re photodynamic treatment was also shown in 4T1 cells before the in vivo experiments, alongside the other immunogenicity-related readouts. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:521-535]

## Relationship to pyroptosis and membrane damage

The source places these hallmarks in the context of [[GSDMD]]-mediated pyroptosis rather than apoptosis. During CA-Re photodynamic treatment, cells showed swelling, large membrane bubbles, intact nuclei, and persistent attachment rather than classic apoptotic shrinkage or apoptotic body formation. SEM morphology was also described as matching pyroptosis. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:378-396]

Biochemically, CA-Re plus light increased GSDMD-N and cleaved caspase-1, while GPX4 was downregulated after irradiation. The authors interpret these findings as evidence of efficient GSDMD cleavage, caspase-1 activation, and pyroptotic cell death associated with extensive lipid oxidation. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:397-435]

CA-Re was also reported to remain on the membrane even after membrane destruction, enabling the treatment to self-report progressive membrane rupture. In parallel, CAIX and HIF-1a expression decreased with increasing light dose, and the source presents inactivation of membrane-anchored CAIX as further evidence of membrane rupture. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:436-450]

## In vivo immune relevance

The hallmarks were used as preclinical evidence that CA-Re-treated tumor cells became more immunogenic before testing systemic anti-tumor immunity in vivo. In the bilateral 4T1 mouse model, CA-Re plus light increased dendritic-cell maturation in tumor-draining lymph nodes, increased serum TNF-a, IL-6, and IL-12p70 while decreasing IL-10, and increased infiltration of CD8+ and CD4+ T cells in distant tumors. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:576-606]

The same in vivo section also reports increased levels of the pyroptosis-related inflammasome cytokines [[IL-18]] and [[IL-1b]], alongside inhibition of both primary and untreated distant tumors. The authors therefore connect CA-Re-induced pyroptosis and immunogenicity with activation of dendritic cells, T cell-dependent adaptive immunity, and the abscopal control described in [[Abscopal anti-tumor immunity in a bilateral 4T1 mouse model]]. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:565-615]
