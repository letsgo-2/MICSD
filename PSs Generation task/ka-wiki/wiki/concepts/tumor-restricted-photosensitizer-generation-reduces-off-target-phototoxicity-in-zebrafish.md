# Tumor-restricted photosensitizer generation reduces off-target phototoxicity in zebrafish

This study describes an in vivo [[photodynamic therapy]] strategy in which a photosensitizer is not administered directly in its active form, but is instead generated inside tumor cells after systemic delivery of inactive precursors packaged in [[MOF-199]] nanoparticles. In the reported design, PMOF nanoparticles carry two photochemically inert precursors and use Cu(II)-based MOF-199 both as a carrier and as a source of Cu(I) catalyst after reduction by elevated intracellular [[glutathione|GSH]] in cancer cells, enabling tumor-cell-specific formation of the mitochondria-targeting AIE photosensitizer TPATrzPy-3+. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:41-52, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:156-175]

The rationale is to preserve photodynamic efficacy while limiting off-target light damage. The source notes that conventional photosensitizers can accumulate in non-malignant tissues and skin, causing undesired phototoxicity, whereas localized in situ synthesis inside tumor cells offers a way to confine active photosensitizer production to the target lesion. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:67-75, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:86-103]

## Mechanistic basis

PMOF nanoparticles remained relatively stable in biological media but collapsed in the presence of GSH. Under GSH exposure, Cu(II) in MOF-199 was reduced to Cu(I), the nanoparticle structure broke down, and the loaded precursors and copper ions were released together, leading to in situ formation of TPATrzPy-3+ and activation of strong singlet oxygen generation that was not seen with the precursors alone. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:307-364]

The source further links this selectivity to higher GSH levels in cancer cells. HeLa cells had substantially higher measured GSH than 3T3 cells, and TPATrzPy-3+ fluorescence after PMOF treatment was observed in HeLa cells but not in 3T3 cells or in HeLa cells pretreated with the GSH scavenger BSO. The synthesized TPATrzPy-3+ also colocalized with mitochondria, consistent with tumor-cell-specific generation of a mitochondria-targeting photosensitizer. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:390-430]

## Zebrafish model

The in vivo evaluation used translucent [[zebrafish]] embryos, which allowed real-time fluorescence imaging and tumor-volume monitoring. The authors used a `krasV12` transgenic zebrafish model with hyperplastic liver tumors to test photosensitizer generation, distribution, therapeutic response, and light-associated toxicity. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:507-515]

Several controls were compared with PMOF nanoparticles: free precursors, free TPATrzPy-3+, and TPATrzPy-3+-loaded MOF nanoparticles. Dark toxicity was first examined by soaking 7 dpf zebrafish embryos with these formulations for 24 hours, and all showed good biocompatibility at 1 mM. For therapeutic testing, PMOF nanoparticles, TPATrzPy-3+, or precursors were injected intravenously at 7 dpf, tumors were imaged, larvae were illuminated at 1 day post injection, and tumor changes were assessed at 2 days post injection. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:525-543]

## Evidence for tumor-restricted generation

After intravenous administration, red TPATrzPy-3+ fluorescence was absent in precursor-treated embryos but appeared in embryos treated with PMOF nanoparticles, free TPATrzPy-3+, or TPATrzPy-3+-loaded MOF nanoparticles. However, the spatial distribution differed: in PMOF-treated embryos, TPATrzPy-3+ signal was detected from 1 day post injection and was found only in the tumor region, whereas embryos given free TPATrzPy-3+ or TPATrzPy-3+-loaded MOF nanoparticles showed fluorescence throughout the body from 0 day post injection. The paper interprets this as evidence that PMOF nanoparticles enable cancer-cell-specific photosensitizer synthesis and targeted image-guided delivery rather than body-wide exposure to active photosensitizer. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:548-567]

Importantly, the quantified TPATrzPy-3+ fluorescence intensity in liver tumor regions was reported to be similar among the PMOF, free TPATrzPy-3+, and TPATrzPy-3+-loaded MOF nanoparticle groups. This indicates that the tumor-local photosensitizer signal achieved through PMOF was comparable to the controls even though its overall distribution was more restricted. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:561-567]

## Effect on tumor control and off-target phototoxicity

In tumor-bearing zebrafish, photodynamic treatment with PMOF nanoparticles reduced liver tumor volume by about 40% on average, which the source states was similar to the effect seen with free TPATrzPy-3+ and TPATrzPy-3+-loaded MOF nanoparticles. By contrast, tumors in larvae treated with precursors alone, exposed only to light, or left untreated increased in volume over the same period. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:568-577]

The main evidence for reduced off-target phototoxicity came from whole-embryo light-toxicity testing. Under illumination, the LC50 for free TPATrzPy-3+ and TPATrzPy-3+-loaded MOF nanoparticles was around 1 mM, whereas the LC50 for PMOF nanoparticles was about 4 mM and was similar to the inert precursors. The authors conclude from these results that tumor-restricted photosensitizer generation by PMOF greatly reduces unspecific light toxicity while retaining effective image-guided PDT in zebrafish. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:577-590]

## Interpretation

Within the zebrafish experiments, reduced off-target phototoxicity is attributed to where the active photosensitizer exists rather than to a loss of anti-tumor potency. PMOF nanoparticles produced tumor-localized TPATrzPy-3+ after systemic administration, achieved tumor shrinkage comparable to directly administered active photosensitizer controls, and showed substantially lower whole-organism light toxicity under exposure conditions that were more damaging for formulations already carrying active TPATrzPy-3+. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:553-590, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:610-649]
