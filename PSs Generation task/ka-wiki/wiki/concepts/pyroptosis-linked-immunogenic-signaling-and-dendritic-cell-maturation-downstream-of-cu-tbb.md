# Pyroptosis-linked immunogenic signaling and dendritic-cell maturation downstream of Cu-TBB

Cu-TBB is described as a GSH-activatable copper-bacteriochlorin nanosheet that, once switched to its "on" state in the tumor microenvironment, produces ROS through Cu-driven cascade chemistry and 750 nm light-activated photodynamic therapy. The source reports that these ROS trigger GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis, increase IL-1beta and IL-18 release, and enhance dendritic-cell maturation and T-cell priming, linking pyroptotic tumor cell death to systemic antitumor immunity. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:17-29, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:140-155, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:172-179]

## Pyroptosis activation

In vitro, Cu-TBB showed little toxicity by itself, but when combined with GSH it caused marked cancer-cell death in HeLa, HepG2, and 4T1 cells, with stronger effects after 750 nm laser irradiation. The paper links this response to ROS generation and identifies classical pyroptosis markers, including cleaved caspase-1, N-GSDMD, IL-1beta, and IL-18, as being elevated in Cu-TBB + GSH and Cu-TBB + GSH + laser conditions. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:388-488]

## Immunogenic signaling

The study reports increased release of DAMPs after pyroptosis, including HMGB1, LDH, and surface CRT exposure. These signals are presented as immunogenic outputs of pyroptotic tumor death rather than a direct effect of ROS alone, because blocking pyroptosis with VX-765 reduced DAMP release and also reduced downstream immune activation. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:463-488]

## Dendritic-cell maturation

To test immune activation, the authors co-cultured treated 4T1 cells with mouse bone-marrow-derived dendritic cells in a transwell system and measured DC maturation by flow cytometry. Cu-TBB + GSH increased the proportion of mature DCs, and Cu-TBB + GSH + laser increased it further; VX-765 reversed this effect, supporting a pathway in which pyroptotic tumor cells promote DC maturation through released immunogenic signals. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:474-488]

## In vivo immune effects

In bilateral 4T1 tumor-bearing mice, Cu-TBB inhibited primary tumor growth and also suppressed distant tumor growth and lung metastasis. The in vivo immune analysis showed upregulation of N-GSDMD, cleaved caspase-1, IL-1beta, and IL-18 in tumors, increased CRT and serum HMGB1, and higher proportions of mature splenic DCs plus increased CD4+ and CD8+ T cells, consistent with an adaptive immune response downstream of pyroptosis. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:526-603, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:604-665]
