I’m extracting the claims from the CDT-BODIPY source and drafting a concise wiki page with only sourced statements plus related wikilinks.# Intramolecular charge transfer in CDT-BODIPY

Intramolecular charge transfer (ICT) in CDT-BODIPY is the photoinduced electron transfer from the cyclopenta-dithiophene (CDT) donor units to the BODIPY acceptor core. The source attributes CDT-BODIPY’s dual emission, red-shifted optical behavior, ROS generation, and photothermal performance to this ICT-enabled donor-acceptor design. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:13-29, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:115-130]

## Structure and design

CDT-BODIPY is a water-soluble donor-acceptor-donor conjugated oligomer built from two CDT donor units and a BODIPY acceptor. The CDT units were appended to the 3 and 5 positions of BODIPY through a vinyl pi-bridging bond, and quaternary ammonium side chains were introduced to improve aqueous solubility and support binding to negatively charged microbial membranes. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:16-29, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:286-306]

## ICT-related photophysics

Upon excitation, CDT-BODIPY showed dual fluorescence peaks, with emissions at 463 nm and 730 nm in water. The source states that the stronger red emission in DMSO and the weaker emission in water reflect efficient charge transfer from CDT to BODIPY in DMSO and aggregation-caused self-quenching in water. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:21-29, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:350-398]

The paper also reports that the ICT band contributes to a broad absorption from 540 to 800 nm, with a peak around 640 nm, and that the HOMO and LUMO are distributed over the conjugated structure in a way that supports ICT from CDT to BODIPY. The calculated HOMO-LUMO gap was 1.7 eV, which the authors link to the observed emission properties and photothermal behavior. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:339-349, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:492-509]

## Functional consequences

The study links ICT in CDT-BODIPY to enhanced singlet-to-triplet intersystem crossing and robust reactive oxygen species generation under 660 nm irradiation. The source reports that the resulting ROS consisted mainly of singlet oxygen and superoxide radical, and that the compound also showed a photothermal conversion efficiency of 33.1%. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:23-29, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:399-454, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:455-476]

Because of its dual blue and red emission, CDT-BODIPY was used for fluorescence imaging-guided treatment. The red emission at 730 nm was noted as useful for reducing background fluorescence, and the compound’s light-triggered photodynamic and photothermal effects supported broad-spectrum antimicrobial activity against *E. coli*, *S. aureus*, and *C. albicans*. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:395-398, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:510-543, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:544-605]
