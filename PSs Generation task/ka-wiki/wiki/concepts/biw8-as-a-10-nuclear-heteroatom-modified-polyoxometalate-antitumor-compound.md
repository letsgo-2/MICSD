# {BiW8} as a 10-nuclear heteroatom-modified polyoxometalate antitumor compound

{BiW8} is a [[polyoxometalate]] (POM) antitumor compound described in the source as a newly synthesized, 10-nuclear heteroatom-modified cluster based on {BiW8O30}. The study presents it as a structurally defined POM with antitumor activity and investigates its effects primarily in [[MG63]] osteosarcoma cells. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:64-79, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:136-146]

## Chemical identity and structure

The compound examined in the study has the formula `(Hdmap)3[{CoNa2(H2O)8(BiWO3)}{BiW8O30}]·2H2O` and is abbreviated as `{BiW8}`. X-ray single-crystal diffraction identified it as a triclinic system in the `P-1` space group. The reported structure contains a `{B-beta-BiW8O30}9-` anion, a 5-nuclear heterometallic unit `{CoNa2(H2O)8(BiWO3)}`, and three protonated `dmap` organic molecules. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:148-160]

The source further describes `{BiW8}` as a sandwich structure composed of two centrosymmetric `{B-beta-BiW8O30}9-` polyoxoanions linked by a special 10-nuclear heterometallic oxygen cluster unit `{Co2Na4(H2O)2(Bi2W2O5)}` derived from two 5-nuclear units. The authors note that this 10-core hybrid metallic cluster may favor electron transfer and enhance the compound’s antioxidant reduction capacity. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:161-201]

## Stability and physicochemical features

Infrared spectroscopy showed absorption bands assigned to the `[BiW8O30]9-` anion and to `dmap` organic ligands. Thermogravimetric analysis indicated a two-step weight-loss process attributed first to loss of coordination and lattice water and then to loss of `dmap` ligands. X-ray diffraction comparison between simulated and experimental patterns supported that the product was a pure phase. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:204-234]

The study also reports that `{BiW8}` remained stable in PBS at `pH 7.4` over 48 hours by UV spectroscopy, with no significant spectral changes, and that dynamic light scattering showed an average particle size of about `6.7-7.4 nm` without substantial change over the same period. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:235-247]

## Antitumor activity across cell lines

Cell viability testing by [[MTT assay]] was performed in one normal cell line, [[HUVEC]], and five carcinoma cell lines: [[MG63]], `RD`, `HepG2`, `Hep3B`, and `MCF7`. At 24 hours, the reported `IC50` values of `{BiW8}` were `895.8 uM` for HUVEC, `127.3 uM` for MG63, `344.3 uM` for RD, `455.0 uM` for Hep3B, `781.3 uM` for HepG2, and `206.3 uM` for MCF7. At 48 hours, the corresponding values were `381.6 uM`, `63.2 uM`, `164.3 uM`, `217.1 uM`, `288.1 uM`, and `84.3 uM`. MG63 cells showed the strongest growth inhibition and were selected for further study. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:248-262]

In MG63 cells, `{BiW8}` showed stronger inhibitory effects than the raw materials `Na2WO4·2H2O`, `CoCl2·6H2O`, and `dmap` at both 24 and 48 hours. The source also states that `{BiW8}` exhibited higher anticancer activity than three other POM drugs tested in the comparison dataset. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:263-278]

Beyond proliferation, the study reports that `{BiW8}` repressed migration and invasion of MG63 cells in wound-healing and Transwell Matrigel assays, supporting a broader antitumor profile in this model. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:279-288]

## Pyroptosis-related cell death

The authors identify [[pyroptosis]] as a cell-death mechanism induced by `{BiW8}` in MG63 cells. Transmission electron microscopy showed cell swelling, pyknosis of nucleoli, central localization of nuclei, disintegrated cell membranes, nuclear condensation, loss of nucleoli, and organelle lysis after treatment, which the authors distinguish from typical apoptosis-associated morphology. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:294-311]

Additional functional evidence came from [[LDH]] release and [[ATP]] assays. After treatment with `127.3 uM` `{BiW8}`, extracellular LDH increased from `150.6 +- 5.831 U/L` in controls to `513.5 +- 25.34 U/L` at 24 hours and `593.5 +- 13.60 U/L` at 48 hours, while ATP levels fell from `100.0%` in controls to `46.85% +- 1.939%` and `25.44% +- 0.2321%` at 24 and 48 hours, respectively. Based on morphology together with LDH and ATP results, the study concludes that `{BiW8}` induced pyroptosis and states that this was the first report of POM-induced cancer-cell pyroptosis. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:325-343]

## Oxidative stress, ROS, and glutathione depletion

The source reports that `{BiW8}` increased intracellular [[reactive oxygen species|ROS]] in MG63 cells. Quantified DCF staining showed ROS levels in treated cells were about twofold those of controls after 24 hours of incubation with `127.3 uM` `{BiW8}`. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:344-354, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:383-390]

To examine the redox basis of this effect, the study evaluated interactions with [[glutathione|GSH]]. The authors report that `{BiW8}` did not show glutathione peroxidase or glutathione reductase activity on its own, but in the complete reaction system it significantly decreased NADPH absorbance, suggesting interference with glutathione-related chemistry. Fluorescence experiments further indicated reaction with GSH but not with GSSG. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:355-374, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:410-420]

In MG63 cells, intracellular GSH content decreased by `59%`, `65%`, `75%`, `94%`, and `97%` after 6, 12, 24, 36, and 48 hours of treatment, respectively. After 48 hours, total glutathione and GSSG were also reduced by `97%` and `95%` relative to 0 hours. The paper states that these cellular findings were consistent with the chemical reaction results. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:421-429]

## Transcriptome-defined antitumor mechanisms

RNA sequencing of MG63 cells treated with `127.30 uM` `{BiW8}` for 24 hours identified `3737` upregulated and `3396` downregulated differentially expressed genes compared with controls. Functional enrichment analysis highlighted pathways related to mismatch repair, base excision repair, DNA replication, cell cycle, and glutathione metabolism, with many genes in these pathways found among the downregulated set. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:430-452]

The study groups key differentially expressed genes into three categories: downregulated genes involved in DNA repair and replication, downregulated genes involved in glutathione metabolism, and upregulated genes involved in pyroptosis. Reported pyroptosis-associated upregulated genes included `CASP3`, `CASP4`, `CASP5`, `GSDMB`, `GSDME`, `IL18`, `NLRP3`, `NLRC4`, `NLRP10`, and `TXNIP`; glutathione-metabolism genes included `GPX1`, `GPX2`, `GPX4`, `GPX7`, `GPX8`, and several `GST` family members; and DNA repair or replication genes included `PARP1`, `MSH2`, `LIG1`, `POLD1`, `POLE`, and others. qRT-PCR was used to validate selected differentially expressed genes. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:458-500]

According to the authors’ integrated interpretation, `{BiW8}` acts through multiple antitumor mechanisms: activation of pyroptosis, suppression of glutathione generation, depletion of glutathione, elevation of ROS, and inhibition of DNA repair or replication-related processes. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:502-565]

## Conclusion

The paper concludes that `{BiW8}` is a 10-nuclear heteroatom-modified `{BiW8O30}` cluster with antitumor effects in MG63 cells, where it suppresses proliferation, migration, and invasion. The reported mechanism combines pyroptotic cell death, glutathione depletion, increased ROS, and inhibition of DNA repair-associated pathways. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:553-567]
