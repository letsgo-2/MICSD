# NF-kappaB/NLRP3/GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis in benzimidazole-treated GBM cells

Benzimidazoles, specifically flubendazole, mebendazole, and fenbendazole, were reported to inhibit proliferation of human glioblastoma cells and to trigger pyroptosis through the NF-kappaB/NLRP3/GSDMD pathway in U87 and U251 cells. The study also noted that these compounds dose-dependently suppressed DNA synthesis, migration, and invasion, while flubendazole was selected for deeper mechanistic analysis because it appeared slightly stronger than the other two compounds. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:20-26, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:91-113, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:387-410, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:694-749]

In the GBM cell models, pyroptosis was associated with balloon-like membrane protrusions, LDH release, increased annexin V-PI double-positive cells, and upregulation of NLRP3, caspase-1, and GSDMD-N. The source explains that NF-kappaB promotes proinflammatory genes including pro-IL-1beta, pro-IL-18, NLRP3, and caspase-1, and reports that flubendazole increased NF-kappaB expression and nuclear translocation, supporting activation of the NF-kappaB/NLRP3/GSDMD axis. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:512-597]

The study also found that benzimidazoles induced mitochondria-dependent apoptosis alongside pyroptosis, with reduced mitochondrial membrane potential and changes in Bcl-2 family proteins and caspase-related markers. Caspase inhibition with Z-VAD-FMK blocked the cytotoxic, apoptotic, and pyroptotic effects, indicating that the cell death program depended on caspase activity. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:569-615, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:627-644]

In vivo, flubendazole suppressed growth of U87 xenograft tumors in nude mice without obvious adverse effects, supporting the compound’s anti-GBM activity in the model used by the authors. The paper concludes that benzimidazoles may be promising GBM therapeutics because they arrest the cell cycle and induce concurrent apoptotic and pyroptotic cell death. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:645-665, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:750-755]
