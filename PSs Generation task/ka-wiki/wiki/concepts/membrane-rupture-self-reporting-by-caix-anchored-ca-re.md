# Membrane rupture self-reporting by CAIX-anchored CA-Re

## Overview

Membrane rupture self-reporting by [[CA-Re carbonic anhydrase-anchored rhenium(I) photosensitizer|CA-Re]] refers to the reported ability of this [[carbonic anhydrase IX]]-anchored rhenium(I) photosensitizer to both induce loss of tumor-cell membrane integrity during photodynamic treatment and visually track that loss through its persistent membrane localization. In the source, this behavior is presented as part of the design rationale for linking [[Photodynamic therapy]]-driven membrane damage, [[Pyroptosis]], and enhanced tumor immunogenicity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:115-132, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:203-206]

## Basis of self-reporting

The source states that CA-Re is endowed with a CAIX-anchoring feature that allows it to remain fixed on the cell membrane after long-term culture, including after light irradiation disrupts membrane integrity. Because the compound is also luminescent and membrane-localized, it can be used to monitor membrane distribution by confocal microscopy while treatment proceeds. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:117-126, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:153-163, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:217-236]

Confocal imaging in MDA-MB-231 cells showed that after 3 or 24 hours of incubation, CA-Re was distributed on the cell membrane rather than in the cytoplasm, and co-staining with the membrane dye DiI showed good colocalization. The authors attribute this membrane localization to CAIX binding, supported by molecular docking in which the benzene sulfonamide moiety of CA-Re entered the CAIX pocket and coordinated the Zn2+ active center. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:217-236]

## Membrane rupture during PDT

According to the study, CA-Re generates reactive oxygen species through both type-I and type-II photodynamic pathways, with superoxide-related processes and lipid peroxidation accounting for much of the cellular ROS response under irradiation. Because CA-Re remains anchored on the phospholipid-rich cell membrane, the reported lipid peroxidation is interpreted as oxidation damage to membrane components caused by nearby reactive species or direct electron transfer from the excited rhenium complex. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:183-216, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:328-377]

Real-time microscopy showed swelling cells and large bubbles protruding from the plasma membrane during CA-Re photodynamic treatment, while scanning electron microscopy showed flattened cell corpses with intact nuclei. The study interprets these morphological features as consistent with pyroptotic death rather than apoptosis, and western blotting showed increased GSDMD-N and cleaved caspase-1 after irradiation, together with down-regulation of GPX4. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:378-435]

## Self-reporting observations

The source explicitly states that CA-Re could self-report photodynamically induced membrane rupture because of its membrane imaging ability. In the reported confocal observations, most CA-Re remained on the cell membrane rather than entering the cytoplasm even when the membrane had been destroyed after treatment, allowing the gradual loss of membrane integrity to be clearly tracked. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:436-442]

The authors also measured CAIX and HIF-1a expression after irradiation and found that CAIX expression decreased in a light-dose-dependent manner, accompanied by down-regulation of HIF-1a. They describe inactivation of the membrane-anchored CAIX protein as further evidence of membrane rupture. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:443-450]

## Relationship to immunogenicity

Following membrane rupture and pyroptosis, the study measured three hallmarks associated with enhanced tumor immunogenicity: extracellular ATP secretion, cell-surface calreticulin exposure, and HMGB1 translocation from nucleus to cytoplasm. Under irradiation, CA-Re-treated cells showed increased ecto-CRT, reduced intracellular HMGB1 signal consistent with release or translocation, and a concentration-dependent increase in extracellular ATP. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:451-514]

In the authors' model, membrane rupture self-reporting is therefore part of a broader mechanism in which CAIX-anchored CA-Re produces in situ membrane ROS damage, triggers [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]]-mediated pyroptosis, and promotes rapid release of inflammatory cytokines and DAMPs that support dendritic-cell maturation and downstream T-cell-dependent anti-tumor immunity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:115-132, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:576-615, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:627-645]
