# Pyroptotic pancreatic tumor cells drive macrophage M1 polarization, dendritic-cell maturation, and CD8 T-cell activation

This study describes an in vitro immune-activation cascade initiated by [[pyroptosis]] of pancreatic tumor cells after photodynamic treatment with the membrane-anchored photosensitizer `TBD-3C`. In murine [[KPC]] and [[Panc02]] pancreatic cancer cells, the induced pyroptotic cells were reported to stimulate [[macrophage]] M1 polarization, promote [[dendritic cell]] maturation, and support activation of [[CD8+ T cell|CD8 T cells]], linking tumor-cell pyroptosis to broader antitumor immune priming. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:24-31, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:265-283, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:313-316]

## Experimental context

The authors modeled the pancreatic tumor immune microenvironment using pyroptotic KPC or Panc02 cells together with mouse bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs), bone marrow-derived dendritic cells (BMDCs), and spleen-derived T lymphocytes. Pyroptotic tumor cells were generated by treating KPC or Panc02 cells with `TBD-3C` (`10 uM`) followed by white-light irradiation, then co-culturing those cells with immune-cell populations to assess phenotype changes and T-cell responses. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:317-324, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:326-334, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:787-805]

Before these co-culture experiments, the study also reports that `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment caused pyroptotic death rather than apoptosis in KPC and Panc02 cells. Supporting observations included reactive oxygen species generation, cell swelling with membrane bubbling, increased LDH release, GSDMD cleavage, elevated caspase-1 activity, and increased annexin V/PI double-positive cells under irradiation, while obvious apoptotic features were not detected. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:195-216, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:226-264]

The source further states that photodynamic pyroptosis was associated with [[immunogenic cell death]] features. In particular, calreticulin (`CRT`) exposure on the surface of KPC and Panc02 cells increased after `TBD-3C` treatment under irradiation, which the authors frame as a danger-associated signal relevant to antigen-presenting-cell recruitment and immune activation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:267-280]

## Macrophage M1 polarization

To test macrophage polarization, the authors co-cultured pyroptotic pancreatic tumor cells with BMDMs for 48 hours at a `1:1` ratio. They defined M1-like macrophages as `CD86hi MHC IIhi CD206lo` and found that exposure to pyroptotic KPC or Panc02 cells increased CD86 and MHC II expression by about two- to threefold relative to negative controls, while CD206 expression decreased to levels comparable to the `LPS`-stimulated positive-control group. The authors interpret these results as evidence that pyroptotic tumor cells strongly promoted M1-phenotype differentiation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:326-353]

The study also examined function after polarization. BMDMs previously exposed to pyroptotic tumor cells showed significantly increased engulfment of `CFSE`-labeled fresh KPC or Panc02 cells, indicating enhanced phagocytic clearance capacity toward tumor cells. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:354-374]

In an additional re-education experiment, `IL-4`-induced M2-like macrophages were co-cultured with pyroptotic tumor cells for 48 hours. CD86 and MHC II expression rose significantly relative to the negative control and approached the `LPS` positive-control group, which the authors interpret as re-polarization of existing M2-like macrophages toward an M1-like state. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:387-399, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:818-825]

## Dendritic-cell maturation

For [[dendritic cell]] analysis, BMDCs were co-cultured with pyroptotic KPC or Panc02 cells for 48 hours at a `1:1` ratio. Mature DCs were defined by high `CD86` expression, and the study reports that CD86 levels on BMDCs were approximately two- to threefold higher than in the negative control, with an upward trend similar to the `LPS`-stimulated group. The authors conclude that pyroptotic pancreatic tumor cells effectively promoted BMDC maturation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:326-353, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:801-806]

## CD8 T-cell activation

The impact on [[CD8+ T cell|CD8 T cells]] was assessed by co-incubating T lymphocytes with pyroptotic tumor cells in the presence of BMDMs and BMDCs for 48 hours. The authors measured `CD103` and `CFSE` to evaluate maturation and proliferation, respectively, and report that pyroptotic cells significantly promoted both CD8 T-cell maturation and proliferation compared with untreated controls. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:400-410, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:825-835]

CD8 T-cell effector activation was further evaluated through intracellular [[granzyme B]] and [[perforin]] measurements. In co-cultures containing pyroptotic tumor cells together with BMDMs and BMDCs, the percentage of `perforin+` CD8 T cells was about sixfold higher than control and the percentage of `granzyme B+` CD8 T cells was about sixtyfold higher. The study presents these findings as evidence that pyroptotic tumor cells can support strong cytotoxic T-lymphocyte activation when antigen-presenting cells are present. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:411-436, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:832-835]

## Interpretation within the study

The paper frames these immune effects as part of a broader shift from an immunosuppressive "cold" tumor microenvironment to an immunogenic "hot" one. In the authors' interpretation, pyroptotic tumor cells provide both tumor antigens and adjuvant-like inflammatory signals, thereby enabling macrophage polarization, dendritic-cell maturation, and downstream T-cell activation that contribute to antitumor immunity. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:125-143, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:433-436, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:662-674]

Related in vivo findings in the same study are consistent with this interpretation. After local `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment in mouse pancreatic tumor models, the authors observed increased intratumoral `CD8+` cytotoxic T-lymphocyte infiltration and activation markers, reduced expression of inhibitory markers on CD8 T cells, and immune-landscape changes described as conversion toward a hotter tumor microenvironment. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:478-524, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:525-595]
