# Cancer-cell-selective mitochondrial accumulation and phototoxicity of in situ generated TPATrzPy-3+

In this study, TPATrzPy-3+ was generated inside cancer cells from two photochemically inert precursors, `TPA-alkyne-2+` and `MePy-N3`, through a Cu(I)-catalyzed click reaction enabled by GSH-reduced [[MOF-199]]. The resulting product was designed as a positively charged, [[aggregation-induced emission|AIE]] photosensitizer with mitochondrial-targeting capability, so that its formation inside cancer cells could couple selective localization with activatable [[photodynamic therapy|PDT]]. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:156-175, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:177-213]

## Basis for mitochondrial accumulation

The source states that positively charged compounds can accumulate in the mitochondrial matrix because of the negative membrane potential of the mitochondrial inner membrane. TPATrzPy-3+ was therefore designed to contain quaternary ammonium and pyridinium units, which provided both water solubility and a mitochondria-targeting feature expected to amplify ROS-mediated cell killing. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:180-187, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:204-209]

## Cancer-cell-selective in situ generation

HeLa cells were used as a cancer-cell model and 3T3 cells as a normal-cell control. The paper reports a greater than 10-fold difference in intracellular GSH levels, with `68.5 ± 11.4 μg` per million HeLa cells versus `6.3 ± 2.2 μg` per million 3T3 cells, and identifies GSH as a cancer-cell-specific trigger for GSH-responsive PMOF nanoparticles. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:378-397]

Confocal imaging showed that TPATrzPy-3+ fluorescence was evident in PMOF-NP-treated HeLa cells and colocalized with commercialized MitoTracker, similar to cells treated directly with TPATrzPy-3+. In contrast, TPATrzPy-3+ signal was almost indiscernible in precursor-treated HeLa cells, in BSO-pretreated HeLa cells subsequently exposed to PMOF NPs, and in PMOF-NP-treated or precursor-treated 3T3 cells. The authors interpret these differences as evidence that TPATrzPy-3+ synthesis is cancer-cell-specific and triggered by elevated GSH. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:398-417]

Additional trafficking experiments indicated that after 4 to 8 hours of incubation, PMOF-derived precursor signal was mainly located in lysosomes, whereas after 16 hours most signal had translocated to mitochondria. The source uses this pattern to support that PMOF NPs remain intact during uptake and then degrade after internalization by cancer cells to release precursors for intracellular synthesis. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:417-430]

HPLC analysis of HeLa cell lysates further supported intracellular production of TPATrzPy-3+. Based on a maximal endocytosed precursor amount of `5.81 μM` per million HeLa cells at 8 hours, the calculated yield of intracellular TPATrzPy-3+ synthesis was `21.4%`, `61.2%`, `70.4%`, and `70.5%` at 8, 12, 16, and 24 hours, respectively, indicating that production was essentially complete by 16 hours in vitro. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:431-460]

## Phototoxicity profile in cultured cells

Under white-light irradiation, the free precursors showed only minor toxicity toward both HeLa and 3T3 cells, whereas TPATrzPy-3+ itself potently ablated both cell types with an `IC50` of around `10 μM`. PMOF NPs showed similar light toxicity to TPATrzPy-3+ in HeLa cells, but their light toxicity was inhibited in 3T3 cells, indicating selective killing of cancerous HeLa cells over normal 3T3 cells. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:465-478]

When HeLa cells were pretreated with [[buthionine sulphoximine|BSO]] to lower intracellular GSH, the `IC50` of PMOF NPs increased to more than `50 μM`. The paper also reports no obvious dark toxicity for precursors, PMOF NPs, PMOF NPs plus BSO, or TPATrzPy-3+ in either HeLa or 3T3 cells, supporting the conclusion that phototoxicity from PMOF NPs depends on GSH-activated in situ generation of TPATrzPy-3+. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:491-498]

Flow-cytometry analysis of apoptosis and necrosis is described as showing that the activated cell ablation was mediated by a mitochondrial apoptotic pathway caused by efficient photosensitization from mitochondria-targeting TPATrzPy-3+. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:487-490]

## In vivo selectivity and unspecific light toxicity

In `krasV12` transgenic zebrafish with hyperplasic liver tumors, red TPATrzPy-3+ fluorescence appeared in PMOF-NP-injected embryos from 1 day post injection and was detected only in the tumor region. By contrast, embryos injected directly with TPATrzPy-3+ or with `TPATrzPy-3+@MOF` nanoparticles showed fluorescence throughout the body from 0 day post injection. The authors interpret this distribution difference as evidence that PMOF NPs enable cancer-cell-specific TPATrzPy-3+ synthesis for targeted image-guided delivery. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:507-567]

After PDT, liver tumors in larvae treated with PMOF NPs shrank by about `40%` on average, similar to the shrinkage observed with TPATrzPy-3+ and `TPATrzPy-3+@MOF` NPs, whereas tumors grew by `40%–100%` in precursor-treated, light-only, or untreated groups. In parallel, the `LC50` under light was around `4 mM` for PMOF NPs, compared with about `1 mM` for TPATrzPy-3+ and `TPATrzPy-3+@MOF` NPs, indicating markedly reduced unspecific light toxicity for PMOF NPs despite maintained antitumor PDT efficacy. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:568-590]

## Significance in the study

The paper presents cancer-cell-selective mitochondrial accumulation and phototoxicity of in situ generated TPATrzPy-3+ as a consequence of three linked features: GSH-triggered intracellular synthesis in cancer cells, mitochondrial targeting by the positively charged product, and light-activated `^1O2` generation by the resulting AIE photosensitizer. In the authors' framing, this combination enables potent cancer-cell ablation with reduced unspecific phototoxicity compared with direct administration of the active photosensitizer. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:167-175, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:465-498, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:568-590]
