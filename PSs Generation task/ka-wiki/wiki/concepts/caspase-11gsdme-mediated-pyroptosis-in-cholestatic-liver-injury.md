# Caspase-11/GSDME-mediated pyroptosis in cholestatic liver injury

[[Cholestatic liver injury]] in this study is linked to a sterile pyroptotic pathway in which pathologically elevated [[bile acids]] trigger mitochondrial permeability transition ([[MPT]]), leading to activation of [[caspase-11]] and downstream cleavage of [[GSDME]] through [[caspase-3]]. The authors identify this pathway as a major driver of bile duct ligation (BDL)-induced cholestatic liver failure in mice. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:611-660]

## Overview

The paper concludes that caspase-4/11-initiated and GSDME-executed pyroptosis underlies cholestatic liver failure. In the authors' model, bile acids act as endogenous danger signals that induce MPT rather than directly binding caspase-4/11, and this mitochondrial stress response activates a distinct pyroptotic program under sterile conditions. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:58-66, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:223-235, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:661-669]

## Mechanistic pathway

Bile acids such as deoxycholic acid and chenodeoxycholic acid activated caspase-11 in mouse macrophages and promoted pyroptotic features including HMGB1 and IL-1alpha release, PI positivity, and cell death. This bile acid-induced death was independent of [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3]] and largely unaffected by caspase-1 loss, but was markedly reduced in Caspase-11-deficient cells. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:145-190]

Unexpectedly, this bile acid-triggered pyroptosis depended on [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]] rather than [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]]. The source reports that bile acids activated caspase-11 but did not generate the pore-forming GSDMD p30 fragment; instead, caspase-3 was activated and GSDME was cleaved to its p30 fragment, while GSDME-deficient cells and CASPASE-3- or GSDME-silenced HepG2 cells were resistant to pyroptosis. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:191-222]

The upstream trigger was [[mitochondrial permeability transition]]. Bile acids induced persistent MPT, marked by mitochondrial cristae loss, dissipation of mitochondrial membrane potential, and opening of the permeability transition pore. Inhibition of ANT with bongkrekic acid blocked bile acid-induced MPT as well as activation of caspase-11/4, caspase-3, and GSDME, supporting ANT-facilitated MPT as the initiating event. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:236-302]

## [[apaf-1-pyroptosome|Apaf-1 pyroptosome]]

The study proposes that MPT activates caspase-11/4 through an [[Apaf-1 pyroptosome]]. Apaf-1 was found to interact with caspase-11/4, and this interaction was functionally required for MPT-triggered activation of the caspase-4/11-caspase-3-GSDME axis. In APAF-1 knockout HepG2 cells, bile acids and lonidamine failed to activate caspase-4, caspase-3, or GSDME and failed to induce pyroptotic cell death and LDH release. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:347-367, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:392-467]

In the authors' reconstituted system, Apaf-1 activated caspase-4 only in the presence of ATP and cytochrome c, after which caspase-4 activated caspase-3. The paper therefore defines an Apaf-1/caspase-4 or Apaf-1/caspase-11 complex assembled during MPT as a pyroptosome that is distinct from the apoptosome and channels signaling toward GSDME-mediated pyroptosis. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:417-467, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:574-609]

## Evidence in cholestatic liver injury

To test pathophysiologic relevance, the authors used a [[bile duct ligation]] mouse model of cholestasis. BDL caused acute liver injury, marked accumulation of bile acids in the circulation, upregulation of ANT1, and persistent MPT in liver tissue, assessed by mitochondrial cristae loss. Serum IL-1alpha, IL-1beta, and HMGB1 were increased early after BDL. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:613-625, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:691-703]

In liver tissue from BDL mice, the study detected formation of the Apaf-1/caspase-11 pyroptosome complex together with caspase-11 activation and cleavage of caspase-3 and GSDME. In primary mouse hepatocytes, bile acids likewise induced MPT, caspase-11 activation, and GSDME cleavage, all of which were blocked by bongkrekic acid pretreatment. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:639-646, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:696-697]

## Genetic evidence for a dominant role

Caspase-11-deficient mice were resistant to key BDL-induced pyroptotic and inflammatory changes. Compared with wild-type mice, Caspase-11 knockout mice showed reduced caspase-3 activation, reduced GSDME cleavage, and lower serum secretion of IL-1alpha, IL-1beta, and HMGB1 after BDL. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:652-654, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:698-699]

The strongest functional evidence came from GSDME-deficient mice: BDL-induced mortality was completely rescued in GSDME knockout animals. Although the paper notes that contribution from the caspase-1/GSDMD pathway cannot be excluded, the authors conclude that the caspase-11-caspase-3-GSDME pathway is dominant in determining BDL-induced cholestatic liver failure. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:647-660, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:700-701]

## Relationship to other pyroptotic pathways

The source notes that caspase-1 activation and increased IL-1beta release were also present in BDL mice, and GSDMD was partially cleaved to the pore-forming p30 fragment by caspase-1 while also being cleaved to an inactive p43 fragment by caspase-3. However, the overall interpretation of the study is that, in cholestatic liver injury, the [[caspase-11]]-[[caspase-3]]-[[Gasdermin E|GSDME]] route is the predominant pyroptotic mechanism. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:647-660]

## Significance

The paper frames cholestatic liver injury as an example of sterile, mitochondria-driven pyroptosis. More broadly, it argues that MPT can be sensed by an [[apaf-1-pyroptosome|Apaf-1 pyroptosome]] that activates caspase-4/11, linking mitochondrial stress to GSDME-mediated pyroptosis in noninfectious disease states. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:133-142, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:661-680, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:749-760]
