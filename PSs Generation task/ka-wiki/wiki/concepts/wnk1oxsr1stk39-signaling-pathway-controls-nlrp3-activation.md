# WNK1/OXSR1/STK39 signaling pathway controls NLRP3 activation

The [[WNK1]]/[[OXSR1]]/[[STK39]] signaling pathway is described as a negative regulator of [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation in macrophages. In this study, WNK1 sensed reduced intracellular chloride and, through OXSR1 and STK39, acted on cation-chloride cotransport mechanisms that balance intracellular `Cl-` and `K+`, thereby limiting caspase-1 activation, `IL-1beta` release, and [[pyroptosis]]. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:17-25, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:305-310, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:392-403]

## Pathway context

The source explains that canonical [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation requires a priming step and an activation step, and that `K+` efflux and `Cl-` efflux are among the mechanisms implicated in the activation signal. The study was motivated by the fact that WNK kinases regulate intracellular ion homeostasis and cell volume, raising the question of whether this pathway also regulates NLRP3 activation in macrophages. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:45-57, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:125-133]

WNK kinases are presented as serine/threonine kinases that become activated during osmotic stress or low intracellular `Cl-`. Activated WNK signaling phosphorylates the effector kinases [[STK39]] and [[OXSR1]], which regulate the SLC12 family of cation-chloride cotransporters and thereby control cellular `Na+`, `K+`, and `Cl-` flux. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:96-105]

## Evidence that the pathway suppresses NLRP3 activation

Pharmacologic inhibition of WNK kinases increased NLRP3 inflammasome activation in LPS-primed bone marrow-derived macrophages stimulated with ATP, nigericin, or MSU crystals. The study reports increased caspase-1 activation, `IL-1beta` production, ASC oligomerization, propidium iodide uptake, and LDH release after treatment with the pan-WNK inhibitor WNK463, indicating enhanced inflammasome activation and pyroptosis. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:135-160]

A specific WNK1 inhibitor, WNK-IN-11, produced similar results, and WNK1 inhibition also increased the number of NLRP3 speck-containing cells in NLRP3-EGFP macrophages. These findings support WNK1 as the dominant WNK family member acting to inhibit NLRP3 oligomerization and downstream activation in this system. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:170-208]

Genetic evidence was consistent with the inhibitor studies. Conditional deletion of WNK1 in macrophages increased NLRP3 activation and pyroptosis across multiple priming conditions and after stimulation with ATP, nigericin, MSU, or imiquimod, while the study states that WNK1 loss augmented the activation step rather than the priming step. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:230-261, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:268-290]

The effect appeared selective among inflammasomes tested. According to the source, WNK1 inhibition or deletion did not alter NLRC4, NLRP1, or non-canonical caspase-11 inflammasome activity, although AIM2 activity increased through a mechanism that the authors note was not regulated by `Cl-` or `K+` in the same way as NLRP3. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:291-304]

## Role of OXSR1 and STK39

The paper explicitly tested whether WNK1 regulation of NLRP3 depends on its canonical downstream kinases OXSR1 and STK39. Inhibition of OXSR1/STK39 with closantel or rafoxanide increased propidium iodide uptake, `IL-1beta` release, LDH release, and caspase-1 activation after ATP or imiquimod stimulation in primary and immortalized macrophages, indicating that canonical WNK1/OXSR1/STK39 signaling is required to restrain NLRP3 activation. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:323-338, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:404-414]

The source further states that OXSR1/STK39 activation phosphorylates cation-chloride cotransporters, which then promote cation and chloride uptake. In this model, the downstream cotransport machinery is the effector arm through which the WNK1/OXSR1/STK39 pathway counteracts the ion loss associated with NLRP3-triggering stimuli. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:339-345, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:395-403]

## Proposed mechanism

The study proposes a chloride-sensing mechanism. When intracellular `Cl-` falls during NLRP3 stimulation, WNK1 becomes activated and phosphorylates OXSR1 and STK39, which in turn activate `Na+/K+/Cl-` cotransporters including SLC12A2, SLC12A1, and SLC12A3. The activated cotransporters offset `Cl-` and `K+` loss by promoting ion influx, thereby damping further NLRP3 inflammasome activation. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:392-403, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:815-819]

Experiments in chloride-free medium supported this model. Wild-type macrophages showed greater `IL-1beta` release, LDH release, and caspase-1 activation after nigericin stimulation in chloride-free medium, while WNK1-deficient macrophages showed no additional increase over their already elevated response in normal medium. The authors interpret this as evidence that loss of cotransporter activity, whether by removing extracellular chloride or by deleting WNK1, produces a similar enhancement of NLRP3 activation. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:420-450, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:470-476]

Direct ion measurements further linked the pathway to intracellular ion retention during NLRP3 stimulation. In response to nigericin, ATP, or imiquimod, wild-type macrophages lost less intracellular `Cl-` and `K+` than WNK1-knockout or WNK463-treated cells, supporting the conclusion that WNK1-dependent cotransporter regulation limits the ionic changes associated with NLRP3 activation. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:452-493, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:506-512]

The study also concludes that combined loss of intracellular `Cl-` and `K+` is what drives strong NLRP3 activation. In chloride-free or potassium-free media, NLRP3 activation was strongest when both ions declined, and excess extracellular chloride or potassium blocked intracellular `K+` decline and suppressed NLRP3 activation in both wild-type and WNK1-deficient cells. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:494-544, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:663-672]

## In vivo relevance

The pathway also affected NLRP3-dependent innate immune responses in vivo. In an MSU-induced peritonitis model, mice with myeloid WNK1 deletion showed higher `IL-1beta` levels in peritoneal exudate and increased neutrophil infiltration after both 6-hour and 16-hour MSU exposure compared with wild-type littermates. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:572-595, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:699-705]

## Specificity within the WNK family

Although WNK4 is also chloride-sensitive, the study reports that WNK4 knockout did not increase NLRP3 activation, and combined WNK1/WNK4 deficiency did not produce a stronger phenotype than WNK1 loss alone in vitro or in vivo. The authors therefore conclude that WNK1, but not WNK4, is critical for regulation of NLRP3 activation in this setting. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:596-618]

## Clinical implications noted by the study

The paper notes that inhibition of the WNK1 pathway at multiple levels, including WNK inhibitors, OXSR1/STK39 inhibitors, and thiazide inhibition of downstream cotransporters, enhanced NLRP3 activation. It therefore suggests that pharmacologic targeting of this pathway for hypertension could potentially aggravate NLRP3-mediated inflammatory conditions such as [[gout]]. ^[chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:380-390, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:682-693, chloride-sensing-by-wnk1-regulates-nlrp3-inflammasome-activation-and-pyroptosis.md:762-779]
