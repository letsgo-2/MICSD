# Dual-emission fluorescence of CDT-BODIPY

Dual-emission fluorescence of [[CDT-BODIPY]] refers to the observation that this donor-acceptor-donor conjugated oligomer emits both a blue and a red/near-infrared fluorescence band after photoexcitation. The molecule was designed from two [[cyclopenta-dithiophene|CDT]] donor units and one [[BODIPY]] acceptor unit, and the source attributes the dual emission to [[intramolecular charge transfer|ICT]] within this D-A-D structure. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:16-25, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:115-126]

## Structural basis

In CDT-BODIPY, CDT serves as the electron donor and BODIPY serves as the electron acceptor. The CDT units were appended to the 3 and 5 positions of the BODIPY core through vinyl pi-bridging bonds, so that upon photoexcitation charge transfer can occur from CDT to the BODIPY segment, shifting optical behavior into the near-infrared region. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:268-299]

The source further states that the donor and acceptor energy levels are suitable for intramolecular charge transfer from CDT to BODIPY. Quantum-chemical calculations showed frontier orbitals distributed over the conjugated structure and a HOMO-LUMO gap of 1.7 eV, which the authors say agrees well with the observed emission properties. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:492-503]

## Emission behavior

CDT-BODIPY showed multiple emission peaks depending on excitation wavelength. Upon excitation at 378 nm, the compound displayed two emission peaks in DMSO at 440 and 724 nm, and in water at 463 and 730 nm. The authors assign the blue emission to the CDT donor and the red/near-infrared emission to the ICT band. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:350-359, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:362-370]

The article highlights the aqueous-state dual emission at 463 and 730 nm as a useful feature of CDT-BODIPY. In the paper's abstract and conclusion, these two peaks are presented as characteristic dual-emission signals of the material and as part of its fluorescence-guided photoactive behavior. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:21-25, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:607-617]

## Solvent dependence

The relative strength of the two emission bands depended strongly on solvent. The near-infrared emission was much stronger in DMSO than in water, which the authors interpret as evidence of more efficient charge transfer from CDT to BODIPY in DMSO. This difference also changed the observed fluorescence color: CDT-BODIPY in DMSO showed purple fluorescence under 365 nm UV light, whereas in water it showed blue fluorescence. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:378-385]

The source states that charge transfer is evidently inhibited in water. It also reports lower fluorescence quantum yield and shorter average lifetime in water than in DMSO, and explains this decrease by aggregation-caused self-quenching because amphiphilic CDT-BODIPY forms micelles or aggregates in aqueous solution. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:320-326, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:386-393]

## Functional significance

The dual-emission property was presented as enabling convenient [[fluorescence imaging-guided therapy]]. The source notes that blue and red fluorescence provide additional options for signal collection, and specifically that the 730 nm red emission can help avoid biological background fluorescence. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:394-398]

In microorganism imaging experiments, fluorescence signals from CDT-BODIPY were collected in both a blue channel and a red channel. The blue channel covered about 430-530 nm and the red channel about 650-750 nm, excited by 405 and 635 nm lasers, respectively, and both channels showed strong signals overlapping with [[Escherichia coli|E. coli]], [[Staphylococcus aureus|S. aureus]], and [[Candida albicans|C. albicans]]. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:510-528]

The paper further states that bright red fluorescence after binding to biomacromolecules suggests enhanced ICT in a less polar environment than water. On that basis, the dual blue/red fluorescence was described as a practical way to localize microorganisms for imaging-guided treatment. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:537-543]

## Relationship to other photophysical properties

The same ICT process linked to dual emission is also described as beneficial for singlet-to-triplet [[intersystem crossing|ISC]] through a spin-orbital charge-transfer ISC mechanism, which supports [[reactive oxygen species|ROS]] generation. The paper therefore presents dual-emission fluorescence as one part of a broader balance among radiative emission, photodynamic activity, and photothermal conversion in CDT-BODIPY. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:289-302, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:477-509]
