# Membrane-anchoring AIE photosensitizers for photodynamic therapy

Membrane-anchoring [[aggregation-induced emission]] (AIE) photosensitizers are a set of red-emissive compounds developed for [[photodynamic therapy]] studies in cancer cells, with the cited supporting information centered on the compounds TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C. The source frames these materials as membrane-anchoring AIE photosensitizers and documents their synthesis, characterization, imaging, reactive oxygen species testing, and cell-based evaluation. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:8-12, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:97-106, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:163-181]

## Overview

The supporting information describes three cancer cell lines used to evaluate these photosensitizers: HeLa human uterine carcinoma cells, 4T1 mouse breast tumor cells, and C6 rat glioma cells. These cells were cultured in DMEM supplemented with fetal bovine serum and gentamicin at 37 °C under 5% CO2. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:37-42]

In cell imaging experiments, cells were incubated with different AIE photosensitizers at 10 μM for 1 hour and imaged by confocal laser scanning microscopy using a red emission channel. For membrane co-staining, HeLa cells treated with the AIE photosensitizers were subsequently stained with CellMask Green plasma membrane stain and Hoechst 33342 for nuclei before imaging. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:186-198]

## Compounds and synthesis

The document provides synthetic routes for TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C. TBD-1C was prepared from compound 5 by reaction with trimethylamine solution in methanol, giving a red solid in 66% yield, and was characterized by NMR and high-resolution ESI mass spectrometry. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:97-106]

TBD-2C was prepared from compound 10 using a similar trimethylamine treatment, yielding a red solid in 53% yield and characterized by NMR and high-resolution ESI mass spectrometry. TBD-3C was synthesized from compound 11 according to previous literature and then converted with trimethylamine solution in methanol to give a red solid in 22% yield, followed by NMR and mass spectrometric characterization. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:163-181]

Supplementary schemes identify the synthetic route to TBD-1C in Scheme S1 and the routes to TBD-2C and TBD-3C in Scheme S2. Supplementary figures also include NMR and mass spectra for the final compounds. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:246-248, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:283-290, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:324-339]

## Photophysical and ROS-related evaluation

The supplementary figures include photoluminescence spectra of TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C in toluene/methanol mixtures with varying toluene fractions, along with plots of relative emission intensity at 650 nm as a function of solvent composition. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:339-341]

For [[reactive oxygen species]] testing in solution, ABDA at 50 μM was used as an indicator to detect ROS generation by TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C at 5 μM in DMSO/PBS (1:99, v/v) under white light irradiation from 400 to 700 nm at 30 mW/cm2. The decrease in ABDA absorbance at 378 nm was monitored, and Rose Bengal at 5 μM was used as a benchmark. Corresponding absorption changes are presented in Figure S18. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:182-185, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:342-343]

Flow cytometry studies were used to determine mean fluorescence intensity and cell labeling efficiency after incubation of cells with different AIE photosensitizers for selected times, with 10,000 events counted per sample. The supplementary figures include time-dependent flow cytometry labeling data for TBD-3C in 4T1 cells. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:199-203, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:349-351]

## Photodynamic therapy evaluation

Cell ablation studies used a CCK-8 assay to evaluate viability before and after photodynamic treatment. Cells were incubated with different AIE photosensitizers at concentrations from 0 to 25 μM for 2 hours, then designated wells were exposed to white light at 40 mW/cm2 for 10 minutes, followed by 24 hours of further culture before viability measurement at 450 nm. Cell viability was calculated as the absorbance ratio relative to cells incubated with culture medium only. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:204-212]

The supplementary figures also include dark-toxicity data for HeLa, C6, and 4T1 cells treated with TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C, indicating that cell viability under dark conditions was separately assessed. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:351-352]

## Cell death and pyroptosis-related assays

The supporting information evaluates pyroptosis-related outcomes using several assays. Lactate dehydrogenase release into culture supernatants was measured with a commercial LDH kit, while IL-18 and IL-1β in culture supernatants were quantified by ELISA. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:213-216]

Scanning electron microscopy was used to observe cell morphology after incubation with AIE photosensitizers for 1 hour and light irradiation at 40 mW/cm2 for 2, 5, or 10 minutes, followed by fixation and dehydration. Supplementary figures describe pyroptotic bodies in dying cells and compare cells with and without pyroptosis after irradiation. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:217-222, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:358-374]

Western blotting was performed on cells receiving control treatment or treatment with TBD-1C, TBD-2C, or TBD-3C under light or dark conditions. The analysis included antibodies against mouse GSDMD, cleaved GSDMD, caspase-1, cleaved caspase-1, caspase-3, cleaved caspase-3, Bax, Bcl-2, and β-actin, with band quantification carried out using ImageJ. Supplementary figures also include a protein-protein interaction network analysis covering pyroptosis-related proteins such as GSDMD, caspase-1, IL-18, and IL-1β, as well as apoptosis-related proteins including caspase-3, Bax, and Bcl-2. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:223-231, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:371-374]

Flow cytometry with annexin V-FITC and propidium iodide was used to distinguish apoptotic and pyroptotic 4T1 cells. In the description provided, annexin V single-positive cells indicate apoptotic cells, whereas double-positive cells for annexin V-FITC and PI indicate pyroptotic cells. A supplementary figure presents these flow cytometry results for TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C under dark conditions or after light illumination at 40 mW/cm2 for 10 minutes. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:232-240, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:380-381]
