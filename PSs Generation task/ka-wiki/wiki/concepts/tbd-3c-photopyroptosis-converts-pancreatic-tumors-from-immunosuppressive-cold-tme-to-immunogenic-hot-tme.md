# TBD-3C photopyroptosis converts pancreatic tumors from immunosuppressive cold TME to immunogenic hot TME

`TBD-3C` is a membrane-targeted, aggregation-induced emission ([[AIE]]) [[photosensitizer]] used in [[photodynamic therapy]] to trigger [[pyroptosis]] in pancreatic cancer cells. In this study, the authors present pyroptosis induced by light-activated `TBD-3C` as a way to overcome the immunosuppressive [[tumor microenvironment|TME]] that limits pancreatic cancer immunotherapy. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:15-31, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:153-176]

## Overview

The paper states that pancreatic cancer is difficult to treat and that immunotherapy has been limited largely by the disease’s complex immunosuppressive microenvironment. It frames pyroptosis as a highly inflammatory form of immunogenic cell death that can relieve immunosuppression, promote systemic immune responses, and convert a “cold” TME into a “hot” TME with increased tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:56-66, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:120-176]

In the authors’ model, light-activated `TBD-3C` induces pyroptotic death of pancreatic tumor cells, and the resulting inflammatory and immunogenic signals reshape the immune landscape. The reported effects include promotion of [[macrophage]] M1 polarization, [[dendritic cell]] maturation, and activation of [[CD8-positive T lymphocytes|CD8+ cytotoxic T lymphocytes]], together with inhibition of both primary and distant tumors. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:21-31, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:596-674]

## How TBD-3C induces pyroptosis

`TBD-3C` localized mainly to the plasma membrane of the murine pancreatic cancer cell lines `KPC` and `Panc02`. Under white-light irradiation, treated cells showed increased reactive oxygen species, cell swelling, and large membrane bubbles, which the study identifies as typical morphological features of pyroptosis. Pyroptotic death was further supported by increased lactate dehydrogenase release, cleavage of [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] to `N-GSDMD`, increased caspase-1 activity, and a rising proportion of annexin V/propidium iodide double-positive cells with longer irradiation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:178-216, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:226-264]

The source also reports that `TBD-3C` showed negligible dark cytotoxicity in both cell lines, while irradiation enabled efficient cell killing, with reported `IC50` values of `4 uM` in `KPC` cells and `0.5 uM` in `Panc02` cells. The authors note that obvious apoptotic features were not detected under `TBD-3C` irradiation conditions, supporting pyroptosis as the dominant death mode in this system. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:243-264]

## In vitro immune activation

The study tested whether `TBD-3C`-induced pyroptotic tumor cells could trigger [[immunogenic cell death]]-related and immune-activating effects. In irradiated `KPC` and `Panc02` cells, cell-surface [[calreticulin]] (`CRT`) increased, consistent with danger-associated signaling relevant to antigen-presenting-cell recruitment and antigen presentation. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:267-280]

Using co-culture systems with bone marrow-derived macrophages, bone marrow-derived dendritic cells, and spleen-derived T lymphocytes, the authors found that pyroptotic tumor cells promoted macrophage polarization toward an M1-like phenotype and induced dendritic-cell maturation. Markers associated with M1-like macrophages (`CD86`, `MHC II`) increased, the M2-associated marker `CD206` decreased, and `CD86` expression on dendritic cells increased relative to untreated controls. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:316-353]

Macrophages exposed to pyroptotic tumor cells also showed stronger phagocytic clearance of fresh `KPC` and `Panc02` cells. In addition, existing `M2`-like macrophages could be re-educated toward an `M1`-like state after co-culture with pyroptotic tumor cells, as indicated by increased `CD86` and `MHC II` expression. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:354-399]

When T lymphocytes were co-incubated with pyroptotic tumor cells together with macrophages and dendritic cells, `CD8+` T-cell proliferation and maturation increased. The study also reports marked increases in perforin-positive and granzyme B-positive `CD8+` T cells, indicating activation of cytotoxic T-lymphocyte function. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:400-436]

## Remodeling the local tumor microenvironment

In subcutaneous `KPC` and `Panc02` tumors in immunocompetent `C57BL/6` mice, intratumoral `TBD-3C` followed by light irradiation significantly inhibited tumor growth, reduced tumor weight, prolonged survival, and caused no reported body-weight loss. The study further reports no hemocytolysis, no apparent organ toxicity, and no abnormal blood biochemistry, supporting good biocompatibility under the tested conditions. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:439-493]

Evidence for in vivo pyroptosis and immunogenic cell death included increased tumor `LDH` release, increased `GSDMD` cleavage, higher `HMGB1` and `CRT` expression, and increased `ATP` release after `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment. Tumor sections also showed reduced `Ki-67`, reduced `alpha-SMA`, and increased `CD8+` cytotoxic T-lymphocyte infiltration relative to controls. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:463-486]

Flow-cytometry analysis of intratumoral T cells showed a higher proportion of infiltrating `CD8+` cytotoxic T lymphocytes after treatment. Their activation-associated markers `CD69` and `CD103` increased, while `PD-1` expression on cytotoxic T lymphocytes decreased. Serum `TNF-alpha` and `IFN-gamma` concentrations also increased, consistent with a stronger systemic antitumor immune response. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:494-524]

## Conversion from cold TME to hot TME

High-dimensional `CyTOF` analysis of `KPC` tumors showed a broad shift in the tumor immune landscape after `TBD-3C` photodynamic pyroptosis. The treated tumors contained altered distributions across major immune populations, including `CD4+` T cells, `CD8+` T cells, macrophages, natural killer cells, B cells, and dendritic cells. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:525-545]

Within this remodeled landscape, the overall expression of the immune-suppressive markers `PD-L1`, `Lag3`, and `Tim3` was significantly reduced, while `IFN-gamma` expression on `CD8+` T cells increased. The numbers of immune-active cell populations such as `gamma delta T` cells, `NK` cells, and `CD4+ TEMRA` cells increased, whereas immunosuppressive [[myeloid-derived suppressor cells|MDSCs]] decreased. The authors summarize these changes as successful conversion of the pancreatic tumor microenvironment from an immunosuppressive “cold” state to an immunogenic “hot” state. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:546-595]

## Effects on distant tumors

To test whether local treatment could produce systemic antitumor immunity, the study used mice bearing both a subcutaneous `KPC-Luc` tumor as the local tumor and an orthotopic pancreatic `KPC-Luc` tumor as the distant tumor. `TBD-3C` was injected only into the local tumor, followed by irradiation. Under these conditions, the treatment inhibited growth of both the local subcutaneous tumor and the untreated distant orthotopic tumor. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:596-625]

The treated mice also showed an increase in splenic effector memory T cells, which the authors interpret as an immune memory effect associated with distant-tumor inhibition. In the distant orthotopic tumors, the treatment group had increased infiltration of `CD8+` T cells, `IFN-gamma+ CD8+` T cells, and `perforin+ CD8+` T cells, along with reduced `Tregs`, reduced monocytic and granulocytic `MDSCs`, and increased `M1` macrophages. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:627-660]

## Significance

The study concludes that membrane-targeted `TBD-3C` enables precise, light-controlled induction of pyroptosis with high biocompatibility and substantial antitumor activity in pancreatic cancer models. In the authors’ interpretation, the key therapeutic effect is not only direct local tumor killing but also immune remodeling that strengthens antigen-presenting-cell maturation, cytotoxic T-cell infiltration, and broader antitumor immunity in both local and distant tumor settings. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:663-674]
