# GSDME Overexpression in Esophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma

## Overview

[[GSDME]] is reported to be overexpressed in [[esophageal squamous cell carcinoma]] (ESCC) tissue relative to normal esophageal tissue in the cited study. The paper examined public datasets and patient samples, and then studied how GSDME participates in [[pyroptosis]] during [[photodynamic therapy]] (PDT) in ESCC models. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:101-103, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:267-281]

## Evidence for Overexpression in ESCC

The study states that GSDME mRNA expression in esophageal tumor tissue was higher than in normal esophageal tissue in the GEPIA database. It also reports that GSDME copy number was higher in ESCC in TCGA, and that ONCOMINE showed higher GSDME expression in human ESCC tumors than in paired adjacent tissues. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:267-278]

These database findings were supported by analysis of patient specimens. The authors report that western blotting and PCR showed significantly higher GSDME expression in tumor tissue than in normal tissue. Their patient sample set included four histopathologically confirmed ESCC cases. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:105-112, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:279-281, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:332-335]

## Functional Context

The paper frames GSDME as a member of the gasdermin family involved in gasdermin-mediated programmed necrosis. Upon caspase-3 activation, GSDME is cleaved to release an N-terminal effector domain that forms membrane pores, leading to rapid plasma membrane rupture and release of intracellular contents and pro-inflammatory mediators. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:79-90]

In ESCC cells, the authors report that PDT induced pyroptotic features including cell swelling, large membrane bubbles, membrane pore formation, increased LDH release, and increased pro-inflammatory factors such as TNF-alpha, IL-1alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6, and IFN-gamma. In this setting, they observed increased N-terminal GSDME and cleaved caspase-3, while N-GSDMD and cleaved caspase-1 were not observed. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:282-309, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:318-324]

## Relationship to Caspase Signaling

The study reports that GSDME expression was related to caspase-3 and caspase-8 expression in esophageal tissue datasets, and that caspase-3 expression was also related to caspase-8 expression. In cell experiments, inhibition of caspase-3 or caspase-8 reduced pyroptotic morphology, lowered the pyroptosis ratio, improved CCK-8 readouts, reduced LDH release, and inhibited IL-1beta release and N-GSDME liberation after PDT. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:356-405]

GSDME knockdown did not prevent caspase-3 activation after PDT, but it reduced pyroptotic morphology and cell swelling. The authors interpret this as placing GSDME downstream of caspase-3 in PDT-induced pyroptosis in ESCC. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:322-329, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:351-355, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:510-518]

## Relationship to PKM2

The paper also connects GSDME-associated pyroptosis to [[PKM2]]. PKM2 expression was reported as higher in esophageal cancer datasets, and PKM2 mRNA levels were related to those of caspase-3, caspase-8, and GSDME in ESCC. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:418-441]

When PKM2 was overexpressed in ESCC cells, PDT-induced pyroptotic morphology was reduced, swelling decreased, cell death was lowered, and LDH and IL-1beta release were inhibited. The authors also report reduced N-GSDME and reduced cleavage of caspase-3 and caspase-8 under these conditions, supporting a PDT-related PKM2/caspase-8/caspase-3/GSDME axis. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:442-453, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-595]

## In Vivo Findings

In mouse xenograft models using control, GSDME-silenced, or PKM2-overexpressing cells, PDT increased serum LDH and IL-1beta compared with controls. These increases were lower in the GSDME-silenced and PKM2-overexpressing PDT groups than in the PDT-only group. Immunohistochemistry and western blotting further showed lower activated caspase-3 and lower N-GSDME in these modified groups than in PDT-only tumors. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:468-489, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:617-620]

## Interpretation in the Source

The authors conclude that GSDME is overexpressed in ESCC and functions as a mediator of PDT-induced pyroptosis through a pathway involving PKM2, caspase-8, and caspase-3. They present this as the first in-depth demonstration of a PKM2/caspase-8/caspase-3/GSDME mechanism for PDT-induced pyroptosis in ESCC. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:26-33, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:491-498, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-632]
