# Decitabine restores GSDME and STING expression to enable combined pyroptosis and cGAS-STING activation

[[Decitabine]] is presented in this study as an epigenetic component that restores intracellular [[GSDME]] and [[STING]] expression in breast tumor cells, thereby enabling a combined strategy in which [[photodynamic therapy]] can trigger both [[pyroptosis]] and [[cGAS-STING pathway|cGAS-STING]] signaling. The paper frames insufficient GSDME and STING expression as a limitation for existing pyroptosis-inducing and STING-activating approaches, and uses decitabine to overcome that barrier in a light-controlled nanotheranostic system. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:49-71, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:120-132]

## Rationale

The study reports that in breast cancer, the genes `DFNA5` and `TMEM173`, which encode GSDME and STING, were downregulated relative to normal tissue and that this downregulation was strongly correlated with promoter methylation. The authors therefore proposed pharmacologic demethylation as a way to restore these proteins and improve the ability of photodynamic treatment to engage both pyroptotic death and innate immune signaling. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:284-303]

The paper also explains the mechanistic logic for this combination. In its model, decitabine relieves promoter hypermethylation to reinstate GSDME and STING expression, while photodynamic treatment generates reactive oxygen species that both activate caspase-3 and damage mitochondria. Caspase-3 can then cleave upregulated GSDME into the pore-forming N-terminal fragment associated with pyroptosis, and mitochondrial damage can release cytosolic mitochondrial DNA that activates the [[cGAS-STING pathway]]. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:122-132, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:224-257]

## Evidence that decitabine restores GSDME and STING

Among the DNA methyltransferase inhibitors tested in MCF-7 cells, decitabine was the only agent reported to clearly restore both targets. At `20 uM`, decitabine increased GSDME expression by `70.3%` and STING expression by `40.8%` relative to PBS-treated cells, whereas azacytidine and `gamma-oryzanol` showed no evident effect on either protein. On that basis, the authors selected decitabine for the combination strategy. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:304-316]

To reduce systemic side effects and optimize delivery, the study loaded decitabine into `DSPE-PEG2000` micelles to form `NP2`. The authors state that `NP2` preserved the epigenetic therapeutic effect of free decitabine, with no significant difference between `NP2` and free decitabine in restoring GSDME and STING expression. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:345-365]

## How restored expression changed the photodynamic response

Before decitabine was added, `NP1`-mediated photodynamic treatment in MCF-7 cells generated intracellular reactive oxygen species, caused mitochondrial dysfunction, increased cleaved caspase-3, and promoted cytosolic escape of mitochondrial DNA, but it did not significantly upregulate key proteins involved in pyroptosis or the cGAS-STING pathway. This result is presented as evidence that the upstream triggers alone were insufficient when GSDME and STING expression remained low. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:224-257]

With decitabine pre-incubation through `NP2`, the same photodynamic treatment produced a different cell-death and signaling profile. The combined `NP1+NP2+L` condition was associated with pyroptotic morphology, including cell swelling and large plasma-membrane bubbles, while `NP1+L` alone showed apoptotic shrinking and rounding. Western blotting further showed increased GSDME-N in the combined treatment group, which the authors interpret as conversion of caspase-3-driven apoptosis into GSDME-dependent pyroptosis. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:366-385]

Additional pyroptosis-associated readouts supported that interpretation. In the `NP1+NP2+L` group, extracellular ATP reached `163.2 nM`, which was `1.6-fold` higher than with `NP1+L` and `29.9-fold` higher than with PBS, and supernatant LDH was also significantly increased. The study treats the concurrent rise in GSDME-N, ATP release, and LDH release as validation that decitabine-enabled GSDME restoration allowed the photodynamic treatment to induce pyroptosis. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:375-385]

## Enabling cGAS-STING activation

The same decitabine-enabled combination also strengthened activation of the [[cGAS-STING pathway]]. In MCF-7 cells treated with `NP1+NP2+L`, phosphorylation of STING, `TBK1`, and `IRF3` was markedly increased relative to PBS-treated cells, and flow cytometry for phosphorylated STING together with confocal imaging for phosphorylated IRF3 showed the same trend. The authors conclude that adding `NP2` significantly enhanced the ability of photodynamic treatment to activate this pathway. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:386-401]

The study links this signaling to increased tumor-cell immunogenicity. It reports that proinflammatory cytokines and immunogenic factors released through pyroptosis and STING-pathway activation increased the proportion of `CD80+ CD86+` mature dendritic cells in vitro, supporting the idea that restored STING expression allowed mitochondrial-DNA-triggered innate immune signaling to propagate into antigen-presentation responses. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:401-406]

## In vivo significance

In orthotopic `4T1` tumor-bearing mice, the combined `NP1+NP2+L` treatment produced the strongest antitumor effect, with a reported tumor regression rate of `70.9%`, compared with `29.8%` tumor inhibition for `NP1+L`. Tumor analysis also showed higher phosphorylation of STING pathway components and higher GSDME-N expression in the combined-treatment group, consistent with concurrent activation of pyroptosis and STING signaling in vivo. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:463-490, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:514-523]

Immune readouts in vivo further connected this mechanism to broader antitumor immunity. The combined treatment increased serum `TNF-alpha`, `IL-6`, and `IFN-beta`, increased mature dendritic cells and [[natural killer cell|NK cells]], raised intratumoral `CD8+` and `CD4+` T-cell infiltration, elevated `IFN-gamma+ CD8+` T cells, reduced [[myeloid-derived suppressor cell|MDSCs]] and regulatory T cells, and increased splenic effector and central memory T-cell populations. The authors interpret these findings as evidence that decitabine-enabled restoration of GSDME and STING supports both innate and adaptive antitumor immunity, including long-term immune memory. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:523-571]

## Overall interpretation

In the paper’s overall model, decitabine functions as the enabling epigenetic switch for a dual innate-immunity strategy. By reversing promoter methylation and restoring GSDME and STING expression, it allows photodynamic stress to be translated into both GSDME-mediated pyroptosis and mtDNA-driven cGAS-STING activation, which together amplify cytokine release, antigen-presenting-cell maturation, T-cell immunity, and antitumor memory responses. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:595-621, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:781-785]
