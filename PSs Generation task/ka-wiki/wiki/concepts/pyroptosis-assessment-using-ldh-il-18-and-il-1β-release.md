# Pyroptosis assessment using LDH, IL-18, and IL-1β release

Pyroptosis can be analyzed by measuring lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) released into cell culture supernatants and by quantifying the cytokines IL-18 and IL-1β in those supernatants. In the cited study, LDH release was measured with the CytoTox 96 Non-Radioactive Cytotoxicity Assay Kit according to the manufacturer’s instructions, and IL-18 and IL-1β were quantified by ELISA. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:213-216]

This readout was used as part of the study’s pyroptosis analysis alongside other assays such as western blotting and flow cytometry. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:213-216, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:223-240]

## What the assay measures

LDH release reflects loss of membrane integrity, which is consistent with pyroptotic cell damage. IL-18 and IL-1β are cytokines measured in culture supernatants as additional indicators of pyroptosis. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:213-216]

## How it was performed in the source study

Cells were treated under the study’s experimental conditions, and pyroptosis was evaluated by assaying the culture supernatants for LDH activity and cytokine release.
