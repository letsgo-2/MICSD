# Antitumor activity in T47D 3D multicellular spheroids and stemness inhibition

This study reports that the photocatalytic superoxide generator `NI-TA`, when combined with photoirradiation, showed antitumor activity in [[T47D]] [[3D multicellular spheroids]] and reduced markers associated with stemness. The spheroid model was used to represent a solid-tumor-like microenvironment and to examine whether photoinduced [[pyroptosis]] could affect cancer cells with strong stem-like properties. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:26-28, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:657-669, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:741-743]

## Model context

The authors used T47D 3D multicellular spheroids with diameters of about `400-500 um` as a model of the solid tumor microenvironment. They note that 3D multicellular spheroids are of intermediate complexity between 2D culture and in vivo tumor models, and that T47D cells readily form these spheroids because of their strong stemness. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:662-670]

## Antitumor activity in spheroids

Confocal imaging showed that `NI-TA` could penetrate into the core of the T47D spheroids. After photoirradiation (`50 mW/cm^2`, `20 min`), the treated spheroids showed effective `O2−•` production, evidenced by intense DHE fluorescence. Under these conditions, more than `95%` of tumor spheres exhibited size reduction in bright-field imaging. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:671-678]

Additional evidence of antitumor activity came from live/dead staining and ATP measurements. Calcein AM/PI staining supported the conclusion that cells within the 3D spheroids were killed after `NI-TA + light` treatment, which the authors attribute to `O2−•` generation and pyroptosis activation. In parallel, ATP levels in spheroids treated with `2.5 uM` `NI-TA` plus photoirradiation fell to less than `1%` of the control level, consistent with extensive loss of cell viability after treatment. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:679-690]

The source also states that these antitumor effects were not reproduced by the control compound `NI-DF` under otherwise similar conditions, which the authors present as support for the specific efficacy of `NI-TA` in this spheroid system. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:682-684]

## Stemness inhibition

During analysis of possible anti-spheroid mechanisms, the authors found reduced expression of the cancer stem cell markers `CD133` and `ALDH1` after treatment with `NI-TA` under photoirradiation. In the `5.0 uM` `NI-TA + light` group (`50 mW/cm^2`, `20 min`), `CD133` and `ALDH1` levels declined to `10.4%` and `4.7%` of the corresponding control values, respectively. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:692-702]

Based on these marker changes, the authors conclude that `NI-TA + light` inhibited stemness and ablated cancer stem cell-like populations in the T47D multicellular spheroids. In the paper’s conclusion, they summarize the spheroid results as showing excellent antitumor efficacy together with an ability to inhibit stemness. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:703-707, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:741-743]

## Mechanistic framing within the study

Within the study, the spheroid effects are framed as part of a broader mechanism in which photoexcited `NI-TA` generates superoxide radicals and induces pyroptosis through the [[caspase-3/GSDME pathway]], rather than the canonical [[caspase-1/GSDMD pathway]]. The authors suggest that this approach could be relevant for managing hypoxic [[cancer stem cells]] in solid tumors. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:20-27, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:509-516, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:562-567, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:703-707]
