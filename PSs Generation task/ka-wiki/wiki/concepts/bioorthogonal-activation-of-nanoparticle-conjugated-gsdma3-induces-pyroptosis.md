# Bioorthogonal activation of nanoparticle-conjugated GSDMA3 induces pyroptosis

## Overview

This study used a [[bioorthogonal chemistry]] system based on [[Phe-BF3]]-triggered desilylation to release cargo proteins from nanoparticle conjugates inside tumour cells. Applied to [[Gasdermin A3|GSDMA3]], the system enabled controlled liberation of active GSDMA3 from a nanoparticle-conjugated precursor and thereby induced [[pyroptosis]] in cultured cells and in mouse tumours. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:26-33, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:315-338, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:356-360]

The work framed this approach as a way to probe the biological consequences of pyroptosis in cancer. The authors reported that selective activation of nanoparticle-conjugated GSDMA3 in tumours caused inflammatory tumour-cell death and was later associated with strong anti-tumour immune effects. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:33-41, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:893-904]

## System design

The underlying chemical trigger was a desilylation reaction catalysed by Phe-BF3, a cancer-imaging probe that can enter cells. The authors designed a silyl-ether-containing linker that, upon desilylation, undergoes cleavage and releases the attached client molecule. After comparing TBS, TES, and TIPS variants, they identified the TES group as the most effective choice for the Phe-BF3-responsive system. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:27-29, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:47-64, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:298-314]

To adapt the platform for protein delivery, the authors attached the silyl-phenolic ether linker to gold nanoparticles and conjugated protein cargo to create nanoparticle-protein constructs. In this format, TES-linked nanoparticle-conjugated GFP was efficiently released by Phe-BF3, and the same strategy was then extended to GSDMA3. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:315-338, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1159-1196]

## GSDMA3 construct and release mechanism

The authors used [[Gasdermin A3|GSDMA3]] and mutated both cysteines in its C-terminal domain so that conjugation to nanoparticles would occur only through the N-terminal domain. They prepared a noncovalent GSDMA3(N + C) complex, conjugated it to 60 nm nanoparticles through the TES ether linker, and designated the product NP-GSDMA3. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:361-363, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:470-476, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1188-1196]

Upon incubation with Phe-BF3, GSDMA3(N + C) was released from NP-GSDMA3 and remained capable of pore formation on liposome membranes. The study therefore established that the bioorthogonal trigger could liberate a functional gasdermin effector rather than merely detach an inert protein payload. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:475-477, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1590-1603]

## In vitro induction of pyroptosis

When HeLa, EMT6, 4T1, or primary bone-marrow-derived macrophages were treated with NP-GSDMA3 together with Phe-BF3, the released gasdermin N domain accumulated at the membrane and the cells displayed pyroptotic morphology. Treatment with either NP-GSDMA3 alone or Phe-BF3 alone did not induce pyroptosis. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:477-485]

The induced cell death was further supported by lactate dehydrogenase release and by annexin V/propidium iodide staining measured with flow cytometry. A pore-forming-deficient mutant, GSDMA3(mut) carrying E14K and L184D substitutions, blocked pyroptosis under the same treatment conditions, indicating that pore formation by GSDMA3 was required for the effect. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:481-488, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1274-1292, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1590-1619]

The reported fraction of cells undergoing pyroptosis after NP-GSDMA3 plus Phe-BF3 treatment was about 40% in HeLa cells, 35% in EMT6 cells, and 20% in 4T1 cells. Primary bone-marrow-derived macrophages died more extensively, which the authors attributed to their strong phagocytic capacity. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:488-492]

## Tumour-selective activation in vivo

In 4T1 tumour-bearing BALB/c mice, PET and biodistribution experiments showed tumour enrichment of both Phe-BF3 and nanoparticle-conjugated cargo, with overlap of NP-GSDMA3 and Phe-BF3 occurring in the tumour. The nanoparticle conjugate also showed uptake and cytosolic distribution within 4T1 tumour tissue by transmission electron microscopy. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:339-355, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:493-500, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1628-1639]

The source reports that about 15% of cells in 4T1 tumours showed nuclear mNeonGreen signal after administration of NP-mNeonGreen-NLS with Phe-BF3, indicating in vivo release from the nanoparticle system in a subset of tumour cells. This matched the study’s broader conclusion that the desilylation platform was compatible with nanoparticle-mediated delivery and selective activation in tumours. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:347-355]

In vivo propidium iodide staining showed more pyroptotic cells in tumours treated with NP-GSDMA3 and Phe-BF3 than in control tumours, but these propidium-iodide-positive cells represented only a small fraction of the total tumour-cell population. The authors noted that this was consistent with the roughly 20% pyroptosis observed in cultured 4T1 cells. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:634-643, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1328-1337]

## Relationship to anti-tumour effects

After three rounds of treatment with NP-GSDMA3 and Phe-BF3, 4T1 tumours underwent marked shrinkage and tumour burden became negligible by day 25. Neither NP-GSDMA3 alone, Phe-BF3 alone, nor NP-GSDMA3(mut) with Phe-BF3 caused tumour shrinkage, and a similar regression pattern was also observed in the EMT6 model. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:502-511, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:617-629]

Intratumoural injection of NP-GSDMA3 and Phe-BF3 produced the same regression of 4T1 tumours as intravenous administration, which the authors interpreted as evidence that the anti-tumour effect resulted from GSDMA3 activation at the tumour site. They further showed in a doxycycline-inducible system that pyroptosis in only 10-30% of inoculated tumour cells was sufficient to halt tumour growth. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:634-650]

The study also states that 4T1 cells lacked endogenous [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]], and IL-1beta, so the observed effects were investigated using exogenous GSDMA3 activation rather than native tumour-cell gasdermin execution. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:499-501, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1640-1644]

## Immune context

The anti-tumour effect was immune-dependent. Tumour regression was absent in athymic Nu/Nu mice, and depletion of either CD4+ or CD8+ T cells blocked regression induced by NP-GSDMA3 and Phe-BF3. Treated tumours also showed increased infiltration by CD3+, CD4+, and CD8+ T cells, together with a reduced proportion of FOXP3+ CD4+ regulatory T cells. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:35-36, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:834-846]

Single-cell RNA sequencing of tumour-infiltrating CD45+ leukocytes showed increased CD4+, CD8+, and natural killer cell populations after treatment, alongside reduced monocyte, neutrophil, and myeloid-derived suppressor cell populations. The source also reports increased M1 macrophages, reduced Arg1-positive M2-like cells, upregulation of chemotactic and lymphocyte-activation genes such as `Ccl5`, `Cxcl9`, `Cxcl10`, `Cd69`, and `Klrk1`, and upregulation of effector genes including `Ifng`, `Gzma`, `Gzmb`, `Gzmk`, and `Fasl`. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:847-865]

Serum and tumour levels of IL-1beta and IL-18 increased after NP-GSDMA3 and Phe-BF3 treatment, and HMGB1 also increased. Neutralization experiments showed that blocking IL-1beta severely inhibited tumour regression, whereas anti-IL-18 had a partial effect and anti-HMGB1 had no effect, leading the authors to conclude that IL-1beta is required for the anti-tumour immunity induced by tumour-cell pyroptosis. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:867-877, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1720-1734]

## Combination with checkpoint blockade

The 4T1 tumour model was described as resistant to anti-PD1 monotherapy. One reduced, ineffective round of NP-GSDMA3 plus Phe-BF3 treatment also failed to prevent tumour growth on its own, but when that single round was followed by anti-PD1 treatment, the study observed marked tumour shrinkage and reduced tumour weight. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:37-41, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:877-892]

The authors interpreted this result to mean that pyroptosis-induced inflammation can convert a poorly inflamed tumour microenvironment into one that responds better to [[immune checkpoint blockade]]. In the paper’s conclusion, they propose that direct gasdermin activation may offer a way to improve cancer immunotherapy and to study the anti-tumour immune functions of immunogenic cell death. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:890-904]

## Safety observations

Mice treated with NP-GSDMA3 and Phe-BF3 showed neither weight loss nor abnormalities in major organs including liver and kidney in the reported assays. The source also states that serum alkaline phosphatase, calcium, and phosphate levels, as well as skeletal imaging findings in mice treated with Phe-BF3, were similar to PBS controls. ^[a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:511-516, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1217-1228, a-bioorthogonal-system-reveals-antitumour-immune-function-of-pyroptosis.md:1661-1679]
