# Dendritic-cell maturation and inflammatory cytokine release after photopyroptosis

Dendritic-cell maturation and inflammatory cytokine release after photopyroptosis refers to the response of immature [[dendritic cells]] after exposure to supernatants collected from photodrug-treated tumor cells. In this study, immature DC2.4 cells were co-cultured for 24 hours with supernatants generated after photodrug treatments, and the investigators then assessed dendritic-cell maturation markers and cytokine release. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:306-311]

## Experimental setup

The source states that immature dendritic cells (DC2.4 cells) were seeded in 24-well plates and co-cultured for 24 hours with supernatants from photodrug-treated tumor cells. After co-culture, the cells were collected and incubated with anti-mouse [[CD40]] and anti-mouse [[CD86]] on ice for 30 minutes, and expression of these markers was measured by [[flow cytometry]]. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:306-310]

The supporting figure describes four culture-medium conditions used for this assay: tumor supernatant alone, fresh medium, tumor supernatant with `M-TOP`, and tumor supernatant with `M-TOP+Light`. It further identifies the readouts as flow-cytometric analysis of `CD40` and `CD86` expression in DC2.4 cells, corresponding statistical analyses, and measurement of `TNF-alpha` release from dendritic-cell supernatants. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:476-480]

## Maturation readouts

In this experiment, dendritic-cell maturation was evaluated through expression of the surface markers `CD40` and `CD86` on DC2.4 cells after incubation with photo-treated tumor supernatants. The source presents these markers as the basis for assessing maturation in the context of tumor-cell phototreatment. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:307-310, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:476-479]

## Inflammatory cytokine release

The methods specify that release of `TNF-alpha` and `IL-1beta` in dendritic-cell supernatants was measured by [[ELISA]] after exposure to the treated tumor supernatants. The supporting figure explicitly highlights the `TNF-alpha` release profile as one of the reported outcomes. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:310-311, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:479-480]

## Relationship to photopyroptosis

The dendritic-cell assay was performed using supernatants collected after photodrug treatment of tumor cells in a study focused on pyroptotic oncotherapy. Elsewhere in the same methods, the authors describe flow-cytometry assays for identifying apoptosis and pyroptosis after `M-TOP` phototreatment and analysis of inflammatory factors, including `IL-1beta` and `LDH`, in tumor-cell supernatants. This places the dendritic-cell maturation experiment in the context of responses to tumor-cell [[photopyroptosis]]. ^[microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:248-257, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:263-289, microsoft-word-supporting-information-reversion.md:306-311]
