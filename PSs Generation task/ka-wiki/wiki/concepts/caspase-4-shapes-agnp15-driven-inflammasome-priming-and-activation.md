# Caspase-4 shapes AgNP15-driven inflammasome priming and activation

`Caspase-4` is presented in this study as a regulator of how `AgNP15` activates the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] in human THP-1 monocytes. The source reports that `AgNP15` induces [[ER stress]], rapid `ATF-6` degradation, [[pyroptosis]], caspase-1 processing, `IL-1beta` secretion, and ASC pyroptosome formation, and concludes that the resulting inflammasome response is regulated by caspase-4. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:26-50]

## Study context

The paper examined 15 nm silver nanoparticles (`AgNP15`) in human THP-1 monocytes and compared responses across concentrations. Low concentrations induced ER-stress-related responses without cell death, whereas the highest tested concentration (`25 ug/ml`) produced an atypical ER stress response associated with `ATF-6` degradation and pyroptotic cell death through [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:132-137, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:358-385]

At `25 ug/ml`, `AgNP15` caused a form of cell death distinct from apoptosis. After exposure, THP-1 cells became annexin-V- and PI-positive rapidly, and the nanoparticles induced processing of caspase-4 and caspase-7 but not caspase-3, while caspase-4 activity increased strongly. The authors therefore interpret the death program triggered by `AgNP15` as non-apoptotic and compatible with pyroptosis. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:386-456]

## Evidence that AgNP15 activates the inflammasome

The study links `AgNP15` exposure to canonical readouts of inflammasome activation. At `25 ug/ml`, the nanoparticles induced appearance of the caspase-1 `p20` fragment, significantly increased caspase-1 activity, and increased extracellular `IL-1beta` secretion in LPS-primed THP-1 cells. `AgNP15` also induced ASC pyroptosome formation, with about 20% of cells showing pyroptosome structures under the tested conditions. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:485-540]

Specific involvement of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] was tested directly. Knockdown of `NLRP-3` reduced `AgNP15`-induced `IL-1beta` secretion, and inhibition of caspase-1 with `YVAD-CHO` completely abolished `IL-1beta` release, showing that the response depended on both `NLRP-3` and caspase-1. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:541-579]

## How caspase-4 shapes priming and activation

To define the role of `caspase-4`, the authors used caspase-4-deficient THP-1 cells and matched vector-control cells. In these experiments, `NLRP-3` and ASC protein levels remained relatively stable across conditions, but `pro-IL-1beta` protein was undetectable in caspase-4-deficient cells even after LPS priming. The authors interpret this as evidence that caspase-4 is essential for inflammasome priming at the level of `pro-IL-1beta` synthesis. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:580-612]

At the same time, loss of `caspase-4` did not block downstream inflammasome activation by `AgNP15`. Caspase-1 cleavage still occurred in LPS-primed caspase-4-deficient cells, caspase-1 activity was increased relative to control cells, and caspase-4-deficient cells showed reduced `IL-1beta` secretion but increased PI positivity and more pyroptosome formation. Based on these combined results, the paper concludes that caspase-4 supports priming of the inflammasome while negatively regulating its activation by `AgNP15`. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:597-612]

The discussion restates this dual role more explicitly. The authors write that caspase-4 is required for inflammasome priming because its absence severely impairs `pro-IL-1beta` synthesis, yet its absence also increases active caspase-1, PI-positive cells, and pyroptosome-positive cells. They further connect the priming defect to reduced `NF-kappaB` transduction in caspase-4-deficient THP-1 cells, citing prior evidence that caspase-4 interacts with `TRAF-6` and that `IL-1beta` production and inflammasome priming depend on `NF-kappaB`. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:836-886]

## Relationship to ATF-6 and ER stress

The caspase-4 findings are embedded in a broader mechanism centered on [[ATF-6]] and [[ER stress]]. `AgNP15` at `25 ug/ml` rapidly induced `ATF-6` degradation or processing within 1 hour, and this concentration was also the one that activated the inflammasome. Inhibiting `ATF-6` processing, especially with the `S2P` inhibitor `1,10-phenanthroline`, reduced caspase-1 activity, `IL-1beta` secretion, and the proportion of PI-positive cells. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:613-642, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:693-720]

In the study’s overall model, `AgNP15` drives an ER-stress-linked pathway in which `ATF-6` acts as an upstream switch for [[pyroptosis]] and [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation, while caspase-4 tunes the outcome by promoting the priming step but restraining full inflammasome activation. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:47-50, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:609-612, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:823-833]

## Cell-type differences noted in the source

The source also reports that baseline inflammasome-related protein expression differed among THP-1 cells, primary human monocytes, and macrophages. In particular, resting primary monocytes showed detectable basal `NLRP-3` and `pro-IL-1beta`, and `AgNP15` alone could trigger significant `IL-1beta` secretion in these primary monocytes without LPS priming, whereas THP-1 cells and macrophages required priming for mature `IL-1beta` secretion. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:643-679, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:761-776]
