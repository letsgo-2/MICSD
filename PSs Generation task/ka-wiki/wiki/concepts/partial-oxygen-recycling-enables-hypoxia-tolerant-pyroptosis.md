# Partial oxygen recycling enables hypoxia-tolerant pyroptosis

NI-TA is a photocatalytic superoxide radical generator reported to trigger pyroptosis in cancer cells under photoirradiation. The study states that NI-TA works through a partial-O2-recycling mode of action and can induce effective cancer cell ablation even under hypoxic conditions at 2% O2. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:17-28, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:66-75]

## Mechanism

The proposed mechanism begins with photoexcitation of NI-TA, followed by intersystem crossing to the triplet state and electron transfer to molecular oxygen to generate superoxide radical (O2−•). The paper reports that this design favors a Type-I process and suppresses the Type-II pathway that would generate singlet oxygen, because NI-TA’s triplet-to-ground-state energy gap is below the 3O2/1O2 band gap. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:171-195, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:334-360, photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:378-450]

The source explains the partial oxygen recycling concept as a cascade in which O2−• is converted through superoxide dismutase, the Fenton reaction, and the Haber-Weiss reaction to regenerate oxygen and other reactive species, allowing oxidative stress to continue at low oxygen tension. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:569-599]

## Pyroptosis pathway

Under irradiation, NI-TA induced pyroptotic morphology in T47D cells, including bubble-like pyroptotic bodies and membrane lysis. Western blotting showed increased cleaved caspase-3 and GSDME-N, while cleaved caspase-1 and GSDMD-N did not change appreciably, indicating that pyroptosis proceeded through the caspase-3/GSDME pathway rather than the canonical caspase-1/GSDMD pathway. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:482-567]

The paper also reports elevated IL-1beta release and LDH leakage after NI-TA plus light treatment, consistent with pyroptotic membrane damage and inflammatory cytokine release. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:536-552]

## Hypoxia tolerance

NI-TA retained anticancer activity under hypoxia. In T47D cells, photoirradiated NI-TA produced an IC50 of 3.51 uM at 2% O2, close to the 2.80 uM value under normoxia, and it also showed efficacy in BT474 and MDA-MB-231 cells under hypoxia. The control compound NI-DF showed essentially no anticancer effect under the same conditions. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:600-638]

The authors similarly observed pyroptotic morphology under hypoxia and concluded that NI-TA induces pyroptosis even in hypoxic environments because it can generate biologically significant O2−• at low oxygen tension. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:639-656]

## 3D spheroids

In T47D three-dimensional multicellular spheroids, NI-TA plus photoirradiation penetrated the spheroid core, produced strong O2−• signals, reduced spheroid size in over 95% of samples, and lowered ATP levels to less than 1% of control. The treatment also reduced the stemness markers CD133 and ALDH1, indicating antitumor activity in a hypoxia-relevant solid-tumor model. ^[photocatalytic-superoxide-radical-generator-that-induces-pyroptosis-in-cancer-cells.md:657-707]
