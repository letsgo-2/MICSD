# Rat MIRI and neonatal cardiomyocyte OGD/R as a paired validation model for aesculin

This study used a paired in vivo/in vitro validation model to test whether [[aesculin]] (AES) protects against myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury (MIRI) and associated [[NLRP3 inflammasome]]-mediated [[pyroptosis]]. The in vivo arm used MIRI-injured rats, and the in vitro arm used neonatal rat cardiomyocytes (NRCMs) exposed to oxygen-glucose deprivation/restoration (OGD/R).^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:28-35, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:132-137]

## Model design

The rat model was established by ligating the left anterior descending coronary artery for 0.5 hours of ischemia, followed by 4 hours of reperfusion. NRCMs were isolated from 2-day-old Sprague-Dawley rat pups and then subjected to 120 minutes of oxygen-glucose deprivation followed by 4 hours of restoration. Together, these two systems were used to assess AES effects across whole-animal and cardiomyocyte settings.^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:155-165, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:258-272]

## Treatment schema

In rats, AES was given intraperitoneally at 10 or 30 mg/kg after reperfusion. In NRCMs, AES was added at 1, 3, or 10 μM at the start of restoration. The study also included MK-2206, an allosteric Akt inhibitor, to test whether AES effects depended on Akt signaling.^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:159-165, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:268-272]

## Readouts in rats

The rat arm measured reperfusion arrhythmias, hemodynamic changes, infarct size, myocardial injury biomarkers, inflammatory mediators, caspase-1 activity, histopathology, and TUNEL positivity. The study reports that AES reduced arrhythmias, improved hemodynamic function, decreased infarct-related injury markers, lowered IL-1β, IL-18, and TNFα, and reduced NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1, and cleaved GSDMD levels.^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:166-216, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:317-416]

## Readouts in NRCMs

The NRCM arm measured cell viability, cell death, mitochondrial membrane potential, TUNEL staining, and western blot markers of NLRP3 inflammasome activation. AES improved viability, reduced LDH release and TUNEL positivity, preserved mitochondrial membrane potential, and decreased NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1, cleaved IL-1β, and cleaved GSDMD in OGD/R-injured cells.^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:273-307, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:417-436]

## Mechanistic pairing

The paired model was used to connect tissue-level cardioprotection with a cellular mechanism. In both rats and NRCMs, AES was associated with suppression of NLRP3 inflammasome activation and pyroptosis. In NRCMs, the effect was linked to increased phosphorylation of Akt and GSK3β and reduced NF-κB p65 phosphorylation; MK-2206 abolished these benefits, supporting Akt/GSK3β/NF-κB involvement.^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:428-475]
