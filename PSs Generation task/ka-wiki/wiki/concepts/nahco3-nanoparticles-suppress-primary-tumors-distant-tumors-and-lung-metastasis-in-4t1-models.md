# NaHCO3 nanoparticles suppress primary tumors, distant tumors, and lung metastasis in 4T1 models

`NaHCO3 nanoparticles` (`NaHCO3 NPs`) were reported to suppress local tumor growth, inhibit untreated distant tumors, and reduce lung metastasis in [[4T1]] breast cancer models in [[BALB/c]] mice. In the study, the particles were presented as a drug-free inorganic nanomaterial that combines [[pyroptosis]] induction with regulation of [[lactic acid metabolism]] to strengthen antitumor immunity. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:66-72, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:141-147, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:502-506]

## Overview

The paper frames the antitumor effect of `NaHCO3 NPs` as the result of two linked actions. First, the particles are weakly alkaline and can neutralize tumor-associated acidity, which the authors describe as a way to regulate lactic acid metabolism and relieve the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment. Second, they release large amounts of `Na+` inside tumor cells, increasing intracellular osmolarity and activating caspase-1/GSDMD-dependent pyroptosis together with [[immunogenic cell death]]. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:124-140, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:175-187, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:279-302, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:319-332]

In vitro, the authors selected [[4T1]] cells because of their high glycolytic activity. In these cells, `NaHCO3 NPs` showed concentration-dependent cytotoxicity, increased intracellular `Na+`, elevated ROS, caused swelling with large bubbles and membrane rupture, increased cleaved caspase-1 and `GSDMD-N`, and promoted release of `LDH`, `IL-1beta`, `ATP`, and `CRT`. The same experiments also showed increased intracellular and extracellular pH and reduced extracellular lactic acid after nanoparticle treatment. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:205-239, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:240-302, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:333-368]

## Primary 4T1 tumor suppression

The in vivo antitumor study used female `BALB/c` mice bearing `4T1` tumors. In the primary tumor model, tumor size and body weight were monitored every two days. The control and free sodium bicarbonate groups both showed rapid tumor growth, whereas the `NaHCO3 NPs` group showed significant tumor-growth inhibition. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:391-394, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:447-454]

The authors also report that nanoparticle-treated mice showed no obvious body-weight loss, no evident main-organ damage, and no hemolytic reaction, which they interpret as evidence of low toxicity and good biosafety. Histology of tumor sections showed the most severe tumor-cell death and the highest `CD8+` T-cell infiltration in the `NaHCO3 NPs` group. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:454-464]

## Distant 4T1 tumor inhibition

To test whether the immune effects extended beyond the treated tumor, the study used a distal `4T1` tumor model. After three drug administrations to mice bearing a left-back `4T1` tumor, the authors inoculated `4T1` cells again on the right back to simulate a distant tumor focus. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:465-471]

In this model, distant tumors received no direct nanomaterial treatment, yet the `NaHCO3 NPs` group showed significantly inhibited distant-tumor growth relative to the control and free sodium bicarbonate groups. The paper interprets this result as evidence that treatment initiated an effective systemic antitumor immune response. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:471-475]

This distant-tumor result is closely related to the study’s broader immune findings and can be cross-referenced with [[Abscopal anti-tumor immunity in a bilateral 4T1 mouse model]], although the present source describes a sequential distal-tumor challenge rather than the bilateral design used in that separate page.

## Lung metastasis suppression

The study also evaluated antimetastatic activity in a lung-colonization setting. After three administrations in `4T1` tumor-bearing `BALB/c` mice, the investigators injected `3 x 10^5` `4T1` cells in `100 uL` PBS through the tail vein to induce lung metastasis, and lung tissue was later examined by `H&E` staining. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:477-485]

According to the reported histology, the `NaHCO3 NPs` group had the fewest lung metastatic lesions among the groups tested. The authors conclude that the nanoparticles effectively inhibited pulmonary metastasis in addition to suppressing primary and distant tumors. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:484-490, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:502-503]

## Immune changes associated with the antitumor effect

The paper links these in vivo outcomes to both immune activation and reduced immunosuppression. After triple treatment, `NaHCO3 NPs` produced the highest dendritic-cell maturation in tumor-draining lymph nodes and increased intratumoral `CD4+` and `CD8+` T cells. Reported values for the nanoparticle group reached `38.5%` for `CD4+` T cells and `1.3%` for `CD8+` T cells, corresponding to more than `1.6-fold` and `2.3-fold` of the control group, respectively. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:394-415]

At the same time, the nanoparticles shifted tumor-associated macrophages from an `M2`-dominant state toward `M1`, decreasing `M2 TAM` from `26.0%` to `15.5%` and increasing `M1 TAM` from `11.2%` to `24.7%`. They also reduced the frequencies of [[myeloid-derived suppressor cells|MDSCs]] and [[regulatory T cells|Treg]], supporting the authors’ conclusion that `NaHCO3 NPs` both activate antitumor immunity and alleviate the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:416-433, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:497-503]

## Significance

In the authors’ interpretation, the key finding is that a simple, drug-free `NaHCO3` nanomaterial could simultaneously address two common barriers to [[cancer immunotherapy]]: weak immune activation and a lactic-acid-associated immunosuppressive microenvironment. Within the `4T1` models used here, that combined mechanism was associated with suppression of primary tumors, inhibition of untreated distant tumors, and reduced lung metastasis. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:53-72, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:491-506]
