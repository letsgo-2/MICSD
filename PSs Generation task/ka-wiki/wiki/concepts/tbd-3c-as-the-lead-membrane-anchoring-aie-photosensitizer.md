# TBD-3C as the lead membrane-anchoring AIE photosensitizer

**TBD-3C** is the three-cationic-chain member of the TBD-R series of membrane-anchoring [[aggregation-induced emission|AIE]] photosensitizers developed for [[photodynamic therapy]] and pyroptosis-oriented cancer cell ablation. In the reported series, TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C share the same TBD aromatic core, while differing in the number of cationic chains used to tune membrane anchoring. The study identifies TBD-3C as the optimized membrane-anchoring photosensitizer and the one with the highest light-triggered cancer-cell ablation efficiency. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:20-29, activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:129-149]

## Design within the TBD-R series

The TBD-R photosensitizers were designed by conjugating the TBD core and a phenyl ring with cationic chains. TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C contain one, two, and three cationic groups, respectively. The TBD core was selected to provide fluorescence turn-on behavior and photosensitization, while the cationic chains were used to confer membrane-anchoring capability. The source states that membrane anchoring increased as the number of cationic chains increased. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:125-145, activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:156-167]

All three molecules showed broad absorption from 300 to 500 nm, weak red emission centered at 650 nm, and typical AIE behavior with weak emission in molecular states and strong red emission in aggregate states. They also showed efficient ROS generation under irradiation, with similar ABDA decomposition rates across the series, indicating that the main distinction among the three compounds was not ROS-generation capacity but membrane-anchoring behavior. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:168-188]

## Evidence that TBD-3C was the lead compound

In confocal membrane-localization studies in HeLa cells, TBD-3C showed the strongest overlap with a membrane tracker, with a Pearson's correlation coefficient of 0.95, compared with 0.88 for TBD-2C and 0.75 for TBD-1C. The source also notes that TBD-1C showed clearly observable cytoplasmic fluorescence, whereas TBD-3C showed excellent membrane anchoring. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:189-219]

Across multiple cancer cell lines including [[4T1]], HeLa, and C6, all three photosensitizers labeled cell membranes, but TBD-3C labeled them considerably more strongly than TBD-1C or TBD-2C. Flow-cytometric analysis agreed with the microscopy results, and the source reports that TBD-3C staining increased over time and could successfully stain membranes within 30 minutes. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:220-238]

In light-activated cytotoxicity experiments, TBD-3C had the lowest reported IC50 values among the three compounds in 4T1, HeLa, and C6 cells: 7.1, 7.1, and 7.9 microM, respectively. The corresponding IC50 values were higher for TBD-2C and highest for TBD-1C, while all three showed negligible dark cytotoxicity. The source attributes the stronger ablation effect of TBD-3C to its superior membrane-anchoring capacity. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:239-258]

## Functional consequences of stronger membrane anchoring

The study links TBD-3C's stronger membrane anchoring to more effective in situ ROS production at the plasma membrane, resulting in direct membrane damage. During real-time and electron-microscopy observation after irradiation, treated cells showed swelling and bubble-like protrusions on the membrane. These protrusions were described as pyroptotic bodies, and pyroptotic morphology was reported to be more obvious with TBD-3C than with TBD-1C or TBD-2C. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:259-330]

LDH-release assays in 4T1 cells further supported this ranking. Under irradiation, LDH release followed the order TBD-1C at 22.1%, TBD-2C at 59.7%, and TBD-3C at 78.7%, with negligible release in the dark-control conditions. Similar trends were observed in HeLa and C6 cells. The source presents these data as evidence that membrane-anchoring photodynamic treatment induced cell death through the [[pyroptosis]] pathway, with TBD-3C producing the strongest effect. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:331-345]

## Association with pyroptosis dominance

Mechanistic analysis in 4T1 cells showed that photodynamic treatment with TBD-R photosensitizers increased cleaved [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] and cleaved [[Caspase-1]], with the strongest increase reported for TBD-3C. The supernatant levels of the pro-inflammatory cytokines IL-1b and IL-18 also increased after treatment, and again TBD-3C showed the highest increase among the three compounds. The authors describe the mechanism as ROS generation under light irradiation leading to caspase-1 activation, GSDMD cleavage, membrane pore formation, and cytokine release. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:345-398]

The study also examined apoptosis-related markers and reports that apoptosis and pyroptosis co-existed during membrane-targeting photodynamic treatment. However, apoptosis was more pronounced with TBD-1C, whereas pyroptosis increased with higher membrane anchoring and became more dominant with TBD-3C. Flow-cytometry results showed the highest proportion of double-positive pyroptotic cells for TBD-3C and the lowest proportion of annexin V single-positive apoptotic cells among the three tested photosensitizers. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:399-441]

## Interpretation in the paper

The paper presents TBD-3C as the lead member of the series because its three cationic chains gave it the strongest membrane anchoring, which in turn enabled localized ROS production, direct membrane damage, stronger pyroptosis-associated signaling, and the highest cancer-cell ablation efficiency under light irradiation. In the authors' framing, TBD-3C best demonstrates the design principle that increasing membrane anchoring can shift photodynamic cell killing toward pyroptosis. ^[activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:142-149, activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:320-330, activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:390-398, activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membraneanchoring-aie-photosensitizer-design-new-prospect-for-photodynamic-cancer-cell-ablation.md:443-463]
