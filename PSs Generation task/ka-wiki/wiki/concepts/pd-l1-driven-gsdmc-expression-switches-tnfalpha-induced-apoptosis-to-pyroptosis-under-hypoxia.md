# PD-L1-driven GSDMC expression switches TNFalpha-induced apoptosis to pyroptosis under hypoxia

This mechanism was described in breast cancer cells as a non-immune-checkpoint function of [[PD-L1]]. In the cited study summary, hypoxic conditions enabled PD-L1 to translocate to the nucleus through `p-Y705-Stat3`, and nuclear PD-L1 (`nPD-L1`) was reported to switch [[TNFalpha]]-induced [[apoptosis]] to [[pyroptosis]]. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:451-464]

The pyroptotic switch was linked to [[Gasdermin C|GSDMC]] and [[caspase-8]]. The source states that `GSDMC` can be cleaved by caspase-8, and specifically reports that in MDA-MB-231 breast cancer cells under death-receptor signaling, caspase-8 cleavage of GSDMC switched TNFalpha-induced apoptosis to pyroptosis. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:264-271, pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:460-463]

The review presents this finding as a clarification of a previously unclear role for nuclear PD-L1 in breast cancer cells. It notes that PD-L1 is classically known for suppressing anti-tumor immunity through interaction with [[PD-1]], but that the hypoxia-associated nuclear localization of PD-L1 revealed an additional function connected to pyroptotic cell death. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:451-459, pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:464-465]

The same summary also reports that several chemotherapeutic agents, including daunorubicin, doxorubicin, epirubicin, and actinomycin D, could induce nuclear PD-L1 and GSDMC expression together with caspase-8 activation, thereby inducing pyroptosis in MDA-MB-231 human breast cancer cells. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:466-470]

Within the review’s breast-cancer overview table, this mechanism is condensed as a specific breast-cancer-associated pyroptosis pathway: under hypoxic conditions, nuclear PD-L1 switches TNFalpha-induced apoptosis to pyroptosis, with GSDMC being cleaved by caspase-8. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:679-685]
