# GSDME expression and methylation state govern pyroptosis competence, therapy sensitivity, and prognosis in cancer

[[GSDME]] is presented in the source as a key determinant of whether cancer cells undergo [[pyroptosis]] rather than [[apoptosis]] after caspase-3 activation. In the caspase-3/GSDME pathway, active caspase 3 cleaves GSDME to generate an N-terminal fragment that perforates the plasma membrane and provokes pyroptosis; when GSDME expression is low, apoptosis occurs instead. The review therefore identifies GSDME expression level as a primary determinant of the transition between these two death programs. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:341-355, pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:361-385]

The review further links low GSDME expression in tumors to [[DNA methylation|hypermethylation]] and impaired pyroptosis competence. It states that low levels of GSDME, generated by hypermethylation of GSDME mRNA in tumor cells, impair the ability of many tumor cells to undergo pyroptosis, stimulate tumor growth, and may enhance resistance to chemotherapy while hindering immunotherapy effectiveness. Conversely, targeting GSDME methylation to restore pyroptosis is described as a way to improve treatment response. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1590-1617]

## Mechanistic role in pyroptosis

Chemotherapeutic drugs including [[doxorubicin]], [[cisplatin]], and actinomycin D can activate caspase 3, which then cleaves GSDME and triggers pyroptosis. Although this route does not require an inflammasome to initiate membrane pore formation, the resulting GSDME N-terminal fragment can enable NLRP3 inflammasome activation and further increase IL-1beta and IL-18 production. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:215-216, pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:341-357]

Beyond direct tumor-cell killing, the review describes GSDME-mediated pyroptosis as immunologically active. GSDME-driven pyroptosis produces IL-18, IL-1beta, and HMGB1 and can convert “cold” tumors into “hot” tumors, promoting antitumor immunity. In mouse models with GSDME-expressing tumors, GSDME knockout increased tumor development, whereas ectopic expression inhibited tumor development; this tumor suppression was mediated by killer cytotoxic lymphocytes and was associated with greater NK-cell and CD8-positive T-cell infiltration and better tumor-associated macrophage phagocytosis. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:693-705]

The source also notes that [[granzyme B]] from NK cells can cleave GSDME at the same site as caspase 3 and can also activate caspase 3, placing GSDME within immune-cell-mediated tumor killing pathways as well as drug-triggered pyroptosis. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:413-418]

## Methylation and restoration of GSDME function

The review repeatedly frames demethylation as a practical way to restore GSDME-dependent pyroptosis in cancer. It states that [[decitabine]] (DAC) can demethylate GSDME, restore normal GSDME expression, and boost antitumor treatment effectiveness. More broadly, the review highlights pyroptosis-inducing agents that activate either the caspase-1/GSDMD or caspase-3/GSDME pathways as promising antitumor approaches, with particular emphasis on strategies targeting GSDME methylation. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1604-1617]

In breast cancer specifically, DAC is reported to trigger GSDME expression by DNA demethylation, which boosts pyroptosis and increases the chemosensitivity of MCF-7/Taxol cells to Taxol. A breast-cancer nanomedicine carrying indocyanine green and DAC is likewise described as combining caspase-3 activation with DNA-methylation inhibition to raise GSDME expression and induce photo-activated pyroptosis. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:886-888, pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:931-938]

In lung cancer, the review describes an inhaled nanomedicine loaded with DAC and doxorubicin that promoted pyroptosis by reversing GSDME silencing and upregulating caspase 3 expression, thereby suppressing lung tumor growth and promoting immunological memory. In colorectal cancer, DAC was combined with chemotherapeutic nanodrugs to predemethylate and upregulate GSDME in colon adenocarcinoma cells before cisplatin delivery activated the caspase-3 pathway and induced pyroptosis. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:827-840, pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1247-1257]

## Therapy sensitivity

The review links higher GSDME expression to greater sensitivity to several anticancer treatments. In lung cancer, GSDME expression is reduced in tumor tissue, whereas GSDME overexpression increases chemosensitivity; higher GSDME also facilitates chemokine release that affects T-cell activation and infiltration. The review further states that both paclitaxel and cisplatin induce pyroptosis in lung cancer cells through caspase-3/GSDME activation, and that cisplatin may be especially beneficial in lung cancers with high GSDME expression. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:780-797]

The same pattern appears in other tumors discussed by the review. In gastric cancer, GSDME converts 5-FU-induced caspase-3-dependent apoptosis into pyroptosis, while simvastatin upregulates caspase 3 and GSDME to cause pyroptotic death, and combining BIX-01294 with cisplatin lowers gastric-cancer cell viability through GSDME-mediated pyroptosis. In colorectal cancer, lobaplatin and the FXR agonist GW4064 both enhance antitumor effects through [[caspase-3gsdme-mediated-pyroptosis|Caspase-3/GSDME-mediated pyroptosis]], with GW4064 specifically reported to increase oxaliplatin chemosensitivity. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1112-1116, pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1143-1154, pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1225-1246]

The review also links GSDME to sensitivity in breast and ovarian cancer models. In breast cancer, doxorubicin-induced ROS activates JNK phosphorylation and causes pyroptosis via the caspase-3/GSDME pathway, while tetraarsenic hexoxide induces caspase-3/GSDME-dependent pyroptotic death and inhibits proliferation and metastasis in triple-negative breast cancer. In ovarian cancer, BI 2536 induces pyroptosis through the caspase-3/GSDME pathway and accumulates CD8-positive T lymphocytes at tumor sites, while bexarotene provokes pyroptotic death through a caspase-4/GSDME cascade. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:895-920, pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1419-1445]

## Prognostic relevance

The source describes GSDME-related measures as useful prognostic biomarkers in cancer. In lung cancer, elevated GSDME expression is associated with higher postoperative survival and lower lymph-node metastasis, suggesting prognostic value. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:854-866]

In breast cancer, the review states that GSDME methylation is a promising biomarker for diagnosis and prognosis. It also notes that breast cancers show lower levels of some pyroptosis effector proteins than nearby normal tissue, and that broader pyroptosis-related gene models can stratify progression, overall survival, relapse, immune-cell infiltration, and immune-checkpoint features. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1067-1088]

## Cancer-type context

In breast cancer, the review states that GSDME is substantially downregulated and inhibits cell proliferation and invasion, and that GSDME methylation was identified only in estrogen-receptor-expressing cell lines in the cited study. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:869-880]

In ovarian cancer, the source contrasts greater GSDMD and lower GSDME protein levels in the tumor microenvironment, and reports that serous ovarian cancer shows higher GSDMD and GSDMC but lower GSDME and PJVK expression. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1365-1373]

Across cancers, the review’s overall position is that GSDME has major influence on tumor growth, chemosensitivity, and response to immunotherapy because its expression state governs whether treatment can engage pyroptosis. This makes GSDME expression and methylation state relevant both as biomarkers and as therapeutic targets. ^[pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1573-1589, pyroptosis-and-inflammasomes-in-cancer-and-inflammation.md:1590-1617]
