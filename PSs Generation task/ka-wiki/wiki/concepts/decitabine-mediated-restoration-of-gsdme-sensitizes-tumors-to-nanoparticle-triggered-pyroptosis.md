# Decitabine-mediated restoration of GSDME sensitizes tumors to nanoparticle-triggered pyroptosis

[[Decitabine]]-mediated restoration of [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]] refers to a strategy in which the DNA methyltransferase inhibitor decitabine (DAC) is used to reverse suppression of GSDME expression in tumor cells, thereby making those cells more susceptible to pyroptosis triggered by nanoparticle-delivered therapies. The source describes this approach as a way to improve pyroptosis-based cancer treatment in tumors where GSDME is low because of DFNA5 gene hypermethylation. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:306-330, cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:1094-1099]

## Rationale

The source states that hypermethylation of the DFNA5 gene causes GSDME protein expression in most tumor cells to be much lower than in normal cells. Because [[caspase-3gsdme-mediated-pyroptosis|Caspase-3/GSDME-mediated pyroptosis]] depends on GSDME availability, this low expression is presented as a reason for unsatisfactory treatment efficacy and severe side effects, and as a barrier to initiating pyroptosis in many tumors. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:306-321, cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:1094-1096]

DAC is identified as one of the most frequently used DNA methyltransferase inhibitors. At low dose, it can inhibit DFNA5 methylation and recover more normal GSDME expression, which the review presents as a sensitizing step before nanoparticle-based pyroptosis induction. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:327-330]

## DAC plus cisplatin nanoliposomes

One example summarized in the source combined DAC with a tumor-targeted nanoliposome carrying cisplatin, termed `LipoDDP`. In this system, DAC restored GSDME expression, while LipoDDP activated caspase-3-involving pyroptosis in cancer cells, producing tumor-cell swelling and leakage of cellular contents. The liposomal carrier is described as offering good biocompatibility, high drug-loading efficiency and stability, long blood circulation time, and an enhanced [[enhanced permeability and retention effect|EPR effect]]. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:322-337]

Following DAC plus LipoDDP treatment, genes associated with apoptosis, necrosis, and pyroptosis were all upregulated overall. The review notes that the pyroptotic process was accompanied by apoptosis and necrosis, described here as “secondary necrosis.” ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:338-342]

The same combined treatment also increased immune-associated readouts. The source reports greater dendritic-cell maturation (`CD11c+CD80+CD86+`) in lymph nodes and conversion of naive `CD8+` T cells into central memory `CD8+` T cells in the spleen, suggesting activation of systemic immune responses. In vivo, LipoDDP administered after DAC pretreatment produced strong tumor suppression and also efficiently suppressed metastasis. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:343-354]

## DAC in a biomimetic nanoparticle for photoactivated pyroptosis

A second example used a biomimetic nanoparticle (`BNP`) with a `PLGA` polymeric core and a cell-membrane shell, co-loading DAC and [[indocyanine green|ICG]]. The cell-membrane shell is described as providing low immunogenicity and tumor-targeting capability, while ICG converted low-dose near-infrared irradiation into local hyperthermia at the tumor site. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:367-379]

In this formulation, tumor accumulation of BNP allowed photoactivation to pierce the tumor-cell membrane and cause a sudden increase in cytoplasmic `Ca2+`. This promoted cytochrome c release and caspase-3 activation. At the same time, DAC-upregulated GSDME enhanced caspase-3-catalyzed GSDME cleavage, which ultimately caused pyroptosis. The review adds that GSDME expression increased markedly after incubation with BNP, while after photoactivation GSDME expression decreased and GSDME N-terminal fragment levels together with caspase-3 cleavage increased, indicating activation of the pyroptosis pathway. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:379-388, cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:422-425]

## Immunologic effects

The source presents nanoparticle-triggered pyroptosis after DAC-mediated GSDME restoration as immunostimulatory. In the BNP system, `IL-6` and `TNF-alpha` secretion by bone-marrow-derived dendritic cells increased, and `CD86` and `CD11c` surface expression also increased, supporting immune activation after photoactivated pyroptosis. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:426-430]

In vivo, this BNP plus photoactivation strategy efficiently suppressed both primary and distant tumors, with no obvious tissue injury or weight loss reported. The source further notes higher proportions of `CD4+` and `CD8+` T cells in spleens and distant tumors, as well as splenocytes that induced significant cell death and secreted high levels of `IFN-gamma`, indicating systemic T-cell activation relevant to solid-tumor immunotherapy. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:431-444]

## Broader significance

In the review’s concluding discussion, hypermethylation-driven low GSDME expression is described as a common reason that pyroptosis is difficult to initiate in tumor cells. The authors therefore highlight synergistic treatment with demethylating drugs that upregulate GSDME as a way to enhance sensitivity to chemotherapeutic agents and reduce drug resistance. Within that framework, DAC-mediated restoration of GSDME serves as a sensitizing mechanism that can make nanoparticle-triggered pyroptosis more effective and can support anti-tumor immune responses. ^[cell-death-mediated-by-the-pyroptosis-pathway-with-the-aid-of-nanotechnology-prospects-for-cancer-therapy.md:1094-1110]
