# Photopyroptosis-driven antitumor immunity without added immunoadjuvants

Photopyroptosis-driven antitumor immunity without added immunoadjuvants refers to an antitumor treatment mode in which a [[photosensitizer]] alone, after light activation, induces [[photopyroptosis]] and thereby stimulates antitumor immune responses without the use of additional immune adjuvants such as PD1-based combination therapy. In the source study, this concept is demonstrated with the synthetic molecule `1d`, which was reported to achieve in vivo immunotherapy and high tumor inhibition efficiency by itself. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:27-31, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:143-146, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:517-523]

## Overview

The source presents photopyroptosis as an emerging branch of [[photodynamic therapy]] in which light-activated photosensitizers trigger pyroptotic cell death. It further states that pyroptosis-associated inflammation can instigate potent antitumor immune responses and amplify immunotherapy effects, which motivated the development of a molecular design strategy for efficient photopyroptosis and antitumor immunotherapy. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:15-31, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:55-62]

In this study, the authors proposed a structural design principle that clamps cationic moieties such as pyridinium or imidazolium onto a photosensitive core. This design was intended to strengthen electrostatic interactions with mitochondria, increase localization to both the inner and outer mitochondrial membranes, and maximize local photogenerated reactive oxygen species to trigger [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]]-mediated pyroptosis. The representative compound `1d` was selected for mechanistic and in vivo immunotherapy studies because it could be excited by red light with deeper tissue penetration and lower energy. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:112-145, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:243-273]

## Mechanistic basis

Cellular studies in EMT-6 cells showed that `1d` localized preferentially to mitochondria, with a Pearson correlation coefficient of `0.91` in mitochondrial colocalization analyses, and HIS-SIM imaging indicated distribution at both inner and outer mitochondrial membranes. After irradiation, the `1d + L` group showed more than 16-fold higher intracellular ROS fluorescence than `1d` without irradiation, supporting the idea that concentrated mitochondrial ROS production is a dominant trigger for the downstream cell-death program. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:243-274]

The treated cells also showed features consistent with pyroptotic injury and inflammatory death signaling. The source reports increased LDH release, reduced intracellular ATP activity with increased extracellular ATP, membrane fragmentation by Bio-TEM, mitochondrial membrane potential loss, mitochondrial structural destruction, cellular contents outflow, and upregulation of HMGB-1 in the `1d + L` group. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:286-339]

At the protein level, the control and dark `1d` groups showed no significant difference in caspase-3 and GSDME, whereas the `1d + L` group showed high caspase-3 levels and obvious GSDME-N expression, which the authors interpreted as activation of pyroptosis. The study therefore frames the pathway as a photoproduced ROS-caspase-3-GSDME route linking light activation to pyroptotic tumor-cell death. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:99-101, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:340-347]

## High-efficiency photopyroptosis

The source emphasizes that this system was designed for unusually efficient photopyroptosis. It reports that EMT-6, Hepa1-6, and MCF-7 tumor cells developed bubbling and rupture under `640 nm` light irradiation, and that swelling with membrane bubbling could begin after only `10 s` of CLSM laser exposure. The authors further state that this corresponds to a low light dosage of `0.4 J cm−2`, much lower than values reported in related work. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:23-26, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:356-380, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:536-550]

Flow-cytometry analysis with annexin V and propidium iodide showed that the pyroptosis proportion of EMT-6 cells increased continuously after irradiation, with the majority of cells reaching the annexin V+/PI+ state within `5 h`. The study also reports that nearly all observed cells displayed bubbling under CLSM laser conditions and that pyroptosis could still be triggered at reduced power density, although with longer irradiation time. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:360-380]

## Immune activation

The study links photopyroptosis to immune activation through both molecular and tissue-level readouts. It notes that HMGB-1, described as an immune-related key factor, can be released during photopyroptosis, and transcriptome analysis comparing control, `1d`, and `1d + L` samples showed significant upregulation of immune-related genes in the irradiated group. KEGG enrichment further associated the differentially expressed genes with immune pathways including the `IL-17`, `TNF`, and `NOD-like receptor` signaling pathways. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:335-339, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:402-429]

In vivo, blood analysis after `1d + light` treatment showed significant increases in white blood cells, granulocytes, lymphocytes, and monocytes, which the authors interpreted as an enhanced inflammatory response. Flow cytometry of EMT-6 tumors further showed that proportions of both `CD4+` and `CD8+` T cells were significantly higher in the `1d + L` group than in control or dark-treatment groups. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:499-510]

Tumor-section analyses were consistent with both pyroptosis and cytotoxic immune engagement. Immunofluorescence showed cleaved caspase-3 and GSDME N-terminal signals in tumors from the `1d + L` group, while immunohistochemistry showed overexpression of `CD8` and `Granzyme B` compared with the other groups. The source interprets these findings as evidence that photopyroptosis substantially awakened the immune system and enabled strong tumor immunotherapy efficacy. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:510-519]

## In vivo antitumor efficacy without added immunoadjuvants

For in vivo evaluation, EMT-6 tumor-bearing mice were divided into control, `1d`, and `1d + L` groups. Fluorescence imaging indicated that `1d` persisted in tumor tissue with higher fluorescence than in major organs, and body-weight tracking showed nearly no change during treatment. H&E staining of major organs also showed no significant histopathological abnormalities, supporting the reported biological safety of the treatment conditions. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:430-460, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:483-490]

Under these conditions, tumors in the `1d + L` group were strongly restrained, and by day 16 the EMT-6 tumor inhibition rate reached `79.5%`. In a second in vivo model using Hepa1-6 tumors, the `1d + L` group also showed significant inhibition or elimination of tumors, with a reported inhibition rate of `88.7%`. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:491-498, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:524-535]

A central point of the report is that these immune and antitumor effects were achieved using the synthetic photosensitizer alone, without other immunoadjuvants. The authors explicitly note that no auxiliary immunoadjuvant such as `PD1` was required and that adding PD1 produced only slight further improvement in the combined-treatment comparison they cite. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:517-523]

## Significance in the source

Within the source, this concept is presented as evidence that rationally designed mitochondrial-targeted photosensitizers can couple highly efficient [[photodynamic therapy]] to immune-relevant [[pyroptosis]] and produce meaningful antitumor immunity without added immune adjuvants. The authors conclude that the strategy enables efficient photopyroptosis, strong therapeutic efficacy through sole use of the photosensitizer, and a potentially important advance for immunotherapy-oriented PDT design. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:537-554]
