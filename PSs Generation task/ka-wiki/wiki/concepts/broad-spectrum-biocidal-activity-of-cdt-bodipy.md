# Broad-spectrum biocidal activity of CDT-BODIPY

[[CDT-BODIPY]] showed broad-spectrum biocidal activity in the reported study by effectively suppressing the growth of three different pathogenic microorganisms: [[Escherichia coli|E. coli]], [[Staphylococcus aureus|S. aureus]], and [[Candida albicans|C. albicans]]. The paper presents this activity as part of a single-wavelength-triggered antimicrobial strategy that combines [[photodynamic therapy]] and [[photothermal therapy]]. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:25-29, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:127-130, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:607-624]

## Overview

The study describes CDT-BODIPY as a water-soluble D-A-D conjugated oligomer built from two cyclopentadithiophene (CDT) donor units and a [[BODIPY]] acceptor. Its antimicrobial performance was linked to light-triggered generation of reactive oxygen species and moderate photothermal heating under 660 nm irradiation, together with fluorescence properties that support imaging-guided use. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:16-29, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:115-130, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:613-617]

The source also states that CDT-BODIPY contains quaternary ammonium side chains that improve aqueous solubility and promote affinity for negatively charged microbial surfaces. This binding behavior was considered important because it places the photoactive material close to microbial membranes, where generated ROS and heat can act more effectively. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:303-306, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:519-536]

## Target microorganisms

Broad-spectrum activity was demonstrated against representatives of Gram-negative bacteria, Gram-positive bacteria, and fungi: E. coli, S. aureus, and C. albicans, respectively. The paper notes that these organisms have different cell-wall structures, yet all displayed strong association with CDT-BODIPY in fluorescence imaging experiments. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:26-27, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:510-528, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:618-623]

Under confocal laser scanning microscopy, both blue and red fluorescence channels produced strong signals that overlapped well with all three microorganisms. The authors interpreted this as evidence that CDT-BODIPY could bind efficiently to the microbial cells, aided by electrostatic attraction and hydrophobic interactions. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:521-543]

## Antimicrobial activity under light

The antibacterial and antifungal assays were performed by incubating microbial suspensions with CDT-BODIPY, exposing them to a 660 nm laser at `0.3 W/cm^2` for 5 minutes, diluting the suspensions, and counting colony-forming units after plating. Under these light-irradiation conditions, the growth of all three microorganisms was inhibited effectively. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:251-259, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:544-550, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:575-583]

According to the reported results, more than 95% of microbes were killed even at a CDT-BODIPY concentration of `4 ug/mL` under 660 nm irradiation, and inhibition increased to 99% at `10 ug/mL`. The study therefore estimated the minimal bactericidal concentration for E. coli, S. aureus, and C. albicans to be about `10 ug/mL`. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:575-583]

## Dark toxicity

The source reports that microbial viability also declined with increasing CDT-BODIPY concentration in the dark, showing that some growth inhibition occurred without irradiation. For E. coli and S. aureus, this dark toxicity was moderate and caused less than 50% bacterial death; the authors attributed it mainly to the intrinsic antimicrobial effect of the quaternary ammonium groups. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:551-560]

In C. albicans, dark toxicity was stronger: nearly 90% of growth was inhibited at `10 ug/mL` CDT-BODIPY. The paper suggests that this difference may be related to the distinct composition and structure of fungal cell walls. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:570-574]

## Basis of the biocidal effect

CDT-BODIPY generated ROS under 660 nm irradiation, with the source identifying [[singlet oxygen]] and superoxide radical as the main species detected. The authors state that this ROS profile is consistent with participation of both type I and type II photodynamic processes. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:399-454, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:616-617]

The compound also showed moderate photothermal behavior in water. At `0.3 mg/mL`, irradiation with a 660 nm laser at `1.5 W/cm^2` increased temperature by `18 degrees C` after 12 minutes, and the photothermal conversion efficiency was calculated as 33.1%. The paper states that the observed temperature rise was sufficient to kill bacteria. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:455-476]

Based on microscopy and activity data, the authors propose that CDT-BODIPY first binds microbial surfaces with high affinity and then, upon irradiation, damages cells through a combined effect of quaternary ammonium-associated membrane disruption, local hyperthermia, and toxic ROS. SEM images after treatment showed deformed cells, collapsed membranes, and holes on microbial surfaces, supporting a membrane-damaging mechanism. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:584-605]

## Imaging-guided aspect

CDT-BODIPY exhibited dual fluorescence emissions at 463 nm and 730 nm in water, and the study highlights these signals as useful for locating microorganisms during treatment. The red emission was noted as advantageous for avoiding biological background fluorescence, while blue and red confocal channels both visualized the bound material on microbial cells. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:350-398, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:521-543, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:611-613]

## Conclusion

In this study, broad-spectrum biocidal activity refers to the ability of CDT-BODIPY to inhibit or kill E. coli, S. aureus, and C. albicans under a single 660 nm light source. The reported effect depended on a synchronous PDT/PTT mechanism, supported by microbial binding, ROS generation, photothermal heating, and observed structural damage to the treated cells. ^[intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:124-130, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:575-605, intramolecular-charge-transfer-based-conjugated-oligomer-with-fluorescence-efficient-photodynamics-and-photothermal-activities.md:607-624]
