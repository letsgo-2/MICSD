# Canonical inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis pathway

The canonical inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis pathway is one of the principal pathways leading to [[pyroptosis]]. In this pathway, inflammatory caspase-1 activates the execution machinery centered on [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], producing a lytic and pro-inflammatory form of programmed cell death. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:212-219, pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:242-267]

Pyroptosis is characterized by pore formation in the plasma membrane, cytoplasmic swelling, chromatin condensation, and eventual osmotic lysis. More broadly, pyroptotic death is mediated by gasdermin family proteins and is associated with release of intracellular contents, including inflammatory mediators such as [[IL-1beta]] and [[IL-18]], making it a pro-inflammatory cell-death process. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:107-120, pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:175-197]

## Initiation

In the canonical pathway, recognition of [[damage-associated molecular patterns|DAMPs]] such as fibrinogen, heat shock proteins, and DNA, and/or [[pathogen-associated molecular patterns|PAMPs]] such as flagellin, glycans, and lipopolysaccharides, by [[pattern recognition receptors|PRRs]] triggers activation of cytosolic signaling complexes called [[inflammasomes]]. These inflammasomes are typically composed of a sensor protein, an adaptor, and an effector caspase. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:242-250]

A variety of PRRs participate in this upstream sensing context, including NOD-like receptors and toll-like receptors, but only a subset is described as directly assembling inflammasomes and activating caspase-1. The sensors named in the source for this subset are [[NLRP1 inflammasome|NLRP1]], [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3]], NLRP4, [[AIM2 inflammasome|AIM2]], and Pyrin. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:250-258]

## Inflammasome assembly and caspase-1 activation

After activation, most of these sensor proteins interact with the adaptor protein [[ASC]] (apoptosis-associated speck-like protein containing CARD). ASC promotes caspase-1 activation through recruitment and cleavage of procaspase-1. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:258-262]

Caspase-1 is the defining inflammatory caspase of the canonical pathway. The source contrasts this pathway with the non-canonical route, which instead relies on caspase-4 and caspase-5 in humans or caspase-11 in mice. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:217-219, pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:268-286]

## Execution by GSDMD

Once activated, caspase-1 cleaves autoinhibited [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] at its linker region, freeing the lethal N-terminal fragment. More generally for gasdermins, cleavage removes C-terminal autoinhibition, allowing the N-terminal domain to translocate to membranes, oligomerize, and form beta-barrel transmembrane pores. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:224-239, pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:263-267, pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:299-305]

These GSDMD-derived membrane pores disrupt osmotic balance, promote plasma-membrane rupture, and enable release of intracellular inflammatory contents. This pore-forming step is the direct effector mechanism that produces pyroptotic cell lysis. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:236-239, pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:299-304]

## Cytokine maturation and release

In addition to activating GSDMD, caspase-1 matures pro-[[IL-1beta]] and pro-[[IL-18]] into IL-1beta and IL-18. These cytokines are then released through the membrane pores formed by GSDMD-N. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:263-267]

The source also describes pyroptosis more generally as a process that releases DAMPs together with inflammatory cytokines, thereby initiating or perpetuating inflammatory responses after cell rupture. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:190-197]

## Functional significance

Pyroptosis is presented as a defense mechanism that principally helps protect against pathogens by releasing immunogenic cellular contents and inflammatory cytokines. In the canonical inflammasome pathway, this defense function is coupled to PRR-driven inflammasome activation and caspase-1-dependent execution. ^[pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:33-45, pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:190-197, pyroptosis-at-the-forefront-of-anticancer-immunity.md:242-267]
