# AgNP15-induced ATF6 degradation links ER stress to NLRP3 inflammasome activation

`AgNP15-induced ATF6 degradation links ER stress to [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation` describes the central finding of a study in human monocytes showing that 15 nm silver nanoparticles (`AgNP15`) triggered an atypical [[endoplasmic reticulum stress|ER stress]] response marked by rapid [[ATF6]] degradation and, at higher concentrations, coincident activation of the NLRP3 inflammasome and [[pyroptosis]]. The authors present this as a new mechanistic connection between ER stress signaling and inflammasome activation. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:15-18, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:26-32, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:47-50]

## Study context

The study examined how `AgNP15` affect human THP-1 monocytes, with follow-up experiments in primary human monocytes and macrophages. The paper frames the work around prior evidence that some nanoparticles can induce ER stress and cytotoxicity, while noting that the cellular and molecular toxicity mechanisms of silver nanoparticles in humans were still incompletely understood. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:19-26, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:51-63, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:175-199]

The authors distinguish among the three canonical ER stress sensors, [[PERK]], [[IRE1]], and ATF6, and focus on ATF6 because it normally responds to unfolded proteins by moving to the Golgi and being cleaved by Site-1 and Site-2 proteases to generate an active transcription factor. Against that background, the study tested whether silver nanoparticle exposure altered these pathways in ways that could help explain inflammatory cell death. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:64-82, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:613-621]

## ER stress response to AgNP15

`AgNP15` produced concentration-dependent ER stress signatures in THP-1 cells. After 24 hours, lower concentrations activated the PERK pathway and induced HSP-70 synthesis, whereas `25 ug/ml` caused a different pattern that included HSP-90 degradation and loss of ATF6 protein. IRE-1 phosphorylation decreased rather than increasing, and GRP-78 levels were largely unchanged under the tested conditions. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:358-385]

A key observation was that ATF6 degradation occurred rapidly. At `25 ug/ml`, ATF6 loss was already evident after 1 hour, alongside degradation of other components such as HSP-70, while total IRE-1 and HSP-90 remained relatively stable at that early time point. The paper interprets these findings as evidence that `AgNP15` induce ER stress-related events with unusually rapid effects on the ATF6 sensor. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:372-385]

## Link to pyroptosis and inflammasome activation

At higher concentration, the ER stress phenotype coincided with a rapid, non-apoptotic cell-death program. After 1 hour of treatment with `25 ug/ml AgNP15`, THP-1 cells became annexin-V- and propidium iodide-positive, unlike staurosporine-treated cells at the same time point, and `AgNP15` processed caspase-4 and caspase-7 but not caspase-3. The study therefore concluded that the cell death induced by `AgNP15` was distinct from apoptosis. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:386-397, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:425-456]

The same `25 ug/ml` condition also produced hallmark features of inflammasome-driven pyroptosis. The study detected the caspase-1 p20 fragment, increased caspase-1 activity, strong secretion of `IL-1beta` in LPS-primed cells, and formation of ASC-containing pyroptosomes in about 20% of cells. Together, these observations were used to identify `AgNP15` as an inducer of inflammasome activation and pyroptotic death in human monocytes. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:485-540, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:751-760]

## Evidence for NLRP3 dependence

The paper directly tested whether the response involved the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]]. In THP-1 cells, siRNA-mediated reduction of NLRP3 expression significantly decreased `AgNP15`-induced `IL-1beta` secretion, supporting a requirement for NLRP3 in the inflammatory response. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:541-555]

The response was also dependent on [[caspase-1]]. Pharmacologic inhibition with `YVAD-CHO` completely abolished `IL-1beta` secretion after `AgNP15` stimulation, confirming that the downstream output of the pathway required caspase-1 activity. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:571-579]

## ATF6 as the mechanistic link

The central mechanistic experiment used inhibitors of Site-1 protease and Site-2 protease, the enzymes involved in ATF6 cleavage. The Site-2 protease inhibitor `1,10-phenanthroline` strongly prevented ATF6 processing and appearance of the cleaved fragment, while the Site-1 protease inhibitor `PF-429242` had a weaker effect. Tunicamycin was used as a positive control and similarly induced ATF6 processing, but over a longer time course. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:613-631]

ATF6 processing correlated with caspase-1 processing and activity, `IL-1beta` secretion, and the proportion of propidium iodide-positive cells. Importantly, blocking ATF6 processing, especially with the Site-2 protease inhibitor, reduced caspase-1 activity, reduced `IL-1beta` release, and lowered the percentage of PI-positive cells. The authors interpret these results to mean that ATF6 degradation is crucial for `AgNP15`-induced pyroptosis and acts as the link between ER stress and NLRP3 inflammasome activation. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:632-642, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:693-720, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:823-834]

## Role of caspase-4

The study further examined [[caspase-4]] using caspase-4-deficient THP-1 cells. Loss of caspase-4 markedly impaired expression of pro-`IL-1beta`, even after LPS priming, but did not prevent caspase-1 cleavage. In those deficient cells, `AgNP15` instead produced higher caspase-1 activity, more PI-positive cells, and more pyroptosome formation, while `IL-1beta` secretion was reduced relative to control cells. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:580-612]

Based on these results, the authors conclude that caspase-4 is important for the priming step of the NLRP3 inflammasome, particularly pro-`IL-1beta` synthesis, but may negatively regulate the extent of inflammasome activation after `AgNP15` exposure. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:609-612, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:858-886]

## Cell-type differences

When the authors compared THP-1 cells with primary human monocytes and macrophages, they found different basal expression patterns for pro-`IL-1beta` and NLRP3. Primary monocytes showed basal pro-`IL-1beta` expression and could secrete significant `IL-1beta` in response to `AgNP15` without LPS priming, whereas THP-1 cells and macrophages required priming for mature `IL-1beta` secretion. In all three cell types, however, LPS priming followed by `AgNP15` produced the strongest response. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:643-679, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:761-776]

## Significance

The study presents `AgNP15` as a model in which nanoparticle-induced ER stress, specifically rapid alteration of ATF6, is mechanistically tied to inflammasome activation and pyroptotic inflammatory death. The authors emphasize that this was, to their knowledge, the first report implicating ATF6 in activation of the NLRP3 inflammasome, and they frame the finding as relevant to understanding the toxicology of silver nanoparticles used in medical and other applications. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:16-18, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:47-50, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:685-720, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:786-797]
