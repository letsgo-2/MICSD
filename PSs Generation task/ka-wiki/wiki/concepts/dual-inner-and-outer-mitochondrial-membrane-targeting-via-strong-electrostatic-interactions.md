# Dual-inner-and-outer mitochondrial membrane targeting via strong electrostatic interactions

Dual-inner-and-outer mitochondrial membrane targeting via strong electrostatic interactions is a molecular design concept presented for photosensitizers that induce [[photopyroptosis]]. In the source, the design uses cationic moieties such as pyridinium or imidazolium clamped onto a photosensitive core so the molecules can sufficiently reach both the inner and outer mitochondrial membranes through strong electrostatic interactions. This targeting is proposed to maximize photogenerated reactive oxygen species at the mitochondrial site and thereby promote [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]]-mediated pyroptosis. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:19-25, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:115-122]

## Design principle

The reported structural principle combines a photosensitive core with cationic peripheral groups to create sufficient mitochondrial targeting. The authors state that strong electrostatic interactions are crucial for this targeting, and describe the resulting structural factors as enabling recognition of both the inner and outer mitochondrial membranes together with centralized mitochondrial ROS photoproduction. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:107-120, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:539-547]

The source presents compounds `1a-1d` as conforming to this design and several comparison compounds as references. According to the authors, compounds with high electrostatic interaction strength strengthened mitochondrial targeting and facilitated possible photopyroptosis, whereas comparison compounds lacking cationic periphery or having weaker electrostatic interaction showed insufficient targeting or less pyroptosis. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:203-214, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:235-242]

## Electrostatic basis

To characterize electrostatic interaction strength, the study measured binding constants between the compounds and ATP as a standard anionic model. The source reports `K1a = 2.6 x 10^5 M^-1`, `K1b = 3.7 x 10^5 M^-1`, `K1c = 2.4 x 10^6 M^-1`, and `K1d = 7.4 x 10^5 M^-1`, while compound `4`, described as having relatively weaker electrostatic interaction, showed `K4 = 3.3 x 10^4 M^-1`. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:215-234]

The source links these differences to molecular features. For example, amide groups in `1a` were said to enhance electron withdrawal of the pyridinium salts and improve electrostatic interaction, while the ester-containing comparison compound `4` had lower charge and weaker interaction. Imidazole cations in `1b` and multiple pyridinium modifications in `1c` and `1d` were likewise described as supporting strong electrostatic interaction. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:215-234]

## Evidence for dual-membrane mitochondrial localization

In cellular colocalization studies with `1d`, the mitochondrial Pearson correlation coefficient was `0.91`, higher than results from other organelle studies, which the authors interpret as demonstrating mitochondrial targeting ability. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:243-251]

The source then reports high-resolution HIS-SIM imaging showing that `1d` simultaneously located at both the inner and outer mitochondrial membranes. The authors describe this as superior to commercial mitochondrial probes that mostly target cristae, and use it as direct evidence for dual-membrane recognition. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:252-259]

## Functional consequence

After establishing mitochondrial localization, the study reports that irradiated `1d` produced strong intracellular ROS signals, with the `1d + light` group showing more than 16-fold fluorescence intensity relative to `1d` without irradiation in a DCFH-DA assay. The authors argue that substantial targeting to both mitochondrial membranes concentrates ROS photoproduction at mitochondria, which benefits subsequent pyroptosis triggering. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:260-279]

The broader mechanistic model in the source is that centralized mitochondrial ROS activates a `ROS-caspase-3-GSDME` pathway leading to photopyroptosis. Consistent with that model, the irradiated `1d` group showed cell membrane fragmentation, mitochondrial damage, increased `HMGB-1`, high caspase-3, and obvious `GSDME-N` expression. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:99-101, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:323-345]

## Role in the reported photopyroptosis strategy

Within the study, dual targeting of the inner and outer mitochondrial membranes is part of a general design strategy for highly efficient [[mitochondrial-targeted photodynamic therapy]]. The reported outcome was a photopyroptotic trigger within `10 s` of CLSM laser irradiation at a light dosage of `0.4 J cm^-2`, which the authors describe as highly efficient relative to prior work. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:23-26, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:356-380, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:537-549]

The source further places this design in an immunotherapy context. Photopyroptosis induced by the representative compound `1d` was associated with immune-response-related transcriptomic changes, increased inflammatory blood-cell counts, increased tumor-infiltrating `CD4+` and `CD8+` T cells, and strong in vivo tumor inhibition without added immunoadjuvants. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:402-429, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:491-523]
