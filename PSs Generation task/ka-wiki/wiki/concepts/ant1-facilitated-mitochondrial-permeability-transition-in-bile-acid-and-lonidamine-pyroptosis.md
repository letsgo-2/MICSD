# ANT1-facilitated mitochondrial permeability transition in bile-acid and lonidamine pyroptosis

ANT1-facilitated mitochondrial permeability transition refers to the role of [[ANT1]] in enabling persistent [[mitochondrial permeability transition|mitochondrial permeability transition (MPT)]] that leads to pyroptotic cell death in response to bile acids and [[lonidamine]]. In this study, bile acids and the ANT1-targeting agent lonidamine induced MPT, which then promoted [[caspase-4]]/[[caspase-11]] activation, [[caspase-3]] cleavage, and [[Gasdermin E|GSDME]]-dependent pyroptosis rather than the LPS-driven [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] pathway. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:58-66, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:133-140, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:317-346]

## Overview

The source identifies MPT as a central upstream event in sterile pyroptosis triggered by bile acids, calcium overload, or ANT activators. In this framework, ANT1 does not merely correlate with cell death; the data support it as an important facilitator of MPT that enables downstream assembly of the [[Apaf-1 pyroptosome]], activation of caspase-4/11, and execution of pyroptosis through caspase-3 and GSDME. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:58-66, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:133-140, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:677-688]

## Bile-acid-triggered MPT and pyroptosis

In macrophages, deoxycholic acid and chenodeoxycholic acid activated caspase-11, promoted HMGB1 and IL-1alpha release, and caused pyroptotic morphology and PI positivity. This bile-acid-induced cell death was independent of canonical [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] signaling and caspase-1, but was strongly reduced in Caspase-11-deficient cells, supporting a caspase-4/11-dependent pyroptotic program. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:145-190]

The execution phase of bile-acid pyroptosis depended on GSDME rather than GSDMD. Although bile acids activated caspase-11, they did not generate the pore-forming GSDMD p30 fragment. Instead, bile acids induced caspase-3 activation and GSDME cleavage to p30, and GSDME-deficient macrophages as well as CASPASE-3- or GSDME-silenced HepG2 cells were resistant to bile-acid-induced pyroptosis. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:191-222]

Mechanistically, bile acids did not directly bind recombinant caspase-4/11 and did not directly activate these caspases in a cell-free system, indicating that a cell-intrinsic intermediate was required. The study then showed that bile-acid treatment induced MPT, marked by loss of mitochondrial cristae, calcein-AM/CoCl2 evidence of pore opening, persistent dissipation of mitochondrial membrane potential, and impaired inner mitochondrial membrane integrity. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:223-240, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:268-276]

Among tested modulators of MPT pore components, the ANT inhibitor [[bongkrekic acid]] (BKA) was the key protective intervention. BKA significantly reduced bile-acid-induced MPT, cell death, LDH release, and activation of caspase-11/4, caspase-3, and GSDME, whereas cyclosporin A and N-ethylmaleimide did not prevent this pyroptosis. The authors therefore conclude that bile-acid-induced pyroptosis may be initiated by ANT-facilitated MPT. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:276-302]

The study also links ANT-mediated MPT to rapid ATP redistribution. Bile-acid-induced MPT was accompanied by an immediate increase in cytoplasmic ATP within 30 minutes, followed by decline below basal levels after 60 minutes, consistent with impaired mitochondrial ATP generation after MPT. The Discussion proposes that ANT1, as an adenine nucleotide translocator, may facilitate the immediate release of ATP from mitochondria to cytoplasm, a step considered pivotal for pyroptosome assembly. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:293-302, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:682-688]

## Lonidamine as an ANT1-linked trigger

The paper identifies [[lonidamine]] as an adjuvant anticancer agent targeting ANT1. In macrophages, lonidamine induced persistent MPT and pyroptotic cell death, with activation of caspase-11 and caspase-3, GSDME cleavage, and increased IL-1alpha and LDH release. These effects occurred in an ANT1-dependent manner. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:317-340]

Parallel experiments in HepG2 cells showed that both lonidamine and bile acid induced pyroptotic death that was largely abolished by BKA pretreatment and by ANT1 silencing. ANT1 knockdown also reduced activation of caspase-4, caspase-3, and GSDME under these conditions. These results support ANT1 as a shared upstream facilitator for both bile-acid- and lonidamine-triggered MPT pyroptosis. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:339-346, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:429-437]

## Link to the [[apaf-1-pyroptosome|Apaf-1 pyroptosome]]

The study places ANT1-facilitated MPT upstream of [[Apaf-1]]-dependent signaling. Under MPT conditions, Apaf-1 specifically interacted with caspase-4/11 rather than functioning through caspase-9, and APAF-1 knockout prevented bile-acid- and lonidamine-induced activation of caspase-4, caspase-3, and GSDME as well as pyroptotic cell death. The authors term this MPT-induced signaling platform the [[Apaf-1 pyroptosome]]. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:347-367, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:392-467]

More broadly, the paper distinguishes MPT-driven pyroptosis from MOMP-driven apoptosis. High-dose bile acids and lonidamine favored Apaf-1/caspase-4 pyroptosome assembly, whereas apoptotic conditions favored Apaf-1/caspase-9 apoptosome assembly. The authors further report that MPT caused earlier cytoplasmic cytochrome c release and immediate ATP release, and that caspase-4 recruitment to Apaf-1 required a higher ATP level than caspase-9 recruitment. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:522-610]

## Pathophysiologic relevance

In a bile-duct ligation model of cholestatic liver injury, mice showed ANT1 upregulation, persistent MPT, formation of the Apaf-1/caspase-11 pyroptosome complex, caspase-11 activation, and cleavage of caspase-3 and GSDME in liver tissue. In primary mouse hepatocytes, bile acids likewise induced MPT, caspase-11 activation, and GSDME cleavage, all of which were blocked by BKA pretreatment. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:613-646, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:691-699]

The authors conclude that although ANT may not be an indispensable structural component of the permeability transition pore, their results support ANT1 as an important facilitator of bile-acid-induced MPT and the downstream pyroptotic program. They further suggest that defining MPT-driven cell death as caspase-4-sensed pyroptosis may be relevant to diseases involving sterile mitochondrial injury, including cholestatic liver failure and ischemic tissue damage. ^[apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:675-688, apaf-1-pyroptosome-senses-mitochondrial-permeability-transition.md:749-760]
