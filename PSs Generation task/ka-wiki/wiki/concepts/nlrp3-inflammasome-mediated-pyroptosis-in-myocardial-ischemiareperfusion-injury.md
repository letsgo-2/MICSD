# NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis in myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury

## Overview

[[Myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury|Myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury (MIRI)]] is described as a serious condition that occurs with blood reperfusion and reoxygenation after an ischemic episode. It can increase myocardial infarct size and worsen cardiac dysfunction, and the source identifies cardiomyocyte death, oxidative damage, mitochondrial dysfunction, and aberrant immunoinflammatory responses as major features of its pathophysiology. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:75-87]

Within this context, [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3 inflammasome]]-mediated [[Pyroptosis]] is presented as an important mechanism in MIRI. The source states that emerging evidence widely implicates NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis in MIRI pathophysiology and notes that anti-inflammatory strategies, especially those targeting NLRP3 inflammasome inactivation, have gained attention as a possible approach to ameliorate MIRI. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:109-117]

## Inflammasome and pyroptosis framework

The source describes NLR proteins including [[NLRP1 inflammasome|NLRP1]], [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3]], and [[NLRC4 inflammasome|NLRC4]] as intracellular sensors of pathogen and danger signals that function as innate immune effectors. These proteins can recruit [[ASC|apoptosis-associated speck-like protein containing CARD]] and procaspase-1 to assemble inflammasomes, and the activated inflammasome can then induce active caspase-1. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:88-96]

Pyroptosis is defined in the source as an inflammatory form of programmed cell death characterized by plasma membrane pore formation, release of [[IL-1β]] and [[IL-18]], and cell lysis. In the discussion, pyroptosis is further characterized by cleavage of caspases and gasdermins, membrane pore formation and rupture, and discharge of proinflammatory cytokines, with the NLRP3 inflammasome playing a pivotal role. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:109-112, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:513-522]

During pyroptosis, inflammatory caspases including [[Caspase-1]] cleave and activate [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], which is responsible for forming membrane pores. In the study, cleaved caspase-1, cleaved GSDMD, and cleaved IL-1β are treated as downstream effectors of active NLRP3 inflammasome signaling in myocardial tissue and cardiomyocytes. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:374-382, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:519-522]

## Evidence in MIRI models

The study investigated this process in two models: rats subjected to in vivo MIRI by left anterior descending coronary artery ligation for 0.5 hours followed by 4 hours of reperfusion, and neonatal rat cardiomyocytes subjected to [[oxygen-glucose deprivation/restoration|OGD/R]]. These systems were used to examine myocardial injury, inflammatory mediators, pyroptosis, and proteins related to NLRP3 inflammasome activation. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:33-44, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:258-272]

In rat hearts after MIRI, myocardial levels of [[TNF-α]], IL-1β, and IL-18 increased, indicating inflammation after reperfusion injury. Western blotting also showed higher myocardial NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1, and cleaved IL-1β in MIRI rats than in sham-operated rats, which the authors interpret as evidence that activated NLRP3 inflammasome signaling is heavily involved in MIRI development. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:365-383]

The rat model also showed a marked increase in TUNEL-positive cardiomyocytes, elevated cleavage of caspase-1 and GSDMD, and higher caspase-1 activity. Based on these findings, the source concludes that pyroptosis is widely implicated in MIRI in this model. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:384-416]

In OGD/R-injured neonatal rat cardiomyocytes, protein levels of NLRP3 together with active fragments of caspase-1, IL-1β, and GSDMD were notably increased compared with normal cardiomyocytes, indicating NLRP3 inflammasome activation during OGD/R insult. OGD/R exposure was also associated with reduced cell viability, increased cell death, increased TUNEL positivity, and loss of mitochondrial membrane potential. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:417-427, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:430-436]

## Signaling context

The source links NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis in cardiomyocytes to the [[Akt/GSK3β/NF-κB pathway]]. In OGD/R-injured cardiomyocytes, NF-κB p65 phosphorylation increased, while phosphorylation of GSK3β at Ser9 and Akt at Ser473 decreased. The discussion states that activated Akt phosphorylates GSK3β at Ser9 and thereby deactivates it, while inactive GSK3β prevents NF-κB p65 Ser536 phosphorylation and activation; active NF-κB then promotes expression of NLRP3 inflammasome-associated modules such as NLRP3, pro-IL-1β, and pro-IL-18. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:437-453, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:548-569]

The schematic summary in the source further states that, following MIRI insults, [[Toll-like receptors]] activate NF-κB and prime the NLRP3 inflammasome. In this framework, NLRP3 inflammasome activation sits downstream of inflammatory signaling and upstream of caspase-1, GSDMD cleavage, cytokine maturation, and pyroptotic injury. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:522-525]

## Therapeutic relevance in the source study

The paper evaluates [[Aesculin|aesculin (AES)]] as a modulator of this pathway. In both rat MIRI and cardiomyocyte OGD/R models, AES reduced inflammatory mediators, suppressed NLRP3 inflammasome activation, and decreased markers associated with pyroptosis, including active caspase-1, cleaved GSDMD, cleaved IL-1β, caspase-1 activity, and TUNEL-positive cardiomyocytes. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:45-50, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:365-416, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:430-436]

AES also attenuated broader injury outcomes in the study, including reperfusion arrhythmias, hemodynamic dysfunction, infarct size, myocardial injury biomarkers, histopathologic damage, loss of cardiomyocyte viability, and mitochondrial membrane potential impairment. The authors conclude that AES protected against MIRI by suppressing NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis and that this effect was associated with increased Akt activation and disruption of GSK3β/NF-κB signaling. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:45-50, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:318-364, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:571-575]

Support for the signaling interpretation came from experiments with [[MK-2206]], an allosteric Akt inhibitor. In OGD/R-treated cardiomyocytes, MK-2206 abolished AES-associated improvements in viability, cell death, TUNEL positivity, mitochondrial membrane potential, NLRP3 inflammasome suppression, and GSK3β/NF-κB pathway inhibition. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:454-475]

In this source, NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis is presented as a major inflammatory cell-death mechanism contributing to myocardial damage during ischemia/reperfusion.
