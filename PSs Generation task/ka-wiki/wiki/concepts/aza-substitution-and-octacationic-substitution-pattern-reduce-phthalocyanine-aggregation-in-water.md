# Aza substitution and octacationic substitution pattern reduce phthalocyanine aggregation in water

The study reports that, among water-soluble phthalocyanine derivatives, aza substitution in the macrocycle and substitution at nonperipheral positions both reduced aggregation in water. The authors also state that having eight cationic substituents was essential for monomerization in water, while tetrasubstituted analogs with the same cationic group remained significantly aggregated. ^[jm5014852-114.md:69-95, jm5014852-114.md:154-187]

## Overview

The paper examined novel zinc, magnesium, and metal-free octasubstituted phthalocyanines bearing triethylammonioethylsulfanyl groups, along with an aza analogue. The goal was to develop water-soluble phthalocyanines and assess how macrocycle substitution pattern affected absorption, aggregation, and photodynamic behavior. ^[jm5014852-114.md:19-34, jm5014852-114.md:63-75]

## Aggregation behavior in water

In DMF, the phthalocyanine derivatives showed sharp monomer-like absorption bands. In water, the nonperipheral compounds `2Zn-Et` and `3Zn-Et` retained spectra similar to DMF and were described as essentially nonaggregated, while `1Zn-Et` showed spectral changes typical of H-aggregates. Fluorescence quantum yields also supported aggregation for `1Zn-Et` and `1Mg-Et`. ^[jm5014852-114.md:124-127, jm5014852-114.md:154-177]

The authors interpret these results to mean that aza substitution in the macrocycle and shifting substituents to nonperipheral positions both help reduce aggregation in water. They further conclude that eight charged substituents are required for monomerization under these conditions, since related tetrasubstituted compounds still aggregated regardless of substitution position. ^[jm5014852-114.md:177-187]

## Structural context

The target compounds were designed with eight cationic charges attached either at peripheral or nonperipheral positions, and the study also included an aza analogue previously reported active against Hep2 cells. The source notes that substitution position and aza substitution substantially influence the main absorption band. ^[jm5014852-114.md:69-95]
