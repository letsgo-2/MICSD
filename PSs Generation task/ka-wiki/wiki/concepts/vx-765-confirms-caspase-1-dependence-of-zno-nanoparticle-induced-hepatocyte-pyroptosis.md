# VX-765 confirms caspase-1 dependence of ZnO nanoparticle-induced hepatocyte pyroptosis

VX-765 was used in this study to test whether ZnO nanoparticle-induced hepatocyte pyroptosis depended on caspase-1. The authors report that, after VX-765 pretreatment, cleaved caspase-1 and GSDMD-NT were reduced, mature IL-1beta and IL-18 secretion fell, membrane perforation and LDH leakage were diminished, TUNEL positivity decreased, and ZnO NPs-induced cell death was prevented, supporting a caspase-1-dependent canonical pyroptosis mechanism. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:645-667]

## Evidence

The paper states that caspase-1 is the key inflammatory caspase distinguishing pyroptosis from apoptosis, and that VX-765 was selected to define its importance in ZnO NPs-triggered pyroptosis. At a 10 uM inhibitory dose, VX-765 downregulated cleaved caspase-1 and GSDMD-NT while leaving GSDMD-FL largely unchanged. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:647-654]

With VX-765, mature IL-1beta and IL-18 secretion was also reduced. The treated HepG2 cells showed more intact membranes under SEM, less LDH leakage, fewer AV-PI double-positive cells, lower TUNEL-positive rates, and reduced ASC/caspase-1 colocalization. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:654-665]

## Interpretation

These findings indicate that ZnO NPs-induced hepatocyte pyroptosis proceeds through the canonical caspase-1 pathway rather than an unrelated death route. The authors summarize this as caspase-1-dependent hepatocyte pyroptosis that can be alleviated by VX-765. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:666-667, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:1130-1133]
