# D1 phototherapy generates systemic antitumor immunity with distant-tumor suppression and memory T-cell responses in bilateral 4T1 tumors

In a bilateral [[4T1]] mouse tumor model, local treatment with the membrane-targeted photosensitive dimer `D1` plus light (`D1+L`) was reported to generate a systemic antitumor immune response that affected both the treated primary tumor and the untreated distant tumor. The study frames this effect as pyroptosis-mediated [[photoimmunotherapy]], in which local [[photodynamic therapy]] and [[photothermal therapy]] cooperate to induce immunogenic tumor-cell death and broader antitumor immunity. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:141-169, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:494-509]

## Experimental context

The in vivo antitumor and immune studies used BALB/c mice bearing 4T1 tumors. For the systemic-immunity experiment, the authors established a bilateral axillary 4T1 tumor model and monitored both the treated primary tumor and the untreated distant tumor every two days after therapy to assess whether local `D1` phototherapy produced a systemic effect. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:465-483, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:494-503]

`D1` was delivered by intratumoral injection, and fluorescence imaging showed retention at the tumor site 24 hours later. Upon irradiation of the `D1`-accumulated tumor, the tumor temperature rose by about `65 °C` within 1 minute and remained near that level for the next 5 minutes, consistent with a strong in vivo photothermal effect alongside the compound’s reported photodynamic activity. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:467-483]

## Distant-tumor suppression

In the bilateral model, the study reports that the primary tumor in the `D1+L` group completely regressed within 7 days. The untreated distant tumor was also reported to be completely inhibited, which the authors interpret as evidence that local `D1` phototherapy elicited systemic antitumor immunity rather than only a local cytotoxic effect. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:499-509]

By day 14, measurements of tumor weight and volume showed stronger suppression in the `D1+L` group than in the comparison groups shown in the study, including the monomer control `M1+L`. The paper states that `D1` effectively inhibited distant tumors through systemic immunity via photoimmunotherapy, whereas `M1+L` lacked the same degree of systemic immune activation. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:505-509, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:552-557, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:565-573]

## Immune-cell responses

To probe mechanism, the authors analyzed immune markers in tumors by [[flow cytometry]]. In primary tumors on day 5 of treatment, `D1+L` increased expression of `CD103` on dendritic cells, which the paper interprets as evidence of increased mature dendritic-cell presence in the tumor microenvironment. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:537-546, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:582-583]

The same analysis showed high `CD4` and `CD8` T-cell signals in the `D1+L` group, indicating induction of both helper and effector T-cell responses. The paper further states that `D1+L` had a greater ability to activate effector T cells than `M1+L`. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:559-565, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:582-584]

In the bilateral model, the proportion of `CD8+` T cells reached `8.37%` in the primary tumors after `D1` photodynamic therapy and `22.1%` in the distant tumors. The study presents the higher `CD8+` infiltration in distant tumors as a key sign that in situ treatment of the primary tumor activated a systemic immune response. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:566-573, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:582-585]

## Memory T-cell responses

The study also examined `CD44+/CD62L-` expression on `CD3+CD8+` T cells in distant tumors as a marker of memory T-cell activation. This population was highly expressed in the `D1+L` treatment group and reached `22.4%`, which the authors cite as evidence of immune-memory formation after therapy. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:574-576, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:584-585, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:593-607]

In the conclusion, the authors state that `D1`-mediated treatment produced long-term immune responses that could help prevent tumor recurrence, and they specifically connect immunogenic cell death with dendritic-cell maturation, T-cell clonal expansion, adaptive antitumor effects, and obvious immune memory. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:608-631]

## Cytokine evidence for systemic immunity

Serum [[cytokine]] measurements by [[ELISA]] showed that the `D1+L` group had significantly higher levels of proinflammatory factors than the control group, including `IL-6`, `IL-18`, `IL-1beta`, `TNF-alpha`, and `IFN-gamma`. The authors interpret this cytokine profile as evidence that `D1+L` stimulated the strongest systemic antitumor immune response among the tested groups. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:594-607, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:585-586]

## Mechanistic link to pyroptosis and immunogenic cell death

The proposed basis for the systemic effect is local tumor-cell [[pyroptosis]] and [[immunogenic cell death]]. In 4T1 cells, light-activated `D1` increased cleaved caspase-1 and cleaved `GSDMD-N`, promoted release of `LDH`, `IL-1beta`, and `IL-18`, and produced morphological features consistent with pyroptosis. The study also found increased `CRT` exposure and redistribution of `HMGB1`, supporting induction of immunogenic cell death. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:382-425, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:456-463]

The article’s overall interpretation is that `D1` generates type-I reactive oxygen species at the tumor-cell membrane and that its photothermal effect reinforces this injury, accelerating pyroptosis and release of inflammatory and antigenic signals. According to the authors, these events promote dendritic-cell maturation, T-cell activation, systemic tumor control, and immune memory in the poorly immunogenic 4T1 model. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:154-169, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:608-635]
