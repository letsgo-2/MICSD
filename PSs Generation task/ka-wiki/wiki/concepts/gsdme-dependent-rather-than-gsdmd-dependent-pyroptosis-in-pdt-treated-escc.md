# GSDME-dependent rather than GSDMD-dependent pyroptosis in PDT-treated ESCC

In [[esophageal squamous cell carcinoma]] (ESCC), the cited study reports that [[photodynamic therapy]] (PDT) induces pyroptotic cell death through a [[GSDME]]-dependent pathway rather than a [[GSDMD]]-dependent one. The authors describe PDT-treated ESCC cells as forming pyroptotic blebs, undergoing membrane rupture, and releasing intracellular contents and pro-inflammatory mediators, and they frame this process as part of the antitumor effect of PDT in ESCC.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:23-33]

## Overview

Pyroptosis is presented in the source as a gasdermin-mediated form of programmed necrotic cell death. In the canonical description summarized by the paper, gasdermin cleavage releases an N-terminal effector domain that forms pores in the plasma membrane, leading to membrane rupture and release of inflammatory mediators such as IL-18 and IL-1beta. The study focuses on how this general process is executed in PDT-treated ESCC cells.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:79-90, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:101-103]

The paper identifies a specific signaling axis in PDT-treated ESCC: PDT suppresses [[PKM2]], activates [[caspase-8]] and [[caspase-3]], and then causes GSDME cleavage, releasing N-GSDME to trigger pyroptosis. The authors summarize this as the PKM2/caspase-8/caspase-3/GSDME axis.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:28-31, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:496-498, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-590]

## Evidence for pyroptosis after PDT

After PDT, Eca109 and Ec9706 ESCC cells showed morphology consistent with pyroptosis: peripheral swelling, large membrane bubbles, nuclear pyknosis, cytoplasmic swelling, and multiple membrane pores. PDT also increased propidium iodide-positive cells, elevated LDH release, and increased TNF-alpha, IL-1alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6, and IFN-gamma, supporting membrane rupture and inflammatory mediator release. Similar findings were also observed with other photosensitizers tested in the study.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:282-309, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:316-317]

## Why it is described as GSDME-dependent rather than GSDMD-dependent

The core distinction in the paper is the differential behavior of gasdermin and caspase markers after PDT. In western blot analyses of PDT-treated Eca109 and Ec9706 cells, the authors did not detect N-GSDMD or cleaved caspase-1, but they did detect increased N-terminal GSDME and cleaved caspase-3. Based on these findings, they conclude that PDT-triggered pyroptosis in ESCC proceeds through GSDME rather than GSDMD.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:318-324, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:509-518]

The discussion explicitly contrasts this result with the traditional view of pyroptosis as GSDMD-induced cell death. The authors state that PDT increased N-GSDME and cleaved caspase-3 without affecting GSDMD or caspase-1 levels, and they interpret this as evidence that PDT induces pyroptosis in ESCC through the caspase-3/GSDME route instead of a GSDMD/caspase-1 route.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:499-518]

## Role of caspase-3 and GSDME

The study places GSDME downstream of caspase-3. In GSDME-silenced ESCC cells, PDT still activated caspase-3, but pyroptotic morphology was abrogated, cell swelling was reduced, and the characteristic large red-stained bubbles seen in control PDT-treated cells were diminished. Cell survival remained similar to vector controls, while LDH and IL-1beta release were only slightly elevated, indicating that GSDME is required for the full pyroptotic phenotype induced by PDT.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:324-355]

Pharmacologic inhibition further supported this pathway. Pretreatment with the caspase-3 inhibitor Ac-DEVD-CHO reduced the pyroptosis ratio, decreased pyroptotic-like morphology, improved CCK-8 readouts, inhibited LDH release, and reduced IL-1beta and N-GSDME. The discussion states that specific caspase-3 inhibition and GSDME downregulation completely eliminated pyroptosis in PDT-treated cells, reinforcing the interpretation that caspase-3-mediated GSDME cleavage is the operative execution step.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:385-405, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:512-518]

## Role of caspase-8 upstream

The source also places [[caspase-8]] upstream of caspase-3 in this PDT response. Expression analyses showed a relationship between GSDME, caspase-3, and caspase-8 mRNA levels in esophageal datasets, and pharmacologic inhibition of caspase-8 with Z-IETD-FMK reduced pyroptotic morphology, lowered LDH and IL-1beta release, and inhibited caspase-3 cleavage. By contrast, caspase-3 inhibition did not prevent caspase-8 cleavage, which the authors interpret as evidence that caspase-8 participates upstream in activating caspase-3 during PDT-induced pyroptosis.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:358-405, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:519-532]

## Connection to PKM2

The proposed pathway begins upstream with PKM2. The study reports that PKM2 expression is elevated in ESCC datasets and that PKM2 expression correlates with caspase-3, caspase-8, and GSDME. When PKM2 was overexpressed in ESCC cells, PDT-induced pyroptotic morphology was abrogated, swelling was reduced, cell death decreased, LDH and IL-1beta release were inhibited, and N-GSDME as well as cleaved caspase-3 and caspase-8 were detected only in the PDT-treated overexpression setting at reduced apparent effect. The authors interpret these findings as evidence that PDT induces pyroptosis by downregulating PKM2.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:406-453, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:546-549]

The paper also tested the glycolysis inhibitor [[2-DG]]. In combination with PDT, 2-DG abrogated pyroptotic morphology and inhibited LDH, IL-1beta, and N-GSDME despite slightly decreased CCK-8 viability, while having no obvious effect on PKM2 expression relative to PDT alone. The authors therefore distinguish overall cytotoxicity from the specific pyroptotic program and argue that glycolytic state influences the efficiency of PDT-induced pyroptosis.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:454-467, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:552-565]

## In vivo support

In xenograft models, PDT increased serum LDH and IL-1beta, increased cleaved caspase-3, decreased PKM2, and increased N-GSDME. These effects were reduced in GSDME-silenced, PKM2-overexpressing, and 2-DG-treated PDT groups, supporting the same PKM2/GSDME-centered pyroptosis pathway in vivo that was observed in cultured ESCC cells.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:468-489]

## Significance

Within the scope of this study, the main conclusion is that PDT-treated ESCC undergoes pyroptosis primarily through a GSDME-dependent mechanism linked to caspase-3 and caspase-8 activation, not through the GSDMD/caspase-1 pathway. The authors present this as a mechanistic explanation for part of PDT’s antitumor action in ESCC and as a basis for considering PKM2 and the Warburg effect as relevant modulators of this response.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-632]
