# Site-2 protease inhibition blocks AgNP15-triggered pyroptosis and IL-1beta release

In human THP-1 monocytes exposed to 15 nm silver nanoparticles (`AgNP15`), inhibition of [[Site-2 protease]] (`S2P`) blocked key downstream inflammatory and cell-death effects associated with [[pyroptosis]]. The study reports that preventing AgNP15-induced degradation and processing of the ER stress sensor [[ATF-6]] with Site-2 protease inhibitors completely blocked the nanoparticle effect on pyroptosis and secretion of [[IL-1beta]]. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:26-50]

## Study context

The source investigated how `AgNP15` affects human THP-1 monocytes and found that higher concentrations produced an atypical [[endoplasmic reticulum stress|ER stress]] response associated with rapid ATF-6 degradation, activation of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]], and pyroptotic cell death. At `25 ug/ml`, AgNP15 induced caspase-1 processing and activity, ASC pyroptosome formation, loss of membrane integrity, and IL-1beta secretion, linking the nanoparticle response to inflammasome-driven pyroptosis rather than classical apoptosis. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:27-32, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:132-137, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:485-579]

## Role of Site-2 protease inhibition

To test whether ATF-6 processing was involved in inflammasome activation, the authors used inhibitors of the two proteases that cleave ATF-6: `PF-429242` for [[Site-1 protease]] and `1,10-phenanthroline` for Site-2 protease. The S2P inhibitor strongly inhibited processing of the control substrate `SREBP-1` and drastically prevented AgNP15-induced ATF-6 processing and appearance of the cleaved ATF-6 fragment, whereas the S1P inhibitor reduced ATF-6 processing more modestly. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:613-629]

This blockade of ATF-6 processing was accompanied by reduced inflammasome-associated outputs. ATF-6 processing correlated with increased caspase-1 processing and activity, IL-1beta secretion, and the percentage of PI-positive cells, and treatment with the S2P inhibitor reduced caspase-1 activity, IL-1beta release, and PI positivity. The paper therefore links Site-2 protease-dependent ATF-6 processing to AgNP15-induced pyroptotic cell death. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:629-642]

## Interpretation

The authors interpret these findings as evidence that ATF-6 functions as an upstream control point connecting AgNP15-triggered ER stress to [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation. In the discussion, they state that inhibition of S2P, and to a lesser extent S1P, blocked both ATF-6 processing and inflammasome activation, and they describe ATF-6 as a possible molecular switch for induction of the NLRP3 inflammasome under high-dose AgNP15 exposure. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:693-720]

The same discussion further states that inhibiting ATF-6 processing reduced caspase-1 activity, IL-1beta secretion, and the proportion of PI-positive cells, supporting the conclusion that ATF-6 degradation is related to activation of the NLRP3 inflammasome and pyroptotic cell death rather than apoptotic death. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:823-835]
