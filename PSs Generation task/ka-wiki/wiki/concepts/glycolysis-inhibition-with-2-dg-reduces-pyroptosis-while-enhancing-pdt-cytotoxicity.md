# Glycolysis inhibition with 2-DG reduces pyroptosis while enhancing PDT cytotoxicity

In esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC), the study examined how inhibiting [[glycolysis]] with [[2-Deoxy-D-glucose|2-DG]] affects [[photodynamic therapy|PDT]]-induced [[pyroptosis]]. The authors found that although PDT alone induces GSDME-mediated pyroptosis, adding 2-DG reduced pyroptotic features while slightly increasing overall cytotoxicity. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:26-31, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:454-467]

## Context

PDT in this study induced pyroptosis in ESCC through a pathway involving PKM2 downregulation, caspase-8 activation, caspase-3 activation, and GSDME cleavage. Pyroptosis was associated with characteristic membrane blebbing, LDH release, and release of inflammatory mediators such as IL-1beta. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:28-31, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:296-309, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:496-498]

The paper also linked GSDME expression with the glycolytic pathway in ESCC by gene set enrichment analysis, which motivated testing 2-DG, a glycolysis inhibitor, together with PDT. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:455-459, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:606-607]

## Key findings

When 2-DG was combined with PDT, phase-contrast microscopy showed that pyroptotic morphology was abrogated in Eca109 and Ec9706 cells. In the same combination group, LDH release, IL-1beta release, and N-GSDME levels were inhibited relative to PDT alone, indicating reduced pyroptotic activity. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:459-465, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:605-609]

At the same time, CCK-8 results were slightly decreased in the 2-DG/PDT group compared with the other groups, which the authors interpreted as enhanced cytotoxicity despite reduced pyroptosis. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:462-463, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:552-553]

The combined treatment did not show an obvious effect on PKM2 expression compared with PDT alone, both in cultured cells and in the animal model. In vivo, serum LDH and IL-1beta in the 2-DG plus PDT group were lower than in the PDT-only group, activated caspase-3 was also lower, and N-GSDME expression was reduced. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:465-467, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:476-489]

## Interpretation

The authors concluded that 2-DG enhanced PDT cytotoxicity but inhibited PDT-mediated pyroptosis. They proposed that pyroptosis may be an energy-intensive process linked to the [[Warburg effect]], so glycolysis inhibition by 2-DG may limit the energy supply needed for pyroptosis without materially altering PKM2 expression in this setting. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:552-565]

More broadly, the study presents the effect of 2-DG as evidence that glycolytic state can shape the form of PDT-induced cell death, not just its magnitude. The authors further described the 2-DG and PDT combination as showing significant synergistic inhibition of pyroptosis in PDT. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:584-586, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:594-596]
