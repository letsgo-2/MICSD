# Transcriptional repression of glutathione metabolism by {BiW8}

`{BiW8}` was reported to alter glutathione metabolism in MG63 osteosarcoma cells. In the study, RNA sequencing and qRT-PCR showed that genes involved in glutathione metabolism were downregulated, including `GPX1`, `GPX8`, `GSTM1`, and `GSTT2B`. The authors concluded that `{BiW8}` inhibited expression of glutathione-metabolism-related genes, contributing to decreased intracellular GSH levels and increased ROS levels in treated cells. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:449-452, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:458-464, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:476-482, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:517-527]

## Evidence

The transcriptome analysis found that nearly all genes enriched in the KEGG pathway annotation for glutathione metabolism came from the downregulated gene set. The paper specifically listed 13 downregulated glutathione-metabolism genes, including glutathione peroxidases and glutathione S-transferases. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:447-452, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:458-482]

In the authors’ summary model, `{BiW8}` reduced GSH levels through a combined effect of transcriptional repression and chemical reaction, which in turn increased ROS levels in MG63 cells. The same model places glutathione-metabolism repression alongside pyroptosis activation and DNA repair inhibition as part of the compound’s antitumor mechanism. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:502-527, biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:553-564]
