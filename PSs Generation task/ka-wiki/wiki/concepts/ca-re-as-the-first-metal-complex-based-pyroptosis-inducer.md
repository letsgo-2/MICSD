# CA-Re as the first metal complex-based pyroptosis inducer

**CA-Re** is described in the source as a carbonic anhydrase IX ([[CAIX]])-anchored rhenium(I) photosensitizer that, under light irradiation, induces gasdermin D ([[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]])-mediated [[pyroptosis]] and enhances anti-tumor immunity. The study explicitly states, both in the abstract and conclusion, that CA-Re is the first reported metal complex-based pyroptosis inducer. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:17-30, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:626-645]

## Overview

The work positions CA-Re as a response to two limitations of conventional [[photodynamic therapy]] (PDT): reduced efficacy in hypoxic tumors and the tendency of PDT to induce apoptosis, which is described here as poorly immunogenic. The authors instead aimed for a photosensitizer that would remain effective under hypoxia and trigger a more immunogenic death program, identifying pyroptosis as a suitable mechanism because it is proinflammatory, depends on GSDMD cleavage by inflammatory caspases, disrupts membrane integrity, and promotes rapid release of inflammatory cytokines such as IL-1beta and IL-18. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:41-86]

CA-Re was designed as a CAIX-anchored rhenium(I) photosensitizer that stays associated with the cell membrane over prolonged culture and even after irradiation-induced membrane damage. In the study’s proposed mechanism, this membrane anchoring supports in situ ROS generation, strong lipid peroxidation, membrane rupture, and ultimately GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis together with rapid release of inflammatory cytokines and damage-associated molecular patterns (DAMPs), which then promote dendritic-cell maturation and T-cell-dependent adaptive immunity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:115-132]

## Why CA-Re was notable

The source states that pyroptosis induction offers a new direction for designing highly immunogenic photosensitizers, but also notes that existing pyroptosis inducers were mostly chemotherapeutic drugs and that photosensitizers capable of inducing pyroptosis had rarely been reported. Against that background, the authors present CA-Re as the first metal complex-based example. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:77-86, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:626-629]

The study also emphasizes that CA-Re combined pyroptosis induction with strong hypoxia-tolerant phototoxicity. In MDA-MB-231 cells, dark toxicity was reported as greater than 100 uM, while light-activated toxicity remained in the nanomolar range under both normoxia and hypoxia; under 525 nm irradiation, hypoxic cells showed a light IC50 of 190 nM, and the conclusion highlights a hypoxic light IC50 of 20 nM under the tested conditions. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:267-288, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:307-327, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:405-419, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:627-633]

## Design and membrane anchoring

CA-Re incorporates a benzene sulfonamide-containing ligand intended to bind the zinc active center of CAIX. The paper reports molecular docking in which the sulfonamide moiety enters the CAIX pocket and coordinates the Zn2+ center, with a binding energy comparable to acetazolamide. In cell imaging experiments, CA-Re was observed on the membrane rather than in the cytoplasm after 3 hours and 24 hours of incubation, and colocalization with the membrane dye DiI was used to verify membrane localization. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:134-152, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:217-236]

Further support for CAIX targeting came from enzyme inhibition, western blot, and uptake data. CA-Re inhibited CAIX activity in the in vitro assay, reduced CAIX and HIF-1alpha expression in hypoxic MDA-MB-231 cells, showed higher cellular rhenium content under hypoxia than normoxia, and its uptake was reduced by pretreatment with acetazolamide. The authors interpret these findings as evidence that CA-Re binds CAIX and thereby anchors to the membrane in living cells. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:237-266]

## Evidence for pyroptosis induction

The source distinguishes CA-Re PDT-induced cell death from apoptosis based on cell morphology. Real-time confocal observation showed no apoptotic shrinkage, nuclear fragmentation, or apoptotic bodies; instead, cells formed large plasma-membrane bubbles, became swollen, retained intact nuclei, and often remained attached to the culture plate. Scanning electron microscopy showed flattened cytoplasm with intact nuclei, and the authors state that these features resembled pyroptosis. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:378-399]

Biochemical evidence was provided by western blot analysis. Upon 425 nm irradiation, CA-Re-treated cells showed increased expression of GSDMD-N and cleaved caspase-1, indicating GSDMD cleavage and caspase-1 activation. The study also found downregulation of [[GPX4]], which the authors suggest may reflect exhaustion of lipid-peroxidation defense and may positively regulate pyroptosis in this system. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:397-435]

The paper further links pyroptosis to membrane rupture. CA-Re remained associated with the membrane even after photodamage, allowing the loss of membrane integrity to be visually tracked, and CAIX/HIF-1alpha levels decreased in a light-dose-dependent manner after treatment. The authors interpret this as evidence that membrane rupture and GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis act together to increase tumor immunogenicity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:436-450]

## ROS and lipid peroxidation context

CA-Re generated ROS through both type-I and type-II PDT pathways according to the photophysical experiments. The source reports relatively low singlet-oxygen yields but detection of hydroxyl radical-associated HPF fluorescence and superoxide-associated EPR signals, supporting mixed ROS production. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:183-216]

In cells, ROS-scavenger experiments indicated that superoxide and lipid peroxidation accounted for most of the PDT-induced oxidative signal, whereas singlet oxygen and hydroxyl radical contributed less. Additional intracellular assays showed increased superoxide indicator fluorescence and dose-dependent elevation of malondialdehyde, which the study used as evidence of lipid peroxidation. The authors connect this to CA-Re’s membrane localization, proposing that oxidation of membrane phospholipids contributes to cytotoxicity and pyroptosis-related membrane damage. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:328-377]

## Immunogenic consequences

The study assessed three hallmarks of enhanced immunogenicity after CA-Re plus light treatment: extracellular ATP secretion, cell-surface calreticulin exposure, and HMGB1 translocation from nucleus to cytoplasm. All three changed in the expected direction after irradiation, and ATP release was reported as concentration-dependent and stronger than that induced by hypericin at a much higher concentration. The authors conclude that pyroptotic cells generated by CA-Re PDT had high immunogenicity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:451-514]

In 4T1 cells, the study reports similar immunogenicity-associated changes, including ecto-CRT activation, HMGB1 translocation, ATP secretion, and dose-dependent changes in CAIX, HIF-1alpha, GPX4, GSDMD-N, and cleaved caspase-1 after CA-Re PDT. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:515-535]

## In vivo anti-tumor immunity

In a bilateral 4T1 tumor model in immunocompetent BALB/c mice, only the primary tumor received intratumoral CA-Re and 525 nm irradiation. CA-Re plus light inhibited primary-tumor growth by 78% after 16 days and also inhibited untreated distant-tumor growth by 73%, which the authors interpret as an abscopal effect consistent with systemic anti-tumor immunity. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:536-575]

Immune profiling showed increased maturation of dendritic cells in tumor-draining lymph nodes, with CD80+CD86+ DCs rising from 16.0% to 28.4% in the CA-Re plus light group. The same treatment increased serum TNF-alpha, IL-6, and IL-12p70, decreased IL-10, and raised the proportions of CD3+CD8+ and CD3+CD4+ T cells in distant tumors to about 28.3% and 32.3%, respectively. Immunohistochemistry also showed enhanced CD8 infiltration in both primary and distant tumors, while IL-18 and IL-1beta levels increased, supporting pyroptosis-associated inflammatory signaling in vivo. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:576-615]

This in vivo immune program connects CA-Re-induced pyroptosis to broader anti-tumor effects, including [[Abscopal anti-tumor immunity in a bilateral 4T1 mouse model]]. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:515-615]

## Safety observations

The source reports that CA-Re showed negligible dark toxicity in cell assays and did not produce obvious systemic toxicity in the mouse study. Body weights remained stable during treatment, and H&E staining showed no obvious pathological abnormalities in the liver, heart, lung, kidney, or spleen. ^[a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:560-562, a-carbonic-anhydrase-ix-caixanchored-rheniumi-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-for-enhanced-antitumor-immunity.md:616-625]
