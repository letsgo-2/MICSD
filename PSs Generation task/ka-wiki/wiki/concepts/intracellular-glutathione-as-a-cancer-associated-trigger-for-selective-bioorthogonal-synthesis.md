I’m extracting the claim set from the source lines and will keep the page narrowly tied to what the paper actually says: the GSH trigger, MOF-199 collapse, Cu(I)-enabled click chemistry, selectivity, and the in vitro/in vivo readouts.# Intracellular glutathione as a cancer-associated trigger for selective bioorthogonal synthesis

Intracellular glutathione (GSH) is described in this study as the cancer-associated trigger that enables selective in situ synthesis of a mitochondria-targeting aggregation-induced-emission photosensitizer. The authors propose that elevated GSH in cancer cells reduces Cu(II) in MOF-199 to Cu(I), while also collapsing the nanoparticle carrier to release precursor molecules that then undergo an intracellular click reaction to form TPATrzPy-3+. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:145-150, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:156-175, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:610-619]

## Mechanism

The system uses MOF-199 as both a precursor carrier and a Cu(I) catalyst source. According to the source, GSH in cancerous cells reduces the Cu(II) nodes of MOF-199 to Cu(I), which catalyzes the click reaction between the two inert precursors, TPA-alkyne-2+ and MePy-N3, to generate TPATrzPy-3+ inside cells. The paper also states that GSH exposure causes PMOF to lose crystallinity and collapse, releasing the loaded precursors and copper ions nearly simultaneously. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:268-277, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:307-318, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:350-364]

The selectivity is tied to the higher intracellular GSH level in cancer cells. In the study, HeLa cells contained much more GSH than 3T3 cells, and this difference was used to support the interpretation that GSH functions as a cancer-cell-specific trigger for PMOF NPs. BSO pretreatment lowered the intracellular GSH level and suppressed TPATrzPy-3+ formation. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:390-417, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:491-498]

## Experimental evidence

The authors showed that PMOF NPs were stable in biological media but responded to GSH by generating the desired product signal at around 595 nm, matching TPATrzPy-3+. In HeLa cells, intracellular product formation was tracked by HPLC, and the reported synthesis yield reached 70.5% at 24 h, with completion described by 16 h post-incubation. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:312-364, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:431-460]

Confocal imaging showed that TPATrzPy-3+ signal appeared in PMOF-NP-treated HeLa cells but was almost absent in precursor-treated HeLa cells, PMOF-NP-treated 3T3 cells, precursor-treated 3T3 cells, and BSO-pretreated HeLa cells. The study interprets these patterns as evidence that intracellular GSH activates selective bioorthogonal synthesis in cancer cells. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:401-417]

## Functional outcome

Once synthesized, TPATrzPy-3+ localized to mitochondria and supported light-activated cancer-cell killing. In vitro, PMOF NPs showed selective light toxicity toward HeLa cells over 3T3 cells, while BSO pretreatment raised the PMOF NP IC50 in HeLa cells to above 50 μM. The source also reports low dark toxicity for the tested materials. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:465-498]

The same trigger mechanism was extended in vivo in zebrafish tumor models. After intravenous injection, PMOF NPs produced tumor-localized TPATrzPy-3+ signal, reduced tumor volume after photodynamic treatment, and showed lower unspecific light toxicity than control formulations that already contained the synthesized photosensitizer. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:507-590]
