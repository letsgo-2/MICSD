# Sodium bicarbonate nanoparticles (NaHCO3 NPs) as drug-free pyroptosis and metabolism immunoadjuvants

`Sodium bicarbonate nanoparticles (NaHCO3 NPs)` are described in the source as a simple, drug-free inorganic nanomaterial developed for amplified cancer [[immunotherapy]]. The study presents them as dual-function immunoadjuvants that both induce [[pyroptosis]] and regulate [[lactic acid metabolism]] in the [[tumor microenvironment|tumor microenvironment (TME)]], with the goal of overcoming low immune responses and tumor-associated immunosuppression. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:53-72, sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:119-147]

## Rationale

The source frames two major barriers to effective tumor immunotherapy: an immunosuppressive TME driven in part by lactate accumulation, and the weak immune stimulation associated with conventional apoptosis-dominant tumor therapies. It states that tumor-derived lactic acid contributes to angiogenesis, invasion, metastasis, and suppression of immune functions including dendritic-cell maturation, cytotoxic T-cell activity, and support for regulatory T cells, while pyroptosis is presented as an inflammatory form of programmed cell death that releases abundant antigens and [[damage-associated molecular patterns|DAMPs]] and can more effectively initiate anti-tumor immune responses. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:74-118]

## Preparation and material properties

The nanoparticles were prepared by a fast microemulsion method at room temperature using sodium oleate and ammonium hydrogen carbonate, and the resulting product was later modified with `DSPE-PEG` to improve dispersibility and biocompatibility. Structural characterization in the study showed spherical particles of about `70 nm`, with HRTEM, XRD, and XPS data supporting identification as `NaHCO3`. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:152-187]

The source reports that aqueous dispersions of NaHCO3 NPs were weakly alkaline, with pH rising from `7.83` to `8.76` across `12.5-400 ug/mL`, and that the particles released `Na+` rapidly in buffer, reaching about `85%` release within `24 h` at both `pH 7.4` and `pH 6.5`. These features are presented as the basis for their two main functions: acid-base neutralization and intracellular ion delivery. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:172-187]

## In vitro tumor-cell effects

In `4T1` breast cancer cells, NaHCO3 NPs showed concentration-dependent cytotoxicity, whereas free sodium bicarbonate had negligible killing effects. At `400 ug/mL`, 4T1 viability fell below `10%` with a reported `IC50` of `168 ug/mL`; by contrast, toxicity in `L929` fibroblasts was lower, with an `IC50` above `400 ug/mL`, which the authors interpret as a degree of tumor selectivity. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:202-239]

Mechanistically, the study reports that NaHCO3 NPs increased intracellular `Na+`, raised intracellular osmolarity, and enhanced [[reactive oxygen species|ROS]]. These changes were associated with morphological and ultrastructural features consistent with pyroptosis, including cellular swelling with large bubbles, cytomembrane rupture, cellular cavitation, and mitochondrial swelling and cavitation. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:240-279]

The reported pyroptosis pathway was `caspase-1/GSDMD`-dependent. Immunofluorescence and western blot analyses showed increased cleaved `caspase-1`, and the `GSDMD-N` fragment was upregulated, while the treatment also caused strong release of `LDH` and `IL-1beta`, consistent with membrane pore formation and inflammatory cell death. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:279-302]

## Immunogenic cell death and DAMP release

Beyond pyroptosis, the study states that NaHCO3 NPs also induced [[immunogenic cell death|ICD]]. In the nanoparticle-treated group, extracellular `ATP` increased by about `5.9-fold` versus control and `5.5-fold` versus free sodium bicarbonate, and calreticulin (`CRT`) exposure was also detected. The authors describe these signals as DAMPs that can promote antigen presentation and contribute to anti-tumor immune responses. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:319-332]

## Regulation of lactic acid metabolism

The nanoparticles were also studied as local alkalizing agents for the tumor milieu. In 4T1 cells, intracellular pH-probe measurements showed reduced fluorescence after NaHCO3 NP treatment, indicating increased intracellular pH, while extracellular measurements showed that the nanoparticles maintained medium pH better than free sodium bicarbonate over time. After `24 h`, the pH in the NP group was `7.00`, compared with `6.77` in the free sodium bicarbonate group and `6.74` in the control group. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:333-364]

The source further reports that lactic acid levels in culture medium were dramatically reduced in the NaHCO3 NP group. This is presented as evidence that the particles have potential to regulate lactic acid metabolism and to mitigate acidification associated with tumor-cell glycolysis. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:364-368]

## In vivo immune effects

In a `4T1` tumor-bearing `BALB/c` mouse model, the study evaluated immune-cell populations after three treatments. NaHCO3 NPs produced the highest dendritic-cell maturation in tumor-draining lymph nodes and increased intratumoral `CD4+` and `CD8+` T-cell infiltration; the reported proportions of `CD4+` and `CD8+` T cells in tumors reached `38.5%` and `1.3%`, respectively, exceeding control levels by more than `1.6-fold` and `2.3-fold`. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:382-415]

The treatment also shifted the tumor immune context away from immunosuppression. Flow-cytometry results showed macrophage polarization from `M2` tumor-associated macrophages toward `M1` macrophages, with `M2` decreasing from `26.0%` to `15.5%` and `M1` increasing from `11.2%` to `24.7%`, while frequencies of [[myeloid-derived suppressor cell|MDSCs]] and [[regulatory T cell|Treg]] cells also decreased. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:416-433]

## Antitumor and antimetastatic activity

In the primary tumor model, NaHCO3 NPs significantly inhibited tumor growth, whereas control and free sodium bicarbonate groups showed rapid progression. The study also reports no obvious body-weight loss, major-organ damage, or hemolytic reaction after nanoparticle treatment, and tumor histology showed severe tumor-cell death together with the highest `CD8+` T-cell infiltration among groups. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:447-464]

The authors further tested a distal tumor model and a lung-metastasis model. In the distal model, growth of untreated secondary tumors was significantly inhibited in mice receiving NaHCO3 NPs, which the paper interprets as evidence of effective systemic immunotherapy. In the metastasis experiment, the nanoparticle group showed the fewest pulmonary metastatic lesions. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:465-490]

## Interpretation

The paper concludes that NaHCO3 NPs act as drug-free pyroptosis and metabolism immunoadjuvants by combining two linked effects: pyroptosis/ICD-mediated immune activation and acid-neutralization-mediated relief of immunosuppression. In the authors’ summary, this combined strategy yielded the strongest immunopotentiation, marked by higher frequencies of `CD8` T cells, mature dendritic cells, and `M1` macrophages together with lower levels of `M2` macrophages, `MDSCs`, and `Treg` cells, and it corresponded with inhibition of primary tumors, distal tumors, and metastasis. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:491-506]
