# MRI tumor signal time-course comparison with gadobutrol

This page compares the tumor MRI signal time course reported for [[Gd-PNPs]] and [[gadobutrol]] across six post-administration time points in a CT 26 tumor-bearing mouse model. The source provides exact tumor MRI signal values for each agent at 0 h, 1 h, 3 h, 6 h, 9 h, and 12 h, with `n=3` for both groups. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:130-170]

The underlying tumor model was established by subcutaneous injection of CT 26 cells into the backs of Balb/c mice, and imaging-related tumor measurements were reported once tumors reached about 150-200 mm3. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:67-72]

For [[Gd-PNPs]], the tumor MRI signal increased from baseline at 0 h to a higher level at 1 h, reached its highest reported values at 3 h, and then declined at 6 h, 9 h, and 12 h while remaining above baseline in the listed replicates. The three reported values were 376.5, 391.1, and 289.3 at 0 h; 600.5, 668.3, and 552.1 at 1 h; 1242.9, 1367.9, and 1172.8 at 3 h; 985.1, 1012.2, and 905.2 at 6 h; 767.4, 820.9, and 710.1 at 9 h; and 358.1, 310.8, and 250.4 at 12 h. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:130-151]

For [[gadobutrol]], the tumor MRI signal also rose above baseline early, but its highest reported values occurred at 1 h rather than 3 h, followed by lower values at 3 h, 6 h, and 9 h, with a modest rise again at 12 h relative to 9 h in the listed replicates. The three reported values were 303.6, 387.6, and 303 at 0 h; 812.2, 924.6, and 938.1 at 1 h; 433.8, 549.1, and 551.9 at 3 h; 358.2, 465.7, and 457.8 at 6 h; 322.7, 440.1, and 454.4 at 9 h; and 418.2, 513.8, and 535.1 at 12 h. ^[2021-chen-gadolinium-porphyrin-polymer-nanotheranostics-for-imaging-guided-pdt-si.md:152-170]

In direct comparison, the reported Gd-PNP tumor signal peaked later and at a higher magnitude than gadobutrol, whereas gadobutrol showed its strongest reported enhancement at the earliest post-baseline measured time point after 0 h. Uncited
