# Aesculin suppresses NLRP3 inflammasome activation and downstream pyroptosis

[[Aesculin]] (AES) was studied in rat [[myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury|myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury (MIRI)]] and in [[neonatal rat cardiomyocytes]] subjected to [[oxygen-glucose deprivation/restoration|OGD/R]] to determine whether it affects [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation and related [[pyroptosis]]. The study reports that AES reduced NLRP3 inflammasome activation and improved injury-related outcomes in both models. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:33-50, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:71-73]

## Experimental context

The in vivo model used rats subjected to 0.5 h left anterior descending coronary artery ligation followed by 4 h reperfusion, with AES administered intraperitoneally at 10 or 30 mg/kg after reperfusion. The in vitro model used neonatal rat cardiomyocytes exposed to 120 min oxygen-glucose deprivation followed by 4 h restoration, with AES added at 1, 3, or 10 uM at the start of restoration. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:36-44, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:155-165, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:264-272]

The authors evaluated inflammatory mediators, [[caspase-1]] activity, TUNEL staining, and western blot markers related to inflammasome activation and pyroptosis, including NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1, cleaved [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], and cleaved IL-1beta. They also examined signaling changes in the [[Akt]], [[GSK3beta]], and [[NF-kappaB|NF-kB]] pathway. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:40-44, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:204-216, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:284-307]

## Effects on NLRP3 inflammasome activation

In rat myocardium after MIRI, AES lowered inflammatory mediator levels and reduced molecular markers associated with NLRP3 inflammasome activation. Compared with untreated MIRI rats, the 30 mg/kg AES group showed lower myocardial IL-1beta, IL-18, and TNFalpha, along with decreased NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1, and cleaved IL-1beta; the 10 mg/kg AES group also showed a decrease in IL-18 and cleaved caspase-1. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:365-383]

In OGD/R-injured neonatal rat cardiomyocytes, AES at 3 and 10 uM reduced NLRP3 and the active fragments of caspase-1, IL-1beta, and GSDMD relative to OGD/R plus vehicle. The source interprets these changes as suppression of NLRP3 inflammasome activation during OGD/R insult. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:428-436]

## Effects on downstream pyroptosis

The study links AES-mediated inflammasome suppression to reduced downstream pyroptosis. In rats with MIRI, AES decreased caspase-1 activity, reduced cleaved GSDMD, and lowered the proportion of TUNEL-positive cardiomyocytes, with the strongest reported effects at 30 mg/kg. The paper describes these findings as weakening NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis during MIRI. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:384-416]

In cultured cardiomyocytes, OGD/R reduced cell viability, increased cell death, increased TUNEL-positive cells, and decreased mitochondrial membrane potential. AES at 3 and 10 uM improved these injury readouts, and in parallel reduced cleaved caspase-1 and cleaved GSDMD together with NLRP3-associated signaling markers. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:417-427, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:428-436]

## Proposed signaling mechanism

The study proposes that AES suppresses NLRP3 inflammasome activation through the [[Akt/GSK3beta/NF-kappaB pathway]]. In OGD/R-treated cardiomyocytes, AES increased phosphorylation of Akt at Ser473 and GSK3beta at Ser9, while decreasing phosphorylation of NF-kappaB p65 at Ser536. The authors interpret this pattern as Akt activation, GSK3beta inhibition, and suppression of NF-kappaB signaling, which in turn disrupts NLRP3 inflammasome activation. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:437-453, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:548-569]

Pharmacologic inhibition of Akt with MK-2206 reversed the AES-associated benefits in OGD/R-treated cardiomyocytes. In the presence of MK-2206, AES no longer preserved cell viability and mitochondrial membrane potential to the same extent, and its suppression of NLRP3 inflammasome activation and the GSK3beta/NF-kappaB pathway was abolished. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:454-475]

## Relationship to cardioprotection

Alongside its effects on inflammasome signaling and pyroptosis, AES improved several markers of cardiac injury in the rat MIRI model. Reported benefits included reduced reperfusion arrhythmias, improved hemodynamic parameters, smaller infarct size, lower serum AST, LDH, and cTnT, and less severe histopathological damage and leukocyte infiltration. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:318-364]

The authors conclude that AES protected cardiomyocytes against MIRI by suppressing NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis, and that this effect may depend on increased Akt activation with disruption of the GSK3beta/NF-kappaB pathway. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:71-73, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:570-575]
