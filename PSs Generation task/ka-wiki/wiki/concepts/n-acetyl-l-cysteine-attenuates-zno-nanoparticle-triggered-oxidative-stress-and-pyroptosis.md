# N-acetyl-L-cysteine attenuates ZnO nanoparticle-triggered oxidative stress and pyroptosis

[[N-acetyl-L-cysteine]] (NAC) was used in this study as an antioxidant intervention to test whether reducing oxidative stress could protect hepatocytes from injury caused by [[zinc oxide nanoparticles|ZnO nanoparticles]]. In the authors’ HepG2 model, NAC was applied to reverse ZnO NP-induced oxidative stress and to assess whether this would also lessen activation of the canonical [[pyroptosis]] pathway. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:189-208, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:668-672]

## Context

The paper reports that ZnO NPs disrupted zinc homeostasis, induced oxidative stress in rat liver and HepG2 cells, and triggered canonical pyroptosis marked by assembly of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3-ASC-Caspase-1 inflammasome]], activation of [[caspase-1]], cleavage of [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], and release of IL-1beta and IL-18. Against that background, NAC was evaluated as a protective antioxidant strategy. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:65-72, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:200-208, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:605-641]

## Experimental setting

For the NAC intervention experiment, HepG2 cells were pre-treated with `10 mM` NAC for `1 h` and then co-treated with `20 ug/mL` ZnO NPs for `24 h`. The study evaluated ROS generation, oxidative-stress markers including MDA, CAT, and SOD, antioxidant-pathway proteins Nrf2 and HO-1, cell viability, LDH release, pyroptosis-related proteins including NLRP3, ASC, Caspase-1, and GSDMD, and GSDMD-NT fluorescence. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:1104-1110]

## Effects on oxidative stress

NAC reduced the oxidative-stress response induced by ZnO NPs. The ROS-associated green fluorescence was weaker in the NAC group, CAT and SOD levels were restored, MDA was normalized, and NAC promoted expression of the Nrf2/HO-1 pathway compared with ZnO NP treatment alone. The authors describe these changes as a reduction in oxidative stress together with enhancement of antioxidant pathways. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:670-677]

## Effects on cell injury and pyroptosis

NAC also improved injury-related and pyroptosis-related readouts in ZnO NP-exposed HepG2 cells. According to the CCK-8 assay, cell viability increased, while LDH release declined markedly. In parallel, the protein levels of NLRP3, ASC, Caspase-1, and GSDMD were significantly down-regulated, and cytoplasmic aggregation of GSDMD-NT was attenuated. The paper interprets these findings as inhibition of the pyroptosis pathway by antioxidant treatment. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:678-841]

## Interpretation

The study presents NAC as evidence that oxidative stress contributes to ZnO NP-triggered hepatocyte pyroptosis. In the authors’ interpretation, boosting antioxidant function restricted ZnO NP-induced pyroptosis and points to antioxidation as a potential protection strategy against nanoparticle-associated liver injury. ^[oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:1133-1140, oxidative-stress-related-canonical-pyroptosis-pathway-as-a-target-of-liver-toxicity-triggered-by-zinc-oxide-nanoparticles.md:1169-1180]
