# Aesculin mitigates myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury

[[Aesculin]] (AES) was investigated as a cardioprotective agent in [[myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury]] (MIRI), a condition associated with tissue damage during reperfusion after ischemia. In this study, the authors examined AES in a rat MIRI model and in [[neonatal rat cardiomyocytes]] subjected to [[oxygen-glucose deprivation/restoration]] (OGD/R) to determine whether its effects were linked to regulation of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] and [[pyroptosis]]. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:28-35, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:132-137]

## Study rationale

The source describes MIRI as a serious complication of reperfusion and reoxygenation after ischemia that can enlarge infarct size and worsen cardiac dysfunction. It further notes that aberrant immunoinflammatory responses and cardiomyocyte death are central features of MIRI, and that [[NLRP3 inflammasome]]-mediated pyroptosis has been increasingly implicated in its pathophysiology. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:75-87, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:109-117]

Aesculin is identified in the source as a hydroxycoumarin glucoside and a main effective constituent of Cortex fraxini. The paper notes that AES has reported anti-inflammatory, antioxidative, antithrombotic, and anti-apoptotic activities, but that its role in MIRI and in NLRP3 inflammasome activation in this context had been unclear before this study. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:118-131]

## Experimental models

In vivo, the study used male Sprague-Dawley rats subjected to 0.5 hours of left anterior descending coronary artery ligation followed by 4 hours of reperfusion. AES was administered intraperitoneally at 10 or 30 mg/kg at the start of reperfusion, and nifedipine 10 mg/kg was used as a positive control. The investigators assessed reperfusion arrhythmias, hemodynamic parameters, infarct size, myocardial injury biomarkers, inflammatory mediators, caspase-1 activity, histopathology, and TUNEL staining. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:33-44, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:155-216, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:230-250]

In vitro, [[neonatal rat cardiomyocytes]] were exposed to 120 minutes of oxygen-glucose deprivation followed by 4 hours of restoration. AES was added immediately before restoration at 1, 3, or 10 uM, and the Akt inhibitor MK-2206 was used at 3 uM in mechanistic experiments. The study evaluated cell viability, LDH release, TUNEL positivity, mitochondrial membrane potential, and protein markers related to the NLRP3 inflammasome, pyroptosis, and the [[Akt/GSK3beta/NF-kappaB pathway]]. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:40-44, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:258-307]

## Effects in the rat MIRI model

AES reduced reperfusion injury severity in rats. The source reports that 30 mg/kg AES improved ventricular arrhythmia scores and improved hemodynamic dysfunction, including changes in LVSP, `+dp/dtmax`, `-dp/dtmax`, and LVEDP, while 10 mg/kg AES also improved some hemodynamic measures. AES also reduced infarct size, lowered serum AST, LDH, and cTnT, and mitigated histopathological abnormalities such as edema, ruptured myocardial fibers, cytoplasmic vacuolation, and leukocyte infiltration. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:318-332, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:333-364]

The treatment also reduced myocardial inflammatory mediators. In heart homogenates from MIRI rats, AES lowered IL-1beta, IL-18, and TNFalpha, with stronger effects reported at 30 mg/kg. Western blotting further showed reduced myocardial NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1, and cleaved IL-1beta after AES treatment, consistent with suppression of NLRP3 inflammasome activation. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:365-383]

Markers associated with pyroptotic cell death were likewise decreased. The paper reports increased TUNEL-positive cardiomyocytes, elevated cleaved caspase-1 and cleaved [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], and higher caspase-1 activity in MIRI rats, while AES treatment reduced these measures, especially at 30 mg/kg. The authors interpret these findings as evidence that AES weakened NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis during MIRI. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:384-416]

## Effects in OGD/R-injured cardiomyocytes

In NRCMs, OGD/R reduced cell viability, increased cell death, increased TUNEL-positive cells, and decreased mitochondrial membrane potential. AES at 3 and 10 uM improved these injury-related readouts compared with OGD/R alone. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:417-427]

AES also reduced protein markers of inflammasome activation and pyroptosis in OGD/R-treated NRCMs. The source states that OGD/R increased NLRP3 together with active fragments of caspase-1, IL-1beta, and GSDMD, whereas AES at 3 and 10 uM distinctly reduced these levels. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:428-436]

## Proposed mechanism

The study links AES-mediated protection to modulation of the [[Akt/GSK3beta/NF-kappaB pathway]]. In OGD/R-injured NRCMs, AES increased phosphorylation of Akt at Ser473 and GSK3beta at Ser9, while decreasing phosphorylation of NF-kappaB p65 at Ser536. The source describes phosphorylated GSK3beta at Ser9 as an inactive form that prevents NF-kappaB activation and thereby limits transcription of NLRP3 inflammasome-associated components. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:437-453, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:548-569]

Mechanistic support came from Akt inhibition experiments. MK-2206 abolished AES-associated improvements in cell viability, cell death, TUNEL positivity, and mitochondrial membrane potential in OGD/R-treated NRCMs, and it also reversed AES-mediated suppression of NLRP3 inflammasome activation and GSK3beta/NF-kappaB signaling. The authors therefore conclude that AES suppresses NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis through an Akt-dependent mechanism involving disruption of GSK3beta/NF-kappaB signaling. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:454-475, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:560-569]

## Conclusion

According to the source, AES mitigated myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury in rats and reduced OGD/R-induced injury in cultured cardiomyocytes. The reported protective effects were associated with lower inflammatory mediator levels, reduced NLRP3 inflammasome activation, and decreased pyroptosis, with the study proposing Akt activation and downstream inhibition of GSK3beta/NF-kappaB signaling as the underlying mechanism. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:45-50, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:71-73, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:571-575]
