# NLRP3 inflammasome assembly controls protein nanoparticle accumulation and mechanical deformation in astrocyte pyroptosis

NLRP3 inflammasome assembly controls protein nanoparticle accumulation and mechanical deformation in astrocyte pyroptosis by linking inflammasome production to increased intracellular protein nanoparticle-induced osmotic pressure (PN-OP), higher cytoskeletal tension, and the characteristic swelling and membrane blebbing of pyroptotic astrocytes. In this study, inflammasomes are presented not only as signaling complexes in [[pyroptosis]] but also as intracellular protein nanoparticles that contribute directly to the mechanical phenotype of pyroptotic astrocytes. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:18-30, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:123-130]

## Overview

The source describes astrocytic pyroptosis as a process marked by cell swelling, membrane blebbing, and membrane permeabilization. It frames these shape changes as mechanical outcomes of cytoskeleton remodeling acting against outward osmotic pressure, and investigates whether pyroptosis-associated intracellular protein nanoparticles contribute to those deformations. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:40-43, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:84-88, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:123-130]

Using 4-hydroxytamoxifen and LPS-nigericin astrocyte pyroptosis models, the authors observed increased cytoplasmic osmotic pressure, increased counts of intracellular protein nanoparticles, elevated intermediate filament tension, and increased intracellular calcium and chloride levels during pyroptosis. These observations supported the idea that PN-OP rises during astrocyte pyroptosis and is closely associated with its mechanical features. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:281-309]

## Role of NLRP3 inflammasome assembly

To test whether inflammasome assembly contributes to PN-OP, the study knocked down [[ASC]] and pharmacologically blocked NLRP3 assembly or activation using cantharidin, SP600125, PF431396, and [[MCC950]]. Blocking ASC-dependent inflammasome assembly or inhibiting NLRP3 markedly reduced intracellular osmotic pressure, protein nanoparticle abundance, caspase-1 activity, intermediate filament tension, calcium and chloride accumulation, and membrane permeabilization in pyroptotic astrocytes. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:378-395, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:398-424]

The source further states that immunofluorescence showed increased ASC and NLRP3 inflammasome levels during pyroptosis and that MCC950 strongly reduced both osmotic pressure and nanoparticle accumulation nearly to control levels. NLRP3 inhibition also reduced the abundance of smaller cytoplasmic nanoparticles in the roughly `30-100 nm` range, supporting the interpretation that assembled inflammasomes themselves act as a major nanoparticle pool driving PN-OP. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:407-424]

## Mechanical deformation of pyroptotic astrocytes

The study links NLRP3-dependent PN-OP to the visible deformation of pyroptotic astrocytes. When inflammasome assembly was blocked, the increases in intermediate filament tension, cell swelling, membrane blebbing, and permeabilization were all reduced, indicating that inflammasome-driven nanoparticle accumulation contributes to the mechanical forces shaping pyroptotic morphology. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:384-395, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:420-427]

At the same time, the paper distinguishes deformation from permeabilization. Stabilizing microfilaments and microtubules partly lowered osmotic pressure, protein nanoparticle counts, ion accumulation, and blebbing, but did not significantly reduce propidium iodide positivity or caspase-1 activity. The authors therefore conclude that PN-OP promoted by cytoskeleton depolymerization contributes to swelling and blebbing, yet is not by itself the absolute determinant of pyroptotic membrane permeabilization. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:337-377]

## Relationship to caspase-1 and calcium signaling

[[Caspase-1]] function was positioned downstream of NLRP3 inflammasomes and also as part of a feedback loop that reinforced nanoparticle accumulation. Pharmacologic inhibition of caspase-1 or CASPASE-1 siRNA reduced osmotic pressure, protein nanoparticle counts, intermediate filament tension, calcium and chloride levels, permeabilization, and cytoskeletal fragmentation, while also lowering ASC and NLRP3 immunofluorescent signals. The authors interpret this as evidence that caspase-1 feeds back to promote further NLRP3 inflammasome production and PN-OP elevation. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:432-466]

Calcium release from the endoplasmic reticulum also regulated this system. Increasing intracellular calcium with caffeine raised osmotic pressure and protein nanoparticle counts, whereas reducing calcium release with `2-APB` or dantrolene lowered osmotic pressure, intermediate filament tension, nanoparticle counts, caspase-1 activity, swelling, blebbing, and permeabilization. The study concludes that intracellular calcium is involved in inflammasome production, PN-OP regulation, and the mechanical and membrane changes of astrocyte pyroptosis. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:560-632]

## Relationship to GSDMD-mediated pore formation

The paper places [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]]-NT within the same regulatory network. Overexpression of GSDMD-NT, but not GSDMD-CT, increased osmotic pressure and protein nanoparticle counts and induced swelling, blebbing, permeabilization, and caspase-1 activity. Inhibiting calcium signaling or caspase-1 abolished the GSDMD-NT-induced increases in osmotic pressure, nanoparticle accumulation, and pyroptotic severity, suggesting that GSDMD-NT-associated morphology depends on calcium- and caspase-1-sensitive PN-OP regulation. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:636-683]

## Proposed mechanism

In the authors’ model, assembled NLRP3 inflammasomes act as negatively charged intracellular protein nanoparticles that raise osmotic pressure, likely by adsorbing cations and disturbing electrochemical balance across the plasma membrane. This PN-OP increase, together with cytoskeletal remodeling and elevated intermediate filament tension, promotes astrocyte swelling and membrane blebbing during early pyroptosis. Membrane permeabilization, however, is not explained solely by PN-OP and additionally involves GSDMD pore formation and ion flux. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:721-769]

The discussion summarizes the central conclusion as follows: NLRP3 inflammasomes are the dominant source of intracellular protein nanoparticles that drive PN-OP in astrocytic pyroptosis, while cytoskeleton-derived particles contribute secondarily. Through this mechanism, inflammasome assembly controls the mechanical deformation of pyroptotic astrocytes, especially swelling and blebbing, and interacts with calcium, caspase-1, and GSDMD-dependent pore formation. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:691-720, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:777-811]
