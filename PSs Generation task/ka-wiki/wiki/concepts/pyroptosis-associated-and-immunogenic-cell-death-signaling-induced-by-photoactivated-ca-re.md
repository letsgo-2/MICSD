# Pyroptosis-associated and immunogenic cell death signaling induced by photoactivated CA-Re

## Overview

CA-Re is a carbonic anhydrase IX (CAIX)-anchored rhenium(I) photosensitizer designed as `[Re(CO)3(IP-TT)L]PF6`, where `L` is a sulfonamide-containing ligand intended to engage carbonic anhydrase family active sites. The supporting information frames CA-Re as a photosensitizer that evokes pyroptosis and enhances antitumor immunity. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:8-13, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:156-173]

In living cells, the source describes CA-Re as remaining anchored on the cell membrane rather than entering the cytoplasm even after 24 hours of incubation, which is presented as the basis for functional selectivity toward transmembrane [[Carbonic anhydrase IX]] and [[Carbonic anhydrase XII]] despite similar in vitro inhibition of CAIX, CAII, CAVB, and CAXII. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:439-449]

## Photoactivation and reactive oxygen species

CA-Re was studied as a light-activated photosensitizer under 425 nm irradiation in vitro, with assays specifically set up to detect intracellular reactive oxygen species, singlet oxygen, hydroxyl radicals, superoxide anion, and lipid peroxidation. The methods included ABDA for `^1O2`, HPF for `·OH`, DHE for `·O2-`, DCFH-DA for general intracellular ROS, and an MDA assay for lipid peroxidation. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:196-208, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:259-293]

The source also describes a scavenger-based ROS characterization workflow in irradiated cells. Sodium pyruvate, D-mannitol, tiron, sodium azide, ebselen, and trolox were used to suppress hydrogen peroxide, hydroxyl radicals, superoxide anion, singlet oxygen, peroxynitrite, and lipid peroxide species, respectively, after CA-Re pretreatment and before 425 nm irradiation. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:265-281]

Photophysical and ROS-related supporting figures further document transient absorption behavior, singlet oxygen detection, hydroxyl radical detection, and ESR signals after irradiation. Table S2 assigns calculated excited-state transitions of CA-Re to both intraligand charge transfer and metal-to-ligand charge transfer states, and also lists the `^3O2 → ^1O2` energy relationship relevant to singlet oxygen generation. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:422-433, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:498-507]

## Pyroptosis-associated signaling

The supporting information directly links CA-Re treatment plus light to pyroptosis-associated signaling by including western blot analyses of [[GSDMD]]-N and cleaved [[Caspase-1]] in treated cells. In MDA-MB-231 cells, western blotting for CAIX, HIF-1α, GPX4, and GSDMD-N was performed after 24 hours of CA-Re exposure followed by 425 nm irradiation for 0, 2, 5, or 10 minutes, with lysates collected 2 hours later. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:324-337]

In 4T1 cells, Figure S18 likewise reports western blot analysis of CAIX, HIF-1α, HSP70, GPX4, and cleaved Caspase-1 after CA-Re treatment and 425 nm irradiation for 0, 2, 5, or 10 minutes. Together, these measurements indicate that the study evaluated photoactivated CA-Re in relation to canonical pyroptosis-associated effectors, specifically gasdermin cleavage and Caspase-1 activation. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:465-471]

Scanning electron microscopy was also used to examine cell morphology after CA-Re treatment and photoirradiation, with cells fixed 2 hours after 425 nm exposure. This morphological readout formed part of the signaling and cell-death characterization workflow. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:294-299]

## Immunogenic cell death signaling

The source characterizes [[immunogenic cell death]] signaling through damage-associated molecular pattern (DAMP) measurements. In MDA-MB-231 cells, extracellular ATP was measured 1 hour after photoirradiation, while intracellular HMGB1 and ecto-CRT were assessed 6 hours after irradiation by confocal microscopy and flow cytometry. Hypericin plus light was used as a positive control for ATP release. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:299-323]

Figure S18 extends these ICD-associated DAMP measurements to 4T1 cells, showing confocal imaging of surface HMGB1 and CRT after CA-Re treatment and light exposure, along with analysis of ATP in cell culture supernatants after PDT treatment. These assays support the study’s focus on photoactivated CA-Re as an inducer of DAMP-associated [[immunogenic cell death]] signaling. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:465-469]

## Link to antitumor immunity

The in vivo workflow was explicitly designed to test not only local photodynamic therapy efficacy but also abscopal and immune effects. In a bilateral 4T1 BALB/c mouse model, CA-Re was injected intratumorally into the primary tumor, followed by 525 nm irradiation, while the contralateral tumor was used to evaluate distant antitumor responses. The source states that this design was chosen to attribute inhibition of distant tumors to abscopal effects and antitumor immune responses rather than direct CA-Re accumulation at the distant site. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:338-354, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:376-381]

To investigate in vivo antitumor immunity, the study analyzed dendritic-cell and T-lymphocyte markers in distant tumors and tumor-draining lymph nodes by flow cytometry, using CD11c, CD80, CD86, CD3, CD4, and CD8 staining. Serum cytokines measured by ELISA included proinflammatory IL-12p70, IL-6, TNF-α, anti-inflammatory IL-10, and the pyroptosis-associated cytokines IL-18 and IL-1β. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:355-364, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:477-478]

## Experimental context relevant to signaling interpretation

In cell-based phototoxicity testing, CA-Re showed strong light-dependent cytotoxicity in 4T1 cells with minimal dark toxicity, under both normoxia and hypoxia. Table S3 reports dark `IC50` values above 100 μM and light `IC50` values of `0.036 ± 0.019 μM` at 425 nm and `0.210 ± 0.041 μM` at 525 nm under normoxia; under hypoxia, the corresponding light `IC50` values were `0.042 ± 0.032 μM` and `0.220 ± 0.012 μM`. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:508-520]

This experimental context matters because the pyroptosis- and ICD-related signaling assays were performed under conditions where CA-Re functioned as a photoactivated, rather than constitutively toxic, agent. The supporting methods consistently pair CA-Re treatment with defined irradiation conditions before measuring ROS, DAMPs, pyroptosis-associated proteins, morphology, or immune outputs. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:236-245, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:259-323, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:324-337]
