# Triple functional role of Gd-TCPP within Gd-PNPs

Gd-TCPP is the key building block used to assemble Gd-PNPs. In the reported design, Gd3+ was chelated to TCPP first, and the resulting Gd-TCPP was then incorporated into the supramolecular nanoparticle system through electrostatic self-assembly with PEI and surface coating with mPEG-PGA. ^[d1nr04489c-110.md:138-143, d1nr04489c-110.md:166-173, d1nr04489c-110.md:304-317]

Within Gd-PNPs, Gd-TCPP serves three roles at once: it functions as an [[fluorescence imaging|FL imaging]] probe, a [[magnetic resonance imaging|MR contrast agent]], and a [[photodynamic therapy|photosensitizer]] for PDT. The paper’s conclusion states this explicitly, and the experimental results show preserved fluorescence, strong T1-weighted MR contrast, and singlet-oxygen generation under laser irradiation. ^[d1nr04489c-110.md:344-361, d1nr04489c-110.md:362-376, d1nr04489c-110.md:512-537, d1nr04489c-110.md:590-607]

As an FL probe, Gd-TCPP retained strong emission after chelation and after encapsulation into Gd-PNPs. The reported excitation/emission peaks for Gd-TCPP were approximately 430/650 nm, and the fluorescence quantum yield remained comparable to that of TCPP, indicating that Gd chelation and nanoparticle assembly did not obviously quench fluorescence. ^[d1nr04489c-110.md:344-361]

As an MR contrast agent, Gd-TCPP provides the Gd3+ component that enables T1-weighted imaging. The source reports concentration-dependent positive MR enhancement for Gd-PNPs and a longitudinal relaxivity of 16.157 mM−1 s−1, with the Gd3+ signal on the nanoparticle surface validating MR capability. [[Magnetic resonance imaging]] is therefore built directly into the same Gd-TCPP unit. ^[d1nr04489c-110.md:362-376, d1nr04489c-110.md:321-327]

As a PDT photosensitizer, Gd-TCPP contributes singlet oxygen generation after 635 nm irradiation. The source reports concentration-dependent 1O2 production and strong tumor-cell killing in vitro, and the in vivo treatment arm using Gd-PNPs plus laser irradiation produced the strongest tumor growth inhibition. [[Singlet oxygen]] and [[photodynamic therapy]] are thus tied to the same component. ^[d1nr04489c-110.md:231-253, d1nr04489c-110.md:512-537, d1nr04489c-110.md:540-572]
