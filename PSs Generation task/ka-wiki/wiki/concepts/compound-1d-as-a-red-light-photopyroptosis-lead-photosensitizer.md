# Compound 1d as a red-light photopyroptosis lead photosensitizer

**Compound 1d** is presented in the source study as the representative lead photosensitizer selected from a broader design series for highly efficient [[photopyroptosis]]. The study states that the series was built around a general structural principle in which cationic moieties are clamped onto a photosensitive core to strengthen mitochondrial targeting and local reactive oxygen species generation, and that 1d was ultimately chosen because it can be excited by red light, which offers deeper tissue penetration and lower energy than shorter-wavelength excitation. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:112-145]

Within the compound panel, 1d belongs to the set of design-conforming molecules `1a-1d`, in contrast to reference compounds with altered structural features. The source describes 1d as a multiple-pyridinium-modified porphyrin with a strong electrostatic interaction profile, reporting a binding constant of `K1d = 7.4 x 10^5 M^-1` with an ATP anionic model. This high electrostatic interaction strength is presented as part of the rationale for strong mitochondrial targeting and for facilitating photopyroptotic behavior after irradiation. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:123-145, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:215-242]

## Selection rationale

The study explicitly identifies 1d as the representative reagent for mechanistic and immunotherapy investigation after comparing photosensitizers spanning excitation maxima from the UV to the red spectral region. According to the authors, 1d was preferred because red-light excitation supports deeper tissue penetration and lower energy input, and the compound performed significantly better than previously reported related photosensitizers when compared across reagent concentration, excitation wavelength, and light dosage. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:127-146]

The source also reports favorable photochemical activity for 1d. Its singlet oxygen quantum yield was measured as `PhiDelta = 0.67` in methanol using methylene blue as a reference and DPBF as a scavenger, and additional validation experiments supported photogeneration of superoxide. In cells, inhibition studies with `NaN3` and `Tiron` indicated that `1O2` and `O2-.` accounted for the main share of the total reactive oxygen species induced by 1d during photodynamic treatment. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:148-189, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:279-284]

## Mitochondrial localization

In intracellular localization experiments, 1d showed the strongest association with [[mitochondria]] among the organelles tested. In EMT-6 cells co-stained with Mito-Tracker Red, Lyso-Tracker Red, and LD-Tracker Red, the reported Pearson correlation coefficient for the mitochondrial colocalization study was `0.91`, which the authors interpret as strong evidence of mitochondrial targeting. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:243-251]

Higher-resolution HIS-SIM imaging further indicated that 1d localized to both the inner and outer mitochondrial membranes. The study links this dual-membrane distribution to centralized mitochondrial ROS photoproduction after irradiation, with DCFH-DA fluorescence in the `1d + L` group reported as more than 16-fold higher than in the non-irradiated `1d` group. The source notes that the molecular structure of 1d did not change before versus after irradiation by `1H NMR`, supporting the interpretation that ROS photoproduction was the dominant event. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:252-279]

## Photopyroptotic activity

Under irradiation, 1d produced cellular changes described as consistent with photopyroptosis. The study reports cell bubbling in multiple tumor cell lines, significant [[LDH]] release in the `1d + L` group, reduced intracellular ATP activity, and increased extracellular ATP content. Bio-TEM imaging showed obvious cell membrane fragmentation after light exposure in cells incubated with 1d, while untreated cells and non-irradiated 1d-treated cells retained normal morphology. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:286-293, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:318-339]

The reported mechanism is a ROS-caspase-3-GSDME pathway. Western blot data showed no significant difference in caspase-3 and GSDME levels between control and dark `1d` groups, whereas the irradiated `1d + L` group showed high caspase-3 levels and obvious `GSDME-N` expression, which the authors interpret as activation of pyroptosis. HMGB-1 was also upregulated in the `1d + L` group relative to the other groups. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:335-347]

A central feature of 1d in this report is the speed and efficiency of pyroptotic triggering under red light. After 4 hours of incubation with 1d, EMT-6, Hepa1-6, and MCF-7 cells displayed bubbling and rupture under `640 nm` irradiation, and the study states that cells observed under CLSM could swell and begin membrane bubbling after only `10 s` of laser exposure. The process continued even after the laser was turned off, and flow cytometry showed a progressive increase in annexin V+/PI+ cells, with most EMT-6 cells entering pyroptosis within 5 hours after irradiation. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:356-380]

The source further emphasizes the low light requirement of this response. It states that the photopyroptotic trigger could be achieved within `10 s` under CLSM laser irradiation corresponding to `0.4 J cm^-2`, and that even lower power density (`2 mW cm^-2`) could still induce pyroptotic morphology with longer exposure. The authors present this as markedly more efficient than traditional pyroptosis methods and as a practical advantage of the design strategy embodied by 1d. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:360-380, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:537-554]

## Cytotoxicity profile

The study reports that 1d had weak dark cytotoxicity across tested tumor cell lines, but much higher cytotoxicity after red-light irradiation in a concentration-dependent manner. Reported `IC50` values after irradiation were `6.67 ug mL^-1` for MCF-7, `4.24 ug mL^-1` for EMT-6, and `13.21 ug mL^-1` for Hepa1-6. The authors use this contrast between weak dark toxicity and strong light-activated toxicity to support the suitability of 1d as a photosensitizer lead. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:348-355]

## Immune and in vivo significance

The study links 1d-induced photopyroptosis to anti-tumor immune activation. Transcriptome analysis of control, `1d`, and `1d + L` cell samples showed significant differences, with immune-related genes upregulated in the `1d + L` group and enrichment in pathways including `IL-17`, `TNF`, and `NOD-like receptor` signaling. The source concludes from these data that 1d-triggered photopyroptosis can induce a cellular immune response. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:402-429]

In vivo, the authors used EMT-6 tumor-bearing mice to evaluate anti-tumor efficacy and report that 1d persisted in tumor tissue with higher fluorescence than in major organs 24 hours after injection. In treatment studies, body weight remained nearly unchanged, and H&E staining of major organs showed no significant histopathological abnormalities, which the source presents as evidence of biological safety. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:430-490]

For anti-tumor outcomes, the source reports that the `1d + L` group showed strong tumor restraint, with an EMT-6 tumor inhibition rate of `79.5%` on day 16 and a Hepa1-6 tumor inhibition rate of `88.7%` in a second model. Blood analysis showed increased white blood cells, granulocytes, lymphocytes, and monocytes after `1d + light` treatment, while flow cytometry showed significantly increased proportions of `CD4+` and `CD8+` T cells in tumors from the pyroptosis group. Tumor sections also showed cleaved caspase-3, `GSDME-N`, `CD8`, and granzyme B signals consistent with the reported immune-engaged anti-tumor effect. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:491-535]

## Place in the study

Within this paper, 1d functions as the lead example that ties together the proposed structural design principle, red-light activation, rapid [[GSDME]]-associated photopyroptosis, and downstream anti-tumor [[immunotherapy]] without added immunoadjuvants. The authors conclude that photosensitizers built on this design can use mitochondrially localized ROS to trigger highly efficient pyroptosis and support tumor treatment through the photosensitizer alone. ^[a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:112-145, a-general-molecular-structural-design-for-highly-efficient-photopyroptosis-that-can-be-activated-within-10-s-irradiation.md:517-554]
