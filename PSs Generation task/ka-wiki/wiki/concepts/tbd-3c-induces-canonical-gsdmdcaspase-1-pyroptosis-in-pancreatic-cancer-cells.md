# TBD-3C induces canonical GSDMD/caspase-1 pyroptosis in pancreatic cancer cells

`TBD-3C` is a membrane-targeted [[photosensitizer]] with aggregation-induced emission properties that was applied in [[photodynamic therapy]] to trigger pyroptotic death in pancreatic cancer cells. In this study, the relevant in vitro models were the murine pancreatic cancer cell lines [[KPC]] and [[Panc02]]. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:153-169, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:178-194]

The paper frames pyroptosis as a form of inflammatory programmed cell death in which inflammatory caspases cleave [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]], releasing the N-terminal fragment that forms membrane pores and causes cell swelling, membrane disruption, and release of immunostimulatory contents. Within that framework, the authors present `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment as an inducer of this canonical `GSDMD`/[[caspase-1]] pyroptotic pathway in pancreatic cancer cells. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:125-137, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:226-242]

## Experimental context

`TBD-3C` localized mainly to the plasma membrane of `KPC` and `Panc02` cells, based on overlapping red `TBD-3C` and green membrane-tracker fluorescence. Quantitative analysis indicated that effective membrane anchoring was achieved with `10 uM` `TBD-3C` for `30 min`, which the study then used for the pyroptosis experiments. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:182-194]

To assess photodynamic activity, the investigators exposed labeled cells to white light at `40 mW cm^-2`. Under these conditions, `TBD-3C` treatment increased intracellular [[reactive oxygen species|ROS]] signal relative to control cells, supporting that light activation generated the photodynamic stimulus used in the downstream cell-death assays. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:195-203, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:764-772]

## Evidence for pyroptosis

Real-time imaging showed that `KPC` and `Panc02` cells treated with `TBD-3C` and irradiated for `10 min` developed cell swelling and large bulging bubbles on the plasma membrane. The authors identify these morphological changes as typical features of pyroptosis, and they further report that the extent of pyroptosis increased with longer irradiation times (`0`, `4`, `8`, and `12 min`). ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:203-216]

Biochemically, photodynamic treatment with `TBD-3C` increased [[lactate dehydrogenase|LDH]] release and increased `N-GSDMD` relative to controls in both pancreatic cancer cell lines. The paper describes full-length `GSDMD` cleavage and LDH leakage as typical features of pyroptotic cell death, so these results support `GSDMD`-dependent membrane damage during treatment. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:226-232, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:297-301]

The study also measured [[caspase-1]] activity using a `FAM-FLICA` assay. Flow-cytometry results showed substantially higher fluorescence intensity in the `TBD-3C` plus light group than in the control group, which the authors interpret as increased active caspase-1 produced by photodynamic treatment. Together with `GSDMD` cleavage, this supports a canonical caspase-1-associated pyroptotic mechanism. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:232-242, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:297-301]

Flow cytometry with `PI` and annexin V-`APC` further showed that the proportion of double-positive cells, identified by the authors as pyroptotic cells, rose significantly as irradiation time increased. This time dependence matched the microscopy findings and reinforced that light exposure controlled the strength of the pyroptotic response. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:243-247, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:299-301, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:779-786]

## Relationship to apoptosis

To distinguish this process from [[apoptosis]], the study compared `TBD-3C` treatment with stimulation by staurosporine (`STS`), a known apoptosis inducer. `STS` produced apoptotic features including nuclear fragmentation and `PARP` cleavage, whereas no obvious apoptotic feature was detected in `TBD-3C`-treated irradiated cells. The authors therefore conclude that `TBD-3C` under irradiation barely induced apoptosis in `KPC` and `Panc02` cells. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:247-255]

## Cytotoxicity profile

In `CCK-8` viability assays under illumination, the reported `IC50` values of `TBD-3C` were `4 uM` for `KPC` cells and `0.5 uM` for `Panc02` cells. The source also states that dark cytotoxicity was negligible in both cell lines, indicating that cell killing depended on photoactivation rather than substantial baseline toxicity. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:255-264]

## Significance in the study

The authors conclude from these morphological, biochemical, and flow-cytometric results that `TBD-3C` efficiently induced death of pancreatic cancer cells by pyroptosis after light irradiation. This pyroptotic cell death was then used in the broader study as the initiating event for downstream [[immunogenic cell death]], immune-cell activation, and remodeling of the pancreatic tumor microenvironment. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:240-264, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:267-280]
