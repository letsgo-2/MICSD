# MAVS and TRAF3 link mitochondria to ASC ubiquitination during inflammasome activation

[[MAVS]] proteins are described in the source as mitochondrial proteins involved in [[inflammasome]] activation. They directly associate with [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3]] on the outer mitochondrial membrane, linking mitochondrial status to assembly of the inflammasome complex. ^[pyroptosis-targeting-via-mitochondria-an-educated-guess-to-innovate-covid19-therapies.md:183-189]

The source further states that MAVS can recruit the E3 ligase [[TRAF3]] in a reactive-oxygen-species-dependent manner. TRAF3 then amplifies inflammasome activation by ubiquitinating [[ASC]], the adaptor protein that bridges NLR proteins to caspase-1 recruitment. ^[pyroptosis-targeting-via-mitochondria-an-educated-guess-to-innovate-covid19-therapies.md:137-142, pyroptosis-targeting-via-mitochondria-an-educated-guess-to-innovate-covid19-therapies.md:183-189]

Within the article’s broader model, this MAVS-TRAF3-ASC axis is part of a mitochondria-centered upstream layer of [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3]] regulation. The paper emphasizes that impaired mitochondrial function, especially accumulation of mitochondria-derived reactive oxygen species, is an indispensable second trigger for functional inflammasome assembly after priming signals have induced transcription of inflammasome components. ^[pyroptosis-targeting-via-mitochondria-an-educated-guess-to-innovate-covid19-therapies.md:169-178]

The source also notes that ubiquitinating and de-ubiquitinating events are central to mitochondrial quality control, but says their exact contribution to inflammasome stabilization and [[pyroptosis]] remains unclear. It adds that mitophagy limits NLRP3 activation by removing damaged or stressed mitochondria, while ROS-driven de-ubiquitination of mitochondrial proteins can promote inflammasome complex assembly by blocking mitophagy. ^[pyroptosis-targeting-via-mitochondria-an-educated-guess-to-innovate-covid19-therapies.md:189-199]

In the SARS-CoV-2-focused schematic described in the source, ORF3a promotes inflammasome assembly through TRAF3-mediated ubiquitination of ASC, while TRAF3-ORF3a interaction is also required for NF-kappaB activation and transcription of pro-IL-1beta, pro-IL-18, and NLRP3 genes. This places ASC ubiquitination alongside mitochondrial ROS production and mitochondrial dysfunction as part of the pathway connecting viral stress signals to inflammasome activation. ^[pyroptosis-targeting-via-mitochondria-an-educated-guess-to-innovate-covid19-therapies.md:115-126]
