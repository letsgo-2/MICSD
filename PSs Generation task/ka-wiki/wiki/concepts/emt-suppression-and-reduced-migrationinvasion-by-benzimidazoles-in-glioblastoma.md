# EMT suppression and reduced migration/invasion by benzimidazoles in glioblastoma

In this study, benzimidazole compounds were reported to suppress migratory and invasive behavior in human [[glioblastoma]] cells and to regulate key markers associated with [[epithelial-mesenchymal transition|EMT]]. The effect was described for the benzimidazoles flubendazole, mebendazole, and fenbendazole in U87 and U251 GBM cell lines. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:20-25, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:442-454]

## Context

The paper identified multiple azole compounds as potential anti-GBM candidates through [[weighted gene co-expression network analysis|WGCNA]] of TCGA data and [[Connectivity Map|CMAP]]-based drug repurposing, but experimental testing showed that anti-GBM activity in this panel was confined to the benzimidazole subgroup. RNA-sequencing analysis then suggested that benzimidazoles affect processes including cell migration, cell cycle regulation, and programmed cell death in GBM cells. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:18-25, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:93-113, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:411-441]

## Evidence for reduced migration and invasion

Migration and invasion were assessed in U87 and U251 cells using [[transwell assay|transwell assays]], with Matrigel coating used for the invasion condition. At 24 hours, benzimidazoles dose-dependently inhibited both migration and invasion in both GBM cell lines. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:227-240, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:442-447]

The authors frame migration and invasion as in vitro indicators of malignant behavior and interpret the transwell results as evidence that benzimidazoles reduce metastatic characteristics of GBM cells. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:443-445, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:452-454]

## EMT-related marker changes

Western blot analysis showed that flubendazole dose-dependently decreased the expression of several EMT-associated markers in U87 and U251 cells, including N-cadherin, β-catenin, the transcription factor Slug, and MMP2, while increasing E-cadherin expression. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:281-308, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:448-451]

Based on these marker changes, the study concludes that benzimidazoles inhibit GBM metastatic characteristics by downregulating key EMT markers. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:448-454]

## Relationship to the study's broader findings

The migration, invasion, and EMT findings were part of a broader anti-GBM profile for benzimidazoles in this paper. The same study reports dose-dependent inhibition of proliferation and DNA synthesis, G2/M cell-cycle arrest through the [[P53/P21/cyclin B1 pathway]], and induction of pyroptosis and mitochondria-dependent apoptosis in GBM cells. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:21-29, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:401-410, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:455-471, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:472-615]

In the discussion, the authors summarize these results by stating that benzimidazoles inhibited the proliferation and metastasis of GBM cells, with RNA-sequencing data pointing to regulation of cell migration, cell cycle, and programmed cell death. They further suggest that these compounds may have potential as GBM therapeutics, including possible use for TMZ-resistant disease or in combination with temozolomide. ^[benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:694-722, benzimidazoles-induce-concurrent-apoptosis-and-pyroptosis-of-human-glioblastoma-cells-via-arresting-cell-cycle.md:750-755]
