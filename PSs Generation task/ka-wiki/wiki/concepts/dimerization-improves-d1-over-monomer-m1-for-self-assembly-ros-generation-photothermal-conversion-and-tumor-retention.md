# Dimerization improves D1 over monomer M1 for self-assembly, ROS generation, photothermal conversion, and tumor retention

`D1` is a tumor-membrane-targeted [[photosensitizer]] dimer designed from the same chromophore as the monomer `M1`. In this study, dimerization was presented as a way to improve the material properties and in vivo performance relevant to synergistic [[photodynamic therapy]] and [[photothermal therapy]]. Relative to `M1`, `D1` showed stronger self-assembly behavior, higher aggregation-induced emission, somewhat higher ROS generation, stronger photothermal conversion, and longer retention at the tumor site after intratumoral injection. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:141-156, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:364-381, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:529-536, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:609-625]

## Self-assembly and aggregation behavior

The paper reports that `D1` exhibited significant aggregation-induced emission in poor solvents such as toluene and water, and its self-assembly was supported by TEM and SEM observation of spherical structures with an average diameter of about `110.3 nm` by dynamic light scattering. `D1` also had a positive zeta potential of `+10.68 +/- 0.66 mV`, which the authors linked to favorable permeability and retention behavior and targeting of negatively charged cancer cells. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:217-228]

By contrast, `M1` had better water solubility but did not show aggregation-induced emission in water, and SEM and TEM images showed no self-assembly of `M1`. The solid fluorescence quantum yield was also higher for `D1` than for `M1` (`0.89%` versus `0.23%`), supporting the study’s conclusion that dimerization improved aggregation-related behavior. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:370-375]

## ROS generation

The authors argue that the dimeric structure of `D1` is more favorable for ROS production than `M1`. Theoretical calculations showed that `D1` had a very small `DeltaE_S1T4` of `0.0001 eV` and a `DeltaE_S1T1` of `0.6716 eV`, which the paper states is better than `M1` and more conducive to intersystem crossing and non-radiative transitions associated with ROS generation. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:201-211]

Experimentally, `D1` generated multiple ROS species, including singlet oxygen, hydroxyl radical, and superoxide radical, and showed a reported singlet-oxygen quantum yield of `89.4%`. Although `M1` also showed good ROS generation, its singlet-oxygen quantum yield was lower at `84.03%`, and the paper states that the overall ROS-generation efficiency of `M1` was weaker than that of `D1`. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:229-257, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:376-380]

## Photothermal conversion

The study also attributes better [[photothermal conversion]] to dimerization. In the design rationale, the authors state that the increased intramolecular distance between donor and acceptor groups in `D1` should enhance intramolecular stretching and rotational motions and thereby favor non-radiative decay and photothermal conversion. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:205-211]

In measurements under `520 nm` irradiation, `D1` showed a strong photothermal effect and a calculated photothermal conversion efficiency of about `64.19%`, with good stability across four heating-cooling cycles. The comparison section states that `M1` also had photothermal activity but with lower efficiency at `44.58%`, and that this weaker performance contributed to lower cancer-cell killing than `D1`. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:258-287, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:376-381]

## Tumor retention in vivo

In the in vivo 4T1 tumor model, fluorescence imaging showed that `D1` remained accumulated at the tumor site `24 h` after intratumoral injection. The paper presents this as part of `D1`’s favorable in vivo profile before irradiation and treatment. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:465-484]

`M1` did not show the same behavior in vivo. After intratumoral injection and `24 h` waiting time, the fluorescence of `M1` did not accumulate at the tumor site. The authors attribute this difference to the better aggregation performance of `D1`, stating that `M1` had a weaker enhanced permeability and retention effect and less retention time at the tumor site than `D1`, which was associated with a weaker antitumor effect in vivo. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:529-536]

## Functional significance in the study

The paper’s overall interpretation is that dimerization gave `D1` a better property set than `M1` for synergistic photoimmunotherapy. In the conclusion, the authors specifically summarize that, compared with the monomer sharing the same chromophore, `D1` had preferable aggregation capability together with better ROS-generation efficiency, improved photothermal effect, and enhanced permeability and retention effects. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:621-625]

These differences were also linked to stronger biological activity. The paper states that `M1` killed cancer cells less effectively than `D1`, induced weaker [[pyroptosis]] because of its lower aggregation ability, and lacked the stronger systemic antitumor immune features observed for `D1` in vivo. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:378-381, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:414-415, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:562-576, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:600-607]
