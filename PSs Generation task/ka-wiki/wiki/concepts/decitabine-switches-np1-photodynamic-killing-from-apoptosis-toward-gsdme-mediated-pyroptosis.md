# Decitabine switches NP1 photodynamic killing from apoptosis toward GSDME-mediated pyroptosis

In this study, `NP1` is an oxidation-sensitive nanoparticle loaded with the photosensitizer `TBE`, and `decitabine` is delivered separately in `NP2` as a DNA methyltransferase inhibitor. The authors designed the combination so that decitabine could restore intracellular [[GSDME]] expression, allowing `NP1`-mediated [[photodynamic therapy]] to redirect cell death after light exposure toward pyroptosis rather than remaining predominantly apoptotic. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:59-67, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:120-128]

`NP1` alone showed strong light-dependent cytotoxicity but mainly produced apoptotic features in `MCF-7` cells. Under `808 nm` irradiation, `NP1` increased intracellular ROS, reduced mitochondrial membrane potential, increased cleaved caspase-3 and cytosolic cytochrome c, and caused significant tumor-cell growth inhibition. The same treatment induced a higher apoptosis rate than `NP1` alone, while the authors noted no significant upregulation of key proteins involved in pyroptosis or the [[cGAS-STING pathway]]. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:224-257]

The study linked this lack of pyroptotic response to epigenetic suppression of `DFNA5` and `TMEM173`. Analysis of breast-cancer datasets and tissue staining showed that `DFNA5` and `TMEM173` were downregulated in breast tumors and that their reduced expression was strongly correlated with promoter methylation. Among the tested DNA methyltransferase inhibitors, `20 uM` decitabine increased `GSDME` expression by `70.3%` and `STING` expression by `40.8%`, whereas azacytidine and `gamma-oryzanol` did not show evident effects, leading the authors to select decitabine for combination with `NP1`. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:284-316]

When cells were pre-incubated with decitabine-loaded `NP2` and then treated with `NP1` plus light (`NP1+NP2+L`), overall growth inhibition remained similar to `NP1+L`, but the mode of cell death shifted. Compared with `NP1+NP2+L`, `NP1+L` produced `1.7%` more `Annexin V+ PI-` apoptotic cells and `2.1%` fewer `Annexin V- PI+` pyroptotic cells. Morphologically, `NP1+NP2+L` caused cell swelling and large plasma-membrane bubbles, whereas `NP1+L` caused cell shrinking and rounding, consistent with a change from apoptosis toward [[pyroptosis]]. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:366-375]

The mechanistic evidence for this switch centered on [[GSDME]] cleavage and membrane-damage markers. Western blotting showed that `GSDME-N` expression in the `NP1+NP2+L` group was `1.1` times that of the `PBS` group. The extracellular ATP concentration after `NP1+NP2+L` reached `163.2 nM`, which was `1.6-fold` higher than with `NP1+L` and `29.9-fold` higher than with `PBS`, and supernatant `LDH` was also significantly elevated. The authors interpret the combined increase in `GSDME-N`, ATP release, and LDH release as validation that decitabine transformed `NP1+L`-induced apoptosis into GSDME-mediated pyroptosis. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:375-385]

This shift in death mode was part of a broader immunogenic mechanism. In the combined treatment group, decitabine-restored `STING` and `GSDME` expression enabled `NP1` photodynamic stress to both cleave `GSDME` through caspase-3 activation and activate the [[cGAS-STING pathway]]. The resulting proinflammatory signals increased the population of `CD80+ CD86+` mature dendritic cells in vitro and, in vivo, were associated with higher cytokine levels, greater dendritic-cell and NK-cell maturation, stronger `CD8+` and `CD4+` T-cell infiltration, and reduced immunosuppressive cell populations. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:386-406, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:514-570]
