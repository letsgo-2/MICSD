# NF-kappaB/NLRP3 signaling mediates curcumin's anti-pyroptotic effect in microglia

Curcumin was studied in a mouse model of transient middle cerebral artery occlusion/reperfusion (MCAO/R) and in primary microglia stimulated with LPS plus ATP. The paper reports that curcumin reduced stroke-induced white matter damage and sensorimotor deficits, while also attenuating microglial pyroptosis and inflammation, at least partly through suppression of the NF-kappaB/NLRP3 signaling pathway. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:147-155, omcl1552127-125.md:747-784, omcl1552127-125.md:2452-2455]

## Mechanism

The study proposes that curcumin suppresses microglial pyroptosis by inhibiting NF-kappaB activation upstream of NLRP3 inflammasome activation. In the authors' model, ischemic stroke triggers NF-kappaB signaling, which promotes transcription of proinflammatory and inflammasome-related genes; curcumin reverses this activation and reduces downstream NLRP3 inflammasome activity. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:2377-2402, omcl1552127-125.md:2364-2372]

## In vivo findings

In MCAO mice, curcumin treatment reduced infarct volume, brain tissue loss, and white matter injury, and improved adhesive-removal performance over time. It also reduced GSDMD-positive microglia and caspase-1-positive microglia in the ischemic cortex, corpus callosum, and striatum. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:747-784, omcl1552127-125.md:833-841, omcl1552127-125.md:999-1008]

Western blot analysis of peri-infarct tissue showed that curcumin lowered NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1, GSDMD-N, pro-IL-1beta, and IL-1beta after stroke. The paper also reports reduced activation of the NF-kappaB pathway, including lower phosphorylation of IKKalpha/beta, I-kappaBalpha, and p65 compared with vehicle-treated MCAO mice. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:785-814, omcl1552127-125.md:1655-1671, omcl1552127-125.md:1808-1824]

## In vitro findings

In primary microglia, LPS plus ATP induced NLRP3 inflammasome activation and pyroptosis, increasing NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1, GSDMD-N, IL-1beta, and IL-18 at both protein and mRNA levels. Curcumin significantly suppressed these changes and reduced the pyroptotic response. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:935-956, omcl1552127-125.md:1345-1353]

Curcumin also inhibited NF-kappaB signaling in LPS+ATP-stimulated microglia, reducing phosphorylation of IKKalpha/beta, I-kappaBalpha, and p65. The authors interpret this as evidence that curcumin blocks the upstream NF-kappaB pathway that supports NLRP3 inflammasome activation. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:1811-1824]

## NLRP3 dependence

Loss-of-function experiments supported a central role for NLRP3. In vivo, AAV-based NLRP3 shRNA reduced pyroptosis-related proteins and enhanced curcumin's effects on microglial pyroptosis and white matter protection. In vitro, NLRP3 siRNA similarly lowered pyroptosis markers and further strengthened curcumin's antipyroptotic effect in microglia. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:963-978, omcl1552127-125.md:1197-1204, omcl1552127-125.md:1627-1634, omcl1552127-125.md:1641-1654]

## Conclusion

The study concludes that curcumin attenuates microglial pyroptosis, improves white matter integrity, and improves functional outcomes after stroke through suppression of NF-kappaB signaling and subsequent inhibition of NLRP3 inflammasome activation. ^[omcl1552127-125.md:2474-2481]
