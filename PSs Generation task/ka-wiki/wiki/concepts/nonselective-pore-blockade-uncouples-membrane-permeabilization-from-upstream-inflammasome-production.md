# Nonselective pore blockade uncouples membrane permeabilization from upstream inflammasome production

In pyroptotic astrocytes, the study found that blocking nonselective ion pores with polyethylene glycol 8000 inhibited membrane permeabilization, but did not prevent upstream inflammasome production, caspase-1 activity, or ion influx. This supported the conclusion that membrane pore formation and upstream inflammasome-driven osmotic changes can be partially separated in the pyroptotic process. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:467-509]

## Context

The paper describes pyroptosis as involving inflammasome activation, caspase-1 cleavage of GSDMD, formation of nonselective membrane pores, ion influx, and membrane permeabilization. It also proposes that inflammasomes act as intracellular protein nanoparticles that increase osmotic pressure and contribute to cell swelling and blebbing. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:88-130, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:684-769]

## Experimental finding

Polyethylene glycol 8000 was used to block inflow through nonselective ion channels. In both the 4-OHT and LPS-NIG pyroptosis models, PEG8000 reduced membrane permeabilization, blebbing, and LDH release, and partially lowered cytoplasmic osmotic pressure, intermediate filament tension, and intracellular calcium and chloride levels. However, it did not change caspase-1 activity or intracellular protein nanoparticle production. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:490-509, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:584-590]

## Interpretation

The authors concluded that blocking nonselective pores can stop permeabilization without stopping inflammasome production. In their model, inflammasome-generated protein nanoparticles primarily drive osmotic pressure, swelling, and blebbing, while membrane permeabilization depends on pore formation downstream of GSDMD-NT. ^[inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:736-769, inflammasome-induced-osmotic-pressure-and-the-mechanical-mechanisms-underlying-astrocytic-swelling-and-membrane-blebbing-in-pyroptosis.md:792-810]
