# Cancer-cell-activated in situ synthesis of a photosensitizer for precise PDT

Cancer-cell-activated in situ synthesis of a photosensitizer for precise [[photodynamic therapy|PDT]] is a strategy in which a photosensitizer is formed selectively inside cancer cells from otherwise photochemically inert precursors. In the reported system, this approach was designed to maximize tumor phototoxicity while minimizing off-target damage in normal tissues by restricting photosensitizer formation to the tumor-cell environment. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:36-52, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:86-103]

The study presents [[MOF-199]] as both a carrier and a catalyst source. This Cu(II)-based [[metal-organic framework|MOF]] was used to co-load two precursors, `TPA-alkyne-2+` and `MePy-N3`, and after uptake into cancer cells it could be reduced by elevated intracellular [[glutathione|GSH]] to generate Cu(I), which then catalyzed an intracellular click reaction that produced the active photosensitizer `TPATrzPy-3+`. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:41-48, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:156-175]

## Rationale

The source frames this strategy as a response to a core limitation of conventional PDT: many photosensitizers accumulate non-specifically in skin and non-malignant tissues, causing unwanted phototoxicity and forcing patients to avoid light after treatment. It also notes that although activatable photosensitizers and intracellular drug synthesis have been explored, achieving cancer-cell-specific synthesis after systemic administration remained difficult. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:53-85, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:86-103]

The design further aimed to combine activation with [[mitochondria-targeting]] because ROS-mediated mitochondrial damage can readily induce apoptosis. Accordingly, the final product `TPATrzPy-3+` was designed as a positively charged, water-soluble, [[aggregation-induced emission|AIE]]-active photosensitizer that could accumulate in mitochondria, emit bright red fluorescence, and efficiently generate singlet oxygen under irradiation. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:76-85, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:177-213]

## System design

The active photosensitizer, `TPATrzPy-3+`, was synthesized from two precursors, `TPA-alkyne-2+` and `MePy-N3`, through a Cu(I)-catalyzed click reaction. The precursor-loaded material was termed `PMOF`, and coating it with `F-127` yielded `PMOF NPs` for improved dispersity and biocompatibility. After intravenous administration, the nanoparticles were reported to enrich in tumor tissue, where GSH-responsive collapse of MOF-199 released both precursors and enabled in situ photosensitizer formation. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:151-175, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:319-364]

The paper reports that `TPATrzPy-3+` has a donor-acceptor structure with a small singlet-triplet energy gap, features presented as favorable for intersystem crossing and ROS generation. It also states that the precursors lack the complementary donor or acceptor features needed for efficient photosensitization, which helps keep them comparatively low in phototoxicity before conversion into the active product. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:188-213]

## Activation mechanism

Ex vivo characterization showed that GSH reduced Cu(II) in MOF-199 to Cu(I), as monitored by ESR, and that GSH exposure caused PMOF to lose crystallinity and collapse. Under these conditions, the loaded precursors and copper ions were released in similar profiles, and fluorescence characteristic of `TPATrzPy-3+` emerged, supporting successful GSH-triggered in situ synthesis. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:265-318, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:335-364]

In solution studies, `TPATrzPy-3+` showed red-shifted absorption and emission relative to `TPA-alkyne-2+`, displayed AIE behavior, and generated singlet oxygen efficiently. Its ABDA bleaching rate under white light was reported as comparable to [[Chlorin e6|Ce6]], whereas the two precursors and untreated PMOF NPs showed much lower activity until GSH-triggered activation occurred. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:229-264, cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:333-364]

## Cellular evidence

To test cancer-cell selectivity, the authors compared [[HeLa]] cells with [[3T3]] cells. They quantified substantially higher GSH levels in HeLa cells than in 3T3 cells and used this difference as the basis for cancer-cell-specific activation of PMOF NPs. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:378-397]

Confocal imaging showed mitochondrial TPATrzPy-3+ fluorescence in PMOF-NP-treated HeLa cells, similar to cells treated directly with `TPATrzPy-3+`, while signal was minimal in precursor-treated HeLa cells, in BSO-pretreated HeLa cells, and in PMOF-NP-treated or precursor-treated 3T3 cells. The source interprets these findings as evidence that intracellular synthesis of the photosensitizer is both GSH-triggered and cancer-cell-specific. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:398-417]

Additional imaging suggested that PMOF NPs remained intact during uptake and then degraded intracellularly: after 4 to 8 hours, fluorescence associated with the nanoparticles was mainly localized in lysosomes, while after 16 hours it was mostly found in mitochondria. HPLC analysis of HeLa cell lysates further indicated time-dependent intracellular formation of `TPATrzPy-3+`, with calculated synthesis yields reaching about 70% by 16 to 24 hours after incubation. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:418-460]

## Photodynamic effects in cells

Under white-light irradiation, free precursors showed only minor toxicity in both HeLa and 3T3 cells, while direct `TPATrzPy-3+` treatment potently ablated both cell types. In contrast, PMOF NPs showed strong light toxicity in HeLa cells but inhibited light toxicity in 3T3 cells, indicating selective photodynamic killing associated with cancer-cell-specific photosensitizer generation. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:465-478]

The study also reports that BSO pretreatment, used to reduce intracellular GSH, markedly decreased PMOF-NP phototoxicity in HeLa cells. No obvious dark toxicity was observed for the tested precursors, PMOF NPs, BSO plus PMOF NPs, or `TPATrzPy-3+` in either cell line. Flow-cytometry analysis further suggested that activated cell ablation proceeded through a mitochondrial apoptotic pathway. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:487-498]

## In vivo evidence in zebrafish

The in vivo evaluation used a `krasV12` transgenic zebrafish model with hyperplastic liver tumors. In this model, PMOF NPs, direct `TPATrzPy-3+`, direct precursors, and `TPATrzPy-3+@MOF NPs` were compared to assess tumor-selective photosensitizer generation, photodynamic efficacy, and toxicity. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:507-528]

After intravenous injection, red fluorescence from `TPATrzPy-3+` was absent in precursor-treated embryos but appeared in embryos treated with PMOF NPs, `TPATrzPy-3+`, and `TPATrzPy-3+@MOF NPs`. The key reported difference was distribution: in PMOF-NP-injected embryos, TPATrzPy-3+ signal was detected from 1 day post-injection and only in the tumor region, whereas direct photosensitizer or photosensitizer-loaded controls showed fluorescence throughout the body. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:539-567]

Photodynamically, liver tumors in PMOF-NP-treated larvae shrank by about 40% on average after PDT, similar to the direct-photosensitizer control groups, while tumors in precursor-treated, light-only, or untreated groups increased in volume over the same period. In parallel toxicity testing under light, PMOF NPs showed a substantially higher LC50 than `TPATrzPy-3+` or `TPATrzPy-3+@MOF NPs`, which the paper presents as evidence of reduced unspecific light toxicity alongside maintained anti-tumor efficacy. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:568-590]

## Safety and biodegradation

For longer-term in vivo assessment, BALB/c nude mice received intravenous PMOF NPs or PBS. The reported follow-up included H&E staining, blood biochemistry, and pharmacokinetic analysis. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:591-605]

According to the source, PMOF NPs caused negligible effects on normal organs and blood biochemistry at the administered dose. The pharmacokinetic data were described as showing time-dependent degradation without a tendency to accumulate in the body, supporting the authors’ conclusion that the system had low systemic toxicity and good biodegradability under the tested conditions. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:595-608]

## Significance

The study concludes that cancer-cell-activated in situ photosensitizer synthesis can localize photosensitizer production inside tumor cells after systemic administration, enabling image-guided PDT with potent anti-tumor activity and reduced unspecific phototoxicity. In this implementation, specificity depended on elevated intracellular GSH in cancer cells, GSH-triggered MOF-199 collapse, Cu(I)-catalyzed intracellular click chemistry, and mitochondrial accumulation of the synthesized AIE photosensitizer. ^[cancercellactivated-insitu-synthesis-of-mitochondriatargeting-aie-photosensitizer-for-precise-photodynamic-therapy.md:609-649]
