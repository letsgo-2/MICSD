I’m extracting only the Cu-TBB paper facts and will format them as a concise wiki entry with inline source citations and related wikilinks.# Dual Cu-catalysis and 750 nm photodynamic ROS generation by activated Cu-TBB

Cu-TBB is a copper-bacteriochlorin nanosheet designed as a GSH-activatable pyroptosis inducer. In the tumor microenvironment, elevated glutathione disassembles Cu-TBB into Cu+ and TBB, switching the material from an inactive “off” state to an active “on” state in tumor cells. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:17-29, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:140-153]

The activated system generates reactive oxygen species through two coordinated routes. Released Cu+ drives cascade reactions that produce O2−• and highly toxic ·OH, while released TBB produces O2−• and 1O2 under 750 nm laser irradiation. The paper describes this combined Cu-catalysis and photodynamic process as a basis for selective cancer-cell pyroptosis. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:20-29, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:147-154, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:239-282]

In vitro, GSH activated Cu-TBB fluorescence and increased ROS readouts in cancer cells. The source reports detectable O2−• by ESR after GSH treatment, restored TBB fluorescence, DPBF degradation under 750 nm irradiation, and ABDA/DHR123 evidence consistent with 1O2 and O2−• generation. Cu-TBB also showed strong cellular damage only when GSH was present, with enhanced effects after laser exposure. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:228-282, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:289-387]

The ROS generated by activated Cu-TBB triggered classical GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis. In treated HeLa, HepG2, and 4T1 cells, the paper reports cleaved caspase-1, N-GSDMD, IL-1β, and IL-18 upregulation, while caspase-4 was not detected. VX-765 reduced these pyroptosis markers and lowered inflammatory cytokine release. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:388-488]

This pyroptotic response increased immunogenicity. The study reports HMGB1 release, LDH release, CRT exposure, dendritic-cell maturation, and enhanced CD4+ and CD8+ T-cell responses, indicating that Cu-TBB-induced pyroptosis promoted antitumor immune activation rather than ROS alone. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:463-488, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:604-665]

In vivo, Cu-TBB accumulated in tumors, remained low in most normal tissues, and showed strong antitumor activity in bilateral 4T1 tumor-bearing mice. The paper reports primary tumor inhibition, reduced distant tumor growth, and fewer lung metastatic nodules, with the 750 nm laser group showing the strongest effect. ^[copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:489-525, copperbacteriochlorin-nanosheet-as-a-specific-pyroptosis-inducer-for-robust-tumor-immunotherapy.md:526-603]
