# Photothermal reinforcement increases membrane permeability and DNA-damage responses during D1 phototherapy

In the D1 study, adding the photothermal component to [[photodynamic therapy]] strengthened two cellular responses relative to photodynamic treatment alone: tumor-cell membrane permeability increased further, and DNA-damage-associated signaling became more pronounced. The comparison was made within the D1 phototherapy system by using a lower-power condition that produced a photodynamic effect and a higher-power condition that produced combined photodynamic and photothermal effects. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:426-430, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:479-481]

## Experimental context

D1 is a tumor-cell-membrane-targeted photosensitive dimer designed for synergistic photodynamic and photothermal treatment. In the study, the authors state that irradiation at `0.1 W cm^-2` produced only a photodynamic effect, whereas irradiation at `0.3 W cm^-2` produced both photodynamic and photothermal effects. This setup was then used to compare how the two treatment modes affected 4T1 tumor cells. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:141-156, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:426-430]

## DNA-damage responses

The authors report that photodynamic therapy induced tumor DNA damage, as shown by a significant increase in expression of the DNA-damage marker `gammaH2AX`. When photothermal therapy was introduced alongside photodynamic treatment, DNA damage was further strengthened. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:431-434, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:479-481]

The stronger damage observed in the combined photothermal group was also associated with expression of DNA-damage-repair proteins. `MDC1` and `Ku70` were described as nearly unexpressed in the photodynamic groups, while significant expression was detected only in the photothermal group, which the authors attribute to the relatively stronger damage in that condition. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:435-441, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:480-481]

## Membrane permeability and uptake

The same comparison indicated that photothermal treatment further improved tumor-cell membrane permeability. The paper states that, after photothermal treatment, membrane permeability was further improved and drug uptake was obviously accelerated. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:442-444]

To support this point, the study used [[berberine]] as a fluorescent drug indicator. Berberine uptake after photodynamic treatment alone was significantly lower than in the combined photothermal and photodynamic treatment group, which the authors interpret as probably resulting from a further increase in membrane permeability caused by photothermal treatment. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:444-449, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:479-480]

## Relationship to pyroptosis and immunogenic cell death

The study places these effects within its broader model of D1-driven [[pyroptosis]] and [[immunogenic cell death]]. According to the authors, the photothermal effect accelerated pyroptosis and the increased membrane permeability also contributed to efflux of cell contents, which further strengthened the immunogenic cell death process in tumor cells. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:409-414, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:463-464]

In the paper’s conclusion, this role is summarized more broadly: D1-generated type-I ROS initiated pyroptosis at the tumor-cell membrane, and the photothermal component further accelerated that process while contributing to stronger immunogenic cell death and downstream antitumor immunity. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:609-617, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:618-635]
