# Dual reactive oxygen species photogeneration by CA-Re

CA-Re is a carbonic anhydrase-targeted rhenium(I) photosensitizer described as `[Re(CO)3(IP-TT)L]PF6`, where `L` is a sulfonamide-containing ligand and `IP-TT` is a thiophene-containing bidentate ligand. In the supporting methods, the compound is characterized as the platform used for photochemical detection of multiple reactive oxygen species under light irradiation. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:156-173, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:196-208]

## Overview

The source material shows that CA-Re was evaluated for light-triggered production of more than one reactive oxygen species, specifically [[singlet oxygen]] (`1O2`) and [[hydroxyl radical]] (`·OH`), using separate solution-phase assays and ESR measurements under 425 nm irradiation. This supports describing CA-Re as a dual reactive oxygen species photogenerator in the context of the reported experiments. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:196-218, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:430-433]

## Evidence for dual ROS photogeneration

Singlet oxygen generation was measured with ABDA as a capture agent. In this assay, CA-Re solutions in PBS or acetonitrile were adjusted to an absorbance of about 1.5 at 425 nm, mixed with 100 μM ABDA, irradiated with a 425 nm laser at 20 mW/cm² for increasing times, and monitored by UV-Vis spectroscopy. The supporting figures describe ABDA decay sensitized by CA-Re, and the methods state that singlet oxygen quantum yields were calculated relative to `[Ru(bpy)3]Cl2`. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:196-202, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:430-431]

Hydroxyl radical generation was assessed with hydroxyphenyl fluorescein (HPF), which becomes green fluorescent in the presence of `·OH`. The method used CA-Re at 20 μM and HPF at 10 μM in PBS, followed by 425 nm irradiation at 20 mW/cm² for up to 120 seconds and fluorescence readout from 500 to 640 nm after excitation at 490 nm. A dedicated figure reports fluorescence spectra for CA-Re in aerated PBS using HPF as the `·OH` probe. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:203-208, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:432-432]

Electron spin resonance experiments provided an additional ROS readout. The protocol used DMPO as a spin trap with CA-Re in methanol, followed by 425 nm irradiation for 5 minutes before ESR characterization, and the corresponding figure reports ESR signals obtained under these conditions. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:214-218, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:433-433]

## Photophysical context

The supporting information links CA-Re’s ROS photochemistry to its excited-state behavior. Transient absorption spectroscopy was performed on deaerated dichloromethane solutions after 355 nm pulsed excitation, and theoretical calculations used DFT/TDDFT to analyze ground and excited states. The reported excited-state assignments include both intraligand charge transfer (`ILCT`) and metal-to-ligand charge transfer (`MLCT`) transitions, while the discussion of emission spectra attributes a band near 685 nm to `3MLCT` and a 450-550 nm band to the organic chromophore `IP-TT`, with solvent-dependent dominance. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:184-195, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:410-416, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:498-507]

Table S2 also explicitly lists a `3O2 → 1O2` entry at 710.31 nm, consistent with the reported singlet oxygen sensitization measurements. Together with the separate HPF-based hydroxyl radical assay, the source presents CA-Re as a photosensitizer capable of generating at least two distinct reactive oxygen species under illumination. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:200-202, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:203-208, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:507-507]

## Cellular ROS characterization

In cells, overall intracellular ROS was measured by DCFH-DA after 24 hours of CA-Re treatment followed by 425 nm irradiation. To distinguish ROS contributions, the authors also applied selective scavengers before irradiation: sodium pyruvate for hydrogen peroxide, D-mannitol for hydroxyl radicals, tiron for superoxide anions, sodium azide for singlet oxygen, ebselen for peroxynitrite, and trolox for lipid peroxides. This experimental design shows that CA-Re’s photoinduced oxidative response in cells was analyzed as a multi-species ROS process rather than a single-species event. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:259-280]

Additional cellular assays tracked specific oxidative consequences linked to this photochemistry. Superoxide anion detection used DHE after CA-Re incubation and 425 nm irradiation, and intracellular lipid peroxidation was quantified by an MDA assay after light treatment. Supporting figures also include ROS analysis by flow cytometry and DHE imaging in normoxic MDA-MB-231 cells. ^[2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:282-293, 2022-su-caix-anchored-rhenium-photosensitizer-evokes-pyroptosis-si.md:456-458]
