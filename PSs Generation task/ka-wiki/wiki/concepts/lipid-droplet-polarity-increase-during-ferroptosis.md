# Lipid droplet polarity increase during ferroptosis

Lipid droplet polarity increase during [[ferroptosis]] refers to the reported rise in lipid droplet (LD) microenvironment polarity observed in a ferroptosis model, together with a weakening of the normal polarity gradient within LDs and increasing similarity between LD and cytoplasmic polarity. The source presents this as a previously unreported aspect of LD behavior during ferroptotic progression. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:46-59, polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:79-84]

## Background

The study frames ferroptosis as a form of regulated cell death driven by accumulation of lipid-based reactive oxygen species after deactivation of [[GPX4]]. Because excessive lipid hydroperoxides are expected to trigger rapid responses in LDs, the authors argue that LD structure and polarity should change during ferroptosis, although they note that such LD polarity changes had not previously been monitored directly. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:61-84]

The authors further note that ferroptotic cells show distinctive subcellular morphology, while the nucleus generally retains structural integrity, unlike in [[apoptosis]]. On that basis, they use nucleus imaging as a reference while tracking LD behavior, with the goal of distinguishing ferroptosis-related LD changes from nuclear alterations characteristic of apoptosis. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:111-143]

## How it was monitored

The source used the dual-targeted fluorescent probe `CQPP`, which localizes to LDs and the nucleus and responds to polarity through both ratiometric fluorescence emission and fluorescence lifetime changes. In living HT-1080 cells, green CQPP emission was mainly distributed on LDs, while red emission co-localized with the nucleus, enabling simultaneous observation of both compartments under the same excitation conditions. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:144-171, polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:304-342]

CQPP was characterized as polarity-sensitive because increasing environmental polarity reduced its short-wavelength emission and enhanced its long-wavelength emission, while also shortening fluorescence lifetime over a dielectric range considered relevant to LDs. The authors therefore use both ratiometric imaging and [[fluorescence lifetime imaging|FLIM]] to track polarity changes in situ. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:212-267]

## Observations during ferroptosis

In untreated HT-1080 cells, ratiometric imaging showed that LDs had significantly lower polarity than the surrounding cytoplasm, with a polarity gradient running from the LD core to the surface. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:351-354]

After erastin treatment for 6 hours, green LD-channel fluorescence decreased and red cytoplasmic fluorescence increased slightly, which the authors interpret as evidence of increased polarity. These channel changes indicated that CQPP could monitor erastin-induced LD polarity changes during ferroptosis. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:355-365]

In a time-response model using [[RSL3]], green fluorescence gradually decreased, punctate LD protrusions gradually diffused into the cytoplasm, and red cytoplasmic fluorescence increased slightly over time, while nuclear morphology and nuclear red fluorescence did not change obviously. The resulting ratiometric images showed that the polarity gradient from LD core to surface became less obvious as ferroptosis progressed, accompanied by gradual homogenization of polarity between LDs and the cytoplasm. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:366-385]

## FLIM evidence

FLIM measurements supported the ratiometric results. In untreated cells, CQPP lifetime distributions separated into a cytoplasmic LD-associated region at `0.5-1.5 ns` and a nuclear DNA-associated region at `1.5-2.5 ns`, with representative fitted values of `1.46 ns` for LDs and `2.11 ns` for nucleic acids. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:406-419]

After 6 hours of erastin treatment, a new short-lifetime peak appeared at `0.79 ns`, and the LD-associated region shifted to about `0.77 ns`, while the nuclear lifetime region did not change obviously. The authors interpret these findings as further confirmation that LD polarity increases significantly during ferroptosis. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:420-425]

The study contrasts this with apoptosis induced by cisplatin, where nuclear fluorescence lifetime and nuclear morphology changed clearly, but LD changes were not obvious. Using the nucleus as an internal reference, the authors argue that LD polarity increases progressively during ferroptosis without the nuclear changes typical of apoptosis. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:426-435]

## Significance

The source concludes that ferroptosis is associated with an obvious increase in LD polarity detectable by both fluorescence imaging and FLIM. It also emphasizes two related features of this process: weakening of the LD internal polarity gradient and increasing polarity homogeneity between LDs and the cytoplasm. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:454-460, polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:584-588]
