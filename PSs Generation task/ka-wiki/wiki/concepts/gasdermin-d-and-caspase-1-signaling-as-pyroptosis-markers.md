# Gasdermin D and caspase-1 signaling as pyroptosis markers

[[Pyroptosis]] in this source is assessed using a combination of biochemical, cytokine-release, and flow-cytometric readouts. Within that set of markers, [[Gasdermin D]] and [[caspase-1]] are treated as core pyroptosis-related proteins alongside IL-18 and IL-1β. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:223-231, 2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:371-373]

## Overview

The supporting information describes western blot analysis of cells under different treatments, including control, TBD-1C, TBD-2C, and TBD-3C, with or without light irradiation. In these blots, the primary antibodies included anti-mGSDMD, anti-cleaved mGSDMD, anti-caspase-1, and anti-cleaved caspase-1, indicating that both total and cleaved forms of Gasdermin D and caspase-1 were monitored as pyroptosis-associated signals. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:223-231]

A supplementary figure caption further groups GSDMD, caspase-1, IL-18, and IL-1β as pyroptosis-relevant proteins in a protein-protein interaction analysis. In the same supplementary section, apoptosis-related proteins are listed separately as caspase-3, Bax, and Bcl-2, which distinguishes the Gasdermin D/caspase-1 axis from the apoptosis markers examined in parallel. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:371-374]

## Gasdermin D as a marker

The source specifically measured both mGSDMD and cleaved mGSDMD by western blot. This means Gasdermin D was not used only as a presence marker; its cleaved form was also tracked as part of the pyroptosis analysis workflow. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:223-231]

Gasdermin D is also named in the supplementary pyroptosis protein network together with caspase-1, IL-18, and IL-1β. In the context of this study, that places GSDMD within the set of proteins used to characterize pyroptosis-related signaling rather than apoptosis. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:371-374]

## Caspase-1 as a marker

The western blot methods list antibodies against both caspase-1 and cleaved caspase-1. As with Gasdermin D, the assay design therefore captures both the total protein and its cleaved form when evaluating pyroptosis-associated signaling. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:223-231]

Caspase-1 is also included in the supplementary figure’s pyroptosis-related protein set, together with GSDMD, IL-18, and IL-1β. This presentation supports its use in the study as one of the principal molecular markers for identifying pyroptosis-related responses. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:371-374]

## Relationship to other pyroptosis readouts

The study did not rely on Gasdermin D and caspase-1 alone. Pyroptosis was additionally analyzed by lactate dehydrogenase release into culture supernatants, and IL-18 plus IL-1β in supernatants were quantified by ELISA. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:213-216]

Flow cytometry provided another complementary readout. The source states that annexin V can label pyroptotic cells because membrane rupture allows it to diffuse into cells, while propidium iodide stains cells with membrane rupture; cells positive for both annexin V-FITC and PI were interpreted as pyroptotic. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:232-240]

Morphology was also used to support pyroptosis assignment. Supplementary figures describe membrane expansion, release during pyroptosis, and scanning electron microscopy features including pyroptotic bodies, with image annotations distinguishing cells with and without pyroptosis. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:358-370]

## Distinguishing pyroptosis from apoptosis

The experimental design explicitly measured apoptosis-associated proteins in parallel with pyroptosis-associated proteins. Alongside the antibodies for Gasdermin D and caspase-1, the western blot panel included caspase-3, cleaved caspase-3, Bax, and Bcl-2. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:223-231]

The supplementary figures reinforce this separation by labeling GSDMD, caspase-1, IL-18, and IL-1β as pyroptosis-relevant, while listing caspase-3, Bax, and Bcl-2 as apoptosis-relevant proteins. In this source, Gasdermin D and caspase-1 signaling therefore function as part of the marker set used to distinguish pyroptosis from apoptotic cell death. ^[2021-wu-activation-of-pyroptosis-by-membrane-anchoring-aie-photosensitizer-si.md:371-374]
