# PKM2 overexpression suppresses PDT-induced pyroptosis

In esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC), the study reports that [[photodynamic therapy]] (PDT) induces [[pyroptosis]] through a [[PKM2]]/[[caspase-8]]/[[caspase-3]]/[[GSDME]] axis. Within that model, PKM2 functions upstream of caspase-8, caspase-3, and GSDME cleavage, and its downregulation by PDT is associated with pyroptotic cell death. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:28-33, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:491-498]

## Overview

PKM2 was described as highly expressed in many tumors, including esophageal cancer, and its mRNA levels in ESCC were reported to correlate with those of caspase-3, caspase-8, and GSDME. The authors then tested whether enforced PKM2 expression changes the pyroptotic response to PDT in ESCC cells. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:91-99, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:418-441]

## Experimental context

To examine this question, the investigators generated stable PKM2-overexpressing cells using lentiviral constructs and compared them with control cells under PDT treatment. They evaluated pyroptosis-related outcomes by phase-contrast microscopy, Hoechst 33342/PI staining, CCK-8 assay, LDH release, IL-1β ELISA, and western blotting for pathway components. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:118-127, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:163-170, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:183-205, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:206-227, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:442-453]

## Effect of PKM2 overexpression on PDT-induced pyroptosis

Stable PKM2 overexpression suppressed the visible pyroptotic response to PDT in Eca109 and Ec9706 cells. The study states that pyroptotic morphology was abrogated by PKM2 overexpression, and Hoechst 33342/PI staining showed reduced cell swelling in PKM2-overexpressing cells after PDT. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:442-447]

PKM2 overexpression also reduced biochemical markers associated with PDT-induced pyroptosis. Compared with control groups, OE-PKM2 cells showed decreased PDT-induced cell death by CCK-8 analysis, along with reduced LDH release and reduced IL-1β liberation. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:448-450, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:574-577]

At the signaling level, western blotting showed that N-GSDME and cleaved caspase-3 and caspase-8 were detected only in the PDT-treated OE-PKM2 group, indicating that these pyroptosis-related signals were not fully abolished but were present under PDT despite PKM2 overexpression. In the authors’ interpretation, PKM2 overexpression reversed pyroptosis during PDT treatment, supporting a model in which PDT-induced PKM2 downregulation promotes downstream caspase activation and GSDME cleavage. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:451-453, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:546-549]

## In vivo evidence

The study also tested this relationship in mouse xenograft models using control, [[GSDME]]-silenced, and PKM2-overexpressing cells. In serum from PDT-treated mice, LDH and IL-1β increased relative to control, but these increases were lower in the OE-PKM2 + PDT group than in the PDT-only group. Tumor analyses further showed lower activated caspase-3 and lower N-GSDME in OE-PKM2 tumors exposed to PDT than in tumors treated with PDT alone. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:470-489]

## Interpretation

Taken together, the reported data support the specific conclusion that PKM2 overexpression suppresses PDT-induced pyroptosis in ESCC models. In the framework proposed by the paper, PDT promotes pyroptosis by downregulating PKM2, which is linked to activation of caspase-8, activation of caspase-3, and GSDME cleavage; forced PKM2 expression counteracts that response. ^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:496-499, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:546-549, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-595]
