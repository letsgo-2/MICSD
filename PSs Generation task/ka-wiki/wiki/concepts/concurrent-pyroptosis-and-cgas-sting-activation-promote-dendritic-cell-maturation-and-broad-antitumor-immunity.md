# Concurrent pyroptosis and cGAS-STING activation promote dendritic-cell maturation and broad antitumor immunity

This study describes a photo-controllable tumor nanotheranostic strategy designed to activate [[Pyroptosis]] and the [[cGAS-STING pathway]] at the same time in immunotolerant breast tumors. The platform combines a photosensitizer-loaded oxidation-sensitive nanoparticle (`NP1`) with a decitabine-loaded liposomal formulation (`NP2`). In the authors' model, decitabine restores intracellular [[STING]] and [[GSDME]] expression, while photodynamic treatment drives caspase-3 activation and mitochondrial damage, together enabling simultaneous pyroptosis and cGAS-STING signaling. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:59-71, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:120-132]

## Rationale

The paper frames both pyroptosis induction and cGAS-STING activation as ways to strengthen innate immunity in solid tumors that remain poorly responsive to immunotherapy because of an immunosuppressive microenvironment and low immunogenicity. Pyroptosis is described as an immunogenic form of cell death in which caspase activity cleaves gasdermins to generate membrane-penetrating fragments that cause cell swelling and release immunostimulatory contents, while cGAS-STING signaling is presented as a cytosolic DNA-sensing pathway that promotes type I interferon production and other immune-stimulated genes. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:74-109]

A key barrier identified in the study is that both `DFNA5`/[[GSDME]] and `TMEM173`/[[STING]] are downregulated in breast cancer and that this downregulation is strongly associated with promoter methylation. In MCF-7 cells, among the tested DNA methyltransferase inhibitors, decitabine was the one that increased both GSDME and STING expression, leading the authors to use it as the epigenetic component of the combination system. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:284-316]

## Mechanism of concurrent activation

`NP1`-mediated photodynamic treatment generated intracellular ROS, reduced mitochondrial membrane potential, increased cleaved caspase-3, and promoted cytosolic escape of mitochondrial DNA in MCF-7 cells. However, `NP1` plus light alone did not significantly upregulate the key proteins involved in pyroptosis or the cGAS-STING pathway, despite producing upstream signals relevant to both processes. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:224-257]

When `NP2` was added before photodynamic treatment, the dominant death phenotype shifted. Compared with `NP1` plus light alone, the combined `NP1+NP2+L` condition showed morphology characteristic of pyroptosis rather than apoptosis, higher GSDME-N generation, and increased release of ATP and LDH into the supernatant. In parallel, phosphorylation of STING, [[TBK1]], and [[IRF3]] increased, supporting concurrent activation of the cGAS-STING pathway. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:364-406]

## Dendritic-cell maturation

The study directly links the combined treatment to enhanced [[dendritic cell|dendritic-cell]] maturation. In vitro, the proinflammatory cytokines and immunogenic factors released after concurrent pyroptosis and STING-pathway activation increased the proportion of `CD80+ CD86+` mature dendritic cells, indicating stronger innate immune priming by treated breast tumor cells. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:401-406]

The in vivo data in orthotopic 4T1 tumor-bearing mice were consistent with that result. After `NP1+NP2+L` treatment, the proportion of mature dendritic cells increased from about `44.2%` to `66.9%` in tumor-draining lymph nodes and from about `21.1%` to `35.9%` within tumors relative to the PBS group. The authors also observed an increased frequency of [[natural killer cell|NK cells]], which they discuss together with dendritic cells as antigen-presentation-associated innate immune populations responsive to the released inflammatory signals. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:536-544]

## Broad antitumor immunity

In tumor tissues from treated mice, `NP1+NP2+L` increased phosphorylation of STING and its downstream effectors `TBK1` and `IRF3`, while also increasing GSDME-N expression, confirming concurrent pathway activation in vivo. Serum `TNF-alpha`, `IL-6`, and `IFN-beta` were all higher in the combination-treatment group than in the comparison groups, consistent with systemic inflammatory and innate immune activation. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:514-535]

The reported immune response extended beyond dendritic-cell maturation. Within tumors, `NP1+NP2+L` increased both `CD8+` and `CD4+` T-cell infiltration, raised the proportion of `IFN-gamma+ CD8+` tumor-infiltrating T cells by `12.8-fold` versus PBS, and reduced immunosuppressive [[myeloid-derived suppressor cell|MDSCs]] and [[regulatory T cell|Treg]] populations. The authors interpret these findings as evidence that concurrent pyroptosis and cGAS-STING activation elicit both innate and adaptive antitumor immunity. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:545-555, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:568-570]

The study also reports stronger memory-associated immune readouts after the combination treatment. In spleens, the percentages of effector memory T cells and central memory T cells rose from about `2.96%` to `9.81%` and from about `0.71%` to `1.52%`, respectively, after `NP1+NP2+L`, suggesting the establishment of longer-term antitumor immune memory alongside the acute response. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:556-570]

## Antitumor efficacy

In the orthotopic 4T1 mouse model, the combination treatment produced the strongest tumor control among the tested groups. Over 15 days, `NP1` alone and `NP1+NP2` showed little benefit, `NP1+L` produced `29.8%` tumor inhibition, and `NP1+NP2+L` achieved a reported tumor regression rate of `70.9%`. Tumor weight and histologic analyses supported the superior antitumor effect of the concurrent-activation strategy, while body weight, organ histology, and serum biochemistry did not show evident toxicity differences across groups. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:463-490]

## Significance

The authors present this approach as a way to convert an immunologically "cold" breast tumor into a more immunogenic "hot" tumor by combining [[epigenetic therapy]] with [[photodynamic therapy]]. In their conclusion, concurrent activation of pyroptosis and cGAS-STING signaling is proposed to drive proinflammatory cytokine release, mature dendritic cells and NK cells, provoke cytotoxic T-cell-mediated antitumor immunity, and establish long-term immune memory. ^[simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:120-132, simultaneous-activation-of-pyroptosis-and-cgassting-pathway-with-epigenetic-photodynamic-nanotheranostic-for-enhanced-tumor-photoimmunotherapy.md:595-621]
