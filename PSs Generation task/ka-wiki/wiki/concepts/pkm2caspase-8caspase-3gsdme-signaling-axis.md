# PKM2/caspase-8/caspase-3/GSDME signaling axis

The **PKM2/caspase-8/caspase-3/GSDME signaling axis** is a proposed mechanism by which [[photodynamic therapy]] (PDT) induces [[pyroptosis]] in [[esophageal squamous cell carcinoma]] (ESCC). In the cited study, PDT was reported to inhibit [[PKM2]], leading to activation of [[caspase-8]] and [[caspase-3]], cleavage of [[GSDME]], release of the N-terminal GSDME fragment, and pyroptotic cell death. The authors describe this axis as a key pathway underlying PDT-induced tumor cell death in ESCC.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:23-32, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:490-499, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-595]

## Overview

PDT uses a photosensitizer and visible light to generate reactive oxygen species that induce cancer cell death. In ESCC, the study investigated how this treatment connects metabolic regulation and programmed cell death, focusing on PKM2, caspase-8, caspase-3, and GSDME.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:45-56, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:91-100, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:101-103]

Within the study’s model, GSDME functions as the terminal effector of pyroptosis. The paper notes that, after stimulation, GSDME can be cleaved by activated caspase-3 to release its N-terminal domain, which forms pores in the plasma membrane, causes membrane rupture, and promotes release of intracellular contents and pro-inflammatory mediators.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:79-90]

## Proposed signaling sequence

The pathway proposed in ESCC is: **PDT -> PKM2 downregulation -> caspase-8 activation -> caspase-3 activation -> GSDME cleavage -> pyroptosis**. The abstract and discussion both state that PDT may inhibit PKM2 and consequently activate caspase-8 and caspase-3, ultimately releasing N-GSDME and triggering pyroptosis.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:28-31, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:496-498]

Correlative analyses in the GEPIA database supported relationships among members of this axis in esophageal cancer samples. The study reports that GSDME expression was related to caspase-3 and caspase-8 expression, that caspase-3 expression was related to caspase-8 expression, and that PKM2 expression was related to caspase-3, caspase-8, and GSDME expression.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:380-384, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:440-441]

## Evidence for pathway components

### GSDME in ESCC

The study found that GSDME was overexpressed in ESCC relative to normal esophageal tissue across database analyses and patient samples. Higher GSDME expression was observed in GEPIA, TCGA, and Oncomine analyses, and this was consistent with RT-qPCR and western blot results from tumor versus normal tissues.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:267-281]

### PDT induces pyroptotic features

After PDT, Eca109 and Ec9706 ESCC cells showed morphological features associated with pyroptosis, including peripheral swelling, large membrane bubbles, nuclear pyknosis, cytoplasmic swelling, membrane pore formation, increased propidium iodide positivity, elevated LDH release, and increased levels of TNF-alpha, IL-1alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6, and IFN-gamma. Similar effects were also observed with additional photosensitizers tested in the study.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:282-317]

### GSDME cleavage rather than GSDMD/caspase-1 signaling

The authors did not detect N-GSDMD or cleaved caspase-1 in PDT-treated ESCC cells, but they did observe increased N-terminal GSDME and cleaved caspase-3. They interpret these results as evidence that PDT-induced pyroptosis in this setting proceeds through the caspase-3/GSDME route rather than a GSDMD/caspase-1 pathway.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:318-326, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:507-518]

### Role of caspase-3 and caspase-8

Pharmacologic inhibition of caspase-3 or caspase-8 reduced the pyroptosis ratio, decreased formation of pyroptotic-like cells, improved CCK-8 readouts, reduced LDH release, and inhibited IL-1beta and N-GSDME release in PDT-treated cells. The study also reports that caspase-3 inhibition did not prevent caspase-8 cleavage, whereas caspase-8 inhibition suppressed caspase-3 cleavage, supporting a model in which caspase-8 acts upstream of caspase-3 in this axis.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:385-405, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:527-532]

### Role of PKM2

PKM2 was reported to be elevated in ESCC in multiple public datasets and ESCC cell lines. When PKM2 was stably overexpressed, PDT-induced pyroptotic morphology was reduced, cell swelling decreased, cell death was attenuated, LDH and IL-1beta release were inhibited, and N-GSDME plus cleaved caspase-3 and caspase-8 were detected only in the PDT-treated overexpression group. The authors interpret these findings as evidence that PKM2 downregulation contributes to activation of the downstream caspase-8/caspase-3/GSDME cascade.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:418-453, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:546-549]

## Functional perturbation of the axis

Silencing GSDME abrogated pyroptotic morphology after PDT and reduced swelling in ESCC cells, while caspase-3 activation remained unaffected. Cell survival in PDT-treated GSDME-silenced cells was similar to vector controls, although LDH and IL-1beta remained slightly elevated. The authors use this to place GSDME downstream of caspase-3 in PDT-induced pyroptosis.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:325-355, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:512-518]

The glycolysis inhibitor [[2-DG]] did not enhance pyroptosis in combination with PDT. Instead, the combined treatment abrogated pyroptotic morphology and inhibited LDH, IL-1beta, and N-GSDME release, while showing no obvious effect on PKM2 expression relative to PDT alone. The study therefore distinguishes inhibition of glycolysis from direct modulation of PKM2 expression within this signaling context.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:454-467, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:552-565]

## In vivo findings

In nude mouse xenograft models using control, GSDME-silenced, and PKM2-overexpressing cells, PDT increased serum LDH and IL-1beta relative to controls. These increases were reduced in the Si-GSDME, 2-DG, and OE-PKM2 groups treated with PDT. Immunohistochemistry showed increased cleaved caspase-3 and decreased PKM2 in PDT-treated groups, while activated caspase-3 was lower in the 2-DG, Si-GSDME, and OE-PKM2 groups than in the PDT-only group. Western blotting likewise showed lower N-GSDME in these modified groups than in the PDT-only group.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:468-489]

## Interpretation and significance

In this source, the PKM2/caspase-8/caspase-3/GSDME axis is presented as a mechanistic link between cancer cell metabolism, caspase signaling, and pyroptotic execution during PDT in ESCC. The study frames PKM2 as an upstream regulator connected to the [[Warburg effect]], and GSDME as the downstream executor responsible for membrane rupture and inflammatory mediator release.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:91-100, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:539-566, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-632]

The authors conclude that this pathway provides a mechanistic basis for PDT-induced pyroptosis in ESCC and identifies PKM2 as a potentially relevant target in this context. They also report that combining 2-DG with PDT increased cytotoxicity but inhibited pyroptosis, indicating that enhanced tumor cell killing and enhanced pyroptotic signaling were not equivalent outcomes in their experiments.^[2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:552-565, 2021-li-photodynamic-therapy-induces-esophageal-carcinoma-pyroptosis-via-pkm2-caspase-gsdme.md:587-632]
