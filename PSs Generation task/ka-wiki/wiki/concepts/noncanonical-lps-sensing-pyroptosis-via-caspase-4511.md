# Noncanonical LPS-sensing pyroptosis via caspase-4/5/11

Noncanonical LPS-sensing pyroptosis is a [[pyroptosis]] pathway in which intracellular lipopolysaccharide (LPS) from gram-negative bacteria activates inflammatory caspases distinct from the canonical [[inflammasome]] route. In this pathway, the key caspases are caspase-11 in mice and caspase-4 and caspase-5 in humans. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:150-154, pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:180-192]

## Core mechanism

The source states that, unlike caspase-1, the CARD domain of mouse caspase-11 can directly recognize and bind LPS from gram-negative bacteria. LPS-driven oligomerization activates caspase-11 protease activity, and active caspase-11 then cleaves [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD]] to generate the pore-forming N-terminal fragment, GSDMD-NT, which forms membrane pores and induces pyroptosis. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:180-185]

In humans, the equivalent noncanonical pathway is mediated by caspase-4 and caspase-5. The source states that LPS can directly stimulate human caspase-4 and caspase-5 and thereby trigger the noncanonical inflammasome pathway. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:180-192]

## Relationship to the canonical pathway

The noncanonical pathway is described as distinct from the canonical inflammasome pathway because of its mode of activation. Canonical pyroptosis relies mainly on inflammasome complexes such as [[NLRP3 inflammasome|NLRP3]], [[NLRP1]], [[NLRC4]], [[AIM2]], and pyrin, which recruit ASC and pro-caspase-1 to activate caspase-1, cleave GSDMD, and process pro-IL-1beta and pro-IL-18. By contrast, the noncanonical pathway is centered on direct LPS sensing by caspase-11 in mice or caspase-4/5 in humans. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:125-146, pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:150-154, pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:180-192]

At the same time, the noncanonical pathway can feed into canonical signaling. The source explains that LPS activation of caspase-11 can open [[pannexin-1]], a nonselective macroporous protein channel. Pannexin-1 opening promotes K+ efflux, which activates the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] and thereby induces caspase-1-mediated pyroptosis through the canonical pathway. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:186-190]

## Downstream outcomes

As part of pyroptotic execution, GSDMD-derived membrane pores are the central terminal event shared by canonical and noncanonical inflammasome-induced pyroptosis. The source also notes that IL-1beta and IL-18 are released through GSDMD pores, and its figure legend for the noncanonical pathway depicts NLRP3 activation by K+ efflux followed by activation of ASC and caspase-1, with IL-1beta and IL-18 released through the pores. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:143-149, pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:272-274]

More broadly, this pathway sits within the modern definition of pyroptosis as a form of regulated cell death that depends on [[gasdermin family]] pore formation in the plasma membrane and is often, though not always, associated with inflammatory caspase activation. ^[pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:104-109, pyroptosis-a-new-breakthrough-in-cancer-treatment.md:166-174]
