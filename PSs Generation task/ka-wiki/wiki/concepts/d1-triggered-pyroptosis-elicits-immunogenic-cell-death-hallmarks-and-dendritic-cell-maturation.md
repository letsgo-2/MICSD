# D1-triggered pyroptosis elicits immunogenic cell death hallmarks and dendritic-cell maturation

`D1` is a tumor-cell-membrane-targeted photosensitive dimer reported to induce [[pyroptosis]] under light irradiation through combined [[photodynamic therapy]] and [[photothermal therapy]]. In the study, the authors frame pyroptosis as a lytic, immunogenic form of cell death that can intensify [[immunogenic cell death|ICD]] by promoting release of tumor-associated antigens and damage-associated molecular patterns, and they present D1 as a system designed to use this process to strengthen antitumor immunity. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:17-31, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:117-131, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:140-163]

In the authors' description of the mechanism, D1 localizes to the tumor-cell membrane, generates type-I reactive oxygen species under light, and triggers pyroptotic membrane damage that is further reinforced by its photothermal effect. They state that this process promotes release of cell contents and inflammatory cytokines, supports tumor-specific antigen production, and promotes [[dendritic cell maturation]], leading to activated T-cell proliferation and systemic antitumor immunity. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:149-163]

## Pyroptosis induction

D1 showed strong membrane targeting in cancer cells, with colocalization against the membrane dye `DiO` and a reported Pearson correlation coefficient of `0.90`. In 4T1 cells, light exposure produced strong intracellular ROS signals, while dark toxicity was negligible across `0-50 uM`; under `520 nm` irradiation, however, cell survival decreased sharply and overall cell mortality in the D1 group reached `99.1%`. Flow-cytometric analysis indicated that many of these cells underwent pyroptosis after D1 plus light treatment. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:288-305, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:306-363]

The study further documents pyroptotic morphology and signaling in D1-treated 4T1 cells. Upon irradiation, cells swelled rather than shrank, bubbles emerged from the plasma membrane, and membrane damage allowed uptake of Trypan Blue. Western blotting showed increased cleaved [[Gasdermin D|GSDMD-N]] and cleaved [[caspase-1]], while supernatants showed release of [[lactate dehydrogenase|LDH]], `IL-1beta`, and `IL-18`. The authors interpret these findings as evidence that ROS generated by D1 activates caspase-1 at the cell membrane, causing GSDMD cleavage, pore formation, and pyroptosis, with photothermal activity accelerating the process. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:382-415]

## Immunogenic cell death hallmarks

The paper identifies `CRT` and `HMGB1` as key markers used to assess pyroptosis-mediated [[immunogenic cell death|ICD]]. In D1-stained 4T1 cells under light irradiation, `CRT` exposure on the cell surface increased markedly, while `HMGB1`, initially localized in the nucleus, redistributed throughout the cells after treatment. The authors state that these results show that light-induced pyroptosis by D1 efficiently induced ICD and initiated adaptive immune responses. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:416-425]

The source also places these findings in a broader ICD framework. It describes ICD as involving release of damage-associated molecular patterns such as `CRT`, `HMGB1`, and `ATP`, which act as natural adjuvants to promote immune recognition, dendritic-cell maturation, and antigen delivery to T cells. In its conclusion, the paper states that tumor cells undergoing ICD produce DAMPs that promote dendritic-cell maturation, T-cell clonal expansion, adaptive antitumor effects, and immune memory. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:53-67, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:618-621]

## Dendritic-cell maturation and immune activation

In the study's proposed immune cascade, pyroptosis-driven ICD after D1 treatment promotes maturation of dendritic cells and downstream T-cell responses. The article explicitly states that DAMP production during ICD can promote dendritic-cell maturation and that, in the D1 system, maturation of dendritic cells is linked to proliferation of activated T cells and systemic antitumor immunity. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:61-66, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:157-165, pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:618-620]

This was supported in vivo by immune analysis of primary 4T1 tumors on day 5 of treatment. Flow cytometry showed upregulation of `CD103`, described here as a dendritic-cell surface biomarker, in the `D1+L` group, which the authors interpret as indicating that a large number of mature dendritic cells were present in the tumor. The same analysis showed high `CD4` and `CD8` T-cell signals in the `D1+L` group, consistent with formation of helper and effector T cells. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:537-565]

## In vivo antitumor context

The immune effects associated with D1 were examined in bilateral axillary 4T1 tumor-bearing BALB/c mice to test whether pyroptosis-mediated photoimmunotherapy could produce systemic antitumor effects. In this model, the light-irradiated `D1` group completely regressed the primary tumor within 7 days and completely inhibited growth of the untreated distant tumor, which the authors attribute to systemic immunity induced by photoimmunotherapy. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:494-509]

Additional immune readouts were consistent with systemic activation. After D1 photodynamic therapy, the proportion of `CD8+` T cells reached `8.37%` in primary tumors and `22.1%` in distant tumors, and `CD44+/CD62L-` memory-marker expression on distant-tumor `CD3+CD8+` T cells reached `22.4%`. Serum `IL-6`, `IL-18`, `IL-1beta`, `TNF-alpha`, and `IFN-gamma` were also significantly higher in the `D1+L` group than in controls, which the authors interpret as the strongest systemic antitumor immune response among the tested groups. ^[pyroptosismediated-synergistic-photodynamic-and-photothermal-immunotherapy-enabled-by-a-tumormembranetargeted-photosensitive-dimer.md:566-607]
