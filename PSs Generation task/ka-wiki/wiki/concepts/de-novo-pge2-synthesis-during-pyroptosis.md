# De novo PGE2 synthesis during pyroptosis

Pyroptotic cells can synthesize prostaglandin E2 (PGE2) de novo during pyroptosis. In the study, PGE2 release was linked to caspase-1 activation, cyclooxygenase activity, and gasdermin D pore formation, and it appeared late in the pyroptotic process rather than being present only before cell death. ^[oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:50-59, oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:993-1007]

The authors used macrophages undergoing pyroptosis without IL-1beta or IL-1alpha release, referred to as Pyro-1, and found that Pyro-1 supernatants contained PGE2 and other oxylipins. PGE2 was already detectable by 90 minutes after the trigger and increased by 120 minutes, while control cells did not show comparable membrane permeability or LDH release over the same time course. ^[oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:43-59, oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:508-510, oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:955-965]

COX inhibition experiments supported new synthesis during pyroptosis. Overnight treatment with aspirin, ibuprofen, NS-398, or indomethacin reduced PGE2 in Pyro-1 supernatants, and washing off the reversible inhibitors restored PGE2 release, while aspirin continued to block it. Adding aspirin as late as 60 minutes after the pyroptotic trigger still reduced PGE2, indicating that production and release occur during later stages of pyroptosis and depend more strongly on COX2 activity. ^[oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:731-743, oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:993-1006, oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:1877-1908]

The study also showed that PGE2 release depends on caspase-1 and gasdermin D pores. Direct activation of inducible caspase-1 in THP-1 cells led to PGE2 release, while Gsdmd knockout or disulfiram treatment blocked PGE2 release from pyroptotic macrophages. ^[oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:1007-1028, oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:968-976]

Functionally, Pyro-1 supernatants promoted wound closure and cell migration in vitro, and locally delivered Pyro-1 supernatants improved colonic wound repair in vivo. The authors concluded that PGE2 is a major component of the pyroptotic secretome and contributes substantially to its pro-repair activity, alongside other metabolites. ^[oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:231-254, oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:1029-1047, oxylipins-and-metabolites-from-pyroptotic-cells-act-as-promoters-of-tissue-repair.md:1555-1581]
