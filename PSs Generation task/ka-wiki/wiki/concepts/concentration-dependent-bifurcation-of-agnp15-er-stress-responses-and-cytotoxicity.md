# Concentration-dependent bifurcation of AgNP15 ER-stress responses and cytotoxicity

`AgNP15` exposure in human [[THP-1]] monocytes produced two distinct response regimes that depended on concentration. The study reports that low concentrations triggered an ER-stress response without cell death, whereas higher concentrations produced an atypical ER-stress signature marked by rapid [[ATF-6]] degradation together with [[pyroptosis]] and [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:132-137]

## Overview

The authors describe `AgNP15` as inducing concentration-dependent differences in ER-stress markers in THP-1 cells. This response split was linked to divergent cellular outcomes: a nonlethal ER-stress program at lower doses and a cytotoxic inflammatory program at the highest tested dose. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:26-31, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:132-137]

After 24 hours, cell morphology remained unchanged at `1` or `5 ug/mL` `AgNP15`. At `10 ug/mL`, numerous cytosolic vacuoles appeared, and at `25 ug/mL` morphology was markedly altered in a pattern distinct from [[apoptosis]] induced by staurosporine. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:333-344]

## Low-concentration response

At lower concentrations, `AgNP15` activated selected ER-stress markers without evidence of pyroptotic death. The [[PERK]] pathway was strongly activated with `1-10 ug/mL`, low concentrations induced [[HSP-70]] synthesis, and `ATF-6` expression remained similar to control across `0-10 ug/mL`. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:358-373]

The study explicitly states that low concentrations induced an ER-stress response but did not lead to cell death. In the discussion, the authors further note that rapid `ATF-6` processing and [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation were not observed at lower concentrations, distinguishing this regime from the high-concentration response. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:132-137, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:693-701]

## High-concentration response

At `25 ug/mL`, `AgNP15` shifted to an atypical ER-stress pattern. After 24 hours, `IRE-1` was dephosphorylated, the `PERK` pathway returned to a level similar to basal, `HSP-90` was degraded, and `ATF-6` was degraded despite equal protein loading. `ATF-6` degradation occurred rapidly, being detectable after `1 h`. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:358-385]

This high-concentration state was associated with rapid cell death distinct from apoptosis. After `1 h`, cells treated with `25 ug/mL` `AgNP15` were both annexin-V-positive and PI-positive, unlike staurosporine-treated cells at the same time point. At this concentration, `AgNP15` induced processing of caspase-4 and caspase-7 but not caspase-3, and only staurosporine activated caspase-3 in the activity assay. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:386-456]

## Link to pyroptosis and inflammasome activation

The cytotoxic high-dose branch had the defining features of [[pyroptosis]]. Treatment with `25 ug/mL` `AgNP15` generated the `p20` caspase-1 fragment, significantly increased caspase-1 activity, and increased `IL-1beta` secretion in LPS-primed THP-1 cells from `9.5 +/- 2.8 pg/mL` to `298.2 +/- 52.7 pg/mL`. `AgNP15` also induced [[ASC]] pyroptosome formation, with close to `20%` of cells showing ASC aggregates under the tested conditions. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:485-540]

The study identified the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] as the relevant inflammasome complex in this response. `NLRP-3` knockdown significantly reduced `IL-1beta` secretion, and inhibition of caspase-1 with `YVAD-CHO` completely abolished `IL-1beta` secretion, showing that the high-concentration response depends on canonical inflammasome signaling. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:541-579]

## ATF-6 as the bifurcation point

The paper presents `ATF-6` as a key determinant separating the two concentration-dependent outcomes. `25 ug/mL` `AgNP15` induced rapid `ATF-6` processing or degradation within `1 h`, and the discussion states that this effect was not observed at lower concentrations, suggesting that `ATF-6` may act as a molecular switch for inflammasome activation. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:613-642, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:693-705]

Pharmacologic inhibition of `ATF-6` processing strengthened that interpretation. Blocking Site-2 protease, and to a lesser extent Site-1 protease, prevented `ATF-6` processing and reduced caspase-1 activity, `IL-1beta` secretion, and the proportion of PI-positive cells. The authors conclude from these results that `ATF-6` degradation is linked to activation of the [[NLRP3 inflammasome]] and induction of pyroptotic cell death. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:618-642, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:823-835]

## Cell-type context

The same study also examined primary human monocytes and macrophages, showing that baseline inflammasome readiness differed by cell type. Resting primary monocytes had detectable basal `pro-IL-1beta` and responded to `AgNP15` alone with significant `IL-1beta` production, whereas THP-1 cells and macrophages required LPS priming for stronger secretion. These results indicate that the concentration-dependent bifurcation was established in THP-1 cells, but its inflammatory output can vary with cellular context. ^[silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:643-679, silver-nanoparticles-induce-degradation-of-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-sensor-activating-transcription-factor-6-leading-to-activation-of-the-nlrp-3-inflammasome.md:761-776]
