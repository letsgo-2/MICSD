I’m pulling the claim set directly from the paper lines and will keep the page tight: title, mechanism, key experimental evidence, and a short sources section.# Akt/GSK3β/NF-κB signaling mediates aesculin's anti-pyroptotic effect

Aesculin (AES) was reported to suppress [[nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-in-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury|NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis in myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury]] (MIRI) and in oxygen-glucose deprivation/restoration (OGD/R)-injured neonatal rat cardiomyocytes. The study links this effect to increased Akt phosphorylation, increased GSK3β phosphorylation at Ser9, reduced NF-κB p65 phosphorylation at Ser536, and loss of protection when Akt was inhibited with MK-2206. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:45-50, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:428-475]

## Mechanism

In the authors’ model, AES activates Akt, which promotes phosphorylation of GSK3β at Ser9 and thereby restrains NF-κB p65 activation. This is presented as the signaling route through which AES disrupts NLRP3 inflammasome activation and reduces pyroptosis. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:437-453, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:548-569]

## Experimental evidence

In MIRI rats, AES reduced reperfusion arrhythmias, improved hemodynamic function, decreased myocardial injury markers, and lowered inflammatory mediators and NLRP3 inflammasome components, including cleaved caspase-1, cleaved GSDMD, and cleaved IL-1β. In OGD/R-treated NRCMs, AES improved cell viability, reduced cell death, and preserved mitochondrial membrane potential. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:318-416, aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:417-427]

Western blotting showed that OGD/R increased phospho-NF-κB p65 while decreasing phospho-GSK3β and phospho-Akt; AES reversed these changes. The paper also states that MK-2206 abolished AES-mediated protection and NLRP3 inflammasome suppression, supporting Akt as an upstream requirement for the anti-pyroptotic effect. ^[aesculin-suppresses-the-nlrp3-inflammasome-mediated-pyroptosis-via-the-aktgsk3ampx03b2nf-ampx03bab-pathway-to-mitigate-myocardial-ischemiareperfusion-injury.md:437-475]
