# Local TBD-3C photopyroptosis generates systemic control of distant orthotopic pancreatic tumors

Local [[photodynamic therapy|photodynamic]] treatment with the membrane-anchoring photosensitizer `TBD-3C` was reported to inhibit not only an irradiated subcutaneous pancreatic tumor but also a separate, non-irradiated orthotopic pancreatic tumor in the same mouse. In this study, the authors frame that distant-tumor control as a systemic antitumor immune effect arising from [[pyroptosis]] triggered locally by light-activated `TBD-3C`. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:24-31, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:596-621]

## Overview

The paper presents `TBD-3C` as a membrane-targeted, aggregation-induced emission photosensitizer that can induce pancreatic cancer-cell pyroptosis under light irradiation. The reported downstream consequences include macrophage M1 polarization, [[dendritic cell]] maturation, and activation of `CD8+` cytotoxic T lymphocytes, together with conversion of an immunosuppressive “cold” tumor microenvironment into a more immunogenic “hot” state. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:21-31, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:662-674]

Within that framework, the distant orthotopic tumor experiment was used to test whether a single local `TBD-3C` photodynamic intervention could generate systemic immune control beyond the illuminated lesion. The authors state that this strategy significantly inhibited pancreatic cancer growth even in orthotopic xenograft tumors that did not receive additional direct treatment. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:167-176, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:618-626]

## Experimental design

To model local and distant disease, the study used male `C57BL/6` mice bearing two `KPC-Luc` pancreatic tumors: a subcutaneous tumor in the right flank designated the local tumor and an orthotopic tumor established by injection at the tail of the pancreas designated the distant tumor. Each site received `5 x 10^5` cells, and tumor burden in both sites was followed by bioluminescence imaging with [[IVIS]]. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:602-615, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:857-874]

Seven days after implantation, mice were assigned to `PBS` or `TBD-3C` groups based on similar bioluminescence signal intensity. `PBS` or `TBD-3C` dissolved in `PBS` was then administered by intratumoral injection into the subcutaneous local tumor only, and 24 hours later the local tumor was exposed to white light irradiation at `40 mW cm^-2` for `10 min`. The orthotopic tumor was monitored but not locally irradiated in this setup. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:607-615, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:700-708, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:857-874]

## Tumor-control results

Bioluminescence imaging showed that mice receiving local `TBD-3C` photodynamic treatment had marked suppression of both the treated subcutaneous tumor and the untreated orthotopic tumor relative to the `PBS` group. The same pattern was supported by endpoint images and weights of excised tumors, which were lower in the treatment group for both tumor sites. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:614-626, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:700-706]

The authors interpret this dual-site inhibition as evidence that local pyroptosis-inducing PDT promoted effective host antitumor immune responses. In the paper’s conclusion, they describe `TBD-3C` as achieving high-efficiency tumor-growth suppression in both subcutaneous and orthotopic xenograft models. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:618-621, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:663-671]

## Evidence for systemic immune involvement

The study reports a splenic memory-T-cell shift consistent with systemic immune activation. In spleen, the proportion of effector memory T cells (`T_EM`, `CD44hi CD62Llow`) was significantly higher in the `TBD-3C` PDT group than in the `PBS` group, whereas naive T cells and central memory T cells did not differ between groups. The authors interpret this as an immune-memory effect associated with inhibition of distant tumors. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:627-636]

Flow-cytometric analysis of the orthotopic tumor microenvironment showed increased infiltration of `CD8+` T cells, `IFN-gamma+ CD8+` T cells, and `perforin+ CD8+` T cells after local `TBD-3C` PDT, alongside decreased infiltration of [[regulatory T cells|Tregs]]. These changes indicate a more cytotoxic and less immunosuppressive immune context within the distant lesion. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:637-645, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:704-706]

The distant orthotopic tumors also showed reduced frequencies of both monocytic and granulocytic [[myeloid-derived suppressor cells|MDSCs]] in the `CD11b+ CD45+` compartment after treatment. In the same tumors, the proportion of `M1` macrophages increased significantly, while the proportion of `M2` macrophages did not change significantly. The authors present these findings as a clear illustration of immune activation in the distant tumor microenvironment and as support for potential application against tumor metastasis. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:646-661, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:705-707]

## Mechanistic context from the same study

Earlier parts of the study provide the mechanistic basis for the distant-tumor effect. In KPC and Panc02 pancreatic cancer cells, `TBD-3C` localized mainly to the cell membrane and, under white-light irradiation, increased reactive oxygen species, caused cell swelling and membrane bubbling, increased LDH release, promoted `GSDMD` cleavage and caspase-1 activity, and raised the proportion of `PI`/annexin V double-positive cells. The authors interpret these combined findings as evidence that `TBD-3C` kills pancreatic cancer cells primarily through light-triggered pyroptosis rather than apoptosis. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:179-216, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:226-264]

The source further reports that pyroptotic tumor cells enhanced calreticulin exposure and, in co-culture systems, drove M1 polarization of macrophages, maturation of bone-marrow-derived dendritic cells, re-education of `M2`-like macrophages toward `M1`, and proliferation, maturation, and cytotoxic activation of `CD8+` T cells with increased perforin and granzyme B production. These in vitro findings align with the in vivo claim that local photopyroptosis can propagate broader antitumor immunity. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:267-275, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:326-374, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:387-436]

In subcutaneous tumors, `TBD-3C` PDT was also associated with in vivo markers of pyroptosis and [[immunogenic cell death]], including increased LDH release, `GSDMD` cleavage, higher `HMGB1` and calreticulin expression, and increased ATP secretion. The treated local tumors additionally showed lower `Ki-67`, reduced `alpha-SMA`, more `CD8+` T-cell infiltration, increased `CD69` and `CD103` on intratumoral `CD8+` T cells, decreased `PD-1` expression on cytotoxic T cells, and elevated serum `TNF-alpha` and `IFN-gamma`. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:463-524]

Mass-cytometry analysis in the local KPC tumor model further showed a broader shift from an immunosuppressive to an immune-active microenvironment after `TBD-3C` PDT. Reported changes included lower `PD-L1`, `Lag3`, and `Tim3`, higher `IFN-gamma` expression on `CD8+` T cells, increased `gamma delta T` cells, `NK` cells, and `CD4+ TEMRA` cells, and decreased `MDSCs`. The authors summarize these data as conversion of a “cold” tumor microenvironment into a “hot” one. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:525-595]

## Interpretation

In this study, “systemic control” refers to suppression of an untreated orthotopic pancreatic tumor after treatment was restricted to a separate local subcutaneous tumor. The reported combination of distant-tumor growth inhibition, increased splenic effector-memory T cells, greater cytotoxic `CD8+` T-cell infiltration in the orthotopic lesion, and reduced immunosuppressive cell populations supports the paper’s interpretation that local `TBD-3C` photopyroptosis generated a systemic antitumor immune response. ^[pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:618-621, pyroptosis-remodeling-tumor-microenvironment-to-enhance-pancreatic-cancer-immunotherapy-driven-by-membrane-anchoring-photosensitizer.md:627-661]
