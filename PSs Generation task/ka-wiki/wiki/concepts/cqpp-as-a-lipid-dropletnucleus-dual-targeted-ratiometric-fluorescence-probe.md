# CQPP as a lipid droplet/nucleus dual-targeted ratiometric fluorescence probe

CQPP is described as the first lipid droplet (LD) and nucleus dual-targeted ratiometric fluorescent probe for monitoring polarity changes during ferroptosis. The probe uses a donor-acceptor design to provide both ratiometric fluorescence emission and fluorescence lifetime signals, enabling polarity-sensitive imaging in living cells. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:46-59, polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:212-215, polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:446-457]

## Design

CQPP was built from a coumarin donor and a cationic quinolinium acceptor, with an extended aromatic system incorporating a 1-(pyridin-4-yl)piperazine group. The authors state that this combination was intended to support LD/nucleus dual targeting, nucleic-acid binding, and sensitive polarity response. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:145-168]

## Optical response

In solvent studies, CQPP showed two emission channels, with short-wavelength emission around 470 nm and long-wavelength emission around 670 nm. As polarity increased, the 470 nm emission decreased and the 670 nm emission increased, producing a ratiometric response. The probe also showed polarity-dependent fluorescence lifetime changes. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:190-215, polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:218-267]

CQPP also responded strongly to nucleic acids. In titration with ds26DNA, the 670 nm emission increased markedly, the binding constant was reported as `3.97 × 10^6 M^-1`, and the fluorescence lifetime increased from 0.63 ns to 2.10 ns after interaction with DNA. Molecular modeling suggested binding in the minor groove of DNA. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:268-283]

## Cell imaging

In HT-1080 cells, CQPP displayed green cytoplasmic fluorescence associated mainly with LDs and red nuclear fluorescence that co-localized with Hoechst 33342. The probe showed negligible cytotoxicity in LO2, A549, and HT-1080 cells over 24 h at 0-64 uM. The authors also report high photobleaching resistance and good stability in biological media. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:304-342]

## Monitoring ferroptosis

Using erastin or RSL3-induced ferroptosis in HT-1080 cells, CQPP reported increased LD polarity through decreased green emission, increased red emission, and a rising ratiometric signal. FLIM measurements similarly showed that LD lifetime regions changed during ferroptosis, while the nuclear lifetime remained comparatively stable. The authors use this difference to distinguish ferroptosis from apoptosis, where nuclear morphology and lifetime change more clearly. ^[polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:343-385, polaritysensitive-ratiometric-fluorescence-probe-for-monitoring-lipid-dropletsnucleus-change-during-ferroptosis.md:406-457]
