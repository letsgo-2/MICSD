# {BiW8}-associated suppression of DNA repair and replication programs

{BiW8}-associated suppression of DNA repair and replication programs refers to the transcriptional downregulation of genes involved in [[DNA repair]], [[DNA replication]], and related cell-cycle functions in MG63 osteosarcoma cells after treatment with `{BiW8}`. In the study, this effect emerged from RNA sequencing of MG63 cells treated with `127.30 uM {BiW8}` for 24 hours and was presented as one component of the compound’s broader antitumor mechanism. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:430-452]

## Transcriptomic evidence

RNA sequencing identified `3737` upregulated and `3396` downregulated differentially expressed genes in `{BiW8}`-treated MG63 cells relative to controls. Functional enrichment analysis of the top `3000` upregulated and downregulated genes found KEGG pathway enrichment, and nearly all genes annotated to mismatch repair, base excision repair, DNA replication, cell cycle, and [[glutathione metabolism]] belonged to the top `3000` downregulated genes. The authors interpreted this pattern as evidence that `{BiW8}` impaired DNA repair, replication, and glutathione metabolism in MG63 cells. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:430-452]

## Downregulated genes

The study tabulated `21` downregulated differentially expressed genes associated with DNA repair and replication: `APEX1`, `APEX2`, `EXO1`, `FEN1`, `LIG1`, `MSH2`, `MSH3`, `MUTYH`, `NEIL2`, `NTHL1`, `OGG1`, `PARP1`, `PCNA`, `POLD1`, `POLD3`, `POLE`, `POLE2`, `RPA1`, `RPA3`, `UNG`, and `XRCC1`. These genes were grouped by the authors under a DNA repair/replication category in the RNA-seq results. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:458-486]

## Validation by qRT-PCR

To validate the RNA-seq findings, the authors performed qRT-PCR on selected genes with larger absolute fold changes. Among the validated DNA repair and replication-related targets were `PARP1`, `NTHL1`, `MSH2`, `POLD1`, `POLE2`, `RPA3`, `XRCC1`, and `LIG1`, supporting the reported downregulation of this program after `{BiW8}` treatment. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:458-500]

## Relationship to other {BiW8} effects

The paper situates suppression of DNA repair and replication alongside other `{BiW8}`-associated antitumor effects, including [[pyroptosis]], reduced intracellular [[glutathione|GSH]], and increased [[reactive oxygen species|ROS]]. The authors also highlighted decreased expression of DNA repair/replication enzymes such as `PARP1`, `MSH2`, and `LIG1` in their summary schematic of mechanisms. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:502-552]

The authors further discussed a possible connection between redox disruption and DNA damage. They noted prior studies showing that decreased GSH can lead to ROS accumulation, oxidative DNA damage, and activation of the DNA damage response, and proposed that excessive ROS-induced DNA damage resulting from `{BiW8}`-associated GSH reduction may have contributed to cell death in MG63 cells. They therefore suggested that `{BiW8}` may both cause DNA damage and inhibit the DNA repair system in this model. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:517-540]

## Significance within the study

In the study’s conclusion, inhibition of DNA repair was included among the multiple antitumor mechanisms identified for `{BiW8}` by transcriptome analysis and chemical simulation. This suppression was presented as part of a broader multi-mechanism antitumor profile in MG63 cells that also included suppression of proliferation, migration, and invasion. ^[biw8o30-exerts-antitumor-effect-by-triggering-pyroptosis-and-upregulating-reactive-oxygen-species.md:553-567]
