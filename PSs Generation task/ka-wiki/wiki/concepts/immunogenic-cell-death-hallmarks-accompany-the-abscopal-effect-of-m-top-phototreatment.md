# Immunogenic cell death hallmarks accompany the abscopal effect of M-TOP phototreatment

In the bilateral subcutaneous xenograft model used to test distant tumor control, [[M-TOP]] phototreatment prolonged survival relative to control groups and inhibited the growth of both the treated primary tumor and the untreated distal tumor. The study describes this distant anti-tumor activity as an abscopal effect of localized M-TOP phototreatment. ^[mitochondria-targeting-type-i-photodrug-harnessing-caspase-3-activity-for-pyroptotic-oncotherapy.md:343-350]

The distal-tumor abscopal effect was accompanied by immune features associated with [[immunogenic cell death]]. Flow-cytometry analysis of distal tumors showed enrichment of mature [[dendritic cell|dendritic cells]], and immunofluorescence staining of distal tumors detected [[HMGB1]] and [[calreticulin|CRT]] in the `M-TOP+Light` group. The authors interpret HMGB1 and CRT in distal tumors as indicating an association with immunogenic cell death, a form of programmed cell death that triggers an immune response against dying cells. ^[mitochondria-targeting-type-i-photodrug-harnessing-caspase-3-activity-for-pyroptotic-oncotherapy.md:349-356]

This finding fits the broader immune profile reported for M-TOP phototreatment. In vitro, supernatants from M-TOP-phototreated cancer cells significantly enhanced dendritic-cell maturation and inflammatory cytokine release, suggesting that M-TOP-mediated [[pyroptosis|pyroptotic]] cancer cell death can activate anti-tumor immune responses. ^[mitochondria-targeting-type-i-photodrug-harnessing-caspase-3-activity-for-pyroptotic-oncotherapy.md:227-237]

The paper also frames the in vivo work around enhancement of the cancer-immunity cycle. Based on the in vitro anticancer and dendritic-cell activation findings, the authors hypothesized that M-TOP phototreatment could promote dendritic-cell maturation, antigen presentation, T-cell priming, and induction of immunogenic cell death in tumors. In the primary-tumor model, M-TOP plus light increased intratumoral `CD4+` and `CD8+` T-cell infiltration, more than doubled activated `CD8+CD69+` T cells relative to controls, nearly eliminated [[regulatory T cell|Tregs]], increased mature dendritic cells, and reduced `M2`-type macrophages. ^[mitochondria-targeting-type-i-photodrug-harnessing-caspase-3-activity-for-pyroptotic-oncotherapy.md:252-257, mitochondria-targeting-type-i-photodrug-harnessing-caspase-3-activity-for-pyroptotic-oncotherapy.md:265-315]

Within the study, the association between abscopal tumor inhibition and immunogenic cell death hallmarks is therefore based on two distal-tumor observations: enrichment of mature dendritic cells and detection of HMGB1 and CRT after localized M-TOP phototreatment. These markers are presented as accompanying, rather than independently proving, the abscopal effect. ^[mitochondria-targeting-type-i-photodrug-harnessing-caspase-3-activity-for-pyroptotic-oncotherapy.md:349-356]
