# NaHCO3 nanoparticle treatment also triggers immunogenic cell death signals

`NaHCO3` nanoparticles (`NaHCO3 NPs`) were reported to trigger [[immunogenic cell death|immunogenic cell death (ICD)]] signals in addition to inducing [[pyroptosis]] in 4T1 tumor cells. The study frames ICD here as a form of programmed cell death that provokes immune responses through release or exposure of [[damage-associated molecular patterns|DAMPs]]. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:319-324]

In the treated-cell group, the relative extracellular `ATP` level was about `5.9-fold` higher than the control group and about `5.5-fold` higher than the free sodium bicarbonate (`SoB`) group. The authors also detected notable surface exposure of `CRT` in the `NaHCO3 NPs` group. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:324-328]

The paper interprets these `ATP` and `CRT` changes as ICD-associated DAMP signals that can promote antigen presentation and thereby contribute to anti-tumor immune responses. It explicitly concludes that `NaHCO3 NPs` not only initiate pyroptotic tumor-cell killing and inflammatory-molecule release, but also lead to ICD and DAMP secretion. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:328-332]

Within the broader mechanism proposed in the study, ICD induction is paired with pyroptosis and regulation of [[lactic acid metabolism]] to enhance anti-tumor immunity. In the in vivo immune analysis, the authors state that pyroptosis, ICD induction, and lactic-acid consumption together motivated evaluation of immune activation, and they associate these effects with increased [[dendritic cell|dendritic-cell]] maturation and stronger adaptive immune responses in tumor-bearing mice. ^[sodium-bicarbonate-nanoparticles-for-amplified-cancer-immunotherapy-by-inducing-pyroptosis-and-regulating-lactic-acid-metabolism.md:389-405]
