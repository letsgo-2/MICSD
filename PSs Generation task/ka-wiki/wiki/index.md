## Concepts

- **[[biw8-as-a-10-nuclear-heteroatom-modified-polyoxometalate-antitumor-compound|{BiW8} as a 10-nuclear heteroatom-modified polyoxometalate antitumor compound]]** — The source introduces {BiW8}, a structurally characterized 10-nuclear heteroatom-modified {BiW8O30} polyoxometalate with stronger antiproliferative activity against MG63 osteosarcoma cells than its raw materials and several comparison POMs.
- **[[biw8-associated-suppression-of-dna-repair-and-replication-programs|{BiW8}-associated suppression of DNA repair and replication programs]]** — Functional enrichment and validation data indicate that {BiW8} reduces expression of DNA repair and replication genes such as PARP1, MSH2, LIG1, POLD1, POLE2, and XRCC1, suggesting impaired genome-maintenance capacity in MG63 cells.
- **[[biw8-driven-glutathione-depletion-elevates-intracellular-ros|{BiW8}-driven glutathione depletion elevates intracellular ROS]]** — {BiW8} approximately doubles ROS levels in MG63 cells while sharply reducing cellular GSH over time, and chemical assays indicate it reacts with GSH but not GSSG, linking glutathione depletion to oxidative-stress elevation.
- **[[1zn-et-combines-exceptionally-low-dark-toxicity-with-strong-photodynamic-selectivity|1Zn-Et combines exceptionally low dark toxicity with strong photodynamic selectivity]]** — The peripherally substituted zinc complex 1Zn-Et had very low dark toxicity in HeLa and 3T3 cells together with submicromolar phototoxicity, yielding a reported HeLa TC50/IC50 therapeutic ratio above 3000.
- **[[3-pdtpyp-as-the-lead-antimycobacterial-photodynamic-sensitizer|3-PdTPyP as the lead antimycobacterial photodynamic sensitizer]]** — The meta-substituted tetra-cationic palladium(II) porphyrin 3-PdTPyP is the most effective photosensitizer in the study against rapidly growing mycobacteria.
